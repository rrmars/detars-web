You can afford to open a small kiosk and still struggle to pay yourselves. For these two Malaysian sisters, I would test an own brand first—but **neither option is ready for a lease**. The own-brand budget needs RM 115,121.50 including a working reserve. Its slow-start example still breaks that reserve target. The franchise baseline needs RM 190,000, above the maximum RM 170,000 funding ceiling.

The scenario keeps RM 120,000 savings, at most RM 50,000 borrowing and an April 2027 KL/PJ opening. Borrowing is zero in the baseline, not automatically available money. Research dates from November 6, 2026; recovery verification finished November 7, 2026 MYT. Prices, rules and availability have not been refreshed for this guide.

At 90 cups/day and an assumed RM 8.15 realised average, own-brand cash after RM 4,000 combined monthly owner drawings is only RM 1,441.95 before income tax. Franchise-style cash is -RM 880.50. Go ahead with low-cost recipe and paid-demand tests, not a deposit on a long lease. A sensible validation hurdle here is 110 paid cups/day at the assumed ticket—not the landlord's whole-mall footfall.

## First protect the opening cash and your own safety net

Own-brand opening cash is RM 85,121.50. It includes RM 15,600.00 assumed refundable deposits and RM 5,300.00 prepaid first-month rent. Add RM 30,000 working reserve and just RM 4,878.50 remains outside the plan. Personal emergency money must be separate; that small remainder is not a comfortable household buffer.

The build assumes 150 sqft at RM 175/sqft, not a quoted unit. ## Why the franchise headline does not solve the budget

[Coolblog's investment page](https://www.coolblog.com.my/franchise-what-is-the-investment) says from RM 130,000, a RM 30,000 fee, a 5-year term and RM 1,357 monthly royalty. Its [FAQ](https://www.coolblog.com.my/franchise-faq) gives about RM 160,000 inclusive average capital, RM 1,280 royalty and no advertising fee. Both pages are undated and conflict. The model uses the inclusive average and higher royalty; it does **not** add the franchise fee again.

A separate reserve is conservative because the amount of working capital already included is unspecified. With it, this baseline is RM 20,000 above maximum funded resources and RM 70,000 above savings. The lower headline could work only after fee inclusion and every exclusion are resolved; it is not evidence of an affordable all-in package.

[Tealive's official support article](https://tealive.freshdesk.com/en/support/solutions/articles/153000091021-how-can-i-join-tealive-as-a-franchisee-in-malaysia-) gives RM 400,000–650,000, modified November 27, 2023; it is historical screening, not a kiosk offer. [WargaBiz's July 10, 2023 article](https://wargabiz.com.my/2023/07/10/cost-to-open-a-franchise-like-tealive-or-7-eleven/) gives Each A Cup RM 100,000+ capital, RM 20,000 fee and 5% royalty. Fee inclusion and current terms remain unknown. Only two first-party brand-level investment disclosures were established, not three complete current packages. Check registered-franchise status and agreement terms before pursuing any brand.

## What the break-even figures really mean

Every case assumes 30 trading days/month, the same realised price, volumes and channel mix for both brands. Own contribution is RM 5.97/cup; franchise-style contribution is RM 5.62. The franchise adds a **hypothetical 25% food-supply uplift**, unchanged packaging and the flat royalty. This is not Coolblog recipe economics or an earnings forecast. No brand-led sales uplift is assumed.

| Case | Cups/day | Sales RM/month | Operating cash RM/month | After owners/debt RM/month | Bare / livelihood break-even cups/day | Payback before / after drawings, months |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Own conservative | 50 | 12,225.00 | -1,726.30 | -5,726.30 | 60 / 82 | None / None |
| Own base | 90 | 22,005.00 | 5,441.95 | 1,441.95 | 60 / 82 | 15.64 / 59.03 |
| Own good | 140 | 34,230.00 | 14,402.27 | 10,402.27 | 60 / 82 | 5.91 / 8.18 |
| Franchise conservative | 50 | 12,225.00 | -3,619.66 | -7,619.66 | 72 / 96 | None / None |
| Franchise base | 90 | 22,005.00 | 3,119.50 | -880.50 | 72 / 96 | 51.29 / None |
| Franchise good | 140 | 34,230.00 | 11,543.45 | 7,543.45 | 72 / 96 | 13.86 / 21.21 |

The own-brand livelihood threshold is 82 cups/day under these assumptions, not a comfortable sales target. Break-even rounds fixed cash divided by days and positive contribution upward to a whole cup. Livelihood break-even adds drawings and debt. Payback divides opening cash, including tied-up deposits, by positive monthly surplus; reserve is excluded because it remains cash. No deposit refund or resale is credited. Nonpositive surplus means no payback.

These are stabilised-month ratios, not elapsed recovery promises. They exclude income tax, depreciation, separately unquoted permanent halal staffing/certified kitchen, terminal reinstatement and major breakdowns. The after-drawings measure deducts debt service when borrowing changes. It is not an investment return calculation.

## Sort permissions and halal before a commitment

A two-owner business needs a genuine partnership or company, not a jointly labelled sole proprietorship. [SSM's business fees](https://www.ssm.com.my/Pages/Services/Registration-of-Business-(ROB)/table-of-fees/Table-of-Fees.aspx) distinguish RM 30/year for a personal-name sole proprietor and RM 60/year for a trade name or ordinary partnership. [Company incorporation](https://www.ssm.com.my/Pages/Services/Registration-of-Company-(ROC)/Table-of-Fees.aspx) is RM 1,000, with separate name reservation RM 50 per 30 days or part. Professional costs are additional. Ordinary partnership brings personal liability; agree roles, drawings, ownership and exit terms.

[DBKL's published tariff](https://www.dbkl.gov.my/jabatan/jabatan-pelesenan-dan-pembangunan-perniagaan) lists food/beverage premises at RM 3/m² annually. Illuminated signs are RM 100 for the first m² plus RM 15 per additional m²; non-illuminated signs RM 200 plus RM 20. Those counterintuitive labels are as published—not a final kiosk invoice. Classification, minimums, area/faces and extras are unknown. [MBPJ's licensing index](https://www.mbpj.gov.my/ms/node/63) does not establish the actual fee here. Use the council for the address, not both; confirm Malay-prominent, DBP-verified artwork and landlord approval.

[Food-handler requirements](https://www.mbpj.gov.my/sites/default/files/12._perutusan_datuk_bandar_disember_2023.14.12.2023.latest.pdf) include recognised training and anti-typhoid vaccination. The budget covers 4 handlers at RM 50 training plus RM 100 vaccine each, not official provider prices. Check recognised providers, health evidence and MOH/BKKM premises registration.

[JAKIM's amended manual](https://www.halal.gov.my/pdf/pekeliling/PPSHMBIL1-2026.pdf), circular dated February 12, 2026 and effective April 1, 2026, lists RM 20 domestic processing and RM 100/premise/year for a kiosk certificate. More importantly, a new application needs 3 months of full operation; kiosk requirements include permanent Malaysian Muslim staffing throughout shifts, IHCS and a certified preparation kitchen. Owner eligibility, roster and kitchen arrangements are unconfirmed and not fully costed. An April opening would normally mean application around July, then assessment—not promised day-one certification.

Certified ingredients do not certify the outlet. If certification is launch-critical, settle the staffing and kitchen route first. Do not use a halal logo or imply certification before approval, including under a franchise brand.

## FAQ

### Does fitting the opening budget mean we should sign?

No. The slow-start cash falls below the reserve target, personal emergency cash is separate, and equipment, lease and compliance costs remain unquoted. Validate paid sales and replace allowances before signing.

### Can we use a franchise fee as the whole startup cost?

No. The inclusive capital figure already includes its fee, while the cheaper headline has unresolved inclusions. The baseline with a separate reserve exceeds your funding cap. Ask for a complete current agreement and cost schedule before comparing again.

### Do two part-timers let the sisters keep other full-time jobs?

Not in this plan. The sisters still cover most opening hours, preparation and backup. Drawings are compensation cash for both owners, not an extra manager's salary.

### Can halal certification be ready on opening day?

It is not established here. The new-application operation period, permanent staffing, preparation kitchen and approval process must all fit. Treat certification as operating design, not a small fee to add at the end.
