**Worried about spending weeks on a role that cannot hire you in Mexico? Check the country rules, who signs your contract and the Mexico pay band before a long interview exercise. The jobs below are a 2026-11-05 snapshot, not current vacancies or a promise of an April 2027 start.**

**A few tempting details need care. doola does not name Mexico individually. LATAM or Americas wording does not confirm local hiring arrangements. Wikimedia’s US salary is not Mexico pay. RevenueCat’s $205K header leaves currency, period and Mexico treatment unclear. Five years of experience does not replace the senior or specialist skills a role asks for.**

## Start with the work you can show and the date you need

This plan is for a product designer with five years of experience and good English who wants to stay in Guadalajara. You do not need to assume relocation or US/EU work authorisation. Before choosing roles, check your portfolio, strongest domain, current pay, notice period, Mexican work permission and tax eligibility. Aim to start on 2027-04-01, with signed terms ideally accepted by 2027-03-05. Use those dates to plan backwards, not as a promise that hiring will finish on time.

The pay and hiring information was checked on 2026-11-05. Treat it as a starting point, then check the vacancy and terms again before applying. You can use the plan without opening accounts or contacting anyone until you are ready.

## Choose a lane before sending more applications

Start with Mexico-eligible roles that work on US time zones. Keep a smaller Europe/global lane too. Show work you shipped, how you collaborate in English and the hours you can reliably overlap. Focus on mid-level roles; try senior roles only where your actual work fits. A useful starting split is 60% direct US-company roles, 25% agencies/contractor networks and 15% Europe/global companies. Adjust it to your replies, not because those percentages guarantee results.

A foreign headquarters is less useful than a clear answer to “Can you hire me in Mexico?” A direct US-company contractor role may offer less protection than Mexican employment through an employer of record (EOR). The EOR provides the employment setup; it is not a separate job board. Always check what “remote” means for your country.

Set aside 12–15 hours a week while employed, or 18–25 if you have the time. You could offer 09:00–17:00 Guadalajara for US teams. An earlier 07:00–15:00 window may help with Europe, but only if you can sustain it. Agree actual meeting hours and check seasonal clock changes. You do not have to promise a full European office day.

## Leave the tax decision to your accountant, not a salary calculator

**The tax details below reflect 2026 rules, not a promise about 2027. Before signing, ask a Mexican accountant about your residence, existing job, other income, equity and actual working arrangement. Money arriving from abroad is not automatically tax-free.**

| Question | What the rules below can tell you | What to work through with your accountant |
|---|---|---|
| RESICO eligibility | [SAT LISR article 113-E](https://wwwmatnp.sat.gob.mx/articulo/58780/articulo-113-e): qualifying activity has an MXN 3.5 million annual cap with aggregation/exclusions. Company ownership can affect eligibility; narrow exceptions do not make ordinary equity automatically harmless. Wages are a separate category. | Check residence, prior/current receipts, salary/interest, shareholder status and proposed equity before choosing a regime. |
| RESICO ISR basis | [SAT historical release](https://www.gob.mx/sat/prensa/requisitos-y-obligaciones-para-personas-fisicas-que-busquen-permanecer-en-el-resico-003-2024) supports 1%–2.5% on qualifying collected income excluding IVA, without business-expense deductions against that ISR base. Not a universal 1% or all-in tax rate; no personalised bracket calculation. | Review CFDI, collections, annual MXN receipts and separate IVA/social-protection costs. |
| Filing conflict | [RMF 2026](https://www.sat.gob.mx/minisitio/NormatividadRMFyRGCE/documentos2026/rmf/rmf/RMF_2026-DOF-28122025.pdf), published 2025-12-28, rule 3.13.7 relieves the annual return for RESICO income through definitive monthly payments; 3.13.8 preserves other-category obligations. [SAT annual guidance](https://wwwnp.sat.gob.mx/minisitio/DeclaracionAnual/Personas/quienes_deben_presentarla.html) confirms the distinction. The 2024 checklist must not override this relief. | Keep filing monthly. Other income may still need an annual return. Have your accountant check all relevant amendments and the published 2027 rules before relying on this treatment. |
| Registration and transition | [SAT article 113-G](https://wwwmat.sat.gob.mx/articulo/58979/articulo-113-g) lists RFC/CFDI and obligations; RMF 2026 transitional article 15 deferred RESICO e.firma/active Buzón requirements to 2027-01-01. | Prepare before onboarding and check your own deadline again. An older checklist is not enough to establish a November deadline. |
| Ordinary business/professional regime | [SAT monthly service](https://wwwmat.sat.gob.mx/declaracion/33006/presenta-tu-declaracion-de-actividades-empresariales-y-servicios-profesionales%2C-arrendamiento-e-iva%2C-personas-fisicas-de-2025-en-adelante-%28simulador%29) uses income and authorised deductions for provisional ISR and definitive IVA. Regular professional/business income also has annual obligations. | Compare personal facts and supported deductions; RESICO's gross-receipt percentage cannot be reused here. Price ongoing accounting, not just registration. |
| Export IVA | [LIVA article 29](https://wwwmat.sat.gob.mx/articulo/80321/articulo-29) and [regulation article 58](https://wwwmat.sat.gob.mx/articulo/87895/articulo-58) condition 0% export treatment. Besides listed categories, independent personal services wholly used abroad can be relevant; the IT list is not the only route. A foreign customer/USD invoice alone proves nothing. | Map actual design service, use abroad, customer's establishment, banking/payment trail and platform payout. Otherwise standard IVA may apply. RESICO does not settle IVA. |

Ask your accountant to confirm the current brackets under article 113-E and calculate your own position. The rate band alone is not enough. Use the SAT guidance above to prepare questions, not as a complete legal check or a personal tax calculation.

Before a final interview, ask whether Mexico residency, work permission or travel creates a condition. Get the signer and payer’s names and confirm employee or contractor status. Ask for the Mexico gross band, currency, payment dates, leave, health cover, relevant IMSS and equipment, fees, notice and termination terms. Check IP transfer on payment, confidentiality, portfolio permission, liability, exclusivity and dispute handling. If the client cancels its project, does your pay stop? Agree meeting hours, a take-home time limit and onboarding steps before investing heavily.

**Across the sprint, aim for 76 tailored applications and 95 new messages.** You can test a funnel of 8–15 screens → 4–7 portfolio rounds → 2–4 finals → 1–2 offers, but those are working targets, not success probabilities or promises. Track each role’s URL, country rules, pay, contract, application date/channel, stage, reply and next action. Use your actual replies to decide where to spend more time.

Build two deep cases around shipped work. Explain the user problem, constraints, your own role, alternatives, engineering collaboration, decisions and honest results. Show before and after, and say what changed because of your work. Never invent metrics or disclose confidential work. Prepare a 2-minute summary and a 20-minute English walkthrough for each case. Separate your contribution from the team’s, test mobile and prototype links, and add a short systems/accessibility example only if it helps.
