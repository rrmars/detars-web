**You do not want months of study and a nursing-registration pass to leave you short of visa English points. My pick for this Kathmandu nurse is IELTS Academic on computer, aiming for 7 in every skill in one sitting. Keep NPR 79,000 available for a first attempt, a full retake and modest travel and contingency allowances—not a guaranteed pass.**

This plan is for a Nepali applicant, age 27, with four years of nursing experience, studying around shifts in Kathmandu. It gives you 20 weeks × 8 hours = 160 hours, with a first sitting in late March 2027 and April as retake contingency. Rules and published prices are dated November 6, 2026; no future seats or prices are promised. Chinese-speaking nurses can use the same comparison, but Nepal prices, local access and this applicant’s qualification profile do not transfer to them.

IELTS has a corroborated Kathmandu price, short advertised computer-result timing and a live speaking examiner. Your nursing experience may help conversation, but the reading and writing are academic. Start with official samples. If microphone speaking and typing suit you better, trial PTE. If healthcare role-play feels much more natural, trial OET Nursing after checking its payable fee and venue. Choose one test after the diagnostic week; do not keep preparing for all of them.

This is a fit judgment, not a claim that IELTS is easiest for most nurses. No reliable official comparison established that ranking. Lower-looking recalibrated scores do not mean lower language proficiency. Passing English also does not establish that your Nepal qualification meets nursing-registration requirements: registration, a skills assessment and visa eligibility are separate decisions.

## Registration: use the scores for your test date

The NMBA standard took effect on March 18, 2025. Its April 2026 transition policy gives the following targets for tests taken **on or after April 23, 2026**. Use these for the planned sitting—not old PTE 66 or old OET letter-grade advice. The recalibration did not lower required proficiency.

| Accepted test | Listening | Reading | Writing | Speaking | Overall / total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| IELTS Academic | 7 | 7 | 6.5 | 7 | 7 |
| PTE Academic | 58 | 59 | 60 | 76 | 63 |
| TOEFL iBT Australia | 22 | 22 | 23 | 24 | 91 |
| OET Nursing | 350 | 360 | 350 | 360 | Not required |

Every required component and overall minimum matters; a strong total cannot cancel a low skill. IELTS General Training is not this registration route. Use an approved test-centre version, not an at-home test. NMBA also accepts Cambridge C1 Advanced and C2 Proficiency; this guide compares the requested alternatives.

See the [NMBA standard](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?chksum=7XlS%2FQCh6z21A%2FrsD5vd8g%3D%3D&dbid=AP&record=WD24%2F34379), [transition policy](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?chksum=cfj%2B9cu%2FNODqTn37yTOzrw%3D%3D&dbid=AP&record=WD26%2F36006) and [accepted-test version guidance](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/Registration/Registration-Standards/English-language-skills/Accepted-English-language-tests).

### Combining results can help registration—but not automatically your visa

NMBA permits **at most two sittings of the same test within 12 months**, meeting all passing targets across the pair. Do not mix providers. Each sitting must also meet these floors:

| Test | Each-sitting floors: Listening / Reading / Writing / Speaking | Each-sitting overall condition |
|---|---|---|
| IELTS Academic | 6.5 / 6.5 / 6.5 / 6.5 | At least 7 |
| PTE Academic | 53 / 54 / 60 / 66 | At least 63 |
| TOEFL iBT Australia | 19 / 19 / 23 / 22 | At least 91 |
| OET Nursing | 320 / 340 / 350 / 350 | All four subtests each time |

These are combining floors, not pass scores. In Listening/Reading/Writing/Speaking order, IELTS 7.5/7.5/6.5/6.5 can combine with 6.5/7.5/6.5/7 only if both overall bands are at least 7 and the dates qualify. A 6 in either sitting breaks that pair’s eligibility.

Eligible IELTS One Skill Retake (OSR) does not count as an extra sitting under the [NMBA FAQ](https://www.nursingmidwiferyboard.gov.au/Codes-Guidelines-Statements/FAQ/fact-sheet-english-language-skills-registration-standard). Provider eligibility includes taking it within 60 days. If relying on two full sittings plus OSR, all must fit within 12 months. Check full booking eligibility and the eventual visa’s acceptance separately; the budget below reserves a full retake, not an assumed cheaper OSR.

### Do not leave registration too late

Normally, apply within **two years of the test**; when combining, count from the **earlier sitting**. Nepal is not on the standard’s recognised-country list, so Kathmandu employment alone does not extend the result.

Older-result exceptions are conditional. The work route requires continuous qualifying English-language work in a recognised country, at least 26 weeks full-time equivalent per year, and application within 12 months of finishing. The registered-practitioner route starts within 12 months of testing. The study route starts within 12 months of testing, requires a continuous Board-approved program with successful semesters and no unscheduled breaks, and application within 12 months of completion. Read the full standard before relying on any exception.

## Visa English: protect the writing score

**Meeting NMBA English is not automatically worth 10 visa points.** For tests taken on or after August 7, 2025, Home Affairs uses these component thresholds. Each cell is Listening / Reading / Writing / Speaking; all components must qualify.

| Test | Competent: 0 English points | Proficient: 10 English points | Superior: 20 English points |
|---|---|---|---|
| IELTS Academic | 6 / 6 / 6 / 6 | 7 / 7 / 7 / 7 | 8 / 8 / 8 / 8 |
| PTE Academic | 47 / 48 / 51 / 54 | 58 / 59 / 69 / 76 | 69 / 70 / 85 / 88 |
| TOEFL iBT Australia | 16 / 16 / 19 / 19 | 22 / 22 / 26 / 24 | 26 / 27 / 30 / 28 |
| OET | 290 / 310 / 290 / 330 | 350 / 360 / 380 / 360 | 390 / 400 / 420 / 400 |

Only the highest tier counts: 10 and 20 are not added. Competent is a baseline, not an English-points award. These are not your total skilled-visa points, invitation prospects or proof of eligibility. See Home Affairs’ [competent](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/help-support/meeting-our-requirements/english-language/competent-english), [proficient](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/help-support/meeting-our-requirements/english-language/proficient-english) and [superior](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/help-support/meeting-our-requirements/english-language/superior-english) tables.

For a combined registration/proficient target, IELTS Writing rises from 6.5 to 7; PTE from 60 to 69; OET from 350 to 380; TOEFL from 23 to 26. Keep the other skill targets and NMBA overall requirements too.

For invited points-tested pathways, qualifying English generally belongs within the three years before invitation, with points established then. Broad Home Affairs pages describe application timing depending on subclass. **Your visa subclass is unspecified: check its exact rule before relying on either date.** The [Migration Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F1996B03551/latest/text) supplied an October 2026 compilation ending October 31, 2026; amendments after that period were not confirmed. Treat the points and timing above as dated planning guidance, not a checked later compilation.

**Do not carry NMBA combining into migration.** Blanket Home Affairs permission to combine two full sittings was not established. Aim for all visa components in one complete sitting unless the exact visa explicitly accepts your retake arrangement. OSR is accepted only for eligible visas; this is not permission to combine arbitrary full tests.

### TOEFL: select the Australia version, not just the brand

Choose **“Taking TOEFL for Australia”**, producing TOEFL iBT Australia. The ordinary global test introduced a 1–6 scale on January 21, 2026. Do not convert it yourself or assume a comparable total makes it acceptable. The [ETS Australia route](https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/where-to-study/study-in-australia.html) and [bulletin for July 2026–June 2027](https://www.ets.org/pdfs/toefl/toefl-ibt-bulletin.pdf) distinguish the approved Australia test from the updated global one.

ETS reports and verifies scores for two years. That issuer limit is not the migration three-year look-back: save your official report promptly and check practical verification requirements before relying on the longer window.

## What each test will feel like

| Test | Format | What to try before choosing |
|---|---|---|
| IELTS Academic | Computer Listening/Reading/Writing, with Kathmandu also advertising Writing on Paper; live speaking examiner. Academic texts, graph/process description and essay. | Choose real conversation if it suits you; practise academic writing rather than clinical notes. |
| PTE Academic | Centre computer, recorded microphone speaking, typed writing and integrated tasks. Use post-August-2025 preparation. | Try timed tasks and equipment. Speaking 76 is a serious registration target, not an easy shortcut. |
| TOEFL iBT Australia | Centre computer, recorded speaking and integrated academic reading/listening/writing. | Try lecture notes and microphone answers using Australia-specific materials, not the new global format. |
| OET Nursing | Healthcare listening/reading, nursing-specific letter writing and patient/carer role-plays with a human interlocutor. Paper or centre computer depends on venue; computer speaking may use video. | Familiar situations may help, but clinical knowledge does not replace language and communication criteria. |

Use [IELTS Academic samples and examiner comments](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/sample-test-questions/academic-test), [Pearson’s product guidance](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-academic/) and [Cambridge’s OET format guide](https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/exams-and-tests/oet/test-overview/) to test your fit. No population-matched pass-rate comparison supports calling one test easiest.

## Keep a full retake reserve

You do not need UKVI for the stated Australian route. Standard IELTS Academic computer testing is the budgeted option; prices may change before you sit.

| Item | Basis | NPR |
|---|---|---:|
| First IELTS Academic computer test | 1 × 36,000 | 36,000 |
| Full retake reserve | 1 × 36,000 | 36,000 |
| Official public preparation | Free samples; no subscription assumed | 0 |
| Travel/meals allowance | Estimate: 1,000 × 2 visits | 2,000 |
| Price-change/incidental buffer | Estimate, not a provider charge | 5,000 |
| Recommended reserve | 72,000 + 0 + 2,000 + 5,000 | 79,000 |

The fee-only minimum with a retake is **NPR 72,000**. Pass first time and keep the unused reserve. Without the retake, using NPR 1,000 travel and the same buffer gives **NPR 42,000**. Separate-day speaking travel, bank charges, paid feedback and lessons may add cost; tutor prices were not confirmed.

A British Council transfer 5–14 days ahead costs 25% × NPR 36,000 = **NPR 9,000 extra**, not included in NPR 79,000. Transfer 15+ days ahead is free. Choose your shift-compatible date early rather than assuming the buffer covers a late move.

If you choose another test:

- PTE: two exam fees total **USD 497.20**. One official scored practice at **USD 35.99** takes that to **USD 533.19**, before local travel, tax, card FX or future price changes. [Smart Prep](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-academic/preparation/) is free but later needs a learner account; free guided practice is not the paid scored mock.

[Australia-labelled preparation](https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/prepare-australia.html) advertises free practice/planner resources, but complete account-free access was not confirmed. No AUD/USD-to-NPR conversion is assumed. An unknown alternative-test fee is not evidence that it is cheaper.

## A study plan that fits your shifts

Start Monday November 9, 2026 and finish Sunday March 28, 2027. Aim for the week of March 22–28, 2027 if a suitable first-sitting seat exists. These are planning windows, not advertised dates.

Normal week: Writing 3h + Speaking 2h + Listening 1h + Reading 1h + error-log work 1h = 8h. Use four 1h sessions and two 2h blocks. Mock weeks replace these hours; they do not add more. Official public IELTS samples need no account. [British Council preparation](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/exam/ielts/prepare) is optional; IELTS Ready Premium comes with a booking, and login may be needed later.

| Week | Dates | Focus and output | Hours |
|---|---|---|---:|
| 1 | November 9–15, 2026 | Diagnose, then choose: timed listening/reading, both writing tasks and recorded speaking. Diagnostic 3h + review 3h + PTE/OET trial 2h. | 8h |
| 2 | November 16–22, 2026 | Read marking feedback; identify two writing and two speaking habits. Aim for IELTS 7 each, not just overall 7. | 8h |
| 3 | November 23–29, 2026 | Task 1 graphs/trends: give a clear overview without invented causes; draft, compare and rewrite. | 8h |
| 4 | November 30, 2026–December 6, 2026 | Task 2: answer the question with clear position and paragraphs; timed essay, rewrite and grammar diagnosis. | 8h |
| 5 | December 7–13, 2026 | Reading headings and true/false/not-given; explain errors from the passage while keeping writing/speaking blocks. | 8h |
| 6 | December 14–20, 2026 | Listening spelling, plurals, numbers and distractors; mark before replaying. | 8h |
| 7 | December 21–27, 2026 | Practise all three speaking parts with a partner/recorder; develop answers, not memorised speeches. | 8h |
| 8 | December 28, 2026–January 3, 2027 | First mock: simulation 3h + correction 3h + weak-skill work 2h. Reassess April if several skills are far below target. | 8h |

## Questions you may still have

### Is OET automatically easier because I am a nurse?

No. Familiar clinical scenarios may suit you, but language and communication are still assessed. Try official Nursing tasks and confirm the accepted local mode before paying.

### Can registration combining also give me visa points?

Do not assume so. NMBA’s combining rule does not establish Home Affairs acceptance. Target one complete sitting for visa purposes unless your exact visa permits the relevant arrangement.

### Should I use the ordinary updated TOEFL?

Not for this plan. Choose the Australia pathway and its preparation. A global score conversion is not proof of acceptance.

### What if I pass first time?

Keep the unused full-retake reserve. Save the official report and check registration and visa timing before committing to the next application.
