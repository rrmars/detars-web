You may be able to find a tuition-free programme, but tuition is not the main cash hurdle. **Budget: no — PKR 3,500,000 alone is not enough at the assumed exchange rate of €1 = PKR 325.** It buys **€10,769.23**, below even the **€11,904** blocked-account benchmark in the November 2026 guidance. The prudent first-year funding target below is **€17,257 = PKR 5,608,525**, leaving a **PKR 2,108,525** shortfall.

This guide is for a 23-year-old Pakistani applicant in Lahore with a four-year BS Computer Science completed in 2026, CGPA 3.2/4.0, junior-developer experience, family funding of PKR 3.5 million and salary of PKR 180,000/month. The target is an English-taught Computer Science or Data Science master’s at a German public university in **winter 2027/28**, usually starting **October 2027**. The exchange rate is a planning assumption, not a live quote; the 2027 visa financing requirement is unverified.

**Check Passau and Koblenz first, while building a documented funding plan.** Your illustrative German grade is **2.20**, but only if the official degree-award minimum is 2.0/4.0. Module credits and independent academic work may matter more than the headline CGPA. No ranking, admission probability or guaranteed eligibility is implied.

## Check academic eligibility before paying application fees

Your four-year BS CS (2026), CGPA 3.2/4.0 and junior-developer experience give you a starting point, not an admissions decision. Obtain the transcript, grading scale, detailed module descriptions and an official statement of the **minimum CGPA needed for the degree to be awarded**. A single-module pass mark is not the right denominator.

German grade = 1 + 3 × (maximum − earned) / (maximum − degree-award minimum)<br>With max 4.0, earned 3.2 and minimum 2.0:<br>1 + 3 × (4.0 − 3.2) / (4.0 − 2.0) = **2.20**

If the degree-award minimum is instead 2.5, the result is **2.60**; if it is 1.0, it is **1.80**. These are sensitivity examples, not assumptions about your university. Lower German numbers are better. A “2.5 or better” gate accepts 2.20, but not 2.60; a “2.0 or better” gate does not accept 2.20. The final conversion is non-binding until the evaluator applies its regulations.

**Your practical first step:** prioritise programmes with explicit 2.5–2.7 gates or holistic assessment, and keep strict 2.0 routes out unless the actual conversion qualifies. Map mathematics, algorithms, theoretical CS, programming and statistics credits; work experience does not automatically replace missing academic prerequisites. Take IELTS Academic early; aim for 7.0 overall with strong subscores to cover the shortlist, but follow each programme’s actual certificate rules.

## Work backwards from October 2027

### November–December 2026

- Confirm degree-award minimum and HEC-attested degree/transcript; collect SSC/HSSC, passport, module syllabi, CV and references. Make a credit-mapping sheet.
- Prepare IELTS/TOEFL; take the first sitting early enough for a retake. Book GRE only if a chosen programme actually requires it.
- Set a monthly savings target and identify the funding gap. Do not rely on a German part-time job to obtain the visa.
- Check early winter-selection rounds; a December deadline can belong to the following winter or to summer, so read the intake label.

### January–March 2027

- Reopen every programme’s 2027/28 page and portal, verify new deadlines and test rules, and replace projections with published dates.
- Prepare complete dossiers ahead of the earliest window, including any newly published early non-EU deadlines. Allow several weeks for uni-assist and any VPD before the university’s final deadline.
- Use direct portals where instructed. A VPD is not itself the final university application.

### April–June 2027

- Finish later applications well before cutoff; keep proof of submission and payment. Review summer/winter labels, document receipt dates and any aptitude-test invitation.
- After a suitable offer, reassess 2027 funding, semester fees, insurer quote and the mission’s current visa process. Prepare the visa dossier without waiting until late summer.
- Apply for university housing when eligible; private housing and deposits need separate accessible cash.

### July–October 2027

- When the offer and finances permit, complete the official online visa route, satisfy pre-check requests, then attend the instructed in-person appointment. A queue place is not a visa.
- Obtain blocked confirmation and continuous insurance; meet enrolment and latest-arrival deadlines. Do not buy non-refundable travel before visa approval.
- If timing slips, check an authorised late arrival or next intake; do not assume deferral is automatic.
- On arrival: use address registration, bank/payout activation, insurer notification, enrolment and residence-permit arrangements as a **planning checklist; verify local deadlines**. Plan the residence-permit application before visa expiry, but do not treat this paragraph as a complete statutory timing rule.

These month-by-month milestones are planning advice, not newly announced university or mission deadlines. Actual dates in the shortlist control.

## Prepare the Pakistan-specific visa and document route

### Lahore uses Islamabad · no verified numerical appointment wait

Punjab is in the German Embassy Islamabad district. The student-visa page directs applicants to the [Consular Services Portal](https://digital.diplo.de/studium): document list and upload → preliminary screening/corrections → appointment instructions. The mission says demand exceeds capacity and applications are handled in received order. **No numerical appointment wait was established from the mission text checked on 7 November 2026**; a 2027 appointment or decision date cannot be promised. Appointment/pre-check waiting and post-submission visa processing are different. The national-visa processing statement says timings vary; the Schengen timeline does not apply.

### Preparation checklist — the live personalized portal list controls

- Signed valid passport; scans/copies and biometric photos in the current required format; national-study application and declarations as the portal specifies.
- Admission/enrolment evidence for the correct intake, and any late-arrival permission; applicable tuition-payment proof. The mission explicitly requires previous school/university certificates and transcripts even if the portal labels them optional.
- Degree/transcripts, SSC/HSSC, official grading-scale/degree-award minimum, test certificate and a coherent CV/study motivation dossier. Prepare the latter as supporting material; exact individual requirements are determined by the portal, not this generic checklist.
- Financial proof: currently **€992/month ×12 = €11,904/year** blocked principal. This Pakistan mission amount applies from 1 Jan 2025. **The required amount for 2027 is not established**; recheck then. The mission can ask for up to two years in particular cases. A qualifying German declaration of commitment or recognised official scholarship is another listed route; a family promise alone is not the same thing. **For Pakistani awards, the mission says a Pakistani-university scholarship is insufficient; an HEC scholarship can be accepted.** Check recognition of the specific award; this is not a claim that HEC is the only acceptable scholarship globally.
- Health cover from entry through the transition to regular student insurance; obtain written start dates and the university’s insurer-notification instructions.
- Current adult national-visa fee **€75**, paid in PKR at the mission’s rate, cash at Islamabad. Budgeted PKR amounts are illustrative, not the counter’s tariff. Follow instructions for originals and in-person identity/biometrics steps.

### APS, HEC, copies and translations are different

**No routine APS requirement for a Pakistan-earned qualification was found.** uni-assist defines APS countries as China, Vietnam and India; Pakistan’s list instead requires school certificates, degree/transcript with HEC stamps, and degree-award minimum. Do not purchase an “APS Pakistan” service. A qualification earned in an APS country needs separate checking.

uni-assist’s **HEC-stamped admission documents** are not the same as consular certified copies. The mission’s copy-certification service says no HEC cross-attestation is required *for that service*; it does not override the admission requirement. For uni-assist, official English/German records need no translation; Urdu records require accepted translations. No blanket visa-specific HEC/IBCC/MOFA chain is established here: the 2027 portal checklist must be checked separately.

### Health insurance at age 23

At age 23 in November 2026, you remain under 30 at the target start. As a concrete **2026 public-insurer example**, TK publishes **€110.38 health + €35.91 long-term care = €146.29/month for childless students from 23**. If you have at least one child, the listed example is €110.38 + €30.78 = €141.16; your parental status still needs confirming. The standard student tariff normally ends at age 30, subject to exceptions. These are insurer-specific current rates, not 2027 prices or a promise of eligibility. The budget uses **€160/month** as a 2027 reserve, already inside living costs. Obtain an enrolment-compatible policy and the electronic insurer notification required by the university.

AA’s 21 Aug 2026 guidance says ordinary Schengen travel cover generally does not suffice for a long stay. Arrange suitable incoming cover for any gap before statutory student cover begins; exact Pakistan entry-cover duration remains unverified and must follow the portal. The €100 entry-cover budget is an allowance, not a quote.

### Part-time work is a supplement, not initial financing

The official summary permits **140 full days or 280 half-days annually**, alternatively **up to 20 hours/week**. Do not add the alternatives into two separate quotas. Check the exact accounting and conditions on the residence document before taking a job; this guide does not establish self-employment or academic-job exceptions. No student-job income is included in the budget or assumed available for visa proof.

### Funding test

- Family: PKR 3,500,000 ÷ 325 = **€10,769.23**.
- Current blocked principal alone: €11,904 × 325 = **PKR 3,868,800**; shortfall **PKR 368,800**, before fees and travel.
- Whole-year target shortfall: PKR 5,608,525 − 3,500,000 = **PKR 2,108,525**.
- Salary scenario: ten full months December 2026–September 2027 at PKR 180,000 = **PKR 1,800,000**. Even saving every rupee gives PKR 5,300,000 combined, **PKR 308,525 short**. At a 60% saving rate: 10 × 180,000 × 0.60 = PKR 1,080,000; combined PKR 4,580,000, short **PKR 1,028,525**. Living expenses in Lahore and continuity of employment matter. Ten salary months are not guaranteed savings, and the money may be needed for visa proof before September 2027.
- FX stress at PKR 350/€: €17,257 × 350 = **PKR 6,039,950**; current blocked principal ×350 = **PKR 4,166,400**. At 325, every extra €100 costs PKR 32,500.

### Provider fees: current, not promised for 2027

**Expatrio:** €119 setup + 12 × €9 = **€227 (PKR 73,775)**. **Fintiba:** €159 + 12 × €9.90 = **€277.80 (PKR 90,285)**, €50.80 (PKR 16,510) more on this narrow fee comparison. That pricing applies to new/reopened accounts from 3 November 2025; existing contracts can differ. This is not a provider quality ranking. New Expatrio accounts from 7 July 2026 no longer require the old €100 buffer. Provider fees are separate from blocked principal, insurance, FX spreads and bank/intermediary transfer charges.

Exactly **12 charged months** are budgeted. Expatrio fees accrue from account opening, not simply arrival; opening earlier can add €9 per extra month (PKR 2,925). Get the full funding instruction and bank quote before transferring. A conditional package refund or free entry insurance is not assumed. GRE fees (if using Saarland), test retakes, a new laptop and exceptional document verification are not priced in this base scenario; add actual quotes if needed.

### Tuition exceptions that change the answer

Baden-Württemberg generally charges non-EU students **€1,500 per semester** (exceptions apply), on top of contributions: add 2 × €1,500 = **€3,000 / PKR 975,000** for year one. In Bavaria, do not assume all public universities are free: TUM’s announcement read on 7 November 2026 gives master’s fees of **€4,600 or €6,800 per semester for winter 2027/28**, depending on programme, plus contributions. FAU’s Data Science also lists **€4,000/semester from summer 2027** for chargeable non-EU entrants; it is not a low-cost substitute. FAU, TUM and Baden-Württemberg programmes are not included in this low-cost shortlist budget.

**Decision:** pursue the applications only alongside a documented funding plan. Close at least the scenario gap, maintain an FX/price cushion, and recheck the official 2027 financing requirement before committing money. Do not treat future earnings, an unawarded scholarship or an unapproved sponsor as available funds. The second year also needs financing.
