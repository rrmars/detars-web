**Want the coast, cafés and sunrise without spending the whole weekend in a car? For three friends from Seoul, stay in Seongsan for both nights. The conditional car budget is ₩544,100 each, including a ₩100,000 reserve: ₩55,900 below the ₩600,000 ceiling. Flights are excluded. The catch is driver eligibility, not petrol: the priced Tamra car requires 26+ and a licence held over one year. If nobody qualifies, the bus version is ₩456,100 each.**

Travel is Friday, October 1, 2027 to Sunday, October 3, 2027, with two nights. Unless stated otherwise, prices and source observations date to November 6, 2026, Korea local date; they are not guaranteed travel-date offers. Assume arrival at CJU around 09:00 and departure around 20:30. These are planning windows, not booked flights. Arrive later? Go straight to Seongsan and move the cafés to Sunday. Fly earlier? Remove the final coast stop and work backwards from your airline's airport buffer.

## Not Chuseok, but still a long weekend

[Office Holidays](https://www.officeholidays.com/countries/south-korea/2027) and [timeanddate](https://www.timeanddate.com/holidays/south-korea/2027) place Chuseok on September 15, 2027, with holidays September 14–16. This trip does not overlap. October 3 is National Foundation Day, with Monday, October 4 as the substitute holiday. October 1 is Armed Forces Day, an observance rather than a regular public holiday. Hangeul Day follows on October 9, with a substitute on October 11. Some calendar dates may be preliminary; check later temporary-holiday announcements.

Expect long-weekend booking pressure and Sunday traffic as a planning judgment, not a measured surcharge. Price rooms, flights and the car together when your dates become bookable. One east-coast base is more useful than a cheap room that adds a cross-island drive.

### Rental prices: an age limit changes the answer

[Tamra's dated K3 2024 display](https://www.jejutamra.com/rent/detail.php?modelid=158&sdate=2027-10-01&shour=10:00&edate=2027-10-03&ehour=18:00&fuel=Gasoline&bohum=wanja) covered October 1, 2027 at 10:00 to October 3 at 18:00: 56 hours. Rental ₩164,100 + UCDW ₩73,500 = ₩237,600/car. That is a public display, not held inventory or a reservation. It requires **26+ and licence acquisition over one year**. If all drivers are 24–25, this offer is not available to you. The budget allows ₩300,000 for rental/cover; re-price changed pickup or return hours. Compare the [date-filtered compact listing](https://www.jejutamra.com/rent/?sdate=2027-10-01&edate=2027-10-03&shour=10:00&ehour=18:00&modeltype=c), then confirm actual stock and final terms before paying.

The [current Tamra listing](https://www.jejutamra.com/rent/) instead showed a November 24-hour K3 rate: base ₩28,600 + CDW ₩5,000 = ₩33,600, or base + UCDW ₩30,000 = ₩58,600. Multiplying that cheap day by three would understate this dated trip.

[LOTTE's official information](https://m.lotterentacar.net/hp/eng/reservation/shortInfo/pay.do) starts small-car eligibility at 21, generally requiring one-year licence tenure, with operator-specific foreign-guest/exchanged-licence exceptions. Published standard reference rates in the 1–3-day band are Spark/Morning ₩115,000/day and Avante/K3 ₩143,000/day: ₩345,000–429,000 for three daily units **before unpriced damage waiver**. They are not live discounted Jeju offers; seasonal/holiday variation applies. An eligible younger driver needs a separate all-in quote, not Tamra's price transferred to LOTTE. Do not assume the ₩300,000 allowance covers it.

**UCDW does not mean everything is covered.** Tamra includes third-party/passenger accident insurance but lists tyres, keys, interior/components, roadside services, major negligence and unregistered drivers among exclusions. It lists ₩300,000 claim deductibles for property, bodily and personal liability categories; English own-vehicle deductible wording is ambiguous, so zero excess is not established. **CDW expires after one accident.** Check whether and how cover can continue after a claim before driving again. Read the [requirements](https://www.jejutamra.com/info/?infopage=1) and [insurance guide](https://www.jejutamra.com/info/?infopage=2), register every driver, photograph the car and confirm the actual agreement. Your ₩100,000 reserve is not an accident-liability cap.

Korean licence holders should bring the valid original Class 2 Ordinary or higher for a small passenger car, and satisfy operator age/tenure rules. Jeju alone does not create an IDP requirement. Foreign visitors using LOTTE's IDP route need an original physical booklet-form convention IDP and passport, not a PDF, photo, translation alone or private “international licence”. Accepted conventions are 1949 Geneva or 1968 Vienna, with the appropriate car class and valid dates; the stated one-year-from-entry period also matters. Carry the original home licence as a precaution, not a newly established universal rule. Names must match. A valid Korean licence may serve foreign residents; operator-specific foreign-guest/exchanged-licence exceptions still control. LOTTE also asks for a Korea address/contact and card. No mainland under-30 surcharge is included here.

### Fuel and bus fares: keep the estimates separate

[KNOC Opinet](https://www.opinet.co.kr/user/dopospdrg/dopOsPdrgAreaView.do) reported Jeju regular gasoline at ₩1,887.78/litre, VAT included, for November 5, 2026, accessed November 6. Estimated 300 km ÷ 13 km/l × ₩1,887.78 = ₩43,564.15, rounded to ₩45,000/car. At 450 km ÷ 12 km/l, the same fuel price gives ₩70,791.75, about ₩70,792. Distance and efficiency are assumptions, not measured routing or a future pump-price forecast. Return at the required fuel level.

[Official adult card fares](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/publicTrafficInformation/generalBusSchedule) are ₩1,150 flat for ordinary trunk/branch buses, including 201. Express 101 starts at ₩2,000 for 20 km, adds ₩200 per 5 km band and caps at ₩3,000. Route 600 has separate fares and is not used for this model. Tap in and out with one transport card each. Eligible transfers are within 40 minutes of exit, up to twice; same-number returns are excluded, and multiple passengers on one card do not all get transfer discounts. Allow ₩5,000 each for card acquisition as an estimate. Buy/recharge before boarding; plan card payment, not cash. The [KTO bus guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=220897) warns about cashless city buses even though the fare table retains cash columns.

Current [101 outward examples](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/schedule/detailSchedule?scheduleId=1950) show airport 11:00 → Hamdeok 11:46 → Seongsan 12:35. [Airport-bound examples](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/schedule/detailSchedule?scheduleId=1951) are Seongsan 13:08 → CJU 14:42, then 14:08 → 15:42: about 94 minutes once boarded, with a missed departure costing time. [201 examples](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/schedule/detailSchedule?scheduleId=2439) show terminal 11:00 → Hamdeok 11:43 → Seongsan 12:44 → Seogwipo terminal 14:18; variants may bypass stops or end early. These are timetable examples, not promised trip-date services or a universal frequency.

For the bus version: Friday take 101 to Seongsan, drop bags and visit Orrrn locally. Saturday walk to sunrise, then use bus/taxi for Olle and the return. Sunday take 201 to Sehwa for the museum and Café Hallasan, then board airport-bound 101 at a served stop such as Sehwa Transit Station. Allow walking/transfers; 101 does not stop at every café. Carry small backpacks: museum/café luggage storage is not assumed. Confirm the travel-date timetable and taxi availability.

## Three days with room to breathe

Route: CJU → Hamdeok → Woljeongri / Sehwa → Seongsan base ↔ Hado / Jongdal → CJU. Road times are planning estimates, not live routing. Add 20–30 minutes to longer legs over the holiday weekend; supplier depot and hotel locations matter. No west-coast detour, Hallasan summit or Udo ferry is squeezed in.

### Rain: swap, do not pile on

Move the museum to Saturday if the trail is wet or wind strong; its ₩1,100 fee is already included, not another paid visit. Have a slow café lunch. Move Olle to Sunday morning only if safe and you can finish, retrieve the car and leave the east coast around 14:00. Otherwise choose a short marked out-and-back and drop the full walk. In a storm, accept losing the walk or sunrise rather than gamble on exposed paths. Check weather alerts and airline status; no trip-date forecast is claimed.
