**Keep Saturday gentle at the Louvre, then give Warner Bros. World one proper day. For two adults and children aged 7 and 10, the AED 3,000 spending plan works ONLY if a genuine four-person hotel room plus four park entries costs AED 2,200 or less, including taxes, child charges and valid Sunday use after checkout. That is a ceiling, not an offer found for your dates. No qualifying package or exact-date family-room quote was confirmed.**

This plan is for Saturday, February 13, 2027 to Sunday, February 14, 2027, driving from Dubai in your existing family car. All itinerary times are UAE time. Prices and source access date from November 6, 2026; they are not February quotations and have not been refreshed for this draft. No booking or contact was made.

## Check the room-and-ticket basket before planning the treats

The hotel is the decision that can break this budget. Enter both children's ages, require an occupancy-approved room for everyone, and get the full price with taxes and supplements. If a rate includes park access, confirm **four passes and Sunday entry after checkout** in writing. “During your stay” alone does not establish that entitlement. Do not pay for tickets twice or assume breakfast is included.

| Family allowance | AED | What must be true |
|---|---:|---|
| Hotel plus four Warner Bros. entries | <span style="white-space:nowrap">2,200</span> | Unquoted planning ceiling; approved four-person room, all charges and valid Sunday tickets included |
| Louvre admission | 140 | Sampled official price: 2 × 70 + 2 × 0; recheck before travel |
| Food and drinks | 450 | Estimates: Saturday lunch 130 + dinner 100 + Sunday breakfast 60 + park lunch 140 + extras 20 |
| Fuel | 120 | Estimate for your existing car; no rental cost |
| Salik and Darb | 40 | Route-dependent allowance, including Salik VAT; not a gate-by-gate quote |
| Self-parking | 0 | Use currently free museum/park parking and a hotel with free self-parking; no valet |
| Small buffer | 50 | Souvenirs and Quick Pass excluded; cannot absorb a major hotel increase |
| Conditional total | <span style="white-space:nowrap">3,000</span> | Not a bookable quotation |

If you buy park tickets separately at the sampled adult price, **4 × AED 345 = AED 1,380**, leaving **AED 820 all-in for the room**. No suitable target-date room at that price was established. For scale, a hypothetical AED 1,200 room + AED 1,380 tickets + AED 800 other allowances totals **AED 3,380**. If your real basket is higher, raise the budget or change the overnight plan; do not squeeze four people into a cheaper occupancy category.

## Drive if you can; the bus is not automatically cheaper for four

Use E11 toward Abu Dhabi, then E12 for Saadiyat and Louvre. Allow **1¾–2¼ hours door to door**, including a short stop, and **30–40 minutes from Louvre to Yas**. These are planning estimates, not live routes. The AED 120 fuel and AED 40 toll allowances exclude rentals, tag purchases and rental-company fees. Louvre and Warner Bros. currently offer free self-parking; the hotel comparison listing also marked the named hotels below as having free parking.

The bus alternative is E100 from Al Ghubaiba or E101 from Ibn Battuta to Abu Dhabi Central—not straight to Louvre or a Yas hotel. AD Mobility listed **AED 25 per person each way**, while an observed RTA E100 planner result displayed **AED 30**. The discrepancy remains unresolved: budget the higher **4 × 30 × 2 = AED 240 return**, then recheck your dated journey. Both children are budgeted at full fare; intercity student concessions do not apply.

Allow roughly **3–4 hours door to door each way** for station access, waiting and transfers; the observed E100 bus leg was 2h10. Add estimated **AED 200–300 local transfers**, plus Dubai station access and any new cards. For this family, that means leaving earlier and shortening the park day, not necessarily saving money.

### Do not reuse the old “Sunday tolls are free” shortcut

Ghantoot and Al Qurm Darb gates have charged **AED 4 per crossing, 24/7, since May 4, 2026**. An E11/Ghantoot route can therefore cost money both days. Other timed Darb gates charge AED 4 at 07:00–09:00 and 15:00–19:00, excluding Sundays and public holidays. Check your route and vehicle/account readiness.

Normal Salik Monday–Saturday base rates are AED 6 at 06:00–10:00 and 16:00–20:00, AED 4 otherwise, with 01:00–06:00 free. Normal Sunday is AED 4 outside that free window. With **5% VAT introduced June 1, 2026**, the calculated charged amounts are **AED 6.30 / AED 4.20 per crossing**. Your starting point changes the gate count.

**These trip dates are likely to fall in Ramadan.** The projected start was February 8, 2027, subject to moon sighting—not a final calendar. Recheck dated toll schedules, attraction hours and daytime dining before departure. For orientation only, the reported **2026** Ramadan Salik bands were Monday–Saturday peak 09:00–17:00, off-peak 07:00–09:00 and 17:00–02:00, free 02:00–07:00; Sunday used off-peak outside the free window except holidays/events. Those are not confirmed 2027 hours.

## Saturday: play at the Louvre, then leave room for a rest

Louvre's sampled admission was **AED 70 per adult, under-18s free: AED 140 for the family**, VAT included. General admission includes the Children's Museum; keep age ID handy. Separate February Children's Museum hours were not established, so go there first. The sampled Friday–Sunday gallery hours were 10:00–20:30, last entry 20:00—not a promise of the children's area's closing time.

| UAE time | A comfortable Saturday |
|---|---|
| 08:00 | Leave Dubai after breakfast at home; keep a comfort-stop buffer and go straight to Saadiyat |
| 10:00–10:30 | Park, security, toilets and admission |
| 10:30–12:00 | Start with A Board Game Adventure, then follow the children's interest rather than every gallery |
| 12:00–13:00 | A few gallery highlights and the dome; let each child choose an object and invent its story |
| 13:00–14:00 | Museum Café lunch, estimated family ceiling AED 130; lighter choices if the menu is dearer |
| 14:00–15:00 | Drive to Yas and check in; allow reception time, with no early room access assumed |
| 15:00–17:30 | Pool, room break or a short walk; keep a dry alternative if it feels chilly |
| 18:00–19:00 | Casual dinner, estimated AED 100 for four, then an early night |

The dated official season programme scheduled **A Board Game Adventure from July 18, 2026 through April 2027**, with chess, carrom, ludo and mancala. That is the better planning lead than generic pages still describing the older space exhibition. Recheck that it is operating on your visit; special workshops are not assumed included.

Museum Café lists a children's menu and grab-and-go food, but the allowance is not a menu quote. For dinner, Yas Mall's food court lists Automatic Restaurant & Cafeteria and Burger King. Allow an estimated **10–20 minutes each way** from your hotel and check Ramadan service hours. The food budget does not cover a hotel buffet or Valentine's set menu.

## Sunday: one park, three priorities, no completion badge

My pick is **Warner Bros. World** for this age mix: cartoons, superheroes, family rides, shows and indoor play. SeaWorld is a substitute if animal exploration matters more than rides. That is a family-fit and queue-management judgment, not evidence that either park will be quieter.

**Measure both children before choosing tickets or rides.** Sampled Warner Bros. admission was **AED 345 adult / AED 295 junior**; junior eligibility for this family was height-based, **below 1.1m**, not simply “under 12”. Each qualifying junior saves AED 50 against the adult budget. The price dialog's default date was November 6, 2026, not your February visit.

Ride restrictions are separate: Tom and Jerry / Green Lantern minimum **105cm**, Fast and Furry-ous **110cm**, Riddler Revolution **120cm**. Check accompanying-adult and health restrictions too. Choose three priorities only after measuring.

| UAE time | A manageable Sunday |
|---|---|
| 08:00–09:00 | Simple takeaway/supermarket breakfast, estimated AED 60 for four; hotel buffet not assumed |
| 09:00–10:15 | Pack, check out and sort luggage. Use bundle tickets only with confirmed Sunday post-checkout validity |
| 10:15–11:00 | Arrive 30–45 minutes before confirmed ride opening; allow security and walking |
| 11:00–12:30 | Try the two highest-priority eligible rides. If posted waits exceed 30 minutes, move on and try later |
| 12:30–13:15 | Quick-service park lunch, estimated AED 140 for four; do not assume outside meals are allowed |
| 13:15–16:15 | Third priority, a show or characters, and play time; estimated AED 20 total extras for snacks/water |
| 16:15–16:45 | Toilets, collect stored bags if needed, and leave Yas |
| 18:30–19:15 | Estimated Dubai arrival, allowing traffic and a short stop; dinner at home is outside the away-food budget |

The sampled park header showed **11:00–20:00**, but February 14 hours, ride openings and maintenance remain unconfirmed. Shift the plan to the dated calendar. Online tickets remove the ticket-counter step, not ride queues; Sunday is not guaranteed quiet. Quick Pass is extra spending and is excluded. Hotel luggage storage is also something to confirm, not an assumed service.

## Offers and weather: useful checks, not hidden savings

- The park advertised **10% off seven-day advance purchases**. Do not automatically take another 10% off AED 345; no advance saving is credited here.
- Sampled multi-park offers were **AED 435 per person for two parks in one day**, or **AED 475 for two parks within six days**. Skip them for this plan: extra value is not extra time.
- No general Dubai/UAE-resident discount applicable to the family was verified. Emirates ID, bank-card and hotel-package eligibility are different questions.
- Tasty Ticket's published offer ends **December 31, 2026**, before this trip. Its **AED 65 adult / AED 55 junior** amounts are meal-voucher values, not cheap admission; do not use them in February's budget.
- Tourism guidance gives **18–28°C for October–March**. That is a broad seasonal range, not a February daily average or weekend forecast. Pack comfortable shoes, sun protection, light layers, swimwear and an extra layer for indoor air-conditioning; check the real forecast nearer departure.

Before paying, settle the all-in family basket, four-pass entitlement, checkout validity, dated ride and Children's Museum hours, and Ramadan arrangements. Then pick those three rides. You do not need another attraction to make the weekend worthwhile.
