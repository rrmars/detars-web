**Worried that the children will be exhausted before you have seen much? For two adults and children aged 6 and 10 at travel, give each day one main outing: Singapore Zoo → Gardens by the Bay → the current Science Centre. For early July 2027, allow S$386, S$321 and S$220 respectively: S$927 for three full days, including reserve but excluding accommodation. Add S$100 for family return airport transfers, bringing it to S$1,027 before hotels, flights and the other exclusions below. If you want Sentosa, swap it for a day—do not squeeze it in as another attraction.**

With children aged 6 and 10, use the S$927 as a starting budget for a Singapore family trip, not a spending target. Keep the rest breaks, compare the tickets you will actually use, and check July 2027 prices before paying.

For Singapore in 3 days with kids, start with the Zoo, Gardens and Science Centre, then compare passes against those outings. Choose your July 2027 dates before deciding which day goes where.

## Give each day a main outing and a proper break

Start your hotel search around **City Hall / Raffles City**. [Fairmont’s location page](https://www.fairmont-singapore.com/destination/) is a useful example of station access, not a checked room deal. Ask for a room genuinely approved for your whole family, or connecting rooms, and get the price for your dates. A Bugis/Bencoolen family room can work just as well if it costs less and has a short, sheltered walk to the MRT.

Use these time blocks as breathing room, not appointments. Traffic, opening hours and queues can change them. Keep the hotel rests even if you feel tempted to add more; there will still be walking.

## Get around without paying for the same journey twice

- **Use a taxi for Mandai in the base plan.** Zoo/Night Safari need EAST; Bird Paradise needs WEST. The [access page](https://www.mandai.com/en/plan-your-visit/getting-to-and-around/getting-to-mandai-wildlife-reserve.html) lists the first M2 Khatib departure at 08:30, too late to rely on for Zoo’s 08:30 opening. Check 2027 times. The [taxi surcharge guide](https://www.mandai.com/en/faq/taxi-surcharge.html) adds S$5 to taxi departures at 13:00–23:59, not private-hire cars. Ordinary surcharges are already within the return allowance.
- **MRT + M2 costs this family S$15 in return bus fares, plus MRT.** The [Mandai shuttle FAQ](https://www.mandai.com/en/faq/shuttle-services.html) charges S$2.50 each way for ages 7+, with under-sevens free: three payers × S$2.50 × two directions = S$15. Once the younger child turns seven, that becomes S$20. Do not apply this shuttle exemption to MRT fares; the rules differ.
- **Give everyone their own payment card or device.** Use two adult contactless cards and separate ordinary EZ-Link cards for the children, budgeting adult fares for all four. Tap in and out with the same physical card or the same device, not a mix. [SimplyGo’s FAQ](https://www.simplygo.com.sg/faqs/cards-and-charms/simplygo/contactless-bank-cards/) lists S$0.60 per foreign Mastercard/Visa per day used; your bank may charge too.
- **Being six does not automatically mean free MRT travel.** The [Child Concession Card page](https://www.simplygo.com.sg/concession-monthly-travel-cards/child/child-concession-card/) requires under seven **and not studying in primary school**. Accompanied children under 0.9m are a separate free-travel group. Before applying, check documents and expiry: ordinary child-card validity ends April 30 in the year the child turns seven, whatever their birthday. That can matter even if they are still six in July. Do not assume a tourist student concession for the older child.
- **Do not add a S$116 Tourist Pass to fares already budgeted for the same trains.** The [pass page](https://thesingaporetouristpass.com.sg/type-of-passes/) excludes M2, Sentosa Express, RWS8 and private premium/express services. My pick for this clustered, taxi-assisted trip is likely pay-as-you-go. Check your actual journeys before treating that as a fully priced saving.

## Book in an order that leaves room to adjust

Set your dates and confirm the hotel can accommodate the family first. Then check July tickets and visit-day terms for Zoo, Gardens and Science Centre, or Oceanarium time slots if you swap. **Do not assume July 2027 tickets have been released or that your dates have space.** Normal free Gardens areas, Children’s Garden and ordinary light shows do not require the core paid ticket, but special events may change access. Hawker meals need no attraction booking; bring small SGD notes and ask what each stall accepts.

Mandai recommends buying official online tickets ahead, but standard payments are non-refundable. Read your actual ticket terms before paying. Feeding sessions, tours, films and shuttle supplements are not automatically included.

Before arrival, follow the [ICA guidance checked November 5](https://www.ica.gov.sg/information-and-e-services/entry-and-departure/entering-singapore/sg-arrival-card-with-electronic-health-declaration): submit the **free SG Arrival Card for each entering traveller within three calendar days including arrival day, and before arriving**. That is not three full days ahead. For example, a July 3, 2027 arrival gives a July 1–3 submission window. **The card is not a visa.** Recheck July 2027 rules before departure. Use [ICA’s official e-service](https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/sgarrivalcard/), save the acknowledgements and use the [entry checker](https://www.ica.gov.sg/information-and-e-services/entry-and-departure/entering-singapore/entry-requirements) for **each actual passport**, route and travel history. Living in Australia does not establish nationality, entry clearance or how long you can stay.

**Can I buy tickets ahead?** [Mandai’s admission FAQ](https://www.mandai.com/en/faq/admission-tickets.html) recommends its official ticketing website before your visit to secure your preferred date. First check whether July 2027 tickets are released; their availability is not confirmed here.
