**Worried about sold-out tickets, a hotel bill that grows at checkout and too much rushing? Give each big Gaudí visit its own morning, keep arrival day loose and stay around Universitat–Urgell in Eixample. For two adults, €1,800 excluding flights is a workable planning envelope—not a secured trip price. The €900 room allowance has no unconditional May offer behind it, and ETIAS requirements for May 2027 remain unconfirmed.**

This plan is for a U.S.-passport couple travelling from Chicago, Thursday, May 6, 2027 to Monday, May 10, 2027: four sightseeing days, four nights, then an airport-only morning. All itinerary times are Barcelona local time. Prices and rules below are dated samples from November 6, 2026, Asia/Singapore (November 5, 2026 UTC), not May quotations or guaranteed future entry rules. Nothing has been booked, and no prices or sources were refreshed for this draft.

### Keep tourist tax beside the room price

The November 2026 research recorded an **enacted May 2027 city surcharge of €6**, not the €8 legal ceiling, alongside Barcelona's regional rates under Law 2/2026. These are dated legal inputs, not a promise that no amendments will occur before your stay. The combined tax fee enters the hotel VAT base; the totals below apply 10% VAT.

| Barcelona hotel category | Rate to March 31, 2027 | Dated calculation for May 6–10, 2027 | Two adults × four nights |
|---|---|---|---:|
| 1–3-star / other hotel category (EB3) | (€2 + €5) × 1.10 = €7.70/person/night | (€2 regional + €6 city) × 1.10 = €8.80/person/night | €70.40 |
| 4-star / 4-star superior (EB2) | (€3.40 + €5) × 1.10 = €9.24/person/night | (€3.40 + €6) × 1.10 = €10.34/person/night | €82.72 |

The dated surcharge schedule is April 1, 2026–March 31, 2027 at €5, then April 1, 2027–March 31, 2028 at €6. Barcelona's regional rates apply from April 1, 2026 without a March 2027 end date in the cited law; the other table's staged April 2027 regional increases concern the rest of Catalonia, not Barcelona city.

The maximum is seven taxable nights per person in one continuous stay; all four here count. Reservation-date protection requires paying **both accommodation and tax, including surcharge, when reserving**. Merely making a booking does not lock the tax. Check amendments and the itemised bill before payment so you neither miss the charge nor pay twice.

### Monday, May 10: airport only

For long-haul departure, aim to reach the correct terminal **three hours before your flight**, or earlier if the airline asks. Leave the hotel roughly **4–4½ hours before departure**, allowing the walk, wait and airport journey. That is a planning buffer, not an airline rule or an invented flight time. ## U.S. passports: keep May's ETIAS question open

**May 2027 ETIAS applicability is unconfirmed.** In the official FAQ inspected on November 6, 2026, ETIAS was not operational and applications were not being collected; a specific launch date would be announced months ahead. That does not establish either “required in May” or “definitely unnecessary”. Do not turn an older launch forecast into an entry rule.

The dated [EU visa-exempt list, Annex II](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32018R1806) and [Spain consular conditions](https://www.exteriores.gob.es/Consulados/nuevayork/en/ServiciosConsulares/Paginas/Consular/Condiciones-de-entrada-en-Espana.aspx) covered ordinary U.S.-passport tourists for **up to 90 days in any 180-day period across Schengen**, not per country. Count other recent Schengen trips. The passport needed issuance within the previous **10 years** and validity at least **three months after Schengen departure**: leaving on **May 10, 2027** implies at least **August 10, 2027**. Leave more margin if renewal is straightforward, and reconfirm requirements for travel.

Carry return/onward details, hotel confirmation and evidence of sufficient funds. Spain's inspected financial figures were for **2026**; a **2027 threshold was not verified**, so check the consulate rather than carrying over an old amount.

The [European Commission notice](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/news/entryexit-system-ees-fully-operational-2026-04-10_en) recorded EES fully operational since **April 10, 2026**. For covered short-stay non-EU travellers it records entries/exits, passport details, facial image and fingerprints instead of stamps. Allow extra time at the **first external Schengen border**: Barcelona on a nonstop from Chicago, normally the connecting airport if entering through Paris, for example—not a second immigration check in Barcelona. Leave more connection time. EES border registration is not a separate advance travel-authorisation purchase in this plan.

Recheck the [official ETIAS status/fee FAQ](https://travel-europe.europa.eu/etias/faq#when-does-etias-enter-into-force) on **March 1, 2027**, again in April and before departure, including transition rules and current processing guidance. If applicable by travel, use the official EU site and apply promptly. The sampled fee was **€20 per fee-paying applicant**, with under-18, over-70 and qualifying EU-family-member exemptions. Hold **up to €40 for two inside contingency**, not as a charge due now. The inspected status did not call for an application or payment; authorisation would not replace the passport or guarantee admission. Check airline document advice before check-in too.

## A few habits, then relax

- Wear a zipped crossbody bag in front on metros, escalators and busy streets; keep phones and wallets out of back pockets.
- At ticket machines, one buys while the other watches bags. Keep luggage beside you and in sight on Aerobús.
- Do not leave a phone on a café table or a bag on a chair. A map, petition or sudden spill can be a distraction.
- At the beach, bring little and take turns swimming; do not leave valuables unattended on the sand.
- Split cards, keep a backup separately and save passport copies securely. On ticket days, securely carry the original photo ID required by the attraction.
- If something is stolen, do not chase. Freeze cards, secure the phone remotely and report to police for insurance. Call **112** in an emergency.

These are general prevention habits, not crime-rate claims or a reason to avoid the old city or public transport. No neighbourhood is risk-free. Leave bulky luggage at the hotel and use the same habits around La Rambla, the beach and busy interchanges.

Before leaving, reconfirm entry rules, airline terminal, tickets, hotel tax/fees, TMB disruptions and weather. Download tickets and offline maps. Pack comfortable shoes, a light layer, towel and power bank. Move the free activities if the weather turns; keep the non-refundable commitments in view.
