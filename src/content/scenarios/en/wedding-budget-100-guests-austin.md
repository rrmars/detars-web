**Worried that the wedding budget will grow with every decision? For 100 guests, this example sets aside $29,557.78 for spending and a separate $3,500 reserve. That leaves $1,942.22 beneath a $35,000 cap.** It works by choosing a simpler wedding, not by getting every full-service option cheaply. Before booking, replace each allowance with a quote that includes all fees.

## Start with the wedding you actually want

Picture Austin, Texas, in October 2027. The date 2027-10-16 is a placeholder Saturday, not a reservation. The example includes made-up family contributions: $20,000 from the couple, $7,000 from Parent A and $8,000 from Parent B. No vendors have been contacted or availability confirmed. Prices were checked on 2026-11-05, not refreshed for your booking dates, so ask again before committing.

Here, you are choosing a restaurant buffet, beer and wine, simple DIY flowers in bud vases, economical outfits and month-of coordination. Wedding bands are included. Separate video, a guest shuttle, honeymoon, rehearsal dinner, engagement ring and guest lodging are not. All amounts stay in USD with the $ notation; no currency conversion is involved.

The bar calculator says $3.25 for the entry tier, while the dedicated price table says $3.50. Budget with the higher $3.50 until the venue confirms it. There is also an 80% adult minimum: ask who counts toward the charged headcount. A child or someone who does not drink may not reduce your bill. These are not guaranteed 2027 bar prices.

Maudie's 18% charge is for cancellation inside 72 hours, not a standard catering service charge. This budget instead allows $25 per guest for food plus $1,500 before tax for labor, travel and setup. October falls outside the venue's summer and through-May-2027 promotions. Check included furniture before renting more: the venue lists 16 tables and 130 chairs. You still need to agree the layout, linens and place settings.

**Choose the service you want before comparing prices.** [Wild Vine Floral](https://wildvinefloral.com/wedding-florals) lists centerpieces at $120–$350 each. The $1,000 pre-tax flower and decor allowance here is for simpler DIY bud vases, not a full professional installation. [Bestow Bridal](https://www.bestowbridal.com/) lists a gown alone at $800–$2,500. Fitting outfits and alterations into the $1,600 pre-tax attire total means economical choices for both partners. These allowances will not buy everything on those price lists.

## Make sure the money arrives before the bill

A workable total can still leave you short on payment day. The example has $5,000 already paid and $7,500 in pooled cash; both are made-up amounts. Parents' promises are not cash until the money arrives. If a parent pays a vendor directly, enter it in Payments as Direct. Do not also record it as a gift into the pooled account.

Record the amount and the payment or receipt date together. Update your as-of date weekly; future-dated entries are not money already paid or received. When you get a quote including all tax and fees, use it instead of the estimated cost, not on top of it. Keep category and payer labels consistent. When adding installments, reduce the existing balance so it is not counted twice. If you use the supplied spreadsheet, its prepared formulas stop at Payments row 204 and Contributions row 104.

The spreadsheet lets you compare 80/100/120 guests and Friday or Saturday before you commit. Those comparisons leave out agreed quote overrides and deposits you cannot recover. They are not promises of savings after cancellation. The cash timeline runs in fixed months from November 2026 through December 2027. It projects forward from current cash; it is not a history of bank balances.

## Before you say yes to a vendor

Treat these as selected public prices, not Austin averages or binding 2027 quotes. Tax, transport, insurance, tips and some rental and extras amounts are still allowances. Get actual prices before relying on them. Your spreadsheet needs up-to-date inputs and a fresh calculation; resolve any payment warnings before trusting its cash totals.

Confirm the date and venue, catering staff and vendor meals, bar minimums and tax, rental delivery and setup, insurance limits and refund terms. Ask family members when their money will arrive, and whether they will transfer it or pay vendors directly. Settle that schedule and protect your reserve before adding floral installations, longer coverage or guest transport.
