**If your family's ceiling is AUD 120,000, none of the five scholarship-free scenarios below fits. The cheapest is Deakin at about AUD 143,608. A lean Deakin route comes to about AUD 118,533 only with an assessed 1.5-year place, a confirmed 25% tuition award and living costs held to AUD 550/week. Those are conditions to secure, not money you already have. Its AUD 1,467 headroom is small.**

This is for one Vietnamese applicant in Ho Chi Minh City with a Bachelor of Finance, IELTS overall 6.5 and no dependants, comparing on-campus coursework degrees in Melbourne and Sydney. Chinese readers can use the same comparison; it does not change the applicant's nationality or eligibility. All money is AUD, with no VND conversion. University fees, rules and source observations below are the November 6, 2026 snapshot, not refreshed quotations or a promise about later rules.

My pick is to investigate Deakin's shortened Finance route and scholarship together, with RMIT as a better-evidenced intake alternative. Keep July 2027 as the calmer main plan unless your documents and funding are ready now. Deferring buys preparation time, not a cheaper Australia. Do not pay a non-refundable deposit or buy flights just to rescue a date.

## Is early entry still realistic?

As of that snapshot, early 2027 was still worth pursuing, but “February intake” does not always mean February classes. UTS starts teaching February 15, 2027 and Macquarie February 22, 2027; the Melbourne examples start March 1, 2027. Admission, acceptance/payment, Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) and the visa need to happen in sequence.

IELTS 6.5 overall is not enough to establish eligibility. Get your band scores and test date alongside your university, transcript, grades, graduation date and subject outlines. Most options need every band at least 6.0; UTS explicitly lists writing 6.0 and leaves the other component minima unstated in its course panel. A Finance degree makes credit plausible, not automatic.

## What the full family budget needs to cover

A lower annual fee can be the more expensive choice. La Trobe's longer route adds both tuition and another half-year of living costs. Compare assessed duration and net tuition, not the annual sticker alone.

The model assumes shared housing, no car, no wages and no scholarship in the first five rows. Living runs for 19 months on a 1.5-year course or 25 months on a 2-year course: nominal study plus a transition month. Actual course and visa dates may differ. Annual living allowances stay flat; the contingency is limited protection against inflation.

## Visa preparation: keep affordability separate from the evidence minimum

Before lodging, check the official [Student visa guidance](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/student-500), [fee page](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/fees-and-charges/current-visa-pricing) and [Migration Regulations, item 1222](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F1996B03551/2026-10-02/2026-10-02/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html), especially if you apply later.

- **Visa fee:** [Study Australia's fee notice](https://www.studyaustralia.gov.au/en/Agent-Hub/agent-news-index/student-visa-application-charge-increase) also records the increase and concession. Standard base 2,500; an eligible valid Vietnamese-passport holder has the ASEAN concession base 2,050. Passport matters, not simply studying in Vietnam. Health, biometrics and payment surcharges are separate.
- **CoE:** an offer alone is not enough. Obtain the valid CoE for an eligible registered course before lodging and keep it valid for decision. Prepare passport, qualifications, transcripts, English evidence and requested health/character documents using the official country/provider checklist.
- **Funds:** the student living amount is 29,710 for 12 months, plus travel and the first 12 months of tuition less evidenced prepaid fees. This is not full-course affordability or a rent forecast. With 48,000 first-year tuition and a separate 2,000 travel-evidence allowance from Vietnam, the arithmetic starting point is 79,710 before deducting evidenced prepaid tuition. That travel evidence input is not the 1,500 flight-spending estimate above. Show genuine access and the family funding trail, not an unexplained last-minute balance.
- **Genuine Student:** explain your circumstances, choice of course/provider/Australia, career benefit and relevant facts, with evidence. [GS guidance](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/student-500/genuine-student-requirement) asks for answers in English, with no more than 150 words per question. Possible future permanent-residence intentions do not automatically count against you; study must be the primary reason.
- **OSHC:** approved cover must start when you arrive, not when classes begin, and remain continuous through the required stay. Include insurer, dates and policy details. Vietnam is not a listed nationality-based exception. Get an actual quote.
- **Work:** coursework students may work up to 48 hours per fortnight in session, not each week; unlimited hours when the course is not in session. Do not work before the course begins. The research-degree exemption is not for these coursework degrees. Check the grant/VEVO conditions. No earnings are deducted from this budget.

### Separate rules, announcements and proposals

The snapshot's [education planning setting](https://www.education.gov.au/managed-system-international-education) was 295,000 new overseas commencements for 2027, unchanged from 2026. [Study Australia](https://www.studyaustralia.gov.au/en/tools-and-resources/news/australia-confirms-international-education-settings-for-2027) explicitly says it is not a hard national visa cap: provider allocations affect processing priority, not an automatic prohibition on grants or a personal place promise.

The retained [integrity-reform notice](https://minister.homeaffairs.gov.au/JulianHill/Pages/student-visa-integrity-reforms-take-effect-today.aspx) distinguishes restrictions effective October 2, 2026 from a new provider-transfer visa stream scheduled for July 1, 2027, not yet in force at the snapshot. It records tighter onshore/family rules and an increase from 6 to 12 months in the principal-course provider-transfer restriction, with exceptions. An eligible Vietnamese applicant can still include qualifying family at initial offshore lodgement, but later subsequent-entrant applications were removed. This budget is single-student only. Do not choose a cheap first course assuming an easy switch; check the final rules before transferring.

The [GS/financial-capacity consultation](https://www.homeaffairs.gov.au/help-and-support/how-to-engage-us/consultations/consultation-on-proposed-reforms-to-the-genuine-student-requirement-and-financial-capacity-framework) was a proposal, not a new requirement: no decision had been made, and submissions were due at noon AEDT on November 23, 2026. Recheck the actual financial threshold when lodging rather than treating a proposal as law.
