Your employer is sending you to Ho Chi Minh City on 1 March 2027 for 3 years. Your children are 9 and 13, the company pays rent up to USD 2,500 a month and half of the school fees, and you do not yet know which school will have a seat, which district to live in, or whether the paperwork will hold the start date. **Short answer: aim for the Korean International School HCMC (KIS) first, keep SSIS as the fallback, and treat seat availability at KIS as the main risk; live in Phu My Hung (District 7) by default, and switch to Thao Dien / An Phu only if the plant is in Saigon Hi-Tech Park; and expect to save roughly VND 116,660,357 a month with KIS if both children get a seat, but only VND 58,713,065 with SSIS (indicative, because many budget lines are planning allowances).**

The guide follows Jihoon (the employee) and Soyeon, with the two children. Pay is KRW 7,500,000 a month plus a USD 1,200 monthly allowance. It was written on 8 November 2026 from public pages only; nobody (schools, agents, the employer, officials) was contacted and nothing was submitted. “Unconfirmed” marks anything that is not from the school, provider or authority’s own page or the legal text. **It is general information, not legal, tax or immigration advice.** The source materials are not published.

## The recommendation in brief

- **School: KIS first, SSIS as the fallback, and seat availability as the risk.** The Korean International School HCMC (KIS, 호치민시한국국제학교) costs about 13% of SSIS tuition for two children (VND 194,760,000 against VND 1,513,495,000 a year). It keeps the children on the Korean curriculum, and the older child will be about 16 on the return, a poor moment to switch systems. The catch: KIS accepts transfers **only when a seat is vacant**, and its eligibility rules (Korean nationality, overseas-Korean registration) were verified only for kindergarten. Its next intake windows are late December (for a March start) and July. SSIS (District 7, US-style, IB Diploma and AP) is the fallback because it is in the same district as Phu My Hung. **Next step:** ask KIS for current vacancies in the two grades and for its December 2026 intake plan.
- **Area: Phu My Hung by default, with the plant location as the deciding fact.** KIS, Lotte Mart, K-Market and KOCHAM are there and, per one agency guide (unconfirmed), Korean-friendly clinics; FV Hospital’s own page lists free Korean interpretation. 3-bedroom examples at or under the USD 2,500 rent cap exist (USD 1,850–2,500 in the examples below; unconfirmed asking prices, and two of the three are stale, 2025). Switch to Thao Dien / An Phu only if the plant is in Saigon Hi-Tech Park (free-flow drive of 17 minutes from Thao Dien against 24 minutes from Phu My Hung, 13.6 km against 21.5 km). **Next step:** ask the employer which plant you will work at before you look at any flat.
- **Money.** At mid-market rates the family receives about VND 171,961,229 a month (USD 6,720; KRW 9,130,476). With KIS the family could plausibly save about VND 116,660,357 (USD 4,559; KRW 6,194,214) a month; with an international school only VND 48,475,357 to VND 58,713,065 (ISHCMC to SSIS). Many lines are planning allowances, not priced facts (see the budget).
- **Work-permit caution.** Ask HR or the employer’s counsel whether the Vietnam entity is in a service sector that qualifies for the intra-company-transfer work-permit exemption. A manufacturing manager may need a normal work permit, which changes the paperwork. Plan the Korean documents for December 2026 and January 2027.
- **The apostille point.** Vietnam reportedly joined the Apostille Convention with effect from 11 September 2026, replacing consular legalisation of foreign documents (unconfirmed; no government site could be opened).

### Returning to the Korean school system

- **Rules come from one office.** The source is one regional education office’s restatement of the rules (Guri-Namyangju), not the Ministry itself (unconfirmed). The receiving school (the principal decides) places the child by adding foreign attendance to the last Korean grade completed; ESL or kindergarten time is not counted. Apostilled enrolment and transcripts are needed, with the apostille waived for schools on the Ministry’s list of foreign accredited schools (a 2014 notice). **Choosing a grade different from actual attendance can later cost special-admission eligibility.**
- **KIS’s own transfer page:** apply at least 2 weeks ahead and settle fees; report cards are issued only for departures in July–August or December–February, so time the return for those months.
- **University “3-year overseas parent” special admission** typically needs at least one high-school year abroad (blog only, unconfirmed). The older child would be in middle-school grade 2 to high-school grade 2 during the posting, so check the target universities’ rules early.
- **Why this matters for the choice:** KIS makes the return paperwork simplest. International schools are workable but add apostille, grade-placement and curriculum-gap risk, especially for the 16-year-old on return.

## Health insurance

- **Company plan or private plan.** No insurer premium could be found publicly (all are quote-form based, and a quote needs personal data, which was not requested), so any comparison is “quote needed”. Ask HR for the policy wording: dependants included? Vietnam and Korea cover? Direct billing at FV? Evacuation? Pre-existing conditions? A private family plan is only worth pricing if the company plan excludes the children or has low outpatient limits.
- **Vietnam compulsory social insurance for foreigners (from 1 July 2025)** applies to fixed-term contracts of at least 12 months (the state page says 12 months, not 1 month). Intra-company transferees are listed as exempt; if hired on a local contract the 8% employee and 17% employer rates apply. Dependants are not covered either way. Confirm the contract structure with HR.
- **Korea–Vietnam social security agreement** (in force 1 January 2024; pension only): a Korean employee posted by a Korean employer stays under Korean pension law for up to 5 years (+3). It does not cover health insurance.
- **Korean NHIS:** stays abroad of at least 3 months can be reported for suspension of benefits and reduced premiums; employee members need the employer to file (the page body was not fully read). Expect no Korean cover in Vietnam, so a Vietnam-valid plan is required. Check the dependants’ status with HR and NHIS (1577-1000).
- **FV Hospital (District 7):** its insurance office arranges direct billing with international insurers ((028) 35 11 34 51); it advertises up to a 20% subsidy for uninsured patients and free interpretation including Korean, 24/7 on request. Fee schedules and the direct-billing partner list were not read. Korean-language claims for City International, Hanaro and CarePlus are unconfirmed.

## What is still open

- **Visa section rests on secondary sources.** No government site was reachable; the decree was read only in part (about 74%). Primary confirmation is missing for apostille status of Korean documents, residence-card fees and terms, the dependant visa route and the “LD2/TT-only card” rule.
- **The budget is allowance-heavy.** About VND 37.1 million of the VND 44.2 million living subtotal is allowances (groceries, eating out, leisure, flights, utilities and management fees, mobile, medical), not priced facts. Treat savings as indicative, with about VND 10 million a month either way as plausible.
- **Return rules come from one regional office,** not the Ministry (the original document was unreadable).
- **Listings are stale or thin.** Eight rent examples (4 in Phu My Hung, 4 in Thao Dien, one already rented, one with an inferred date); several are stale. No evidence on flooding, air quality or peak-hour traffic.
- **Exchange rates.** USD/KRW is ECB-derived, not Bank of Korea; USD/VND is a bank mid-rate, which flatters conversions against real transfer spreads (about 1.5% for USD, wider for KRW).
- **School fees.** KIS fees are for the 2026 school year, not 2027; the BIS column mapping is unverified; bus fees are unpublished; whether the SSIS registration fee applies is unclear; the budget divides annual tuition by 12 and ignores the 10% KIS sibling discount (conservative).
- **Tax and net pay are not modelled:** whether the USD 1,200 allowance is taxed, Vietnam income tax after the 2026 law change, and Korean-side obligations.
- **Employer:** plant location, the sector of the Vietnam entity (permit or exemption), who files, health-plan documents, whether the rent cap covers management fees, and the tax treatment of the allowance.
- **KIS:** current vacancies for the two grades, elementary and middle-school eligibility, the December 2026 intake plan (the 2 November 2026 notice PDFs could not be opened), and bus and lunch fees.
- **Not obtained at all:** insurer premiums, grocery and mobile prices, and petrol prices.
