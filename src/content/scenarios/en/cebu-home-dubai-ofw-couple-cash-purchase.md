If you are earning in Dubai and planning a home for family in Cebu, the biggest risk is committing to a payment plan before you know what happens after the Dubai salaries stop. **Keep saving first; keep a mortgage as a fallback.** If the household can genuinely save **AED 6,000/month net of remittance charges**, a cash purchase near the end of 2030 is plausible within a **PHP 4,500,000 property-price ceiling**. Costs are extra, and this is conditional arithmetic—not an affordability certification.

This example follows Joel, 38, and Marites, 36, a Filipino OFW couple in Dubai with **AED 16,500 combined monthly take-home pay**, **PHP 1,400,000 savings**, two children and their grandmother, and a return around 2030. Expenses, debts, gross qualifying income, Pag-IBIG records, existing MP2 maturity dates and Philippine income after return are unknown. The savings are assumed liquid, debt-free and inclusive of—not additional to—the emergency reserve.

Do not reserve a remote preselling unit just to secure a low advertised “equity” payment or an expiring promotion. If the home is needed earlier, compare verified Pag-IBIG and bank approval on the **same property**, with independent inspection. A manageable long-term mortgage can be better than a short developer balance, but it must not depend on Dubai salaries after they end. Mortgage payments must replace part of future saving; do not count the same income twice.

Rates, rules, advertisements and public remittance indications below were read on **7 November 2026** unless another date is stated. They have not been refreshed for this guide and are not approvals, guaranteed quotes or available-home promises. The underlying source file is not published. This is general planning information, not financial advice.

### What an attractive equity advertisement can hide

**Illustration only:** PHP 4,500,000 × 20% = PHP 900,000 down payment. Spread over 24 months, that is **PHP 37,500/month**—but **PHP 3,600,000** (80%) remains, plus costs. That is not a PHP 37,500 mortgage for the whole home. A reservation fee may be credited or additional; never assume either without the contract.

Monthly payment A = P × r ÷ [1 − (1 + r)^(−n)], where P = principal, r = annual nominal rate ÷ 12, n = number of months. Payments exclude insurance, fees and future repricing. A flat/add-on interest quote needs a different calculation.

**Not ready to use today’s savings as the full deposit:** PHP 1,400,000 − PHP 900,000 down payment − PHP 225,000 provisional costs = PHP 275,000 left, below the provisional PHP 500,000 reserve. A loan approval would not solve that reserve gap. If they buy earlier, mortgage payments must replace part of future saving; do not count the same income twice.

## How an OFW housing loan works—and what remains unconfirmed

Pag-IBIG is not an automatic entitlement to the advertised maximum. For this couple, a PHP 4.5m property does not require borrowing the product ceiling. The approved amount must still fit documented repayment capacity and acceptable appraised collateral; spouses’ incomes cannot simply be added without the required borrower documentation.

### What can be prepared from Dubai?

### Confirmed public online checklist

The official public application-reminder page asks for a completed housing-loan form with 1×1 photo (principal HQP-HLF-068; co-borrower HQP-HLF-069), proof of income, one valid ID with signature and a selfie showing the ID. This is an initial online checklist, not the entire loan-release file. [P6](https://www.pagibigfundservices.com/virtualpagibig/hl/)

The official OFW income page lists an employment contract (employee/employer or POEA standard), employment/compensation certificate (company letterhead, or household employer signature supported by employer ID/passport), and host-country income-tax return. It requires English translation of foreign-language documents. The page lists evidence types; do not assume all are cumulative or invent a UAE tax return where none is issued—use an accepted applicable income document and the current checklist. [P7](https://www.pagibigfundservices.com/virtualpagibig/hl/AppProof.aspx?li=OFW)

### Prepare the paperwork before you commit

As a practical preparation bundle, keep passports/visa or residence ID, marriage/identity records, employment and pay evidence, remittance/bank trail, and consistent name/MID details. The exact current Pag-IBIG property, spouse, insurance and attorney-in-fact requirements remain unconfirmed; obtain the purpose-specific checklist before signing.

In the Philippines, independent representatives can gather title/tax/sale and site information; the lender still needs acceptable collateral, valuation and completion of legal/mortgage conditions before release. Online filing is not ‘the entire house purchase can be completed with scans.’ Bank OFW checklists separately substantiate overseas document preparation and representatives. Use the lender’s current, transaction-specific template and a trusted attorney-in-fact. ### Show the fee and the spread separately

Let total sender budget be B, sender fees F, provider rate q PHP/AED, and recipient deduction D PHP. Then **net PHP = (B − F) × q − D**. Effective value = net PHP ÷ B. Against a same-time independent benchmark m, **FX spread = (m − q) ÷ m**. Do not add spread again after using q in the calculation.

**Teaching example dated 7 November 2026—not market quotes:** B = AED 3,000 and hypothetical m = PHP 15.30/AED. The FX rates and beneficiary deductions below are assumptions, not provider pricing or evidence of which company is cheapest.

| Hypothetical route | Fee within B | Assumed q / spread | Assumed D | Net PHP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Exchange-house style | AED 24.26 | 15.10 / 1.3072% | PHP 150 | 44,783.67 |
| Zero-fee bank style | AED 0 | 15.00 / 1.9608% | PHP 0 | 45,000.00 |
| Zero-fee app style | AED 0 | 15.05 / 1.6340% | PHP 0 | 45,150.00 |

## Verify the seller, the project and the physical home

Recommended safeguards below are a due-diligence workflow—not a clearance of any named property or an allegation against any seller. The LRA title FAQ, original PD 957 and BSP fraud guidance help separate these checks. The DHSUD-branded mirrored guidance below may not reflect the current canonical text. [S1 · 1](https://lra.gov.ph/frequently-asked-questions/) [S1 · 2](https://alagad.dict.gov.ph/~onxzwwhurzzx/buyer/) [S2](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/11/59340) [S3 · 1](https://alagad.dict.gov.ph/~onxzwwhurzzx/maceda-law-ra-6552-legal-faqs/) [S3 · 2](https://alagad.dict.gov.ph/~onxzwwhurzzx/the-maceda-law-and-refund-of-installment-payments-hred-faqs/) [S4](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/SitePages/FinancialStability/BSPVerifier.aspx)

- **Project and phase:** for covered developer sales, independently verify Certificate of Registration and License to Sell for the exact project/phase, not just a company name. Registration is not the same as a license to sell. Private resale exemptions mean not every seller needs a new developer license.
- **Title and authority:** obtain a fresh certified title copy through the Registry of Deeds/LRA route; match owner, lot number, boundaries and annotations. Have an independent Philippine lawyer examine mortgages, adverse claims, ownership/spousal consent, inherited-estate issues and the seller’s SPA. A tax declaration or scan alone is not a verified title.
- **Site:** independent engineer/surveyor or trusted independent inspection, not only an agent’s livestream. Check access/right of way, actual lot boundaries, drainage/flood and slope exposure, structural defects, utility connections, occupancy, unpaid dues/taxes and possession/eviction risk. Bank-owned ‘as-is’ property is not automatically vacant or livable.
- **Family fit:** check a ground-floor sleeping/bathroom option for grandmother, space and safety for the children, schools/healthcare and actual travel times. Do not substitute a citywide traffic or flood stereotype for a site check.
- **Contract:** obtain complete price and payment schedule, credited reservation, equity, balloon/takeout date, fees/taxes, title-transfer timetable, turnover specification, delay remedies, refund/default clauses and the result if the lender approves less. ‘Guaranteed approval’ from a salesperson is not lender approval.
- **Payments:** verify payee and bank instructions independently against the contract and seller identity; pause on last-minute account substitutions, personal-wallet demands or pressure to pay before documents. Retain official receipts and a reconciled ledger. Do not trust a bank logo or screenshot as proof of settled funds.
- **Representative:** use a narrowly drafted property/lender-specific SPA reviewed independently. Avoid blank powers to sell, substitute representatives or receive proceeds unless genuinely needed. Separate payment control from the sales agent; keep copies and spending limits.
- **Pause triggers:** inconsistent lot/title/project names; missing phase license; refusal of an independent inspection; implausibly cheap price without explanation; unverifiable seller identity; guaranteed appreciation/rent; urgency tied to a supposed one-day approval.

### Refund rights are not a blanket exit clause

For covered projects, **PD 957 section 23** addresses non-development according to approved plans/deadlines: after due notice, affected buyers have non-forfeiture/refund rights. It does *not* mean they can stop paying for any reason. Section 24 sends other installment defaults to RA 6552 (Maceda Law); section 25 concerns title delivery on full payment. [S2](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/11/59340)

The DHSUD-branded Maceda FAQs describe earned grace/cash-surrender protections for qualifying installment buyers, including a 50% starting cash-surrender value after at least two years’ qualifying installments, rising by 5 percentage points per year after five years up to 90%, with prescribed cancellation/notice/payment conditions. Under two years, the basic rule includes at least 60 days’ grace, not an automatic refund. These mirrored explanations are **unconfirmed as current canonical copies**; actual coverage, advance payments and the contract need review. Bank mortgage foreclosure is not interchangeable with developer installment cancellation. [S3 · 1](https://alagad.dict.gov.ph/~onxzwwhurzzx/maceda-law-ra-6552-legal-faqs/) [S3 · 2](https://alagad.dict.gov.ph/~onxzwwhurzzx/the-maceda-law-and-refund-of-installment-payments-hred-faqs/)

Do not interpret either ‘all equity is non-refundable’ or ‘all equity is always refundable’ as a reliable summary. Do not stop payments or sign a waiver based on this guide alone.

## 2027–2030: build the option to buy for cash

**Base case: 48 monthly deposits, January 2027–December 2030; purchase at the end of December 2030.** AED 6,000 is the amount converted into house savings *after* remittance charges. PHP 15 per AED is a deliberately illustrative net conversion assumption—not today's market rate or a forecast. No investment return, salary increase or house-price appreciation is assumed.

Monthly net saving = AED 6,000 × PHP 15 = **PHP 90,000** — Annual net saving = PHP 90,000 × 12 = **PHP 1,080,000** — Income remaining = AED 16,500 − AED 6,000 = **AED 10,500/month** for all living costs, family support, fees, insurance, contributions and other commitments. — Saving rate = 6,000 ÷ 16,500 = **36.36%**.

| Period | Opening cash | New net savings | Closing cash before purchase | Decision / payment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2027 | PHP 1,400,000 | PHP 1,080,000 | PHP 2,480,000 | Reconcile membership, expenses and documents. No developer equity commitment in this base case. |
| 2028 | PHP 2,480,000 | PHP 1,080,000 | PHP 3,560,000 | Shortlist areas; arrange independent physical/title checks only when ready to buy. |
| 2029 | PHP 3,560,000 | PHP 1,080,000 | PHP 4,640,000 | Still short of house + costs + protected reserve. Do not count “almost enough” as ready. |
| 2030 | PHP 4,640,000 | PHP 1,080,000 | PHP 5,720,000 | After December deposit: pay PHP 4,500,000 price + PHP 225,000 provisional costs; retain PHP 995,000. |

**Closing-cost allowance:** PHP 4,500,000 × 5% = PHP 225,000; total purchase budget = **PHP 4,725,000**. The 5% is a planning allowance, not a tax rate, statutory fee schedule, or seller quote. It must be replaced by a contract-specific breakdown of transfer/registration/notarial, lender/insurance if applicable, tax allocation and repairs. A property price that excludes mandatory charges must be adjusted before comparing it with the ceiling.

**Reserve:** PHP 5,720,000 − PHP 4,725,000 = **PHP 995,000**. Ring-fence at least PHP 500,000 provisionally, leaving PHP 495,000 for relocation/repairs/contingency. PHP 500,000 is not a validated six-month expense fund: choose the actual reserve from the family's expenses and job-transition risk.

### If “2030” means January, the result changes

Only 36 deposits have been made by 31 December 2029: PHP 4,640,000. Price plus the provisional costs alone exceeds that by **PHP 85,000**; keeping a PHP 500,000 reserve produces a **PHP 585,000 gap**. At the same saving pace, seven more deposits add PHP 630,000, so **end-July 2030** is the earliest whole-month target for this price, costs and reserve. End-December gives more breathing room. Do not stop earning in January 2030 and assume 48 deposits will still arrive.

### Where to keep the money

Separate the PHP emergency reserve from the house pot. Keep near-term purchase money accessible and gradually match the future PHP liability rather than betting everything on one conversion date. New MP2 savings in January 2027 normally mature in January 2032, not 2030; do not rely on early withdrawal to close the purchase. Existing MP2 counts only if its actual maturity and accessible net proceeds fit the deadline. No MP2 dividends are included in these calculations.
