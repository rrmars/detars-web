The €2,400 budget for two excludes international flights and keeps €404.40 unspent. The catch is the room bill: €900 for six nights is a target, not a September offer. Trenitalia's fare search was blocked, so the €100 train allowance is not a verified fare.**

The six sightseeing days end Thursday; Friday is departure only. Stay in Rome September 4–7 and Florence September 7–10, three nights in each. ## Four tickets, not a premium bundle

**Skip the Colosseum's free Sunday, not its security queue.** September 5 is the first Sunday: current free tickets are collected in arrival order. Saving €36 for two is not worth building this relaxed trip around that gamble. Tuesday is the better fit. The Vatican Museums are normally closed that Sunday; Monday is the plan, not a promise of empty galleries. Check [Colosseum tickets](https://colosseo.it/en/tickets/colosseum-roman-forum-palatine/), [sales rules](https://colosseo.it/en/opening-times-and-tickets/) and [Vatican hours](https://www.museivaticani.va/content/museivaticani/en/info/orari-musei-vaticani.html) before choosing your slots. Bring photo ID and use mandatory storage for backpacks and medium/large bags. The pass covers three calendar days starting on the climb date, September 8–10, one entry per monument.
