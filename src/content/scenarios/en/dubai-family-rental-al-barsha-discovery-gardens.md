You want a home that makes school mornings manageable—not just a rent that fits the allowance. **Start with a 2-bedroom in Al Barsha 1, keep Discovery Gardens as your value alternative, and choose The Greens if a shorter work commute is worth the higher household contribution.** Confirm a viable Indian-curriculum school plan before committing to a long lease.

This plan is for parents in their late 30s moving from Bengaluru in March 2027, with children aged 6 and 10, a workplace in Dubai Internet City (DIC), salary of **AED 38,000/month** and a stated **AED 120,000/year housing allowance**. A 2-bed assumes the children can share. If they need separate rooms, restart the search for a genuine 3-bed; a study is not automatically a bedroom.

## Agree the payment schedule before comparing affordability

The lease must specify rent and its payment method under the [2008 tenancy-law amendment](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2009/Law%20No.%20(33)%20of%202008%20Amending%20Law%20No.%20(26)%20of%202007.html). Agree cheque count, dates, payee and every charge before handing over money. The first instalment is normally reserved for commencement, with later cheques post-dated as agreed; confirm bank requirements and funding dates. Do not assume you will have a UAE cheque book on arrival. Transfers or another arrangement need agreement too.

The ads demonstrate **2, 4 and 12 cheques**, not a same-property premium for flexibility. Assuming equal instalments, L1 is **AED 7,333.25**, L4 **AED 41,500** and L5 **AED 27,500** each. Actual dates and rounding belong in the lease.

## Separate spending, refundable deposits and cash needed for keys

The model below is a transparent first-year housing scenario, **not a fully quoted bill**. There is no executed lease, verified cooling schedule or actual unit bill. All figures are AED; model allowances already include VAT where applicable. Deposits tie up cash but are not expenditure.

### What to confirm before using the totals

- **Landlord security and broker:** 5% security and 5% commission plus 5% VAT—5.25% of rent—are **unconfirmed for L1/L4/L5**. L2 advertises these terms, but that does not establish them for the other homes. There is no universal statutory 5% deposit. Agree deductions/refunds and invoiced commission; establish whether a holding payment is refundable or credited, not an extra fee.
- **Ejari:** the [DLD registration service](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/eservices/register-renew-ejari-contract/) publishes AED 177.75 online/REST or AED 220 via trustee, subject to route eligibility. Trustee components total AED 219.75, a AED 0.25 discrepancy with the published total; the model prudently uses 220 once per tenancy year. Agency processing is separate from government fees.
- **DEWA:** [move-in charges](https://dewa.gov.ae/en/about-us/service-guide/consumer-services/move-in) include refundable security of AED 2,000 for an apartment or AED 4,000 for a villa. Small-meter activation is 125 + 10 + 10 + 10 = AED 155; large-meter activation is 300 + 10 + 10 + 10 = AED 330. Meter type, not “townhouse”, determines activation. No separate VAT is invented on these activation components. This model assumes an apartment/small meter; reconfirm charges for your move.
- **Housing fee:** the [DLD FAQ](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/frequently-asked-questions/) gives 5% of annual rent; the [Dubai living guide](https://www.investindubai.gov.ae/en/live-and-work/dubai-real-estate-guide) confirms 12 parts on the utility bill. It is neither VAT nor the landlord's building service charges. Check assessed rental value and occupancy dates on the first bill.
- **Usage:** [DEWA's progressive tariff](https://www.dewa.gov.ae/en/consumer/billing/slab-tariff) has changing fuel surcharges, meter charges and VAT. **AED 600/month is an unconfirmed usage reserve**, excluding housing fee and separate cooling, not an official family tariff. Summer bills can be materially higher.
- **Cooling:** identify provider, demand/RT load, consumption, fuel, metering, VAT, deposit and activation for the actual unit. [Empower's charge explanation](https://www.empower.ae/customer-care/charges-explanation/) includes AED 750/RT/year demand, location-dependent metering and additional fuel/VAT. Its consumption wording is ambiguous: do not derive a bill from its “fils” label without a confirmed tariff schedule. [RSB's 2026 tariff rules](https://rsbdubai.gov.ae/media/baufmpnp/rd10-tariffs-v14.pdf) distinguish caps from approved building charges. [Registration requirements](https://www.empower.ae/customer-care/how-to-register/) do not establish a universal Empower deposit.
- **“Chiller free”:** ask exactly which fixed capacity, consumption, fuel and metering costs the landlord pays. **AED 6,000/year cooling, AED 2,500 refundable cooling reserve and AED 250 setup are unconfirmed budgets**, not official Empower quotes. The annual aggregate already includes demand, consumption, fuel, metering and VAT—do not add them twice. Remove only genuinely included costs; if AC runs on DEWA, recast electricity and remove duplicate separate cooling.
- **Other reserves:** internet, gas, contents cover, minor repairs, moving/furnishing and admin figures are unconfirmed budgets. Check installation/router, minimum term and early-exit fees, gas provider/connection/deposit and optional contents cover. No fibre price substantiates the AED 400/month internet assumption.
- **Maintenance and extras:** [the tenancy law](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2007/Law%20No.%20%2826%29%20of%202007%20Regarding%20regulation%20between%20the%20lessor%20and%20the%20lessee.html) makes landlord maintenance affecting use the default unless otherwise agreed. Read the minor-repair addendum; no universal AED cutoff is assumed. DLD assigns building service charges to the landlord. The model assumes one usable parking space is included; zero extra parking budget does not mean parking is free. Actual building/gas deposits, management/renewal fees, cleaning, pest control and house pool/garden costs need checking.

**First-year gross cash is not all due on arrival.** It includes all rent instalments and refundable deposits. Signing cash excludes the AED 11,500 move/furniture budget and subsequent bills; some setup payments may fall later. Ejari is counted once, not again in extras. No annual brokerage renewal fee or rent-rise forecast is assumed; any contractual renewal charge is additional. Rounded monthly housing-fee equivalents may differ by a few fils from annual billing.

**Test the cooling and rent sensitivities.** If all modelled recurring cooling is genuinely landlord-paid, annual spending falls AED 6,000; setup/deposit changes depend separately on actual coverage. AED 1,000/year more in usage/charges adds AED 1,000. A 10% higher negotiated rent adds 10.5% of the old rent to recurring cost and 11.025% of the old rent to first-year spend under these fixed assumptions; deposits change separately. These are scenarios, not forecasts.

**Keep a separate family relocation reserve.** School fees/bus, temporary lodging, visas/attestation, flights, international shipping beyond the local-move reserve, car/fuel/tolls, groceries, healthcare and other living costs are excluded. No actual building parking/gas deposit or management charge was obtained. The table is not a guaranteed ceiling on your move.

## Know what renewal protection does—and does not—cover

[Decree 43/2013](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2013/Decree%20No.%20%2843%29%20of%202013%20Determining%20Rent%20Increase%20for%20Real%20Property.pdf) ties renewal increases to RERA's approved comparator. The maximum percentage applies to **your existing rent**, not to the index rent. It does not cap a newly negotiated March 2027 tenancy.

| Existing rent below official comparable average | Maximum renewal increase |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 10% | 0% |
| 11–20% | 5% |
| 21–30% | 10% |
| 31–40% | 15% |
| More than 40% | 20% |

Use the official calculator for fractional boundaries/rounding rather than interpolating these integer bands. For illustration only, a comparator of AED 125,000 and existing rent AED 100,000 gives (125,000 − 100,000) ÷ 125,000 = **20% below**. The maximum rise is 5% × 100,000 = **AED 5,000**, giving **AED 105,000**, not an automatic jump to 125,000. This is not an official result for any listed unit. The DLD announcement has apparent decree-number and band-wording errors; use the enacted decree for the bands.

- **Amendment notice:** at least 90 days before expiry unless otherwise agreed. For a hypothetical 31 March 2028 expiry, 90 days before is 1 January 2028. Check the agreed clause and communicate earlier; an increase proposal is not an eviction power.
- **End-of-term eviction:** specified sale, qualifying personal/first-degree-family use, demolition or major restoration grounds need statutory conditions and at least 12 months' notice through notary public or registered mail. Breach/nonpayment has separate procedures, including the prescribed 30-day payment notice for unpaid rent; twelve months does not protect every breach. Qualifying personal-use repossession restricts residential reletting to a third party for at least two years, with potential compensation.
- **During the lease:** unilateral termination is not generally allowed outside agreement/law. Sale alone does not cancel fixed-term occupation. Landlord service disconnection/disturbance is prohibited. Deposit deductions need justification; ordinary wear and tear is excepted. Keep move-in photographs, inventory, meter readings, receipts and written defect reports.
- **If disputed:** retain the contract, Ejari, dated official index result, notices and correspondence. Use DLD/Rental Disputes Center channels instead of withholding rent or accepting an unsupported rise. Relief/costs depend on the case; no filing fee is budgeted.

Read the 2008 amendment together with the 2007 law: the original two-year rent freeze is superseded. **Do not assume “no increase for two years”.** Arabic prevails over conflicting official English legislation. Recheck the law for your actual March 2027 contract and later renewal.

## From arrival to keys: work through the dependencies

These are planning steps, not a guaranteed handover timetable. Employer/residency, school and banking lead times can delay keys. No enquiries, accounts, bookings or forms were submitted for this guide.

1. **November 2026–February 2027:** confirm allowance treatment and payout. Explore school placement, fees and transfer records; prepare passports, employment offer and identity/residency documents. Budget temporary accommodation. Do not commit to an unseen annual home from November advertisements.
2. **Arrival / first week:** coordinate employer residency/Emirates ID and bank/cheque-book readiness. Stay temporarily while prerequisites remain incomplete; lodging is extra. Refresh listings and school options for March rather than trusting old availability fields.
3. **First week onward:** view units and test school/DIC routes at realistic times. Check floor plan, ventilation, AC, plumbing, noise, parking, safety, appliances and defects. Review prior bills. Verify landlord ownership/representative authority and broker registration through official DLD channels; a portal badge is not title verification. These property checks have not been performed here.
4. **Before agreeing money:** obtain written rent for each cheque count, deposit deductions/refunds, commission invoice, cooling allocation/provider/prior bills, maintenance, break clause/notice/early-exit costs, renewal/admin, parking and move permits. Establish school feasibility before a nonrefundable commitment. Record holding-payment refund/offset terms.
5. **Contract and bank readiness:** sign only when contract/addendum, payee, authority and terms agree. Schedule cheques/transfers and collect receipts. Do not pay an unknown third party. Make handover conditional on agreed repairs and utilities.
6. **Ejari:** choose an eligible DLD/REST or trustee route. Prepare the signed unified contract and applicant Emirates ID where required; representatives need appropriate power of attorney. Keep the certificate and pay evidenced charges only. Published trustee processing of 25 minutes excludes waiting and is not total arrival-to-keys time.
7. **Utilities and building access:** ordinary tenant DEWA move-in needs valid Ejari; qualifying free-zone exceptions exist. Pay required security/activation and follow provider instructions. DEWA's target is within 15 working hours after required payment, not immediately on signing. Arrange the correct cooling/gas registration, internet and building move-in permissions. 8. **Keys:** verify repairs, utilities and AC; jointly record meters/inventory; take timestamped photographs and collect keys, access cards and parking rights. Book movers after building permission. Report unresolved defects in writing.
9. **First bills, renewal and exit:** check housing fee, demand/consumption/fuel/VAT and deposits. Set cheque-funding reminders and the agreed renewal-notice deadline; obtain a new official index result at renewal. [DEWA refunds](https://www.dewa.gov.ae/en/consumer/billing/request-for-refund) follow final-account settlement: IBAN refund has no fee; cheque refund is AED 20 + VAT. [Empower final bills/refunds](https://www.empower.ae/customer-care/e-final-bill-procedure/) also have settlement and refund-route conditions. Do not assume a deposit is immediately available to fund your next home.
