**Worried that your parents will be exhausted before lunch? Spend on a convenient hotel and taxis, not another temple. For three adults, including parents both 70, plan ¥165,000 plus ¥15,000 reserve: ¥180,000 for two nights, excluding Shinkansen fares. This is a spending framework, not a bookable package or a guarantee of autumn prices.**

The example trip is Wednesday November 17, 2027 to Friday November 19, 2027, arriving from Tokyo around 10:00 and leaving around 18:00. Those are planning anchors, not booked trains. Prices and sources were checked on November 6, 2026, not confirmed for the travel year. The plan assumes a south-side Kyoto Station hotel and walking below a 6 km ceiling—not a target to use up. Rest for 15–20 minutes every 45–60 minutes, or sooner. Distances, journey times, car waits and taxi ranges are estimates, not a mobility assessment or live routing.

## Look forward to the leaves, without betting the trip on peak red

[Japan-guide](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3953.html) places the usual best colour in the second half of November. These dates are near the early side: expect a possible mix of green, yellow and red, not a guaranteed scarlet garden. **No verified foliage forecast for 2027 was available.** The [JMC release](https://n-kishou.com/corp/news-contents/autumn/?lang=en) dated November 4, 2026 listed Kyoto peak red on December 14, 2026 and peak yellow on November 30, 2026. These are city-level representative-tree indicators for that year, not a forecast for your gardens or trip. Recheck the travel-year forecast and [Kyoto's live reports](https://kyoto.travel/en/autumnleaves/) closer to departure.

Keep to a compact area each day, visit Nanzenji early and skip illumination queues. Shōsei-en and the Botanical Gardens are calmer bets, not empty secrets. This plan deliberately leaves out the Kiyomizu/Arashiyama circuit and Eikandō peak-time crush. Weekdays can help, but autumn crowds remain possible.

## Wednesday: arrive, eat, and see how everyone feels

Allow 10–15 minutes to orient yourselves at Kyoto Station, then 30–45 minutes from platform to hotel with luggage. Use lifts and toilets; leave bags only if pre-check-in storage is offered. Early room access is not assumed. If the train morning was tiring, sit down before sightseeing.

| Time | Gentle plan | Cost or access limit |
|---|---|---|
| 10:00–11:15 | Arrival, toilets, bags and a pause | Confirm hotel storage; no early check-in promise |
| 11:15–12:15 | Early station-area lunch, chairs and short queue | ¥6,000 for three, estimate |
| 12:15–13:20 | Higashi Honganji | Free; winter hours 06:20–16:30. Use the southern Amida-dō entrance and corridor lift/ramps, not the Goei-dō front stairs |
| 13:20–14:45 | Rest, then optional Shōsei-en | Walk about 10–15 minutes; visit 45–60 minutes only if comfortable. ¥700 minimum each / ¥2,100 for three; 09:00–16:00, last entry 15:30 |
| 15:00 onward | Hotel check-in at its stated time, room rest, nearby dinner | Garden-to-hotel estimate 20–30 minutes on foot or 10–20 by taxi, ¥800–1,500 per car. Dinner ¥9,000 for three, estimate |

[Higashi Honganji's hours](https://www.higashihonganji.or.jp/faq/) cover November–February; the [municipal access guide](https://shugakuryoko.kyoto.travel/spot/new東本願寺/) explains the lift/ramp route. From the station north side allow 15–20 minutes on foot, or 25–35 from a south-side hotel including the crossing. A taxi avoids that crossing: 10–20 minutes and ¥1,000–1,800 per car, estimated.

[Shōsei-en's fee and winter hours](https://www.higashihonganji.or.jp/about/guide/shoseien/guide/) are for the outdoor garden, not building interiors. Its [pond paths and bridge](https://english.higashihonganji.or.jp/shosei-en/) do not establish a continuous step-free loop. Ask at the gate for a short out-and-back; skip bridges or the whole garden if surfaces look awkward. **If arrival slips past noon, drop Shōsei-en first.** Lunch, Higashi Honganji and a room rest are enough. Expected walking: about 3–4 km, including station and meal walks.

## Thursday: Nanzenji outside, then a lunch that suits the knee

| 17:30 | Easy hotel-area dinner, or takeaway | ¥9,000 for three, estimate |

[Nanzenji's November paid-area opening](https://www.nanzenji.or.jp/about_rinzaishu/visit) is 08:40–17:00, reception closing 20 minutes earlier; the opening anchor is not an empty-grounds promise. [Exterior grounds are free](https://souda-kyoto.jp/guide/spot/nanzenji.html). The temple's [access notice](https://www.nanzenji.or.jp/information/notices/20200716) warns of gravel, slopes and stairs. Do not climb Sanmon or choose Nanzen-in/upper paths to avoid steps. Optional interiors are not budgeted: Sanmon ¥600, Hōjō garden ¥600, Nanzen-in ¥500 each. An indoor wheelchair at Hōjō does not prove independent step-free entry. Walking estimate: 2.5–4 km, with an early turn-back always allowed.

[Junsei's menu](https://to-fu.co.jp/menu/) supplies the prices; its FAQ retains an older lower figure. [Lunch hours](https://to-fu.co.jp/access/) are 11:00–15:30, last order 14:30, with irregular closures. Going at opening may reduce waiting, not eliminate it. **Lunch is conditional on suitable ground-floor chair seating.** The [FAQ](https://to-fu.co.jp/news/409/) allows upstairs/tatami allocation and does not guarantee chair requests; [priority admission](https://to-fu.co.jp/reserve/) does not secure a chosen table. Settle this before later committing. Leave rather than accept stairs or a long standing queue. No seat, opening date or booking has been secured. The FAQ also says return taxis cannot currently be arranged by the restaurant.

**Backup: change the lunch, not your parents' limits.** Ukihashi at Hotel Granvia Kyoto, M3F in Kyoto Station, has a [Mibu kaiseki lunch](https://jrwest-hotels.jp/granvia-kyoto/restaurant/menu/292/) at ¥7,000 each / ¥21,000 for three, including tax and 15% service, on its November 1, 2026 to December 31, 2026 menu. That is not a travel-year quote. Replacing tofu lunch uses ¥6,000 of the reserve, leaving ¥9,000. [Lunch information](https://jrwest-hotels.jp/en/granvia-kyoto/restaurant/ukihashi/) gives 11:30–15:00, last order 14:00. Leave Nanzenji about 11:00 for an illustrative 12:30 lunch, allowing 35–60 minutes by taxi plus pickup. Confirm an ordinary chair table, lift route and thresholds first; private rooms have a four-person minimum and higher prices, so their chair setup is not assumed for this party.

## Friday: a garden, then plenty of train buffer

| Time | Gentle plan | Cost or access limit |
|---|---|---|
| 08:15–09:30 | Breakfast, checkout, store bags if offered; taxi to Kitayama entrance | Estimate 30–50 minutes, ¥3,500–5,000 per car; aim 09:00–09:30 |
| 09:30–12:00 | Botanical Gardens: short main-path loop, seated break, optional conservatory | ¥500 adult / ¥250 age 65+, ¥1,000 for the group. Carry age ID; allow ¥1,500 if discount cannot be applied |

| 14:30–departure | Sit-down café break, then collect luggage | Collect about 16:00 for illustrative 18:00 train; be inside station at least 60 minutes before your actual train. Supper/bento ¥4,500 for three, estimate |

[Current garden information](https://www.pref.kyoto.jp/plant/usageguidance.html) gives 09:00–17:00, last entry 16:00; conservatory 10:00–16:00, last entry 15:30, included in admission. The regular closure is December 28–January 4, not every Friday; exceptional closures and travel-year arrangements still need checking. The day/night-reversal room normally closes November–April, not the entire conservatory. In rain, shorten the loop; with an unhappy knee, replace the outing with a slow station morning.

The [municipal guide](https://shugakuryoko.kyoto.travel/spot/485/) lists accessible toilets and loan wheelchairs, but loans cannot be reserved or guaranteed. Stay on broad paths, not uneven woodland detours or bridges. Expected walking is 3–4.5 km. Do not carry suitcases through the garden or add a final cross-city sight. Lift queues, toilets and the correct Shinkansen entrance all need time. Wet leaves, gravel, low seats and shoe removal can be harder than distance; stop well before the ceiling if needed.

Under ¥6,000 → ¥200; ¥6,000–under ¥20,000 → ¥400; ¥20,000–under ¥50,000 → ¥1,000; ¥50,000–under ¥100,000 → ¥4,000; ¥100,000+ → ¥10,000. The allowance assumes six person-nights in the ¥400 bracket. Check the actual taxable base and avoid paying twice if tax is already collected.

## Buy fewer transfers, not a pass you will barely use

The [MK tariff](https://www.mk-group.co.jp/kyoto/taxi/), marked as of October 1, 2026, lists ¥470 for 900 m, then ¥100 per 271 m; at 10 km/h or below, ¥100 per 100 seconds under time-distance charging. Moving-only illustrations are ¥970 for 2 km, ¥2,070 for 5 km and ¥3,170 for 8 km per car. Dispatch adds ¥300; time-specified pickup adds another ¥500. These are operator tariffs/calculations, not route quotes, universal city fares or travel-year guarantees. Traffic, app charges and operator choice matter.

[Ordinary flat-zone buses](https://www2.city.kyoto.lg.jp/kotsu/webguide/en/fare/fare_bus.html) cost ¥230 per adult: ¥690 for three on a direct ride, ¥1,380 for two separately charged rides each. Sightseeing express is ¥500 each / ¥1,500 for three; the [Subway & Bus 1-Day Pass](https://www2.city.kyoto.lg.jp/kotsu/webguide/en/ticket/regular_1day_card_comm.html) is ¥1,100 each / ¥3,300 for three. Neither is used here, and no tourist senior discount is assumed. At 2 km a taxi may be close enough to the group bus fare to avoid standing; at 8 km it is clearly dearer. Use buses only when seating and a straightforward route work.

Allow **¥18,000 group transport**. Estimated daily car spending is ¥0–3,300 / ¥6,300–10,000 / ¥7,500–11,000; the high ends exceed that allowance, so keep the reserve. Add 15–20 minutes to find/wait for a car. Three passengers fit a normal taxi, but bulky luggage may need a larger one: leave bags at the hotel while sightseeing.
