**Worried that fees will swallow the money you should be paying yourself? At 40 orders a month with £45 of goods per order, start with Etsy if you need new customers to discover you. Choose Shopify Basic only if your own customers, mailing list or social audience can bring the orders. Test a small Folksy range rather than opening five shops at once. NOTHS needs a quote, and Amazon's conflicting published rates need resolving before you rely on its margin.**

This example is for a Bristol handmade-silver maker planning January 2027. Fees and rules were checked on **November 6, 2026**, not refreshed for this draft. Postage prices are dated samples, other business costs are assumptions, and none is a promise for January. Recheck the applicable rates and requirements before **January 1, 2027**. Nothing has been applied for, opened, ordered or paid for.

### Etsy: pay for discovery, but understand the extra layers

Listings last four months and cost US$0.20 per item/renewal. The illustrative conversion is **£0.80 per US$1**, not a checked exchange rate: £0.16 before VAT per listing event. Five extra events are included each month; no signup promotions are assumed. Listing and payout currencies are both GBP in this example. If they differ, the cited fee schedule adds a **2.5% currency-conversion fee**.

The 6.5% transaction and 0.48% regulatory charges apply to goods and postage; gift wrap can also enter the regulatory base. Payments charges include postage and applicable buyer sales tax. The example adds **20% fee VAT** for the nonregistered UK maker, including relevant processing fees. A valid VAT ID can change what Etsy collects, but does not settle your reverse-charge accounting.

Offsite Ads are charged only on attributed orders. Below **US$10,000 in any consecutive 365 days**, the published rate was **15%**, with eligible opt-out. After reaching that threshold, the rate becomes **12%** and participation stays compulsory. Attribution runs for **30 days**, with a **US$100 fee cap per order**. The comparison assumes **10% of orders attributed at 12%** for an established shop. That is not the right default for every new seller: use 15% if enrolled below the threshold, or zero attribution only when genuinely no charged orders or valid opt-out apply.

An onboarding setup charge may apply; no amount has been verified here. **Etsy's first-month cash result stays “Setup quote needed” until the actual charge—or no charge—is confirmed.** Recurring profit below does not include an invented setup amount.

Folksy is for UK makers and allows international buyers. At **18p including VAT** per charged quantity, monthly Plus at an assumed final £7.50 beats Basic at about **42 charged listing events**, not 42 sales. With five extra events, the 40-order case already crosses that line. An annual £75 payment changes upfront cash; confirm the advertised Plus price's VAT treatment first.

A PayPal account is required as an account option. Its alternative standard domestic commercial rate was **2.9% + 30p**, with payment-type, cross-border and FX differences. Do not add that on top of a Stripe-processed order.

## Hallmarking: weigh each article, then batch compatible pieces

Silver articles **over 7.78g** need a recognised hallmark when sold or described as silver, subject to exemptions. **“925” alone is not the full hallmark.** Each earring counts as a separate article; a detachable pendant and chain are separate too. Check mixed-metal pieces and borderline weights with the assay office, and display the online Dealer's Notice.

London Assay Office's schedule effective **April 6, 2026** charged **£18 per packet for 1–2 articles**, or **£22 per packet + £1.45 per article for 3+**, all before VAT. Ten articles therefore cost **£43.80 including VAT before carriage**, not £1.45 all-in per piece. Sponsor registration was **£72 including VAT for ten years**, with standard laser setup included; an optional punch is extra.

The comparison assumes one homogeneous packet per month, compatible marking method/type/fineness, and whole articles rounded up. It budgets outward insured carriage at £10.85 and **estimated** return base postage £10.85, plus **£3.25 return administration** and a **20% allowance on the return invoice**. Return postage is actual cost, not a verified £10.85 quote. Batch weight/value can require more cover or a higher price. If several packets are needed, the calculation divides articles as evenly as possible and applies each packet tariff; real mixed-fineness batches may be uneven and need a precise quote.

Paper hallnotes add **60p/article before VAT**; handling, priority, failures/resubmission, bespoke marks and logistics can add costs. The default 25% hallmark-needed mix is only a planning assumption. Weigh the stock and replace it before setting retail prices.

Sources, accessed November 6, 2026: [London Assay Office FAQ](https://www.assayofficelondon.co.uk/faqs) and [April 2026 charges](https://www.assayofficelondon.co.uk/media/3247/london-assay-office-price-list-6-april-2026.pdf). These are one office's charges, not uniform UK pricing.

## EU orders have three separate jobs: VAT, border charges and safety

### VAT collection is not the same as import clearance

The dated IOSS rules covered eligible imported consignments with intrinsic value **up to €150**. Etsy collects import VAT for qualifying EU orders: check order details and transmit its IOSS electronically through the carrier—**never write the IOSS number on the parcel**. Shopify is your own shop, not automatic marketplace IOSS. A non-EU seller generally needs an intermediary to use IOSS. Otherwise establish the import-VAT route and tell the buyer clearly about charges before checkout. Above €150 needs a different process. The **€10,000 intra-EU threshold does not exempt GB exports**.

Sources, accessed November 6, 2026: [European Commission IOSS guidance](https://vat-one-stop-shop.ec.europa.eu/one-stop-shop_en), with the import scheme from July 1, 2021, and [Etsy EU import VAT/IOSS](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360000337247-Custom-Fees-and-Physical-VAT-Collection).

### Budget duty and handling separately from postage

The retained Commission guidance described **€3 temporary low-value customs duty per tariff category**, effective **July 1, 2026**, for qualifying distance-sale consignments up to €150, until **July 1, 2028**. It is not automatically one charge per parcel or per physical piece. The business declarant normally pays. The narrow preferential-origin exception requires non-IOSS VAT and an H1 declaration; do not assume a handmade-in-Bristol label alone removes the duty.

Royal Mail's more recent page/guide, updated **October 27, 2026**, also published **€2 handling per declaration line from November 1, 2026**, while the Commission's older **June 8, 2026** page still called the separate handling fee proposed. **The comparison budgets both, rather than assuming the older wording means free handling.** At an assumed **£0.87/€1**, one category gives **£4.35 per EU order**. That is a budget conversion, not a live exchange rate or carrier quote.

Confirm Click & Drop/PDDP checkout, product-ID data and who pays before dispatch. Product IDs were required from November 1, 2026. Some destinations offer DDU; PDDP calculates border charges. Italy has a separate local administration charge. Add any other destination, clearance and compliance costs. In this comparison, buyer VAT is extra to the buyer, but the duty/handling allowance is a **seller cost**; do not quietly move it to the customer or count it twice.

Sources, accessed November 6, 2026: [Royal Mail EU trade changes](https://www.royalmail.com/europeantradeinsights) and [Commission temporary low-value duty guidance](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/news/guidance-and-legal-text-temporary-flat-fee-low-value-imports-which-will-apply-until-1-july-2028-2026-06-08_en). Reconfirm the route and actual bill before January shipping.

### Safety obligations still apply when a marketplace collects VAT

The retained GPSR guidance applied to the **EU and Northern Ireland from December 13, 2024**. Arrange the responsible economic operator, safety assessment/documentation, traceability, contact details and suitable warnings before selling there. A GB maker is not automatically the required EU/NI operator. GB-only sales have a separate regime; this is not a complete product-specific compliance assessment.

Representative/testing and IOSS service costs remain **unquoted**, not unnecessary. Add them to the budget or pause those destinations until ready. Marketplace VAT collection does not discharge product-safety duties.

Source, accessed November 6, 2026: [GOV.UK detailed GPSR guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/general-product-safety-regulations-northern-ireland/eu-regulation-2023988-on-general-product-safety-detailed-guidance).

## Keep tax and customer rights beside your margin

The dated standard UK VAT rate used for taxable fees is **20%**, effective **January 4, 2011**. A nonregistered maker cannot reclaim input VAT. Foreign business services may require reverse-charge accounting, and relevant imported services can count toward the registration test even if you are not registered. **An invoice charging no cash VAT does not establish that you have no VAT obligation.** Ask an accountant to check your actual platform invoices, registration position and income tax/NI rather than treating this fee comparison as a tax return.

Sources, accessed November 6, 2026: [HMRC VAT rates](https://www.gov.uk/vat-rates) and [place of supply/reverse-charge guidance, section 5](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/vat-place-of-supply-of-services-notice-741a).

For ordinary UK online orders, buyers have **14 days to tell you they are cancelling, then 14 days to return**. Refund standard outbound delivery too. “Handmade” is not automatically bespoke. Genuine custom/personalised products and appropriately sealed hygiene goods after opening have narrower exceptions; **earrings are not automatically non-returnable**. Faulty/not-as-described rights remain. Write returns wording that matches what you actually sell and leave enough margin and cash to honour it.

Source, accessed November 6, 2026: [GOV.UK accepting returns and refunds](https://www.gov.uk/accepting-returns-and-giving-refunds). The loss allowance above does not replace these rights or calculate each platform's refund-fee treatment.

## A manageable January launch

- **Before launch:** price your time, weigh each design and packed order, confirm hallmark evidence, postage cover and returns wording. Recheck Amazon's conflicting rate, Etsy setup, Shopify subscription VAT and Folksy Plus invoice treatment. Get the NOTHS quote before ranking it.
- **Choose one main channel:** Etsy for discovery; Shopify if existing customers can already bring those 40 orders. Try a small Folksy range without maintaining five separate catalogues.
- **Before enabling EU/NI destinations:** settle safety arrangements, VAT/import route, product IDs and actual border charges; quote the services currently missing from the budget.
- **Review after eight weeks:** record profit per order after your time, customer source, repeat sales, refunds and hours spent. Add another channel only when it solves a real problem.
