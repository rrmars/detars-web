**Worried about getting the visa back before your holiday? For a Bengaluru couple departing India on 2027-05-15, aim to lodge during 2027-01-18–2027-02-12. Leave room to fix problems instead of waiting for the minimum boundary. First settle your France–Italy day split and arrival date: the first landing airport does not automatically decide where to apply.**

**Please use this as a starting point, not tax, financial or legal advice. An appointment or complete file cannot guarantee visa approval or border admission.**

## Start with your real travel dates

This example is for 2 salaried adult Indian ordinary-passport holders living in Bengaluru, planning a first Schengen holiday of 10 days. Departure from India on 2027-05-15 is fixed; arrival, exit, country split and first external border still need deciding. A visit from 2027-05-15 to 2027-05-24 is only an illustration. The fees and procedures are dated 2026-11-05, not guaranteed 2027 terms. Check them again when you apply.

Keep the itinerary, document checklist and appointment instructions together. The steps below are for you to take later; no application, account, appointment, payment or booking has been made for you.

## Choose where to apply before looking for a slot

Start with the main destination, not the easiest appointment. The [European Commission overview](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/schengen/visa-policy/applying-schengen-visa_en) and [Visa Code](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:02009R0810-20240611) use purpose or duration counted in days. First external-border entry is the fallback when there is no main destination. For the same tourism purpose in both countries:

| Actual itinerary | Where to apply | What not to assume |
|---|---|---|
| France has the longer stay | France | An Italian arrival does not override the main destination |
| Italy has the longer stay | Italy | Landing in Paris does not automatically make France competent |
| Equal stays and no other main destination | First external Schengen border crossed | A Schengen airport connection may be that border |

Use genuine stays, even if another country has easier slots. Each traveller needs a separate type C tourism application, normally to the same competent country for your shared trip. You do not need separate France and Italy visas. Crossing their internal border alone does not require multiple Schengen entries.

## Give yourself time to fix things

| 2027-04-15 | Track outstanding applications; respond to requests and review cancellation dates | Departure does not compel expedited approval |
| 2027-04-30 | Warning boundary, not a filing target | 15 calendar days before assumed 2027-05-15 arrival; too late for Italy's 15-working-day instruction |
| 2027-05-08–2027-05-14 | Check stickers, actual bookings, insurance and border procedures | Resolve errors before departure |
| 2027-05-15 | Depart India if documents and plans permit | Follow actual arrival and granted visa conditions |

There are several different clocks here. The normal EU decision period is 15 calendar days from an admissible application, and can extend to 45 days. Waiting for a slot, transferring the file and returning the passport take extra time. France's local 5-day minimum for some applicants is not a promise. Italy asks for at least 15 working days; its inconsistent “180 working days” wording does not extend the legal 6-calendar-month filing window. If you cannot find a suitable slot, use official support and reconsider refundable plans. Do not apply to the wrong country to save the date.

## Prepare your own files, then check them together

Enter each person's actual details in the [France-Visas wizard](https://france-visas.gouv.fr/en/web/france-visas/assistant-visa), or use the applicable [Italy Bengaluru tourist checklist](https://www.vfsglobal.com/one-pager/italy/india/bangalore/english/pdf/Checklist-TOURISM-(C)-New.pdf). The wizard changes with your answers. Its general link does not save your personalised checklist, so keep the result generated for you.

| What to prepare | France requirements to check | Italy Bengaluru requirements to check |
|---|---|---|
| Bank statements | Last 3 months | Last 3 months, originals with bank stamp and seal on every page |
| Tax and salary | Previous financial year's return plus ITR-V; latest 3 salary slips | Last 3 years' ITR acknowledgements or stated Form 16A alternative; last 3 months' salary slips and employment evidence |
| Employer and leave | Employment contract or certificate; approved leave is useful support, but is not an express item in this wizard checklist | Leave sanction on letterhead with dates, signatory details and signature, plus signatory ID proof |
| Hotels and transport | Full-trip accommodation and return reservation or itinerary, with genuine matching dates | Full-trip accommodation, return travel and internal transport bookings, including the other Schengen country |
| Residence | Use actual residence and applicable checklist | 6 months' jurisdiction evidence where addresses do not correspond; local page also requires 6 months' continuous India residence |

Thinking of using Italy's Form 16A alternative? Ask the official operator or consulate to clarify it; do not replace it with Form 16 yourself. Each person needs their own signed form, identity, employment and finances. Put matching shared bookings in both files. Explain sponsorship and unusual bank credits with documents. Temporarily inflating a balance is not a credible funding plan, and no universal balance guarantees approval.

Check passports before tying bookings to them. They must have been issued within 10 years, have 2 blank pages and stay valid for at least 3 months after actual Schengen exit. For the illustrative exit 2027-05-24, that means at least 2027-08-24. Each traveller needs at least €30,000 medical insurance covering all Schengen countries and the entire stay, including relevant emergency treatment and repatriation. Italy also specifies at least 10 days from travel date. Cover the full trip if longer, recheck accepted insurers, and make sure a shared policy genuinely covers both people.

## Book through the right route, and check the centre

For France, start at [France-Visas India](https://france-visas.gouv.fr/en/inde). Complete each application on the official portal, then use the reference numbers to follow VFS appointment instructions. For Italy, choose Tourism – first issuance on the official Bengaluru page. Use your own contact details and check that both people appear in the appointment coverage. VFS receives documents and biometrics; the consular authority decides.

**Before travelling to Italy's Bengaluru VAC, confirm its street address. The consulate is not the submission centre.** The centre address and detailed account screens have not been reliably confirmed here. Check the centre, reporting time, fees and latest checklist through the official route and your appointment confirmation. No live appointment availability is promised for either country.

EES border registration is separate from visa-application biometrics and ETIAS. These visa-required Indian passport holders should not use ETIAS as a substitute or add an extra visa application for it. Recheck border procedures before travelling. Read each issued sticker carefully: identity, validity, stay duration, entries and territory. Carry accommodation, return, insurance and funds evidence. Having a visa does not guarantee admission.

If you are refused, read the written reasons and that country's appeal instructions and deadline. Deal with the actual issue before deciding whether to apply again. More pages or a different appointment country will not fix false bookings, inconsistent finances, an unclear purpose or inadequate insurance.
