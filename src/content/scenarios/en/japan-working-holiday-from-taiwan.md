**Worried that TWD 250,000 will disappear before your first payday? A careful start looks workable in this example: budget JPY 515,000 for preparation and the first two months, without wages, and protect a separate JPY 250,000 emergency reserve. But settle your visa route before buying a non-refundable flight. June 1, 2027 is not yet a safe booking date for a new applicant.**

This plan is for a 26-year-old living in Taipei and applying under Japan's Taiwan Working Holiday scheme. It uses information checked on November 6, 2026, not confirmed travel-date prices or availability. Reading it in another language does not change Taiwan-specific eligibility.

## First, find out which visa round you can actually use

Did you already apply in October 2026? If not, that window has closed. No 2027 application timetable was found on the [official index](https://www.koryu.or.jp/tw/visa/taipei/working/) in that check. Keep your arrival flexible rather than assuming last year's spring dates will repeat.

| Your position | Published timing | What to do before booking |
|---|---|---|
| Applied in autumn 2026 | Applications October 19, 2026 to October 30, 2026; closed October 26, 2026. Results December 11, 2026; collection December 14, 2026 to June 11, 2027 | This route can fit a June arrival if approved; check your issued documents |
| Already approved in spring 2026 | Collection June 22, 2026 to December 18, 2026; published entry deadline June 22, 2027 | Collect within the window; do not confuse collection with entry |
| New applicant awaiting 2027 | No announced window established here. Spring 2026 applications were April 20, 2026 to April 30, 2026; results June 18, 2026; collection began June 22, 2026 | These are past dates, not a forecast. Late June or July is only a personal fallback, not an official opening |

See the [round dates and eligibility](https://www.koryu.or.jp/tw/visa/taipei/working/guide2026/) and the [spring approval notice](https://www.koryu.or.jp/tw/visa/taipei/working/workingholiday/2026-1/).

**You must live in Taiwan when applying and hold a valid Taiwan passport with an ID number.** The dated guide sets ages 18–30 inclusive at submission and an annual quota of 10,000. Your main purpose must be a holiday, with work incidental, for a stay of up to one year. You need initial living funds, return travel or funds for it, good health, no adverse or criminal record, and insurance. Dependants cannot accompany you unless independently visa-qualified. Since February 2026, up to two lifetime issuances are allowed; unused approvals can count. Confirm your own history before applying.

### Prepare the lasting documents now, the short-lived ones later

Draft your own reason and holiday plan, check passport validity and keep savings accessible. A monthly check of the official page, becoming weekly from March, is a suggested habit—not an announcement schedule. When the next round appears, replace this checklist with its forms and instructions. The latest version here is autumn 2026, with a September 2026 CV update.

| Application item | Dated requirement to prepare against |
|---|---|
| Application, ID and photo | Visa form; Taiwan ID copy front/back; one white-background colour photo, 4.5 × 3.5 cm, taken within six months |
| CV, reason and activity plan | Write your own content in Chinese or Japanese; explain a repeat application when applicable |
| Education and optional evidence | Highest education evidence; name-change evidence if needed; language certificates optional |
| Funds | At least TWD 80,000: original bank/post-office deposit certificate issued within one month; passbook copy not accepted. Relative's funds require original relationship evidence issued within three months |
| Passport | Original plus signed bio-page copy, relevant recent Japan entry/departure pages and applicable previous long-stay visa, landing and residence-card copies |
| Entry/exit record | Original Taiwan certificate issued within 10 days, covering the last three years and locations |
| Format | A4, dark permanent ink, handwritten signatures; generally single-sided, though the application form may be double-sided; official document order |

Use the [document checklist and forms](https://www.koryu.or.jp/tw/visa/taipei/working/guide2026/guide-detail1/). Typed CV/reason/plan is allowed, but the content must be yours. Do not order the time-limited certificates too early.

Submit personally at Taipei or Kaohsiung during the announced window. The [counter procedure](https://www.koryu.or.jp/tw/visa/taipei/working/tabid1405.html) lists 09:15–11:30 / 13:45–16:00 on open days. No online, postal or proxy application; it is not first-come-first-served, and duplicate applications invalidate the submission.

After approval, arrange insurance covering sickness, injury and death throughout your intended stay. For [collection](https://www.koryu.or.jp/tw/visa/taipei/working/guide2026/detail2/), bring the same valid passport, original receipt and formal insurance proof, original plus copy. **The visa is free, but ordinary health insurance alone does not meet this collection requirement; losing the receipt prevents collection.**

There are separate clocks: entry validity runs for one year from the round's first collection day, not your pickup date; the stay of up to one year starts at landing permission. Collection has its own six-month deadline. Read the [official FAQ](https://www.koryu.or.jp/tw/faq/visa/working/) and your issued documents. You do not need a job offer or JLPT pass to apply. The funds threshold is evidence for the application, not a Tokyo spending recommendation.

### Break the phone–bank loop without relying on instant approval

You do not universally need a Japanese bank account first. [DOCOMO's shop rules](https://www.docomo.ne.jp/english/support/procedure/identification/) allow credit-card payment and require a residence card plus foreign passport with current-address evidence; further documents may be requested. If less than three months' stay remains, the own-name credit-card rule applies. These are that provider's rules, not every carrier's.

For Mobal voice-product collection, bring the original passport and order confirmation under its [ID rules](https://helpdesk.mobal.com/support/solutions/articles/205000051089-i-d-requirements-voice-products-); delivery has different verification/address requirements. Its [SIM page](https://www.mobal.com/japan-sim-card/) lists debit/credit card or PayPal. Check device compatibility, voice/SMS, setup, plan price and cancellation before buying.

Try Japan Post Bank once your address/documents are ready. Its [opening rules](https://www.jp-bank.japanpost.jp/kaisetu/kat_gaikokujin.html) require residence-card checks; bring passport and any employment evidence too. Japanese-language help may be requested. Opening can be delayed or refused, and is not offered when remaining permission expires within three months. Keep a backup way to pay rent.

**The six-month rule is not a blanket account-opening ban.** A newcomer under six months who is not employed in Japan can be classified as a foreign-exchange non-resident, affecting transactions. Employment can change that earlier; ask the bank to update the classification. From January 4, 2027, the [published transfer rules](https://www.jp-bank.japanpost.jp/kojin/sokin/gaitame/kj_sk_gt_index.html) expand ATM handling in principle, but checks, exceptions and extra fees remain. Confirm payroll receipt, rent transfers and actual charges before depending on the account. By day 14, aim to have paperwork filed, connectivity and reliable payment—not a promised bank approval.

At JPY 1,300 × 20 hours/week × 4.33 weeks, illustrative gross pay is JPY 112,580/month ≈ TWD 23,953 at the assumed rate. Hours, hiring and take-home pay are not guaranteed. Even before deductions, that is below the monthly spending allowance.

The [Working Holiday work restrictions](https://www.koryu.or.jp/tw/faq/visa/working/) do not impose a programme-specific hours ceiling; do not copy the student 28-hour rule. **The main purpose must still be a holiday. Bars/adult-entertainment and other prohibited premises are not permitted; visa-free tourist entry is not a shortcut to starting work.**

In weeks 3–4, prepare a simple Japanese CV and compare nearby restaurant/retail roles. Ask about written wage, shifts, payroll cutoff/payday, transport reimbursement and employee health/pension enrollment.
