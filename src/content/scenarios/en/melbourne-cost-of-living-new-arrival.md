### Bond and advance rent are different

[CAV's bond guide](https://www.consumer.vic.gov.au/housing/renting/rent-bond-bills-and-condition-reports/bond/bond-amounts-and-paying-a-bond), checked November 5, normally caps a residential bond at one month's rent. Exceptions include rent **above $900/week** or a VCAT-approved higher bond. RTBA holds it separately from rent. The [advance-rent rules](https://www.consumer.vic.gov.au/housing/renting/rent-bond-bills-and-condition-reports/rent/rent-payments-and-rent-in-advance) usually cap prepaid rent at one month, or **14 days if rent is paid weekly**, with the above-$900 exception. Do not offer extra advance rent to strengthen an application. Ask for the exact calendar-month charge and payment frequency: the planning conversion of 52/12 is not a statutory quote.

Then add the office walk and disruption margin. [Official Newmarket departures](https://www.ptv.vic.gov.au/stop/1140) showed 15-minute intervals, while the other source said 30. Missing a train can break a strict 35-minute limit. Walk the route yourself; the walking estimates come from maps, not a journey-engine measurement.

[Transport Victoria's Southern Cross guidance](https://transport.vic.gov.au/plan-a-journey/network-maps/southern-cross-station-map-and-travel-information) says the Cranbourne/Pakenham/Sunbury through line does not run via Southern Cross. Do not rely on an old direct-Southern-Cross route. Check the exact March 2027 timetable before choosing a home.

## Transport: today's discount is not March's price

The [fare tables](https://transport.vic.gov.au/tickets-and-payments/fares/metropolitan-train-tram-and-bus-fares) showed temporary adult Zone 1+2 fares of **$5.70/day**, **$28.50/7-day pass** and **$3.42/day for 28–365-day passes** when checked November 5. Those numeric tables have not been reopened since. The [government announcement](https://www.vic.gov.au/half-price-travel) confirms the usual $11.40 daily cap was reduced to $5.70. [Visitor guidance](https://transport.vic.gov.au/plan-a-journey/visitors-to-victoria) says half-price travel lasts until **January 1, 2027**, with a standard-fares-effective-January-1 heading, but no usable future numeric table was available. **Check the March fare, rather than budgeting from today's discount.**

For planning, double the temporary pass rate to **$6.84/day**, then add a **5% contingency** of your own—not an announced increase. Employee allowance = min(20 × $11.40, 30 × $6.84) × 1.05 = **$215.46**. Partner allowance = 8 × $11.40 × 1.05 = **$95.76**. A 30-day pass means consecutive days, not a calendar month or 30 workdays. Do not treat the conditional [Labor $30/week campaign promise](https://www.viclabor.org.au/our-priorities/capping-fares) as a tariff already in force. Before March, compare actual fares and payment products. The [Free Tram Zone](https://transport.vic.gov.au/tickets-and-payments/myki/cheaper-travel-with-myki) does not make suburban trains free.

## Visa, Medicare and the decision that comes before a lease

**Check both visa grant letters before signing a Melbourne lease.** “Skilled visa” alone does not tell you where you may live or which health cover you need. [Home Affairs regional guidance](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/working-in-australia/regional-migration) calls Melbourne a major city, not a regional-incentive location. The [condition 8579 extract](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/already-have-a-visa/check-visa-details-and-conditions/see-your-visa-conditions?vcid=217) requires designated-regional residence, work and study; the direct page retrieval only showed navigation. **A 491/494 regional visa can conflict with this CBD plan.** Have your own conditions checked before committing rather than treating this guide as a ruling on your visa.

- **If you qualify for Medicare:** [Services Australia](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/enrolling-medicare-if-youre-australian-permanent-resident?context=60092) explains enrolment for permanent residents living in Australia. Only use the $0 private-premium allowance after confirming your entitlement and conditions. Keep the $100 medical reserve.
- **If you need temporary-resident private cover:** [Home Affairs' insurance guide](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/help-support/meeting-our-requirements/health/adequate-health-insurance) explains condition 8501 and possible gaps or out-of-pocket costs. The **$300/month couple allowance**, range $200–400, is not a quote or compliance guarantee. Ask insurers about ages, nationality and reciprocal entitlement, both grants, exclusions, waits, excess, ambulance and exact cover dates.
- **A temporary visa does not always rule out Medicare:** [Services Australia's Ministerial Order guide](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/enrolling-medicare-if-youre-temporary-resident-covered-ministerial-order?context=60092) lists 491 and 494. Health entitlement does not remove their separate regional-location restrictions.
- **Insurance does not automatically remove the ordinary levy:** [ATO's non-entitlement rules](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/medicare-and-private-health-insurance/medicare-levy/medicare-levy-exemption/not-entitled-to-medicare-benefits) require a Medicare Entitlement Statement and relevant dependant conditions. An MES alone is insufficient if your spouse remains entitled. Keep the full levy in both budgets until your actual position is clear.

## What can shrink the surplus?

| Rent rises $100/week | $5,233.55 | $939.78 |
| $95,000 includes super | $4,800.22 | $796.33 |

Read those as separate checks, not a combined worst case. If **both** the salary includes super and you need private cover, only **$496.33/month** remains before more rent or omitted spending. Confirm the contract, start date and first payday before choosing the upper rent limit. In January–February 2027, recheck listings, lease charges, date-specific temporary stays, insurance and fares. Then test the weekday home-to-office trip and the home's heating, metering and condition.

## Questions you may still have

These answers help you decide what to check before booking the move.

**Is $95,000 enough for two people in Melbourne?** With super paid on top, no car and $550/week rent, this budget covers recurring costs with $1,373.11/month left. You still need rental acceptance and permission to live there. If salary includes super and private cover is required, the spare cash falls to $496.33.

**Is $14,785.55 the whole savings target?** No. That covers arrival through the first salary. Keep the separate $13,200.66 essential reserve, bringing the starting amount to $27,986.21 before excluded fees or new quotes.

**Does a 30-minute journey mean an under-35-minute commute?** Not here. The table stops at the CBD station exit and assumes timed departures. Add the actual office walk, waiting and disruption margin. Carnegie leaves particularly little room.
