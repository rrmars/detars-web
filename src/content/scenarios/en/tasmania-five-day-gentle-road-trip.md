**Worried that a short holiday will disappear behind the wheel? Stay three nights in Hobart, one in Swansea and one in Launceston. Choose the Tasman Peninsula rather than Bruny, enjoy MONA and Wineglass Bay, and leave Cradle Mountain out to avoid long drives. Working spend is AUD 2,466.35 for two, with AUD 133.65 spare inside AUD 2,600, flights excluded. That only works if the estimated rooms and rental costs hold: no complete dated accommodation quote was confirmed.**

This is a Melbourne → Hobart / Launceston → Melbourne plan for two adults sharing one room and a small car, from Friday, February 19, 2027 to Wednesday, February 24, 2027. Despite the five-day shorthand, it is five nights across six calendar dates, with four full days between arrival and departure. Flight times are not assumed. Chinese-speaking travellers can use the same plan; language does not establish driving, entry or rental eligibility, which this plan does not verify for your personal documents.

Prices and sources were checked on November 6, 2026, not refreshed for this draft. All money is AUD, including Australian dollar-only hotel and menu figures. Admission prices may change before February 2027. Map readings are dated driving observations, not forecasts; the longer allowances below are planning estimates before substantial stops. Nothing has been booked, signed up for or discussed with suppliers.

## Keep the route gentle—except for the uphill walk

The Tasman Peninsula gives you Port Arthur and an easy cliff circuit without Bruny's island-ferry dependency. Bruny is a reasonable food-led alternative, but this route already has east-coast seafood. Do not add Cradle Mountain: the [detour via its visitor centre](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Coles+Bay/Cradle+Mountain+Visitor+Centre/Launceston/) measured 5h36 / 432 km versus [Coles Bay directly to Launceston](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Coles+Bay/Launceston/) at 2h06 / 175 km. That adds 3h30 and 257 km before a mountain walk. These are route-planning choices, not supplier promises.

## Your days, with permission to skip the extras

### Friday, February 19, 2027: arrive in Hobart

Collect the car, drop bags and walk the waterfront and Battery Point if daylight and energy allow. A late flight simply means skipping the walk. Have fish and chips or a casual dinner, and buy picnic basics and breakfast supplies. Estimated food envelope: $70 for two. Sleep in Hobart, night 1 of 3.

### Saturday, February 20, 2027: market, then MONA

Start Salamanca Market around 8:30am and browse for 1½–2 hours. Published Saturday hours are 8:30am–3pm; entry is free, food extra. The [market FAQ](https://www.salamancamarket.com.au/About-us/Frequently-Asked-Questions) hours were recovered through search rather than direct retrieval and corroborated by the [City of Hobart listing](https://www.hobartcity.com.au/Things-To-Do/Salamanca-Market).

Give MONA roughly 12:30–4:30pm. Sampled opening was Thursday–Monday, 10am–5pm; it is cashless, so bring a payment card. [Official admission](https://mona.net.au/visit) was $45 per non-Tasmanian adult: $90 for two + one $3.50 advance-booking transaction = $93.50. Some artworks cost extra and are excluded. Drive as budgeted, allowing for limited parking. Alternatively, Metro buses 510, 520, 522 and X20 were listed as free until June 30, 2027; leave the car at your accommodation, check the Saturday timetable and allow an estimated 45–60 minutes each way. No Saturday timetable was verified.

No ferry is budgeted. The [official ferry page](https://mona.net.au/visit/ferry) advertised “from $32” and approximately 30 minutes, but did not establish the return/one-way price basis for your sailing. Get the complete return total before changing transport; ferry fare is not museum admission. Estimated food envelope: $105 for two, for market food and a relaxed dinner, not tasting menus or several cocktails. Sleep in the same Hobart room, night 2.

### Sunday, February 21, 2027: Port Arthur and coastal cliffs

Leave around 8am and spend about 9:45am–2pm at Port Arthur, including lunch. [General admission](https://portarthur.org.au/whats-on/generaladmission/) was $55 each / $110 for two, including a 20-minute harbour cruise and short guide talks. Do not add a separate cruise fare; select the cruise time after ticket purchase. Sampled opening was daily 9am–5pm except Christmas Day.

On the return, [Tasmans Arch and Devils Kitchen](https://parks.tas.gov.au/Pages/Tasmans-Arch-and-Devils-Kitchen-Circuit.aspx) is a sealed 600m, 20-minute, Grade 1 circuit. Allow 40–50 minutes with photos, stay behind cliff barriers and use the $98.35 vehicle parks pass described below; no separate lookout ticket. Aim back around 5–6pm. Skip Cape Hauy: it would turn this into a long walking day. Estimated food envelope: $85 for two, with a picnic and an easy Hobart dinner. Sleep in the same room, night 3.

### Monday, February 22, 2027: Hobart to Swansea

Leave after breakfast, not at dawn. Take a lazy lunch, check in and stroll the beach or waterfront for 30–60 minutes, turning back whenever you like. Buy tomorrow's water and walking snacks tonight. Estimated food envelope: $80 for two, for bakery lunch and a casual local dinner. Swansea is the budget stop; Coles Bay would save the next approach drive but needs a separate room quote. Sleep in Swansea for one night, with Waterloo estimated at $220.

### Tuesday, February 23, 2027: Wineglass Bay, then Launceston

Leave Swansea around 7:30am and begin walking around 9am after parking and preparation. [Wineglass Bay Lookout](https://parks.tas.gov.au/explore-our-parks/freycinet-national-park/wineglass-bay-lookout) is 2.6 km return, 1–1½ hours, Grade 3. Allow two hours at your pace. **This is sustained uphill walking with steps, not a flat stroll.** Wear proper shoes, carry water and sun protection, and stop at the lookout: the beach descent and return climb are excluded. Your parks pass covers ordinary walk entry, with no extra lookout fee.

If energy, weather and timing permit, add [Cape Tourville](https://parks.tas.gov.au/explore-our-parks/freycinet-national-park/cape-tourville): 600m, 20 minutes, Grade 1, with an estimated 30–40 minutes of local driving and parking. Same parks pass; skip it if late.

Aim for noon at Freycinet Marine Farm on the way out. Its [menu](https://www.freycinetmarinefarm.com/menu) listed six natural oysters $16, mussels España $26 and fish and chips $26. Sharing oysters and mussels is $42 before drinks, not a bundled deal. The [homepage](https://www.freycinetmarinefarm.com/) listed kitchen 9am–3pm and fresh options until 4pm as winter hours; February service is unconfirmed. Estimated whole-day food envelope: $110 for two.

Leave the Coles Bay area by about 1:30pm; aim for Launceston around 4:30–5pm. Planned road time is about 4 hours, with local park detours extra. No Cradle or Bay of Fires detour. If dangerously hot, stormy or closed, use Cape Tourville only if safe; its coastal views are not the same Wineglass lookout experience. Check PWS alerts. Sleep in Launceston for one night, with Balmoral estimated at $160.

### Wednesday, February 24, 2027: an easy finish if the flight allows

For an afternoon flight, spend 45–60 minutes walking the gardens and First Basin at [Cataract Gorge](https://www.discovertasmania.com.au/things-to-do/nature-and-wildlife/cataractgorgereserve/). The walk-only entry budget is $0; parking may cost extra, and no chairlift or cruise is included. This is not an official tariff guarantee or a promise that every path is open: follow closure signs. For a morning flight, skip it.

Allow another 30 minutes beyond driving for fuel and car return, then the airline's check-in margin. A conservative planning target is the terminal about 90 minutes before domestic departure; use any longer airline requirement. Return full. Estimated food envelope: $50 for two. Balmoral advertises continental breakfast, but confirm your actual rate before relying on it.

## Book the right car period and one parks pass

The [Tasmanian Vacations 2026/27 brochure](https://www.tasvacations.com/assets/pdfs/Tasmanian_Vacations_Brochure2627.pdf#page=11), PDF page 11 / printed page 20, gives the Drive Swift-category hybrid hatch benchmark: $105/day in the 4–6-day band for January 11, 2027 through March 24, 2027. Five 24-hour periods = $525; add the package's $100 Launceston commencing/returning charge and tyre/windscreen cover from $9.90/day × 5 = $49.50, making $674.50. It is published seasonal pricing, not live availability or a guarantee of that exact car.

The $100 is package-specific, not a universal one-way fee. [Drive's FAQ](https://drivecarhire.com.au/about/faq/) permits Hobart–Launceston one-way rentals but says fees and minimum periods may apply. Declare the different airport and compare the full total; do not add another generic $100 fee.

The package includes airport fees, GST/admin/registration, two nominated drivers, unlimited standard-car kilometres and zero standard-car damage excess. **Zero excess is not cover for everything:** reversing, overhead, undercarriage damage, negligence and contract breaches can be excluded; towing/retrieval may be yours. Confirm the agreement and bond. The credit-card hold needs available credit but is not spending. Fuel is full-to-full and excluded from the hire price.

Five billed days work only if return on February 24 is no later than pickup time on February 19. A sixth period plus cover adds $114.90, leaving just $18.75 of the $133.65 buffer. This budget assumes drivers over 25 with at least two years' licensed experience; the brochure adds $11/day for ages 21–25. Flight times, age, card fees and actual cover can change the total. Confirm that your licence and documents qualify; the language of this guide does not decide that.

The [PWS Holiday Vehicle Pass](https://parks.tas.gov.au/explore-our-parks/know-before-you-go/park-passes-and-entry-fees) was $98.35 for up to eight people in one vehicle, valid up to two months. It covers the Tasman and Freycinet park days, not MONA or Port Arthur. Two $49.15 daily vehicle passes, each up to 24 hours and excluding Cradle, total $98.30; the holiday option is just $0.05 more and avoids juggling windows. Buy one vehicle pass, not two per-person passes. Cradle's shuttle is excluded, but you are not going there. Fees are reviewed annually; recheck before buying.

## Leave room for weather before committing

Check [PWS alerts](https://parks.tas.gov.au/explore-our-parks/know-before-you-go/alerts), fire conditions and forecasts before each walk. Keep a jacket in the car even in February. If Tuesday looks poor but Monday good, move Freycinet to Monday: Hobart → roadhead via Swansea is roughly 2h42 by adding the sampled legs, before breaks. Walk, then return about an hour to Swansea; Tuesday can go straight inland to Launceston. That makes Monday a longer driving day. Combined/reverse times are estimates: re-map before changing the plan, and never substitute a walk into unsafe conditions.

Check flight times first, then rooms and the complete rental total, then MONA and Port Arthur. You do not need another distant attraction to make the holiday worthwhile.
