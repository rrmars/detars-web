You can plan a move without assuming your spouse can nurse immediately—but the monthly margin is thin. **Assess the Digital Gold Card first, then arrange the family's HK-specific residence permissions. Assess her own professional-residence route in parallel; keep genuine Taiwan-sponsored employment as the fallback.** Do not start with investment or study just to obtain residence.

This example is an HK UX designer earning **HKD 48,000/month**, an HK registered-nurse spouse and a daughter aged two on **7 November 2026**, planning a mid-2027 move. Savings are **HKD 1,200,000**. At the assumed **HKD 1 = TWD 4.00**, gross income is **TWD 192,000**, monthly allocations **TWD 175,600** and headroom only **TWD 16,400 / HKD 4,100**. **Nursing earnings and childcare subsidies are zero in the model.** Rules and prices below are dated to 7 November 2026, not refreshed offers or approvals for 2027.

**Residence, permission to work and National Health Insurance (NHI) are separate gates.** Resolve them before resigning, paying a rental deposit or relying on a preschool place. This is general planning information, not immigration or tax advice.

## Choose the Hong Kong route before collecting documents

Check each person's permanent status, passports, nationalities and birthplace individually, plus marriage and parentage. An HK identity card alone is not the complete test. BNO holders use the HK route; another foreign passport can change the legal lane. Mainland-born applicants may need specified mainland-status evidence. Do not renounce a nationality merely to fit this plan. [S1](https://www.immigration.gov.tw/5382/5385/7244/7250/7296/%E5%B1%85%E7%95%99/148063/) [S5](https://goldcard.nat.gov.tw/en/tags/hong-kong-macau-residents/)

### Keep the route sequence clear

| Priority / route | What must qualify | Family and work consequences | Fees and timing—not a move-date promise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First: Digital Gold Card · HF198 principal | Criterion 2: qualifying current/former overseas or Taiwan digital employment at TWD 160,000/month; actual duties, industry, CV and accepted salary proof matter. | Principal has open professional-work permission, not tied to a Taiwan employer. UX title and pay alone are not approval. | 1–3 years; HK principal TWD 3,100 including the TWD 500 work-permit component; renewal TWD 800. Published review 30 working days with exclusions; HK final NIA review after WDA qualification can take at least another 1–2 months. |
| Second: HK-family residence · HF199 | Wife and minor child each need the appropriate relationship, identity and principal-permission evidence. | Not automatically issued with his card. Exact HK-dependent spouse open-work-permit access is unconfirmed. | Family may enter with appropriate permission then apply at NIA, or apply overseas after the principal has entered and collected the Gold Card. |
| Parallel: her HK professional qualification · HF160 / family HF161 | NIA lists approved professional qualifications and competent-authority review, including MOHW. HK RN status makes this worth assessing; personal nursing-category eligibility and detailed conditions are unconfirmed. | Residence does not confer Taiwan nursing licensure or establish unrestricted remote-work rights for her dependent husband. | Ordinary HK documents/fees apply; allow professional review beyond the general five-working-day NIA clock. |
| Fallback: Taiwan-sponsored professional employment · HF171 / HF172; talent categories may differ | Real qualifying Taiwan employer/job, employee eligibility and approved work permission. Existing HK remote contract alone is not that route. | Eligible spouse/child may accompany; sponsored permission is not unrestricted outside-work permission. Rebuild the salary/payroll budget. | Complete online WDA cases: 7 working days; general HK NIA checklist: 5 working days, excluding corrections/consultation and other handling. |

Read the [HK Gold Card guide](https://www.immigration.gov.tw/5385/7244/7250/7317/%E5%B1%85%E7%95%99/406403/), [Digital criteria](https://goldcard.nat.gov.tw/en/qualification/field-of-digital/), [HK-family procedure](https://goldcard.nat.gov.tw/en/faq/how-do-my-spouse-and-children-from-hong-kong-or-macao-apply-for-residency-in-taiwan/), [professional/family evidence table](https://www.immigration.gov.tw/media/94735/港澳居民申請居留相關證明文件.pdf) and [open professional-work guidance](https://goldcard.nat.gov.tw/en/tags/work/). For the employment fallback, check [employee/job conditions](https://ezworktaiwan.wda.gov.tw/cp.aspx?n=98C816CDB81AA655) and [processing scope](https://ezworktaiwan.wda.gov.tw/cp.aspx?n=06CB6C2CFA89541D). Published clocks exclude preparation and other stages; do not simply add them into a guaranteed departure date.

**Counterintuitive finding: the eight-year experience criterion and the salary criterion are alternatives, not cumulative requirements.** HKD 48,000 × 12 = HKD 576,000/year. TWD 160,000 ÷ HKD 48,000 = **3.333333… TWD/HKD** is the conversion needed to clear the monthly threshold. At planning FX only, pay is TWD 192,000/month or TWD 2,304,000/year versus the TWD 1,920,000 annual threshold. This clears arithmetic, not qualification.

The [salary FAQ](https://goldcard.nat.gov.tw/en/faq/how-do-i-meet-the-salary-requirements-of-the-gold-card-application/) calls for an annual qualifying tax statement from within three years, divided by 12, and **Bank of Taiwan historical buying-cash rates**, not this guide's round planning FX. Recent pay changes and unavailable tax statements have specified alternative evidence. Reconcile gross pay, employment certificates and CV; document digital-product duties and employer business. Savings are not salary evidence. Choose the genuinely relevant field, not an indiscriminate high-salary application; [qualification selection](https://goldcard.nat.gov.tw/en/qualification/) and [Economy criteria](https://goldcard.nat.gov.tw/en/qualification/field-of-economy/) do not remove field assessment.

### Why investment and study are not the family fallback

| Other route | Published basis | Why not the starting plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Investment · HF162 / HF163 | At least TWD 6,000,000 approved, implemented investment—not a bank balance or home purchase. | Savings at planning FX are TWD 4,800,000: TWD 1,200,000 / HKD 300,000 short before any family reserve. Real operation, approval and renewal obligations remain. |
| Innovative business · HF196 / HF197 | Approved innovation pathway; examples include TWD 2m qualifying venture/crowdfunding or TWD 1m in a qualifying existing innovative business with the required role. | These are distinct alternatives, not universal minimums. A paper consultancy is not enough. Initial 2 years and operational renewal review; unrelated remote work is not automatically authorized. |
| Study · HF168 | Actual approved study, such as a university programme. | No general accompanying-family entitlement from current-student residence. Student work permission normally required; term-time cap 20 hours/week, with holiday exception. Adult tuition is extra. |

## HK payroll does not make Taiwan work tax-free
