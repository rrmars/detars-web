**Worried about spending your Seoul break rushing between sights? For 2 friends sharing a twin room for 3 nights, plan ₩1,405,500 each, including contingency, within a US$1,200 cap excluding flights. Stay near Hongik University Station. Put the palace and Gwangjang Market on Friday, hike on Saturday only if conditions are safe, and keep Sunday local. Enjoy any blossoms you find without making the trip depend on them.**

**Check entry rules before booking around an exemption. The published temporary K-ETA exemption ends on 2026-12-31 (KST); it does not cover 2027-04-15–2027-04-18 as things stand. Check the rules for your exact passport. Unless a new exemption applies, get K-ETA approval before boarding.** An extension could still be announced, so return to the [official exemption notice](https://www.k-eta.go.kr/portal/board/viewboarddetail.do?bbsSn=299707&esntlPnotiMtYn=Y) before you travel.

## Start with your flights and how you want to spend the days

This plan is for 2 friends in their late twenties, travelling from Hong Kong on ordinary Hong Kong SAR passports. It assumes you land at Incheon (ICN) around 08:00–10:00 on 2027-04-15 and fly home around 19:00–21:00 on 2027-04-18. Replace those windows with your actual flights before fixing the arrival and departure days. All times are Korea Standard Time, 1 hour ahead of Hong Kong. Give yourselves 90–120 minutes after landing for immigration and bags before starting the transfer.

The prices and entry information here reflect 2026-11-05, not your departure day. Use them to make a shortlist and a budget, then check the details before paying. Nothing is reserved for you; room availability, entry rules and opening arrangements still need checking.

**The hotel prices seen on 2026-11-05 are examples, not quotes or promised rooms for 2027-04-15–2027-04-18. Treat the spending allowances, journey times and activity slots below as estimates.** US$1 = ₩1,400 is a fixed budgeting assumption, not a live exchange rate. Keep the original currencies when comparing prices so an exchange-rate change does not slip past you.

## Stay where getting home is easy

Look for a Hongdae hotel about 5–10 minutes from a Hongik University Station exit, away from the loudest club streets. The AREX all-stop train gets you there without a change, and Line 2 is useful for sightseeing. Check the station map as well as the hotel's location: being close to an exit does not mean being close to the airport-train platform.

## Make the trip work even if the blossoms are gone

The [KTO blossom forecast](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=1570512) put Seoul's first flowers around 2026-04-03 and peak around 2026-04-10. Those were predictions for that season, not confirmed flowering dates or a forecast for 2027. For 2027-04-15–2027-04-18, it is sensible to expect that the main city peak may be over. A late season or lingering flowers could still help. Check before making a detour, and do not count on festival stalls being open.

Check [Gyeongbokgung's opening hours](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R702000000.do) before setting off: the spring schedule is 09:00–18:00, with last entry at 17:00 and a regular Tuesday closure. [Korea Travel List](https://koreatravellist.com/en/places/gyeongbokgung-palace) lists the ₩3,000 adult fee; confirm the ordinary admission price with the palace before visiting. Leave any guard ceremony as a bonus until you have checked its schedule.

[Inwangsan](https://english.visitseoul.net/nature/Inwangsan-Mountain_/3844) is free to enter and can be approached from Dongnimmun. Set aside 2.5–3 hours with rests, roughly 4–5 km including the approach. These are loose walking allowances, not a measured route. Expect steep steps and exposed granite, not a flat park stroll. Wear grippy shoes, bring water and layers, and turn back early if either of you feels uneasy.

Going to [Gwangjang Market](https://english.visitseoul.net/PalaceArea/Gwangjang-Market/ENP000286) on Friday avoids confusion over general market sections that close on Sundays, while the food alley opens daily. Share bindaetteok, gimbap or cooked noodles so you can try more without over-ordering. [Mangwon Market](https://english.visitseoul.net/MapoArea/Mangwon-Market/ENP037950) pairs well with a riverside walk. Individual stalls can close or change prices, so look for displayed prices and ask before ordering.

If recent photos show good flowers, swap the whole Thursday market-and-river afternoon for lunch near the hotel and Yeouido's Yunjung-ro walk. Do not squeeze it in as an extra cross-city stop. Take Line 2 from Hongik to Dangsan, then Line 9 all-stop to National Assembly and walk; allow 35–45 minutes each way. Look for blossom updates in late 2027-03, then check conditions again on 2027-04-13–2027-04-14. If the petals are gone, keep the shorter Mangwon plan.

## Choose the airport train that actually serves your hotel

**For Hongdae, take AREX all-stop rather than Express.** The [all-stop fare table](https://www.airportrailroad.com/train/normal/fare) lists adult transport-card fares to Hongik of ₩4,650 from T1 and ₩5,250 from T2. A single-use ticket adds ₩100 and a refundable ₩500 deposit. Allow 50–55 minutes on the train from T1 or 60–65 from T2, and 75–95 minutes from the arrivals hall to your hotel after formalities. Those are journey estimates, not your travel-day timetable. Landing at Gimpo (GMP) instead? The listed fare is ₩1,650; allow roughly 15–20 minutes on the train plus station walking.

[AREX Express](https://www.airportrailroad.com/train/express/introduce) goes to Seoul Station, not Hongik. Its displayed fare was ₩13,000, with reference normal fares of ₩18,500 from T1 / ₩19,100 from T2, and published train times of 43 / 51 minutes. Coming back to Hongik adds a separate ₩1,550 fare and roughly 25–40 minutes. Prefer the bus with luggage? [Airport Limousine 6002](https://www.airportlimousine.co.kr/sub/sub01.php?cat_no=2) lists ₩17,000 for adults. Allow about 60–90 minutes on the road from T1 / 80–110 from T2, plus waiting and possible traffic delays. Both bus journeys add about ₩23,500–₩24,700 each compared with the listed all-stop fares. Check the stop, direction and current prices before choosing.

Leave some breathing room between neighbourhoods. Allow 20–30 minutes from the hotel to Mangwon Market, 20–25 minutes walking to the river and 30–40 minutes back to the hotel. On Friday, allow 40–50 minutes to reach the palace via Euljiro 3-ga and Gyeongbokgung. Walk 20–25 minutes to Insadong, then 25–30 minutes to Gwangjang. Reaching Myeongdong via Jongno 5-ga, Jongno 3-ga and Euljiro 3-ga takes roughly 25–35 minutes. Allow 35–45 minutes back via Euljiro 1-ga. Use these door-to-door estimates to avoid crowding the day, then check live directions.

On Saturday, Hongik–Euljiro 3-ga–Dongnimmun takes about 35–45 minutes to the exit. The 20–25-minute uphill approach is already included in the outing's walking allowance. For a Sunday 20:00 flight, finish shopping by 14:30 and leave around 15:00–15:30. For an 18:00 flight, move that plan 2 hours earlier. Your real flight, terminal and airline instructions come first. Use Naver Map or KakaoMap for routes and exits, and do not rely on overnight subway service after a night out.

## Sort entry and everyday essentials before you go

For ordinary Hong Kong SAR passports, the [official country and stay table](https://www.k-eta.go.kr/portal/guide/viewetaalification.do) allows up to 90 days visa-free. That does not automatically mean K-ETA-free. If either of you uses a Document of Identity, mainland PRC passport or BN(O) passport, check its own rules instead. The [age exemption](https://www.k-eta.go.kr/portal/board/viewboarddetail.do?bbsSn=7011&esntlPnotiMtYn=Y) is for travellers aged 17 or younger / 65 or older, so it does not help this pair.

The [K-ETA application guide](https://www.k-eta.go.kr/portal/guide/viewetaapplication.do) lists a non-refundable ₩10,000 fee plus online payment charges. Approval lasts up to 3 years or passport expiry, whichever comes first. Assessment is usually within 72 hours but can take longer, with no expedited service. Check again in 2027-01 and 2027-03. If you need it, applying 2–4 weeks ahead gives you a buffer; that is a suggestion, not a processing guarantee. Use the official website, update changed travel details and remember that approval does not guarantee admission.

A valid K-ETA exempts you from the arrival declaration under the [Immigration guidance](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration/3509/subview.do). If a new exemption lets you travel without K-ETA, fill in the free [official e-Arrival Card](https://www.e-arrivalcard.go.kr/portal/main/index.do?locale=EN) within 72 hours before arrival, unless another declaration exemption applies. Have your passport, flight, hotel address and contact details ready. Being exempt from K-ETA is not the same as holding valid K-ETA approval, so do not skip the declaration on that basis alone.

Get a T-money card each. The [KTO transport-card guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoBscView.do?vcontsId=140663&menuSn=479) lists ₩3,000–₩5,000 for the physical card; travel credit is extra. Start with ₩20,000–₩30,000 and top up as needed. Bring KRW cash rather than counting on a foreign card to reload it; that does not mean every recharge method is cash-only. The [Seoul transport guide](https://english.visitseoul.net/seoul-transportation) lists a subway base fare of ₩1,550 for 10 km and blue/green bus fares of ₩1,500, with distance supplements and possible changes. Tap in and out. Pay-as-you-go keeps this light-travel plan simple; do not buy a pass on assumed savings.

Bring 2 payment methods and choose KRW instead of dynamic HKD/USD conversion. A starting cash float of ₩100,000–₩150,000 each pays for part of this budget; it is not extra spending. Compare roaming with a tourist SIM or eSIM. Check your exact Hong Kong-market handset's eSIM support, unlock status, activation rules, data and hotspot limits, and whether you need a local number. The ₩27,500 below is an allowance, not a phone-plan offer. Save documents offline and pack a suitable plug adaptor. On a night out, stay together, watch your drinks, check ID and age rules, and walk home or use a licensed taxi. Police: 112; fire or ambulance: 119.

You would need to trim at least US$35.25 or choose a cheaper room. Check a real quote before making that trade-off; the search-listing price is not a booking offer.

## Before paying, check the details that could change the plan

Before committing money, check 2027 entry rules and actual tax-inclusive twin-room rates. In late 2027-03 and before 2027-04-14, check blossoms, palace hours and fees, trail access, airport fares and your terminal. The day before the hike, look at rain, visibility and fine dust; use the café fallback if conditions are poor. Submit an arrival declaration in its 72-hour window if needed, but do not leave a K-ETA application that late. Confirm luggage storage and checkout times with the hotel.
