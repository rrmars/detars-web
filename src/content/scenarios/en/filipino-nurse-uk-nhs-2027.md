You can make a serious plan for NHS England without spending all your savings before an offer arrives. **Prioritise a funded trust route: visa, flight and first OSCE paid upfront, plus two months’ accommodation.** For Joy, a Filipino registered nurse in Cebu with four years’ ward experience, PHP 350,000 saved and a September 2027 target, that support is what makes the move affordable. Keep working in Cebu until the offer, sponsorship, visa and Philippine exit clearance are secure. The savings do not safely cover a fully self-funded move or bringing the whole family immediately.

If September means **already working as a registered Band 5 nurse**, aim to arrive in July–August for OSCE preparation and registration. September arrival normally means a supervised pre-registration period first, not automatic Band 5 pay on landing. Neither timeline is guaranteed.

## Start with English and credentials, not a relocation payment

**Budget the new fees:** £167 evaluation +£83 CBT +£794 OSCE +£182 registration = **£1,226 / PHP 91,950**. Fees increased on October 1, 2026; old £140/£153 labels still occur on some NMC pages. Use the dedicated dated costs table, not stale guide links. English, PRC, police checks and travel are extra. See [NMC joining costs](https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/register-nurse-midwife/trained-outside-uk/how-to-guide/check-ready/costs/).

1. **Prepare and apply for qualification evaluation.** Valid passport/name-change evidence, nursing diploma/certificate, Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) registration and Professional Identification Card (PIC), university/transcript details and certified translations where necessary. The UK Nursing and Midwifery Council (NMC) verifies with PRC (and education institution where needed); four years’ ward experience is not a substitute. Pay non-refundable £167. PRC’s UK-NMC verification service uses the NMC 16-digit application reference and a LERIS appointment, then PRC completes NMC’s verification link; published charge PHP 75 / £1 per copy, included within the document allowance. NMC aims at 14 days only after all required responses, not 14 days from first opening an account. See [NMC timing](https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/register-nurse-midwife/trained-outside-uk/how-to-guide/check-ready/how-long-is-it-going-to-take/), [qualification verification](https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/register-nurse-midwife/trained-outside-uk/how-to-guide/eligibility-and-qualification-application/qualification-and-registration/), [PRC Citizen’s Charter](https://www.prc.gov.ph/sites/default/files/PRC_CC_03312025.pdf).
2. **Supply English evidence — usually the practical first priority.** Choose IELTS Academic in-centre (not General Training, IELTS Online/Indicator or One Skill Retake), or OET Nursing. NMC accepts OET Paper/Computer/@Home. For listening, reading and speaking (L/R/S), IELTS requires 7.0 each; writing (W) requires 6.5. OET requires B/350 for L/R/S and C+/300 for W. These are individual-domain requirements, not a single overall average. Standard Academic in-centre British Council PH price is PHP 14,470 / £192.93; UKVI version PHP 16,230 / £216.40, not automatically necessary. OET price not verified, so excluded from this IELTS-based budget. See [NMC accepted tests](https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/english-language-requirements/accepted-english-language-tests/), [IELTS Academic rules](https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/english-language-requirements/accepted-english-language-tests/ielts-academic/), [OET rules](https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/english-language-requirements/accepted-english-language-tests/oet/), [British Council Philippines test prices](https://www.britishcouncil.ph/exam/ielts/book-test).
3. **Combine only eligible full tests if needed.** Two IELTS or two OET tests, not mixed types; within 12 months of each other, and both within 2 years when NMC assesses the complete application. All 4 skills must be tested in each sitting. IELTS floors in *both* sittings: L/R/S 6.5, W 6.0; across them attain 7/7/7/6.5. OET floors in both: L/R/S C+/300, W C/250; across them attain B/B/B/C+. Starting the application does not freeze test validity. See [NMC accepted tests](https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/english-language-requirements/accepted-english-language-tests/), [IELTS Academic rules](https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/english-language-requirements/accepted-english-language-tests/ielts-academic/), [OET rules](https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/english-language-requirements/accepted-english-language-tests/oet/).
4. **Take the correct-field CBT.** After NMC authorisation, use Pearson’s designated route, normally in the Philippines. Part A numeracy: 15 questions / 30 minutes, Part B clinical: 100 questions / 2.5 hours; both first sitting £83. A-only retest £50 / PHP 3,750; B-only £70 / PHP 5,250; full £83 / PHP 6,225. Up to 3 attempts within 1 year, at least 10 days apart; third failure closes application, with 6 months before a new CBT attempt after reapplication. See [CBT rules](https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/toc/toc-nursing-and-midwifery/cbt/).
5. **Get the sponsored offer and prepare the registration submission.** NMC itself allows CBT/OSCE in either order; CBT-before-UK is the practical NHS route and English/CBT must be ready for this pre-registration sponsorship route. Complete health and character declarations, nominate appropriate practitioner/regulator referees, supply national police certificates for qualifying residence, confirm indemnity arrangements and pay £182. To use the first OSCE appointment for original-document/identity checks, **submit the registration application and pay before OSCE**; don't wait for the pass to start this paperwork. Keep original passport, diploma, PRC certificate, national police clearances, name-change evidence and translations ready. A missed ID check can mean another UK appointment at Joy’s expense. See [original-document check](https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/id-check-process-and-requirements/test-of-competence-overseas-route/), [NHS overseas-nurse guidance](https://www.nhsemployers.org/articles/recruitment-overseas-nurses-and-midwives).
6. **OSCE in the UK, then final decision and NMC registration/PIN.** Ten practical stations covering assessment, planning, implementation and evaluation (APIE), skills, values and evidence-based practice. The provider list inspected has Northampton, Ulster and Leeds. Northumbria closed on February 19, 2026 and Oxford Brookes on July 31, 2026, so older five-centre lists are stale. First/full £794 / PHP 59,550; partial resit (up to 7 failed stations) £397 / PHP 29,775. Up to 3 attempts per application; use the stricter employer/sponsorship resit scheduling rules. After 3 failures, application closes and there is a 6-month wait before a new OSCE attempt. Results normally within 5 working days. Final registration requires all exam passes, evidence, payment and ID; NMC complete-application assessment aim 30 days is not an automatic same-day PIN. Do not work as a registered nurse before register confirmation. See [OSCE rules](https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/toc/toc-nursing-and-midwifery/osce/), [registration decision](https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/register-nurse-midwife/trained-outside-uk/how-to-guide/registration-application/registration-decision/).

### Keep your evidence ready for assessment, not just submission

Health evidence needs an appropriate registered practitioner, not a friend or family member, with an assessment within the last 12 months. A managed health condition is not automatically a refusal. Character evidence includes national police clearances for countries where you lived for 12 months cumulatively in the last ten years; UK residence of three months or more can trigger a UK check. Keep current-country clearance under 12 months old through assessment. For former countries, use a certificate issued after departure where possible: the original-document page has an eight-week pre-departure allowance, but the character page can require a newer certificate. Regional police certificates do not replace national ones. Check appropriate indemnity is in place whenever practising; employer cover may suffice, but do not assume it. See [health evidence](https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/register-nurse-midwife/trained-outside-uk/how-to-guide/registration-application/health/), [character evidence](https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/register-nurse-midwife/trained-outside-uk/how-to-guide/registration-application/character/) and [indemnity arrangements](https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/register-nurse-midwife/trained-outside-uk/how-to-guide/registration-application/professional-indemnity/).

**Separate clocks:** complete registration within 2 years of eligibility confirmation; pass CBT and OSCE within 2 years of each other; once both passed the combined ToC lasts 5 years. English validity and sponsorship deadlines remain separate. Prepare before opening: an application not progressed within 3 months can close. See [NMC timing](https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/register-nurse-midwife/trained-outside-uk/how-to-guide/check-ready/how-long-is-it-going-to-take/), [Test of Competence](https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/toc/toc-nursing-and-midwifery/).

**Do not assume an English-medium Cebu degree waives tests.** NMC has specific qualification/recent-practice evidence routes; the Philippines is not on its majority-English list. UK employer supplementary evidence (SIFE) requires qualifying UK employment and is not a shortcut available from four Cebu ward years. Budget a test unless NMC accepts Joy’s actual alternative evidence. See [qualification English evidence](https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/english-language-requirements/qualified-in-english/), [accepted English-speaking countries](https://www.nmc.org.uk/globalassets/sitedocuments/registration/accepted-english-speaking-countries.pdf), [recent practice in English](https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/english-language-requirements/recent-practice-in-english/), [SIFE eligibility](https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/sife/sife-for-applicants/).

**Philippine deployment is a separate gate.** The PRC verification procedure is supported, but the current first-time UK/NHS DMW/MWO checklist and fees remain unconfirmed: the relevant official pages were unavailable or did not show the checklist. Government search listings point to overseas-employment exit clearance and direct-hire restrictions, but are only preliminary information, not a current UK-specific checklist. Before resigning, Joy must establish the employer’s Department of Migrant Workers (DMW) licensed-agency/accreditation or approved direct-hire route, Migrant Workers Office (MWO) contract verification, required orientations, medical and insurance steps, and Overseas Employment Certificate (OEC) or current digital exit-clearance requirements. Confirm any nurse deployment quota or cohort exemption too; none is established here. Do not substitute a tourist departure or returning-worker exemption. Document allowance is not a quote for these unresolved fees. See [DMW online services](https://onlineservices.dmw.gov.ph/OnlineServices/POEAOnline.aspx), [London embassy exit-clearance information (current checklist unconfirmed)](https://londonpe.dfa.gov.ph/120-pages/165-overseas-employment-certificate).

## Work backwards from September 2027

These are planning buffers, not regulator service guarantees. Employer recruitment can take six to 12 months; missing one cohort can move the start date. See [NHS international recruitment toolkit](https://www.nhsemployers.org/intl-kit).

1. **November–December 2026:** passport/name consistency; PRC status, nursing qualification and transcript; experience references/JD; English diagnostic and preparation. Plan for adult nursing field equivalence, but NMC decides eligibility. Collect marriage/birth documents for later without paying for time-sensitive certificates too early.
2. **January–February 2027:** first IELTS Academic or OET Nursing. Open NMC overseas application when ready; arrange university/PRC verification. Budget a second English sitting if needed. Start applications when you are ready to act and can keep the evidence moving.
3. **February–April:** NMC evaluation and CBT authorisation/pass; allow four–eight weeks for document chasing as a planning buffer, longer if verification stalls. Search advertised trust cohorts with sponsorship and supported OSCE; English and recruitment can proceed in parallel.
4. **March–May:** interviews/conditional offer, references, occupational health and written relocation/repayment terms. Ask for a July–August arrival cohort, OSCE slot and pay-until-PIN terms. **By end of May:** if no sponsor/cohort is secure, regard September registered practice as at risk.
5. **May–June:** final sponsor checks, CoS, fresh police/TB evidence; Philippine DMW/MWO contract/deployment route. Preserve required funds for the 28-day evidence window unless sponsor certifies maintenance. Do not resign on a verbal offer.
6. **June–July:** visa application when within three months of CoS start date; usually allow three weeks from identity/documents completion plus contingency. Secure Philippine exit clearance and accommodation; book non-refundable travel only after approvals. See [visa timing](https://www.gov.uk/health-care-worker-visa), [visa documents](https://www.gov.uk/health-care-worker-visa/documents-you-must-provide).
7. **July–August:** arrive, trust induction and supervised OSCE preparation; Registration submission/payment before OSCE, original-document/identity check at OSCE, then exam outcome and register/PIN confirmation. Allow four–eight weeks operationally and retest slack; confirm actual slots with trust later.
8. **September:** registered Band 5 work only after NMC registration. If not ready, work solely in the permitted supervised pre-registration role and keep within sponsorship deadlines; delay Band 5 start rather than practise unregistered. First payroll may be partial/delayed by cutoff: hold two months’ living money.

## Health and Care Worker visa — nurse, not care worker

- **Qualify for the right sponsored role:** confirmed NHS offer, licensed sponsor, electronic Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS), eligible registered-nursing duties/code (2232–2237), qualifying basic salary and English. Nursing is not the care-worker route. Apply within three months of CoS assignment and no earlier than three months before the actual CoS employment start. Usually an overseas decision takes three weeks after identity/documents are complete; allow delays. Grants can be up to five years. See [eligible sponsored job](https://www.gov.uk/health-care-worker-visa/your-job), [visa timing](https://www.gov.uk/health-care-worker-visa), [CoS requirements](https://www.gov.uk/uk-visa-sponsorship-employers/certificates-of-sponsorship).
- **Salary:** normally at least £25,000 and the applicable NHS going rate, whichever is higher; employer must use the relevant nursing pay-band rules, not automatically apply the generic Skilled Worker headline threshold. Current immigration tables can lag actual NHS payroll scales. Recheck the code, hours and current table on the CoS; shifts/overtime should not be needed to make basic salary qualify.
