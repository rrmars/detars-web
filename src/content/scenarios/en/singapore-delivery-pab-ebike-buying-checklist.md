If pedalling through delivery shifts is wearing you out, a power-assisted bicycle (PAB) may help—but it must fit your routes, home and cash budget before its advertised range matters. **Provisionally shortlist the JIMOVE MC at S$1,299. Keep the bicycle if exact-unit approval, safe charging or the complete budget cannot be confirmed. Do not buy on the promise of higher hourly earnings.**

This example is Rahman, **42**, a **Singapore PR in Woodlands**, riding **35 hours/week**, considering an **early-2027** switch with an **S$1,500** all-in ceiling. The MC spending scenario is **S$1,415.90**, leaving **S$84.10** contingency—not a confirmed checkout basket. Accessory caps, reused equipment, tax/fee inclusions and insurance acceptance still need checking.

Prices and source access are dated **7 November 2026**; they are not stock guarantees or early-2027 quotations. Retailer ranges are advertising, not loaded delivery tests. The underlying source file is **not published**. No fresh rules, prices or policies have been checked for this edition.

## Get the legal device and route right first

**LTA requires EN15194 for PABs, not UL2849. Newly sealed/registered PABs must meet EN15194:2017. UL2272 is the separate motorised-PMD standard.** A UL sticker alone does not establish Singapore PAB approval; a charger SAFETY Mark does not approve the whole bicycle. Start with [LTA's PAB requirements](https://onemotoring.lta.gov.sg/content/onemotoring/home/buying/vehicle-types-and-registrations/active-mobility-devices/PAB.html), [riding rules](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/getting_around/active_mobility/rules_and_public_education/rules_and_code_of_conduct.html) and the [approved-model list dated 1 July 2026](https://onemotoring.lta.gov.sg/content/dam/onemotoring/Buying/PDF/PAB/List_of_Approved_PAB_Models.pdf). Approval is retailer/model-specific, non-transferable and not an endorsement. A family-name match does not authenticate an offered unit.

### A bicycle route is not automatically a PAB route

PABs may use roads and cycling/shared paths, **not footpaths or pedestrian-only paths—even with the motor off**. Check each park-connector segment's designation and signs. No expressways, road tunnels or selected prohibited viaducts. Dismount and push on footpath-only final approaches, in crowds and at No Riding sections. Use the [permitted-device map, correct as of 9 March 2026](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/dam/ltagov/getting_around/active_mobility/rules_public_education/rules_code_of_conduct/pdf/where_devices_are_allowed_mar2026_web.pdf).

Cycling/shared paths have a **25km/h** maximum. Roads follow the road speed limit, but assistance must still cut off at **25km/h**. Neither is a target in crowds. The **10km/h** footpath limit for other permitted devices does not legalise riding a PAB there.

- On roads, rider and passenger must wear helmets; wear one every trip as a precaution. Use front white/rear red lights in darkness; the road rule permits a rear red reflector.
- Stop and check before crossing roads. Obey signals and traffic direction, keep as close as practicable to the far left, and stop safely before reading delivery instructions. Do not use a phone while riding.
- Ride **single file on single-lane roads and during bus-lane operational hours**; otherwise two abreast is allowed. **Use an available bicycle lane, not the other roadway.** Do not tow or be towed on roads.
- Road groups may be no more than **5 devices long: 5 single-file riders or 10 two-abreast**. Specified bicycle lanes are exempt from that group-length rule.
- Give way to pedestrians, keep left unless overtaking, slow around others and dismount/push in crowds. These are path-safety guidance, not a list of separate fixed-fine offences.
- After a path accident, stop, help, exchange particulars and make a police report. A policy's claim-notification deadline is a separate requirement.

## Make charging and storage work at home

Use the **original charger provided by the manufacturer with a valid serial-numbered ESG SAFETY Mark**, plus the original approved-specification battery. A matching plug, voltage label or universal-charger advert is not enough. No universal LTA-approved charger shopping list was established.

Follow the [LTA/SCDF fire-safety poster](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/dam/ltagov/getting_around/active_mobility/rules_public_education/rules_code_of_conduct/image/Fire%20Safety%20Tips%20for%20PMDs%20and%20PABs_2022.jpeg) and [safe-charging guidance](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/dam/ltagov/getting_around/active_mobility/rules_public_education/rules_code_of_conduct/image/safe_charging_practices_for_am_devices.jpg):

- Keep the tested configuration. No DIY cell repair, capacity upgrades, parallel/additional packs or controller changes. Replacements must match approved manufacturer/model specifications.
- Stop use/charging for swelling, corrosion, powdery residue, damage, a wet/impacted charger or exposed wires. Have a competent authorised service route inspect it; do not test-charge a suspect pack.
- Let the device cool after use. Charge on a hard, flat surface in a cool, dry, ventilated area, away from combustibles and escape routes; keep children and pets away.
- No prolonged unattended or overnight charging. Unplug when away for long periods. **Do not build a delivery schedule around charging while asleep.**

The [joint LTA/SCDF HDB-estate advisory](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/newsroom/2023/2/media-replies/public-should-use-active-mobility-devices-that-meet-specificatio.html) warns that devices in common corridors/escape staircases can obstruct evacuation and create fire risks; Town Councils may enforce their by-laws. The [SCDF common-area leaflet](https://www.scdf.gov.sg/docs/default-source/media-room-(publications)/posters--leaflets--booklets/publications-(docs)/guidelines-on-hdb-common-areas.pdf?sfvrsn=d403bd9_1) requires clear staircases/landings and access to risers/hose reels. Its **1.2m** small-shoe-rack illustration is **not blanket PAB storage or charging permission**.

Use designated bicycle parking or a safe place inside the flat that does not block the exit. Keep the battery dry, protected and away from heat/combustibles; do not run charging leads into common corridors. A separate PAB-specific HDB storage page was not retrieved, so this is not a blanket HDB ban on compliant PABs inside flats. Safety recommendations and statutory offences are not interchangeable.

If a battery smokes or burns, prioritise evacuation and alerting others; call **995** from safety. Do not carry a burning battery through the flat or attempt a risky rescue. This is conservative safety advice, **unconfirmed as a separately sourced emergency procedure**, not an instruction to fight a lithium-ion fire.

## Use these go/no-go gates before paying

- **Routes:** map real Woodlands pickups/drop-offs on permitted paths/roads, including dismounted approaches. Footpath access can make the ordinary bicycle more practical.
- **Exact unit:** match the approved retailer/model/battery generation, orange seal, registration/plate/transfer, original SAFETY-Marked charger, compliant weight, brakes and cargo mounting. Resolve the MC battery conflict first.
- **Cash:** keep the whole purchase within **S$1,500**, without finance or an aftermarket range upgrade. Get a complete quote and preserve a usable reserve.
- **Home and cover:** make attended charging and unobstructed storage realistic; confirm PAB platform mode and actual work-injury/TPL scope. Do not assume PA insures the bicycle.
- **Economics:** compare several comparable weeks of total online time, payouts, expenses and CPF. If the gain does not clear the relevant hurdle, keep the bicycle or consciously treat the PAB as a comfort purchase, not a guaranteed income investment.

Exact-unit compliance, full policy acceptance, the priced accessory basket and sustainable earnings uplift remain unresolved.
