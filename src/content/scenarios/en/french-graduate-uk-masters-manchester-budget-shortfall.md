You are 23, you live in Toulouse, and you have EUR 44,000 (EUR 38,000 from your family plus EUR 6,000 of your own). You want to know whether a one-year master's in the UK from September 2027 is affordable. **Short answer: do not go ahead as planned; EUR 44,000 does not cover any of the five programmes we priced.** Go only with a cheaper course or more money, and pick Manchester rather than London. The closest case, Manchester Metropolitan University (MMU) MSc Data Science, is still £4,207 (about EUR 5,044) over budget.

This guide was written on 8 November 2026 from public pages only. Nothing was applied for, requested or sent to anyone. "Unconfirmed" marks anything that is not from an official page, is only a search snippet, or is our own inference. **It is general information, not legal, tax or immigration advice.** The source materials are not published.

## The recommendation and why

**Your money.** EUR 44,000 is £36,702.60 at the European Central Bank (ECB) rate of 6 November 2026 (EUR 1 = GBP 0.83415; 44,000 × 0.83415).

- **It does not cover the example programmes.** The Manchester ones need about £54,700 to £55,200 (about EUR 65,600 to EUR 66,200) for one full year. The London ones need about £64,900 to £65,900 (about EUR 77,800 to EUR 79,000). Even tuition alone (£34,800 to £42,900 for four of the five) leaves almost nothing, or nothing, for living costs.
- **Manchester is the realistic city.** The UK government's visa funds rule is £1,171 a month outside London against £1,529 a month in London, and the universities' own living-cost estimates are about £3,050 a year lower in Manchester (£18,188 against £21,240). The Manchester figure comes from a page that covers taught master's and research students; applying it to a taught master's is our assumption.
- **The only example that comes close** is MMU's MSc Data Science at £21,000 (overseas fee): about £40,910 (EUR 49,044) all-in, still £4,207 (about EUR 5,044) over budget. MMU's course length and fee year were not confirmed on the page we read.
- **A scholarship could close that gap, but nothing is guaranteed.** A British Council GREAT Scholarship was worth at least £10,000 for French students in 2026-27. The 2027-28 list is not confirmed.
- **Rule of thumb on today's numbers:** tuition would need to be about £16,800 or less in Manchester (about £13,700 in London) for EUR 44,000 to cover a full year.
- **Part-time work does not fix this.** Working up to 20 hours a week in term time is allowed, but earnings from future work are not among the funds sources GOV.UK lists as accepted for the visa, and we did not quantify any earnings. Do not plan to rely on it.

**Next step:** decide your ceiling first (see "What to do next"), then shortlist courses, not the other way round.

### Break-even tuition

Manchester: 36,702.60 − 558 − 1,164 − 18,188 = **£16,792.60**. London: 36,702.60 − 558 − 1,164 − 21,240 = **£13,740.60**. Programmes at or below those fees would fit EUR 44,000 before any buffer. A 10% buffer (£3,670.26, roughly £3,700) lowers those ceilings by about that amount.

## Student visa checklist (French citizen, applying from outside the UK)

| Item | What GOV.UK and UKCISA say |
| --- | --- |
| Who needs it | French citizens now need a Student visa to study in the UK (post-Brexit). You must be 16 or over, hold an unconditional offer from a licensed sponsor, have enough money and meet the English requirement (and a TB test or ATAS certificate if they apply). |
| When to apply | Earliest 6 months before the course starts (so from about March 2027 for a September 2027 start). A decision usually comes within 3 weeks when applying from outside the UK. |
| Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) | A reference number the university sends once you hold an unconditional offer. You need it to apply, and must apply within 6 months of receiving it. The CAS shows the course fee you must prove funds for. |
| Visa fee | £558 (about EUR 669) from outside the UK. |
| Immigration Health Surcharge (students) | £776 per year (the student rate; £1,035 is the standard rate). For a visa longer than 12 months and up to 18 months you pay the yearly cost plus half. **Our assumption:** a 12-month master's gets a visa of about 16 months, so IHS = £776 + £388 = £1,164. **Unconfirmed:** we did not find the visa-length rule; if the visa were 12 months or less, IHS would be £776. |
| Money for course fees | Enough to pay the course fee for 1 academic year (up to 9 months), as shown on the CAS. |
| Money for living costs | £1,529 a month (London) or £1,171 a month (outside London), for up to 9 months: £13,761 or £10,539. "London" means the City of London and the 32 London boroughs. |
| The 28-day rule | The money must be held for at least 28 days in a row, and the 28-day period must end within 31 days of the date you apply. Allowed: your own money or a parent's (with a letter). Not allowed: overdrafts, crypto, stocks and shares, pensions. |
| France on the "differential evidence" list | French nationals do not have to submit proof of the living-cost funds up front, but "you might be asked to provide this evidence before you get a decision". Plan as if you must prove it. |
| English | Degree level needs CEFR B2. Accepted proof: a UK school or degree qualification, an Ecctis assessment of an English-taught non-UK degree, or an approved secure English language test (SELT). A UK university may also assess B2-equivalent itself. A degree taught in French is not on that list; that is our reading of the list, **unconfirmed** as an explicit rule. Your B2/C1 level suits the requirement but still needs the right proof. |
| Work | Up to 20 hours a week in term time (degree level or above, full-time, sponsor with a track record), full-time outside term time. A "week" runs Monday to Sunday. No self-employment, no public funds. Check your visa and your university's own limits. |
| Dependants | Not for your course. Partners and children may come only with government-sponsored students or on PhD or research-based higher degrees (courses starting on or after 1 January 2024). A taught master's does not qualify. |
| ATAS | Some sensitive subjects at master's level need an Academic Technology Approval Scheme certificate. We did not verify whether French nationals are exempt. **Next step:** ask the university, especially for data-related courses. |

### Do EU students pay international fees?

Yes, in practice. Since 1 August 2021 new EU students are generally charged the overseas rate unless they hold pre-settled or settled status (and meet the residence test). LSE says that without that status you are "likely to be eligible for Overseas fees". King's says EU, EEA and Swiss students "will instead pay the overseas tuition fee rate". Manchester labels its fee "International, including EU, students".

## The Graduate route (working after the master's)

**Dated change, checked on GOV.UK on 8 November 2026:** the Graduate visa lasts 2 years if you apply on or before 31 December 2026, but **18 months if you apply on or after 1 January 2027** (3 years for a PhD). You would finish in about September 2028, so the 18-month version applies to you, subject to further change before then.

| Item | Rule (GOV.UK) |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Apply from inside the UK, while holding a Student visa, after your university has told the Home Office you completed the course; apply before the Student visa expires. You do not have to wait until you have graduated or have been given a certificate. |
| Fee | £937 (about EUR 1,123) |
| IHS | £1,035 per year; for 18 months £1,552.50 (2 years £2,070; 3 years £3,105) |
| Total for 18 months | £937 + £1,552.50 = £2,489.50 (about EUR 2,984 at 1.1988) |
| Rights | Work in most jobs, look for work, be self-employed, do voluntary work; no access to most benefits; it cannot be extended, but you may switch to, for example, a Skilled Worker visa. A decision usually comes within 8 weeks. |

Budget this £2,489.50 for after the master's; it is not in the year budget above. Whether a job could sponsor a Skilled Worker visa afterwards was not researched. **Next step:** re-check the Graduate-route rules and fees closer to 2028; they have changed once already.

### Scholarships a French or EU student might look at

- **Chevening:** the 2027-28 round has closed ("Applications for 2027-2028 Chevening Scholarships are now closed"). It also needs 2 or more years of work experience and a commitment to return home for 2 years. France appears to have a Chevening page (eligibility inferred), but it is not usable for September 2027.
- **GREAT Scholarships (British Council):** France was eligible in 2026-27 (eight master's awards, at least £10,000 towards tuition, French passport and permanent residence in France; each university sets its own deadline). **2027-28 is not confirmed.** This is the most relevant lead.
- **Not open to you:** Commonwealth (France is not eligible), Marshall (US citizens only), Manchester Global Futures and Equity & Merit (France is not in the country lists).
- **UCL and LSE:** UCL Global Master's (£15,000, lower-income, Overseas fee status; the 2026/27 deadline was 7 May 2026). LSE Graduate Support Scheme (£5,000 to £20,000, means-tested; the 2027/28 application is "expected to open in late November 2026").

## What is still unconfirmed

- **IHS and visa length:** the £1,164 assumes a 16-month Student visa; the rule was not read. If the visa were 12 months or less, IHS would be £776.
- **MMU:** course length and fee year were not confirmed; the £21,000 is the typical annual overseas fee shown on its page for a September 2027 start.
- **2026/27 living costs:** the living-cost estimates are for 2026/27 and rents were seen on one day; 2027/28 prices will probably be higher (not quantified). The Manchester estimate comes from a page that also covers research students.
- **GREAT Scholarships 2027-28:** the list is not confirmed, and no scholarship is guaranteed.
- **ATAS:** whether French nationals are exempt was not verified. Also unverified: whether a deposit counts toward visa funds, and whether any 2027 change to Student-visa fees or a student levy will apply.
- **Not found:** UCL and Imperial fees, a London business master's fee, 2027/28 hall prices, an ONS Manchester rent figure, TfL Travelcard prices and the 2027-28 GREAT list.
- **Listings and snippets:** all rent examples, the Turing statement and the French grant reading are unconfirmed.
- **English:** that a degree taught in French is not accepted is our reading of the list, not an explicit rule.
- **Whether a job could sponsor a Skilled Worker visa** after the Graduate route was not researched.
