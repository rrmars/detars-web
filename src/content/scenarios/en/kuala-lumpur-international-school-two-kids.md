Choosing one school is only half the job. You also need both children to get suitable places, enough English support, and a school run that leaves the parents able to reach work. For this **Chinese, non-Malaysian family moving to Kuala Lumpur in July 2027**, the requested places are **Year 3 and Year 7**, the workplaces are **Mont Kiara and KL Sentral**, and the combined annual school budget is **RM110,000**, with a morning journey target of **under 40 minutes**.

**Start with Sayfol. Keep Sri Bestari INTERNATIONAL as a conditional September fallback, not an equally affordable first-year alternative.** Sayfol's first-year comparison is **RM96,879.20 + U**, leaving **RM13,120.80** before unresolved costs. A recurring-fee stress test raises it to **RM99,493.16 + U**. Here and throughout, **+ U means plus unresolved costs**, not a complete all-in quotation. First-year cash is higher: **RM107,839.20 + U**, because you must fund the full deposit, not just its annual allocation.

Start the Sayfol housing search in **Bukit Tunku or Taman Duta**, with a separate school bus/driver or parents splitting the journeys. That is a practical compromise to test, not a proven route. **If every school-day journey must stay below 40 minutes, no verified full match has been established.** There are also **0 confirmed joint vacancies**: rolling admission and a sibling discount do not secure two places.

All prices and school statements below were read on **November 7, 2026**. Five schools have AY2026/27 fee baselines; Taylor's KL is a sixth, supplemental **calendar-2027** comparison. None of the five AY comparisons has a verified **2027/28 invoice** here. These are useful starting points, not refreshed prices, rankings or reservations.

## Settle placement and English support before narrowing the list

Use the requested Year 3/Year 7 rows for comparison, but do not let those labels become assumed offers. It is unclear whether the stated ages refer to today or entry. Birth dates, reports, school cutoffs and assessment must decide placement. ParkCity uses ages **7/11 on September 1** for these rows; Taylor's uses children turning **8/12 during the calendar year**. The labels are not interchangeable.

Mandarin as a subject is not Mandarin-medium teaching. Nor does it guarantee a native-speaker pathway or enough weekly lessons for your child. Ask for each child's actual timetable **after** the English-support decision. Sayfol primary ESL and Sri KDU's English pathway can replace language lessons. Sri Bestari currently publishes **no extra English programme**. HELP's separate bilingual stream ends at **Year 6**; Taylor's ends at **Year 2**. Neither gives the requested older child a bilingual academic stream.

## Compare annual capacity with the cash you must actually pay

All money is **MYR/RM for both children**. The comparison uses:

**B = T + J + D/3 + M + X + A + R + U.**

- **T:** annual tuition after only the stated modelled sibling discount.
- **J:** full first-year application and registration charges—not spread across later years.
- **D:** deposit cash. Only deposits are allocated over **3 years** for comparison. A refundable deposit is tied-up cash, not ordinary spending; no refund credit, interest or refund certainty is assumed.
- **M:** separately identified compulsory charges; **X:** extra one-time equipment allowance.
- **A:** common annual extras allowance; **R:** conservative tax reserve; **U:** unpriced obligations and any excess over allowances/reserve.

First-year cash replaces **D/3 with full D**. Keep both figures: an annual budget can look workable while the upfront cash is not.

## What the six schools mean for your family

### Sayfol: the first budget conversation to have

[Sayfol's AY2026/27 fees](https://sayfol.edu.my/admission/fee-structure-terms-of-payment-academic-year-2026-2027/) give tuition **RM21,420 + RM27,900 = RM49,320**, with no sibling saving assumed. Application **RM1,000 + RM1,000** and registration **RM5,000 + RM5,000** make **J = RM12,000**. The model uses deposits **RM7,140 + RM9,300 = RM16,440**. Separately priced M and X are **RM0.00** in the known-part model—not proof that no further compulsory cost exists. Tax reserve is **RM3,679.20**; the annual deposit allocation is **RM5,480**. With **RM26,400** common extras, B is **RM96,879.20 + U**.

**Clarify the deposit before paying.** The table says **RM7,000 + RM8,000 = RM15,000**, but [enrolment terms §12.2](https://sayfol.edu.my/admission/terms-and-conditions-of-enrolment-and-admission/) require one term's tuition. The model therefore reserves the higher amount, a **RM1,440** difference. The fee page describes refund after one academic year plus full-term notice; §10.1 adds Student Pass rejection/non-renewal and attendance beyond **3 full terms**, alongside paid fees and notice, with refund stated **8 weeks** after leaving. Do not rely on recovery without written clarification. The **RM300** assessment is credited against application upon successful registration, not added again. Technology/activity fees are included; AEP/ESL pricing and actual tax/extras remain unresolved.

Primary and secondary share the published **261 Jalan Ampang** site. The British curriculum follows Cambridge/Pearson pathways, with Mandarin language options in [primary](https://sayfol.edu.my/our-school/primary/) and [secondary](https://sayfol.edu.my/our-school/secondary/), not a promised bilingual stream. If primary ESL replaces a language, check whether Mandarin survives. The [FAQ](https://sayfol.edu.my/frequently-asked-questions/) gives an August school year and year-round admission except upper examination years. The exact 2027/28 first day is unconfirmed; older links on the FAQ mean operational details also need reconfirming.

The [admissions guidelines](https://sayfol.edu.my/admission/admission-guidelines/) say CAT4 for **Years 3–10**, while the FAQ says English/Maths for **Years 7–10**. Prepare for clarification rather than assuming one Year 7 test; a principal interview is possible. Full groups receive waiting-list/provisional acceptance, not a place. Payment deadlines come in the letter; no live queue or school-admission sibling priority is established. The terms require valid immigration status and medical insurance for non-Malaysian pupils, not merely an application in progress.

The published day is **7:15–14:30 Monday–Thursday**, finishing **12:30 Friday**. The [bus page](https://sayfol.edu.my/student-services/school-bus-service-fares/) does not establish a current fare or exact pickup. Plan adult cover for early finishes and bus disruption.

**Placement sensitivity:** unchanged AY2026/27 Year 4 + Year 8 tuition is **RM22,500 + RM29,100 = RM51,600**, **RM2,280** higher. Deposits **RM7,500 + RM9,700 = RM17,200** produce **RM99,549.33 + U** under the same model. This is not prescribed placement or a future invoice.

### Sri Bestari INTERNATIONAL: good tuition value, difficult first year

Known compulsory M is **RM1,640**: primary CCA **RM180 × 3** plus secondary **RM200 × 3 = RM1,140**, and **RM250 × 2 = RM500** annual MOE administration **only if both need Student Passes**. The published primary CCA band is **RM150–RM180 per term**; the model uses the upper end. Meals are already in the common allowance. Year 7 needs a MacBook: **RM6,000** is an unconfirmed one-time equipment allowance, not a school price; existing functional equipment may reduce it. Lockers and actual transport, meals, devices, levy and tax excess remain unresolved.

With deposit allocation **RM6,826.67**, tax reserve **RM4,813.68**, equipment and common extras, B reaches **RM124,268.35 + U**. Removing joining/equipment and the joining-related reserve gives a later-year **same-grade** comparison of **RM98,764.35 + U**, not a forecast after progression. Printed Year 4/Year 8 tuition/deposits match Year 3/Year 7, but extras may change. Do not assume the **first-25-students 50% registration promotion**, the **2% annual-prepayment discount**, or stacking.

[Primary](https://www.sribestariinternational.edu.my/curriculum/primary/) and [secondary](https://www.sribestariinternational.edu.my/curriculum/secondary/) use Cambridge primary/lower secondary/Checkpoint/IGCSE. The [FAQ](https://www.sribestariinternational.edu.my/admissions/faq/) says **no extra English-language programme**: readiness matters for children from Chinese-medium schooling.

Teaching begins in **September**, with end-August orientation—not an August teaching start. The exact 2027/28 first day and fixed deadline are unpublished here. [Admissions](https://www.sribestariinternational.edu.my/admissions/admission-process-and-fee-schedule/) specify Year 3 English/Maths plus interview, Year 7 MidYIS plus interview; the generic secondary page describes different papers. Mandarin/Bahasa assessments follow acceptance. Typical review is **5–7 working days**, with an offer-specific acceptance date. Waitlist handling, joint places and sibling admission priority are unknown; a fee discount is not priority.

Deposit refund requires full-term written withdrawal notice; top-ups occur at **Years 1, 4, 7 and 10**. [Transport services](https://www.sribestariinternational.edu.my/sbis-experience/school-services/) list Mont Kiara/Hartamas, Segambut and Desa ParkCity areas, but independent operators set location-dependent prices and availability. A listed area is not a pickup commitment.

### Sri KDU International Kota Damansara: tuition alone is too high

For Sayfol, start with Bukit Tunku/Taman Duta addresses with access toward Jalan Duta and central KL.
