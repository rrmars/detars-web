Moving with children means lining up school, housing and take-home income, not simply clearing a visa threshold. **Start with the D8 residence route and linked accompanying-family applications from Canada.** Chidi’s active remote employment for a Toronto company fits that purpose; D7’s lower threshold does not turn salary or Ngozi’s freelance work into passive income. Toronto’s ability to arrange the family’s applications together still needs confirming.

This plan follows the **Okafor family**, moving from Toronto in **September 2027 until at least September 2029**: Chidi earns **CAD 165,000 gross a year**, Ngozi’s freelance income is unspecified, and the children are described as **8 and 12**. Canadian nationality is a planning assumption to confirm for each person; birthdays, completed grades, marriage/partnership, parentage and custody must also be checked. Do not infer citizenship from residence or surname.

**School choice is the biggest budget fork.** The public-school first-year liquidity envelope is **€74,100 / CAD 116,574.12**. The illustrative CAISL alternative is **€127,550.21 / CAD 200,661.99**. Both include contingency and a refundable deposit, but **exclude uncalculated income tax and social contributions**. The international option exceeds Chidi’s gross salary before tax; public school still needs a net-income check.

Figures and legal guidance were read **7 November 2026**, not refreshed for this guide. The underlying source materials are not published.

## Start with employer clearance and the school choice

### Visa fit is strong; permission is not automatic

At the historical exchange rate below, CAD 165,000 is about **€104,881.77/year, €8,740.15/month gross**. This is comfortably above the conditional 2027 D8 principal test of **€3,880/month**, but the actual three-month income evidence, foreign work relationship, employer approval and complete family files still matter. Ngozi’s unspecified income is counted as zero in the budget—not required to make Chidi’s headline test work.

### School choice is the financial gate

Public-school planning cash is **€74,100 / CAD 116,574.12** including contingency and refundable deposit, before uncalculated taxes/contributions. An illustrative two-child CAISL option reaches **€127,550.21 / CAD 200,661.99**. The latter exceeds Chidi’s gross salary before tax: it is not a salary-only base plan.

**My recommendation:** proceed with D8 feasibility and employer/tax clearance first; investigate public-school language support for both children, especially the older child, before choosing housing. Keep international schools as a deliberately funded alternative, not an incidental upgrade. Do not sign a non-refundable school or lease commitment on the assumption all four visas/cards will be ready together.

### Bring the family together if the local process permits

Article 58(5) expressly allows simultaneous accompanying-family visa requests; regulation 24-B requires family links and stable resources. Prepare a file for each person. This is different from later family reunification after Chidi holds residence. **Toronto’s ability to synchronize appointments and decisions was not verified.**

For later reunification, the general two-year permit-holding rule has explicit exceptions for dependent minor children and a spouse/partner who is the permit holder’s co-parent/adopter of a dependent minor. Verify marriage/recognized partnership, parentage and custody; the word “family” alone does not prove every condition. Children of either spouse can qualify, with the other parent’s authorization or custody decision where applicable. A waiting-period exemption is not an appointment-speed guarantee. Later reunification has a nine-month legal decision period, with a possible exceptional equal extension in specified cases; the short consular issue stage after approval is not the total wait. Do not plan tourist entry as a substitute for the family-residence process.

## What to prepare in Canada

Use this to prepare under national law, then confirm Toronto’s current instructions before filing. **Toronto’s current local checklist could not be confirmed**, so local appointment mechanics, current CAD visa fees, document validity, insurance wording and translation rules still need confirming before you file.

### Each family file

- Valid Canadian passport, required national-visa application signed appropriately (parent/legal representative for minors), passport photos and identification copies.

- Accommodation and stable financial-resource evidence; route-specific proof above. Confirm the exact local proof format before taking a lease or moving bank funds.

- Travel/medical insurance covering necessary medical care, emergency care and repatriation. Confirm acceptable term and wording.

- Birth certificates identifying parents; marriage certificate or qualifying partnership evidence; adoption/custody/other-parent authorization where relevant.

- Canadian school reports, completed-year records, marks/grading scale and vaccination/prescription records for practical settlement.

### Adults: work and background

- Chidi: contract, foreign-employer declaration establishing remote work relationship, three months’ professional-income evidence and tax-residence documentation. Treat payslips/bank credits as practical supporting evidence, not a verified Toronto statement-count rule.

- Ngozi: relationship file if accompanying; preserve client/service contracts and income records for immigration/work/tax review. Do not assume dependent status removes business-registration obligations.

- Plan fingerprint-based RCMP certified criminal checks for adults; verify Toronto’s exact format and freshness. National regulations exempt under-16s from criminal-record information; do not automatically order checks for these young children.

A mandatory NIF before consular filing, a specific Portuguese bank balance, a blanket twelve-month lease and a fixed insurance coverage amount have **not been confirmed for Toronto**. Get the current local proof requirements before moving money or signing a lease.

Canada’s Apostille Convention entry was 11 January 2024. Route originals by issuer: federal RCMP certificates normally go to Global Affairs Canada; Ontario-issued birth/marriage certificates to Ontario ODS. Ontario notarization can change the competent route. Apostilles are not translations; do not assume English accepted in Toronto removes AIMA’s Portuguese translation requirement.

### AIMA: the separate residence-permit step

A residence visa normally permits **two entries and four months’ stay** to request residence; it is not itself the two-year card. Regulation 14 provides for appointment communication with the visa, but actual scheduling was not verified. If no usable appointment is received, use the current official AIMA channel promptly rather than assuming an online slot or relying on an agent’s guarantee.

- Prepare valid passport and residence visa, signed AIMA application, the foreign-employer/client declaration and residential-address/right-of-use declaration with landlord/host evidence as applicable. Carry family-link, financial and insurance/health evidence and originals/certified copies as required by the current file type. Appointment/in-person filing and identity/biometric collection are separate from visa issuance; local details must be rechecked.

- Keep receipts, current contact details and enough insurance/cash for delay. The current first-permit statutory decision period is **90 days, exceptionally extendable by 30 days with justification**; this is not a guarantee of an appointment or card-delivery date. Do not rely on older automatic-approval assurances: the law changed in September 2026.

- AIMA’s D8 temporary permit is valid **two years from card issuance**, renewable in three-year periods subject to requirements. Family permit validity is linked to the applicable family-residence rules; do not assume all cards will share an issue date. Review absence/renewal/work conditions before frequent travel or client changes.

For Ngozi’s work plans, keep the actual status clear. Article 83 gives permit holders employment/self-employment rights. Article 81(4) also permits a **residence-visa holder** to work under the law while a residence application is pending for reasons not attributable to the applicant. Have her actual visa/application status, start date, business registration and tax compliance checked before she begins; this is not tourist work permission.

## Get a household tax and employer answer before committing

### Canada: ties, not citizenship

CRA assesses residential ties, especially home, spouse and dependants. A two-year intention or Canadian passport does not settle residence. Retaining ties can mean factual residence; treaty residence elsewhere can produce deemed non-residence. Departure may trigger deemed disposition of certain assets and benefit changes.

**Ask:** What departure date follows from actual family travel/home availability? What happens to a retained/rented Toronto home, investments, TFSA/RRSP/RESP, departure valuations/reporting, Canadian-source income and CCB? Which Canadian tax shelters are not recognized in Portugal?

### Portugal: not only 183 days

AT describes more than 183 days in a relevant 12-month period *or* a habitual-home test even with fewer days. Residents generally face worldwide-income taxation. A September move can therefore matter for 2027 even without 183 calendar-year days. AT says report residence/address changes within 60 days.

**Ask:** When does Portuguese residence begin; how is any split year treated; what foreign-income/account reporting applies; how do treaty residence tie-breakers and foreign-tax credits prevent double taxation without removing filing duties?

Treaty Article 4 considers permanent home, centre of vital interests, habitual abode, nationality and then competent-authority agreement. The family’s treaty residence still needs an individual determination.

### What replaced NHR?

Old NHR was repealed for new entrants from **1 January 2024**, with grandfathering/transitional arrangements—not an open option for this first-time 2027 plan. **IFICI** (scientific research and innovation incentive, sometimes marketed as “NHR2.0”) is targeted, not a like-for-like blanket replacement. Article 58-A requires prior five-year non-residence plus qualifying activities/entities. Its **20%** treatment covers qualifying net Category A/B income over the applicable **ten-year** period; it is not 20% on everything and a D8 does not establish eligibility. A Toronto salary for work physically performed in Portugal is not automatically foreign-source/exempt merely because the payer is Canadian.

**Ask the adviser:** Which exact IFICI provision/entity/credentials could Chidi or Ngozi satisfy, if any? The published general registration rule is 15 January after the residence year—therefore **15 January 2028** for a qualifying 2027 start, subject to then-current rules and eligibility. Which authority must certify the activity? Budget without relief until confirmed.

### Toronto employer and Ngozi’s business

- Will the employer authorize Portugal and bear local payroll/withholding, employer registration, employment-law/EOR and permanent-establishment review costs? Working in Portugal can be taxable there despite Canadian payroll.

- Which social-security system applies? The bilateral agreement has a qualifying temporary-assignment provision up to 24 months; a voluntary move is not automatically a qualifying assignment. Is a certificate of coverage available, and what remains outside it?

- Does Chidi’s management/home-office activity create corporate-tax exposure? Ask counsel to review the treaty and current modifying instruments, not only the absence of contract-signing power.

- For Ngozi: Portuguese activity registration, invoicing/VAT, social security, foreign-client income and timing of work permission; her unspecified turnover cannot support a net-income estimate.

Individual tax, contributions, IFICI eligibility and any payroll exemption remain unresolved. Ask the adviser to produce a **net household cash flow before either a school or lease commitment**; no tax relief is assumed in the budgets below.
