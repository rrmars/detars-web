**Your income clears the dated D7 household floor in this example—but the living budget is much tighter. At an assumed £1 = €1.15, £3,200/month after UK tax becomes €3,680. A modest, car-free plan with €1,500 rent and a provisional tax-change margin totals €3,500, leaving only about €180/month. Passing the visa income test does not make a Lisbon-area lease affordable.**

My recommendation is to keep exploring, starting with Almada and a suitable long lease at or below the rent target. Before committing, get your actual post-move pension calculation and insurance terms. The tax margin is not a calculated tax bill, and the insurance allowance is not a quotation for a couple aged 64.

This example assumes two British nationals legally resident in the UK, married or civil partners, both aged 64 on November 6, 2026, with £60,000 saved and an autumn 2027 move in mind. It does not rely on pre-2021 Portuguese residence rights under the Withdrawal Agreement. If your nationality, relationship or residence history differs, check the route before using the checklist.

## First separate the visa floor from your spending budget

The UK [D7 retirement checklist](https://www.vfsglobal.com/one-pager/portugal/uk/english/pdf/D7-RESIDENCE-VISA-FOR-RETIREMENT-RELIGIOUS-OR-FOR-LIVING-FROM-INDIVIDUAL-REVENUES.pdf), dated March 6, 2026, uses the Portuguese minimum monthly wage: 100% for the main adult and another 50% for a spouse. The [government's wage guide](https://www.gov.pt/guias/trabalhar-em-portugal) recorded €920 for 2026. [AIMA's means page](https://aima.gov.pt/pt/temas-transversais/meios-de-subsistencia) supports the household percentages, but its wage paragraph still showed the old 2025 figure of €870; do not use that stale number for the 2026 basis.

The [government's 2027 announcement](https://portugal.gov.pt/pt/gc25/comunicacao/noticias/rendimentos-habitacao-servicos-publicos-o-que-pode-esperar-no-proximo-ano) supplied the €970 scenario; its implementing wage instrument was not verified. Recheck the enacted wage and UK checklist when applying. The annual household totals are calculations using 12 months and the household scale, not separately printed couple bank-balance quotations. Your converted €3,680 is above both illustrated floors. You still need stable, documented, accessible pension income. Savings do not guarantee approval or remove pension-proof requirements. The checklist describes means as net of tax and social-security contributions while explicitly using €920 and €11,040; do not invent a further percentage discount.

### Build a document folder without ordering short-lived papers too soon

Use the main retirement category plus the [accompanying-family-member category](https://www.vfsglobal.com/one-pager/portugal/uk/english/pdf/D8-RESIDENCE-VISA-FOR-ACCOMPANYING-FAMILY-MEMBER-APPLYING-FOR-A-RESIDENCE-VISA.pdf) for the spouse where applicable. The UK site's numbering differs from familiar “D8 digital nomad” shorthand: use the full category title, not the number alone.

| Item | Dated UK requirement and practical next step |
|---|---|
| Pension and funds | Evidence of pension amount and payment frequency; last three months' statements showing receipt; a recent Portuguese bank statement with at least €11,040 for one applicant; NIF proof. The household scale adds 50% for the spouse. Prepare both income-receipt and funded Portuguese-account evidence: the checklist says “one of” but joins its bank bullets with “and”, so do not assume savings alone suffice. |
| NIF and bank account | The NIF is your Portuguese tax number. Resolve identification, account-opening and funding requirements early. Remote opening, joint-account acceptance and a particular bank's approval are not established here. A NIF alone is not visa approval. |
| Accommodation | Applicant-owned deeds; or a lease lasting at least 12 months from intended arrival, with Portuguese tax-registration proof; or a certified host responsibility term with host identity/status and property/right-of-use evidence, plus landlord permission where required. A holiday booking is not the listed rental route. Confirm both applicants can use the address and discuss delay/cancellation exposure before signing. |
| Insurance | Necessary medical expenses, urgent assistance and repatriation for 120 days from intended entry; eligible British applicants may use a duly certified S1. Do not assume ordinary travel insurance covers permanent relocation or one-way travel. Keep cover continuous beyond the visa period. |
| Criminal record | Each adult resident in the UK during the past year needs an ACRO certificate issued within the last 90 days, legalised with a Hague Apostille through the UK Legalisation Office. Other recent residence can require another country's certificate. Allow apostille time without ordering a year early. |
| Identity and statement | Separate signed form, two recent passport photos, passport and biographic copy. Passport validity must extend 90 days beyond the 120-day visa period from intended arrival. Signed statement: reasons, Portuguese address/postcode and stay arrangements. Check both passports now and any applicable UK-status evidence. |
| Relationship | Apostilled marriage or civil-partnership certificate and household means evidence for the accompanying spouse. Each applicant needs a complete file. |

Documents outside English or Portuguese need certified translations. Use the full then-current checklists before submission; older UK PDFs have different criminal-record and insurance windows, and an older handbook's apostille waiver must not override the 2026 checklists. Fees are non-refundable, extra documents may be requested and a complete file does not guarantee approval.

## Work backwards from autumn, without making it an immovable deadline

The dated consular standard was **60 days**, extendable when justified. [VFS UK](https://www.vfsglobal.com/one-pager/portugal/uk/english/) warned that some cases take at least three months and summer appointments fill quickly. Neither figure is an end-to-end promise: appointment hunting, preparation, AIMA and card delivery are separate. No verified UK D7 median or dependable AIMA appointment/card wait was established.

| Planning stage | What to do and when to pause |
|---|---|
| Now to spring 2027 | Classify each pension and calculate post-move net income. Check actual State Pension dates, insurance at entry ages, accessible homes and neighbourhoods. Start NIF/bank preparation and keep traceable pension receipts. This is preparation, not an instruction to file now. |
| Spring to early summer 2027 | Resolve accommodation, funding and spouse evidence. Recheck the enacted wage, fees, checklists and appointment instructions. Order ACRO and apostille near submission so the certificate remains within 90 days. |
| About June–July 2027 for September–October travel | Aim for the earliest permitted filing. VFS allowed submission up to three months before travel, in person through the UK centre for your residence jurisdiction. An appointment and decision are not guaranteed within that window. |
| After a consular decision | Inspect the actual visa dates. The dated residence visa lasts 120 days and allows up to two entries; it is not the residence card. Avoid non-refundable moving commitments before the decision. |
| After arrival | Follow issued appointment instructions and the current AIMA channel; keep originals, means/address evidence and continuous healthcare cover. Do not assume the process is wholly online or the consulate can reschedule AIMA. |

At the separate [AIMA residence stage](https://aima.gov.pt/pt/viver/autorizacao-de-residencia-regime-e-requisitos-gerais-art-o-77-o-n-o-1), general requirements included passport/residence visa, means, an address declaration and lawful-occupancy evidence, relationship proof where relevant, fiscal/social-security registration where applicable, and **health insurance or proof of SNS cover**. The general temporary permit was two years from issue, normally renewable for three-year periods, subject to exceptions. The page described an appointment/in-person procedure and a digital platform still being implemented—not a verified inventory of available slots.

Published application charges were **€110 consular fee + £32.85 VFS service fee per person**, or €220 + £65.70 for two, before optional services, certificates, apostilles, insurance and later AIMA charges. These are 2026 figures, not a 2027 quote. Keep money for delays, temporary accommodation and extended insurance. A September target should not force you to sell up first.

## Healthcare: arrange the bridge, not just the SNS number

The [UK resident-healthcare guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/healthcare-in-portugal-including-madeira) describes SNS registration for registered Portuguese residents, including non-workers. Visit your local centro de saúde with passports, residence evidence and NIFs; it additionally lists NISS for employed/self-employed people. Employment is not a universal resident-access condition.

**Ask the health centre to confirm coverage and financial responsibility, not just issue a number.** The [Portuguese SNS-number service page](https://www.gov.pt/servicos/pedir-o-numero-de-utente-do-sns) says foreigners receive a número de utente at the first public-healthcare encounter, but the number alone does not guarantee SNS payment of expenses. Keep entitlement and residence papers together. Registration does not promise an immediately assigned family doctor or a particular waiting time; prescriptions, dental work and uncovered care still need a budget. The UK page's older €5/€15 fee examples are not used here as current charges.

### Do not assume an S1 just because you have retired

Both applicants being 64 on November 6, 2026 places their birth years in the [legislated State Pension age 67 cohort](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/state-pension-age-timetable/state-pension-age-timetable). **Do not budget on State-Pension-based S1 cover for an autumn 2027 move.** Exact birthdays and any separate qualifying exportable-benefit entitlement still matter. A private pension alone does not establish S1 eligibility.

If you later qualify for a UK-issued S1, register it with Portuguese social security and the health centre; check dependant status. It supports care on the same basis as Portuguese residents. [GHIC](https://www.nhs.uk/using-the-nhs/healthcare-abroad/apply-for-a-free-uk-global-health-insurance-card-ghic/) is for eligible temporary visits, not permanent-resident insurance, private care or repatriation. An unexpired card does not prove continuing entitlement after a move.

## Pensions and tax: calculate the new net income before a lease

**A D7 is not a pension tax holiday.** [NHR was repealed from January 1, 2024](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/en/tax-information/getting-started-in-portugal/non-habitual-resident/register-as-a-non-habitual-resident/Pages/default.aspx), with existing and tightly defined transitional protections for qualifying earlier cases. Those do not create a fresh autumn 2027 retirement offer. Do not use old 0% or 10% foreign-pension stories; the research/innovation replacement is not an automatic pensioner concession.

Use the **2025 UK–Portugal treaty**, not the old 1968 treaty. [HMRC's status page](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/portugal-tax-treaties) records entry into force on December 29, 2025, application in Portugal from January 1, 2026 and UK Income Tax application from April 6, 2026. The [treaty text's URL](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/portugal-tax-treaties/2025-uk-portugal-double-taxation-convention-not-in-force) still says “not-in-force”, but its inspected title/status said in force.

| Pension type | General position once Portuguese treaty-resident | What to confirm |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary private/occupational pension | Article 17 generally allocates taxation only to residence country: Portugal | Scheme classification and UK treaty-relief procedure. UK withholding is not proof of final UK liability. |
| UK State Pension | Generally analysed under the residence-country rule, not government service merely because DWP pays it | This is an interpretation of the treaty wording, not a scheme-specific ruling. Check classification and your actual entitlement date. |
| Qualifying UK government-service pension | Article 18 generally reserves tax to the paying state: UK for a UK government-service pension paid to a UK national | A recipient who is not a UK national but is Portuguese can be taxed in both countries under the new wording. Government-business service has a separate carve-out. “Public sector” alone is not enough to classify a scheme. |

[UK pension-tax guidance](https://www.gov.uk/tax-on-pension/tax-when-you-live-abroad) explains why treaty allocation and relief matter. Article 21 provides double-tax relief where both countries may tax and can allow exempt income to influence the rate on other income. Tell HMRC about the move and get help with residence/tie-breakers and relief; do not assume PAYE stops automatically.

Portugal's ordinary personal income tax is IRS. The following dated **marginal rates apply to taxable income, not the whole gross pension or an effective household tax rate**. [CIRS Article 68](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs68.aspx) and [Decree-Law 212/2026](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/legislacao/diplomas_legislativos/Documents/Decreto-Lei-212-2026.pdf) recorded publication on November 4, 2026 and commencement on November 5, 2026, with reductions applying to 2026 income from January 1. These are not verified 2027 tables.

| Taxable-income band, EUR | Marginal rate |
|---|---:|
| Up to 8,342 | 12.2% |
| Above 8,342–12,587 | 15.2% |
| Above 12,587–17,838 | 20.7% |
| Above 17,838–23,089 | 23.6% |
| Above 23,089–29,397 | 30.6% |
| Above 29,397–43,090 | 34.6% |
| Above 43,090–46,566 | 43.1% |

[Article 68-A](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs68a.aspx) additionally records solidarity tax: 2.5% on the €80,000–€250,000 taxable-income slice and 5% above €250,000. For joint assessment its test uses half joint taxable income and doubles the result. These are not an individual pension assessment.

A short first calendar year is not automatically tax-free. [Article 16](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs16.aspx) includes more than 183 days in a relevant 12-month period and a habitual-home test that can apply sooner. Review the arrival year, UK/Portugal overlap and any large pension withdrawals before acting.

Your £3,200 is already **after UK tax**. It cannot establish your future Portuguese liability or take-home income. Give a UK–Portugal tax adviser gross annual pension statements for each spouse, scheme types, ownership split, UK deductions, other income, lump-sum plans, dates of birth and arrival/residence dates. Compare available filing choices and deductions. Replace the temporary budget margin with that result rather than subtracting an entire Portuguese bill from an unchanged UK-net figure.

### Keep savings as a cushion, not another pension

From the illustrative €69,000, ring-fence **€10,000–€15,000 for moving**: rent/deposit or advances, temporary stays, travel, shipping, documents and fees. That is an estimate, not a statutory charge or landlord entitlement. It leaves about €54,000–€59,000. A six-month emergency pot at €3,500/month is **€21,000**, held within the remaining savings, not another bill paid to someone.

Visa-support funds remain your money. Do not count the same euros as untouched proof-of-funds reserves and freely spendable relocation money. A reserve can absorb a delayed start; it cannot make recurring overspending sustainable.

## Your go/no-go test

Keep the first move modest and reversible. Commit only when a real, suitable lease and confirmed post-move net pension leave a recurring surplus without drawing on savings for everyday months. With this plan's €1,500 rent and provisional tax margin, €180 is thin.

Before a lease, settle pension tax and actual insurance terms. Before submission, recheck the enacted 2027 wage, UK D7/family checklists, fees, application window and accommodation wording. Before departure, inspect visa dates, AIMA instructions, healthcare start/end dates, accessible reserves and a delay fallback. You can explore neighbourhoods now without pretending those expensive questions are already answered.
