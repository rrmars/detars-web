Worried about supporting both of you before your partner finds work? My read is that one **CAD85,000 gross salary can cover a modest, car-free Toronto one-bedroom**, if rent stays around **$2,300 or less with heat and water included**. After **$4,258.40 spending** and a separate payroll cushion, this budget leaves **$942.33/month**. There is not much room for everything to cost more: the stress case falls **$301.17/month short**. Aim to bring about **$24,000 in starting cash**, or **$35,000 with a six-month essentials reserve**, rather than just first and last rent.

### What the allowances do—and do not—buy

The prices here were checked **November 5, not checked again while preparing the article**. Ask for current prices before choosing a service:

- **Utilities:** The $80 base allowance needs heat/water included and no electric space heating. [Toronto Hydro’s rates](https://www.torontohydro.com/for-home/rates) covered November 1, 2026–April 30, 2027, not May. The 9.1/14.6/18.9 cents/kWh figures cover electricity only. Ask about fixed delivery charges, tax and rebates to estimate the whole bill. The workbook allows $150 under stress.
- **Phones:** Two $35 BYOD plans × Ontario 13% HST = **$79.10**. The [Public Mobile page](https://publicmobile.ca/en/plans) advertised $35/50GB 5G, without handset financing or India roaming. Check the current offer and any extras you need. Lean/stress allow $25/$45 before tax per person; ask for actual offers at those prices.
- **Insurance/internet:** [Square One](https://www.squareone.ca/tenant/ontario/toronto) advertised insurance from $15, but **$35 is your household allowance, not a quote**. Confirm both spouses’ coverage and deductible. No usable price was available from [Beanfield](https://www.beanfield.com/en/tor/residential/internet/); get an exact-address quote and check building availability rather than treating $75 as its price.
- **Food and household:** $750 for groceries assumes Indian staples, produce/protein and mostly home cooking; $600 needs careful shopping. Price your own basket: this is not a checked shopping list. Keep toiletries/cleaning, laundry and dining separate. Dining already includes tax/tips. [CRA tax guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html), read November 5, supports Ontario’s 13% HST and zero-rated basic groceries. **Do not add 13% again across the budget**.
- **Health:** Keep $100 monthly for routine drugs, dental and vision outside ordinary OHIP coverage. If you have an expensive ongoing condition, replace that allowance with your actual needs. Check employer benefits; none is confirmed. Bridge emergency insurance belongs in arrival cash. Initial winter/work purchases are separate from recurring clothes/leisure too.

**Excluded:** debt repayments, remittances, annual India visits, ongoing Indian housing, car/parking, children, pets, immigration fees, professional licensing/major credential exams, shipping a container, expensive ongoing medical care and any unknown employer deductions. Add relevant costs before concluding that your own household fits.

### A May arrival needs its own tax analysis

The separate 2026-rule withholding example starts with $7,083.33 gross, **$404.10 CPP, $115.46 EI and about $1,303.59 tax per month**, yielding $5,260.18. Eight full payments total **$56,666.64 gross**; CPP/EI do not reach annual caps and CPP2 is zero in that example. Annual limits are not simply prorated to eight months because employment begins in May. Actual pay frequency, first partial period, TD1 claims and deductions can differ; annual net divided by 12 is not an expected first cheque.

For your first return, check the applicable versions of [CRA’s 2026 Ontario tables](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/payroll/t4032-payroll-deductions-tables/t4032on-jan/t4032on-january-general-information.html) (effective January 1, 2026), [January T4127](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/payroll/t4127-payroll-deductions-formulas/t4127-jan/t4127-jan-payroll-deductions-formulas-computer-programs.html) and [newcomer-return guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/international-non-residents/individuals-leaving-entering-canada-non-residents/newcomers-canada-immigrants/completing-return-newcomers.html). These were read November 5 for the initial plan, not comprehensively rechecked for this article. The newcomer page covered 2025 returns; do not use its dollar examples as 2027 amounts.

For your first return, gather your residency date, resident-period worldwide income, foreign taxes, pre-arrival income and your spouse’s worldwide income. Pre-arrival income matters for the nonresident-period credit test. Resident days can affect credits. The nonresident-period 90% test is not just about Canadian pay after arrival. **No Canadian job does not mean zero spouse income.** Do not multiply full-year tax by eight-twelfths or spend a hoped-for refund. Check 2027 CRA payroll/TD1/TD1ON with your employer and get a personal first-return calculation.

| Last-month rent deposit | $2,300.00 | Tied-up prepayment, not damage fee or extra recurring rent |
| First + last at signing | $4,600.00 | Liquidity checkpoint only; **not added again** below |
| Monthly essentials used for reserve | $3,808.40 | Base spending less $150 dining, $100 clothes/leisure and $200 contingency |
| Three months essentials | $11,425.20 | Includes first month's rent; assumes no income receipts during reserve period |
| Six months essentials | $22,850.40 | Alternative reserve, not additional to three-month reserve |
| **Setup + last rent + three-month reserve** | **$23,575.20** | Practical rounded target about $24,000 |

Do not add $4,600 signing cash to either landing total: first rent is inside the living reserve and last rent is already added. Choose a reserve variant; do not add both. Keep temporary housing until your dates are fixed, since it can overlap the initial lease. Then map payments through your first payday: these totals do not establish day-by-day cash sufficiency or cover every future lease commitment. The reserve protects against interrupted income; it does not predict six months of losses while your wife looks for work.

## Rules that can change the plan

### Lease cash and rent control

Before handing over lease cash, check what each payment is for. The [LTB guide](https://tribunalsontario.ca/documents/ltb/Brochures/Guide%20to%20RTA%20(English).html), reread November 5, limits last-rent deposits to one month for an RTA-covered monthly tenancy. Annual guideline-linked interest may go toward a permitted deposit top-up rather than arrive as cash. First rent pays for the current month. The [standard-lease guide](https://www.ontario.ca/page/guide-ontarios-standard-lease) and [O. Reg. 516/06 section 17](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/060516#BK21), read November 5, distinguish prohibited mandatory damage/pet deposits from refundable key/fob deposits capped at expected direct replacement cost. Keep key deposits separate from rent-deposit interest rules. Put both spouses, included utilities and lawful rent before incentives in the lease. Check protection first if you share the landlord’s kitchen/bathroom: that can take the tenancy outside the RTA.

Check the home’s first residential occupancy date before relying on rent control. [Ontario rent-increase guidance](https://www.ontario.ca/page/residential-rent-increases), read November 5, gave the **2027 1.9% guideline**, not a limit on a new tenancy’s asking rent. First residential occupancy after **November 15, 2018** can mean an exemption; a renovation or advertised construction year does not settle this. For a May 2027 tenancy, normal first increases would generally wait 12 months and need 90 days’ proper notice. You would therefore need the **2028**, not 2027 guideline; check that 2028 figure, which remains unverified here.

**Ask about air-conditioning charges even when electricity is included.** The reread LTB guide allows separate seasonal electricity charges for a window/portable air conditioner installed on or after **July 1, 2026**. The cost must reflect actual electricity or a reasonable estimate and stop when use ends; normal 90-day/12-month timing does not apply to this seasonal increase. Ask what an “all utilities” offer means for you. Ordinary rent-increase protection does not fix every bill.

### OHIP: no old three-month wait is not automatic healthcare eligibility

Check your own OHIP route before deciding on private cover. Ontario’s [registration guidance](https://www.ontario.ca/document/resources-for-physicians/registration-ontario-health-insurance-coverage), selectively reread November 5, confirms that the old three-month wait ended in March 2020. The [application page](https://www.ontario.ca/page/apply-ohip-and-get-health-card) and [document list](https://www.ontario.ca/page/documents-needed-get-health-card), also reread, still require eligible status, residence/presence and an application. A job offer alone is not enough. If you use the valid-work-permit route, you need full-time work in Ontario for an Ontario employer; your spouse may also qualify. Ask for a signed, dated employer letter giving full-time status, occupation, start date and **intended employment of at least six months**—you do not have to have completed those six months. Permanent residence is another qualifying route, but residence and application rules still apply.

Apply in person with status, Ontario-residence and identity evidence. The general presence tests are **153 days in any 12 months and 153 of the first 183**, subject to exceptions. Establish each spouse's coverage start date before relying on it. Visitors are not covered. This page does not determine visa eligibility, spouse work permission or entry rights.

The $750 bridge reserve is conditional protection for an actual uninsured period, **not a universal three-month insurance requirement**. Age, pre-existing-condition stability, deductibles, limits and post-arrival waiting clauses need a real policy assessment. If either person stays ineligible, add continuing premiums rather than stop after three months. [OHIP coverage guidance](https://www.ontario.ca/page/what-ohip-covers), read November 5, excludes routine dentist-office care, glasses and outpatient prescriptions in general; other programs may apply. Employer drug/dental benefits and emergency hospital/doctor coverage are not interchangeable.

### TTC: do not buy two obsolete ordinary monthly passes

Use the same eligible card/device consistently for each person. The [TTC fare page](https://www.ttc.ca/Fares-and-passes), reread November 5, shows **$3.30 electronic adult fares and a 47-fare calendar-month cap: $155.10 per adult**. The ordinary adult monthly pass ended August 31, 2026. The $143 adult 12-month contract and $128.15 postsecondary passes remain, but this budget does not commit you to a long contract. Cash/PRESTO Tickets do not qualify for the cap. A physical card and wallet can keep separate counters, so avoid switching and keep tapping after you reach the cap.

Count paid two-hour transfer windows, not every boarding, under the [capping rules](https://www.ttc.ca/riding-the-ttc/Updates/TTC-monthly-fare-capping). Your wife’s lean/base/stress allowances use **16/24/47** paid windows. Add GO/UP/airport premiums separately if needed. The [May 2026 announcement](https://www.ttc.ca/news/2026/May/Fare-capping-coming-to-the-TTC-in-September), rechecked for the article, says **40 paid trips in 2027** but does not confirm the exact May start date. If the fare stays $3.30 and you both hit the cap, that would mean **$132/person or $264/couple**. Keep the conservative $155.10/person maximum until the change is confirmed for you; it is not a verified May 2027 fare.

## Questions you may still have

Before relying on the budget, check what the salary, arrival cash and health-cover assumptions mean for you.

**Is $85,000 enough if my partner has no job yet?** At base costs here, yes: your wife’s earnings stay at zero and $942.33/month remains. Stress costs exceed planning income by $301.17. Add your own excluded commitments and actual employer deductions before relying on that answer.

**Does $24,000 mean I will spend it all immediately?** No. The exact $23,575.20 target combines $9,850 setup cash allowances, $2,300 tied-up last rent and $11,425.20 retained essentials reserve. It is neither a fee total nor cash on top of three more months' rent.

**Does no OHIP waiting period mean I can skip private cover?** First establish each person’s eligibility and coverage date. The $750 is a conditional reserve, not a quote. Price your own cover: you may need less, more or a longer period than assumed.
