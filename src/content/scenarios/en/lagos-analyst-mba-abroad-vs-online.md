You do not need to leave Lagos to move your career forward. For this 30-year-old Nigerian bank credit analyst, my recommendation is to **keep the job and favour an online MBA**. Explore Gies' Forté funding first, with Boston University as the lower-sticker-price alternative. Neither is fully funded by the planned US$25,000 savings pot once extras and an emergency reserve are included.

The profile matters: six years of experience, NGN 14 million annual gross earnings, one adult and no dependants. This comparison is dated November 7, 2026, for mainly autumn 2027 study. Prices, admissions dates and immigration rules below have not been refreshed since then. Degree results, management responsibilities and English evidence still need checking; experience alone does not establish eligibility.

Keeping US$5,000 untouched leaves an online funding gap of **US$11,378–US$12,316** before existing Lagos living costs. Over 24 months, that is about US$474–US$513 each month, or 55–59% of current gross earnings. A grant, employer contribution, more saving or a slower affordable schedule must close that gap. If none works, defer rather than borrow against an imagined foreign salary.

## Which programme fits the career you actually want?

### Birmingham: a shorter move, but still a large funding gap

The [Birmingham MBA](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/study/postgraduate/subjects/business-and-management-courses/mba) is a one-year, on-campus September 2027 programme. Tuition is £40,410 for 2027. Normally you need a 2:1 degree or equivalent, at least three years' experience, usually five, and evidence of career progression and managerial responsibility. Your six years fit the duration. Show actual people, budget or project responsibility rather than relying on the credit-analyst title.

GMAT is explicitly not required, though a submitted score can be considered. GRE policy is not stated. Academic equivalence and English requirements remain personal checks. Prepare a CV, a personal statement of at most two pages and academic/work references; two work references can substitute where no academic reference is available. An interview may be requested.

The visa-applicant deadline is **July 2, 2027**, earlier if places fill. Funding needs an earlier start. The [MBA scholarships](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/study/scholarships-funding/mba-scholarship) offer Purposeful Leader awards up to £20,000 and Responsible Business awards up to £15,000. All nationalities and domiciles are eligible for consideration. You need an offer, then a separate scholarship application, statement and responsible-business essay. Assessment is rolling. The published cycle opens in October 2026, with first and final deadlines in January and March 2027; exact days and live form status are not confirmed. Do not assume the awards combine.

Even the maximum Purposeful Leader illustration leaves £20,410 tuition. A tuition discount does not pay your rent, visa or food.

### Sheffield: lower UK tuition, with a conditional half-fee award

The [Sheffield MBA](https://sheffield.ac.uk/postgraduate/taught/courses/2027/mba-master-business-administration-mba) is one year, full-time, starting September 2027. Its [published overseas fee](https://sheffield.ac.uk/api/course-fees/pgt/MGTT01/2027) is £37,630 for 2027–28, £2,780 below Birmingham. You need a 2:2 degree or equivalent and three years' postgraduate full-time experience with management responsibility and progression. IELTS is 6.5 overall, with 6 in each component, or an accepted equivalent.

No GMAT/GRE requirement is listed in the programme/application materials. That is not an explicit promise that scores can never be requested. The [application guidance](https://sheffield.ac.uk/management/mba/how-apply) asks for four supporting-statement answers of 200 words each and an online interview after initial selection. The course page says references are not requested.

The [August 26, 2027 visa-applicant deadline](https://sheffield.ac.uk/postgraduate/deadlines) is university-wide, not a separately confirmed MBA deadline or seat guarantee. Apply well before it. The general closure is September 3, 2027 at 17:00 BST; the university advises CAS application by September 6, 2027. Its September 27, 2027 standard teaching date is not a separately confirmed MBA induction schedule.

The [Sheffield MBA Scholarship Scheme](https://sheffield.ac.uk/funding/sheffield-mba-scholarship-scheme) offers 50% tuition: an award and remaining tuition of £18,815 each at this fee. Eligible international offer-holders are automatically considered, with no separate form. Awards are competitive and rolling. Exceptions-process entrants are excluded; self-funding is required, and other University scholarships cannot be combined. Accept and pay the required tuition deposit by the award-letter deadline; no universal date or deposit amount is established.

The international study trip has a £100 non-refundable deposit, visa and other extras. It is excluded from the essential budget below and is not a tuition deposit. Trip visa approval is not guaranteed, and missing the trip does not entitle you to a refund.

### DeGroote: a Canadian recruiting window, not the affordable option

[McMaster's standard DeGroote Full-Time MBA](https://mbarecruit.degroote.mcmaster.ca/program/mba-programs/full-time-mba/) takes **20 months across two academic years, not 24 months**. It is not the co-op MBA. September 2027 entry includes an optional four-month internship; no earnings from it are assumed. Finance and Strategic Business Valuation specialisations suit this background. A 16-month route gives up specialisation. Confirm the final 2027 teaching location before arranging housing: programme information also describes a campus transition.

[International tuition](https://mbarecruit.degroote.mcmaster.ca/program/finances/) is approximately C$107,300 for the programme, labelled 2027/2028, not an annual fee or an all-in invoice. Exact future supplementary fees and health coverage remain unconfirmed.

[Admissions](https://mbarecruit.degroote.mcmaster.ca/admissions/) normally require an honours bachelor's degree, a recommended B average and two years' post-degree experience. A B+ over the last two years can support a discretionary Fall 2027 GMAT/GRE waiver; recipients take a quantitative refresher. Six years in banking is not itself a waiver. GRE and specified alternatives, including passed CFA Level II, are accepted. The [FAQ](https://mbarecruit.degroote.mcmaster.ca/admissions/frequently-asked-questions/) calls GMAT 555+ or GRE 308, with Q157/V151, competitive guides rather than guaranteed-admission thresholds. IELTS 7 overall or TOEFL 100 overall are listed; an English-medium exemption needs university evidence, not nationality alone.

The next round and scholarship-competition deadline is **November 25, 2026**. October 15, 2026 has passed; **March 14, 2027** is the final international deadline. Do not use May 31, 2027 as this Nigerian applicant's deadline. Prepare two references, a CV, transcripts and the Kira video interview; the application fee is C$150.

Eligible domestic and international applicants are automatically assessed for MBA Admissions Awards of C$2,000–30,000. The C$14,000 average belongs to 2026, not a promised 2027 award. The FAQ still shows an older C$1,000 lower bound. Separate [competition instructions](https://mbarecruit.degroote.mcmaster.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/109/2025/06/DeGroote-School-of-Business-Admission-Scholarship-Application-Instructions.pdf) show up to C$15,000, but the document is from 2025 and its 2027 value and stacking are unconfirmed. Forté support is described for eligible women; this applicant's gender and the school's monetary award are unknown. Keep committed funding at zero until you have a written award.

### Gies: pursue funding, then test the workload

The [Gies tuition page](https://giesonline.illinois.edu/tuition-costs) gives US$27,288 at Fall 2026–Summer 2027 rates. The [student fee breakdown](https://onlinestudents.giesbusiness.illinois.edu/newly-admitted/tuition) explains why: 72 credits × (US$375 tuition + US$4 student fee). Using tuition alone would miss US$288. This is not a locked 2027–29 price; materials, payment charges, residence-dependent taxes and future increases need checking.

The [iMBA](https://giesonline.illinois.edu/explore-programs/online-mba) normally permits 24–60 months. [Admissions](https://giesonline.illinois.edu/explore-programs/online-mba/admissions) expect at least three years' substantial full-time work and recommend a 3.0/4.0 GPA. GMAT/GRE is optional, but the committee may request scores. English evidence is separate. Expect credentials, statements, two references and a possible Zoom interview.

Forté offers **up to US$20,000 toward tuition**, with all nationalities and gender identities eligible for consideration under the [programme FAQ](https://giesonline.illinois.edu/explore-programs/online-mba/faqs). The Gies–Coursera Scholarship advertises 70% of tuition, but complete Nigerian eligibility, fee coverage and stacking are not established. Do not budget it as an entitlement or apply the percentage blindly to tuition plus fees.

Funding applications follow the degree application. General guidance says early April for Fall, early September for Spring and no Summer scholarships; exact autumn 2027 dates are unconfirmed. The September 3, 2026 Spring scholarship-priority date has passed. March 2027 entry has a January 14, 2027 final application deadline, **not a reopened funding deadline**. January entry's November 5, 2026 deadline has also passed.

No campus trip is mandatory. But online does not mean self-paced: expect live sessions, team projects and 10–15 hours per class each week, or roughly 20–30 hours for two classes. The [curriculum](https://giesonline.illinois.edu/explore-programs/online-mba/curriculum) offers focus/elective choices alongside its required cores. Financial Management is a core, not an optional finance concentration. Test this workload against your actual bank hours before paying.

### BU: cheaper sticker price, but no institutional online scholarship

[Boston University's Online MBA admissions page](https://www.bu.edu/questrom/graduate-programs/mba-programs/online-mba/admissions/) advertises US$25,000 total for Fall 2026 starters, subject to change. The 2027 price is unconfirmed. Use that stated total, not six times the rounded US$4,167 module advertisement. Application costs US$125 before any waiver. Books/materials are recommended at US$350–450 per semester; six terms give US$2,100–2,700, with US$2,400 used here.

The [programme FAQ](https://www.bu.edu/questrom/graduate-programs/mba-programs/online-mba/) says institutional Online MBA scholarships are unavailable. US federal loans are not assumed available to this Lagos-based Nigerian. GMAT/GRE is optional, but quantitative aptitude and a bachelor's degree or equivalent still matter. There is no hard work-experience minimum; successful candidates typically have at least six years.

The [academic format](https://www.bu.edu/questrom/graduate-programs/mba-programs/online-mba/academics/) is 45 credits in six integrated modules, plus required zero-credit Module 0. Consecutive study takes 24 months; planned breaks can extend completion up to six years. Modules cannot run concurrently. Allow 15–20 hours a week. Live sessions are about two hours at 7am or 8pm Eastern Time; some attendance is required and team-assigned, so switching freely is not assured. There is no mandatory campus attendance. Check assigned times against Lagos work commitments.

### Keep debt and currency risk in the decision

There is no verified loan offer for this applicant. Any lender must accept the exact school, programme, nationality and residence, with written currency, interest, fees and disbursement terms. Canadian domestic aid and US federal aid are not assumed.

An illustrative **US$40,000 loan at 12% APR over 10 years** costs about US$574/month, before fees or study-period capitalised interest. That is roughly NGN 772,904/month, or 66% of current gross monthly earnings at the reference rate. Net pay and living expenses make repayment tighter. This is a stress example, not a lender quote.

The FX anchors are November 6, 2026: [CBN NFEM](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/rates/exchratebycurrency.html) NGN 1,346.7945/US$, [Bank of Canada USD/CAD](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/valet/observations/FXUSDCAD/json?start_date=2026-11-06&end_date=2026-11-06) C$1.4113/US$, and [GBP/CAD](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/valet/observations/FXGBPCAD/json?start_date=2026-11-06&end_date=2026-11-06) C$1.8857/£. Thus £1 is about US$1.336144, and US$25,000 is £18,710.56 or C$35,282.50. These are reference conversions, not executable bank quotes.

NGN 14 million/year is about US$10,395. The US$25,000 target is NGN 33,669,862.50. A 20% rise in NGN per dollar makes it NGN 40,403,835, an extra NGN 6,733,972.50. That is not the same as a 20% fall in the naira's dollar value. Savings already held in USD avoid that conversion risk for that portion, but not GBP/USD or CAD/USD movements. A 10% rise in those currencies' USD cost raises the overseas model's dollar cost by 10%.

## Treat work permission as an option, not a job offer

### UK: plan on 18 months under the dated rules

The [Graduate visa](https://www.gov.uk/graduate-visa) lasts two years for applications on or before December 31, 2026, but 18 months from January 1, 2027. An autumn 2027 starter would normally finish in 2028, so this plan uses **18 months**, not two years. Complete an eligible course with Student permission, meet UK-study conditions, remain in the UK and apply before permission expires after the sponsor reports completion. The route is not automatic and cannot be extended; a longer stay needs another eligible route.

[Outside-London financial evidence](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa/money) is unpaid first-year CAS tuition plus 9 × £1,171 = £10,539 maintenance, normally held for 28 consecutive days with evidence ending within 31 days of application. This is proof of funding, **not extra living spend** and not a full-year allowance. The savings leave only about £8,172 for tuition after that floor, before other costs.

Post-study application/IHS costs are outside the study budget: at these rates, £937 + 1.5 × £1,035 = £2,489.50, plus job-search living. Recheck before applying.

### Canada: a shorter master's can still support a three-year PGWP

[McMaster's DLI listing](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit/prepare/designated-learning-institutions-list.html), O19395535729, includes DeGroote/Burlington, but listing alone does not guarantee every programme or applicant qualifies. Under the [master's duration rule](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/about.html), effective February 15, 2024, an eligible master's of at least eight months can support a three-year PGWP even below two years. Do not pay for a longer course solely for that potential duration.

[Eligibility conditions](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/eligibility.html) include normally full-time study, a final-semester exception, at least 50% in class in Canada under the relevant rules, application within 180 days and CLB/NCLC 7 in all four language skills. Master's graduates have no field-of-study restriction. An admissions English waiver does not waive PGWP language evidence. Passport expiry can shorten permission; PGWP is not permanent residence or a job guarantee.

[Living proof](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit/get-documents/financial-support.html) for one person outside Quebec is C$23,448/year from September 1, 2026, excluding tuition and travel and updated annually. Savings leave C$11,834.50 after that floor for first-year tuition and travel. Show first-year resources and a credible whole-programme plan; the actual first-year tuition instalment is unconfirmed. Do not add the floor again to the C$50,000 living budget. [Permit fees](https://ircc.canada.ca/english/information/fees/fees.asp) are C$150 for study, C$85 biometrics if required, and C$255 for the later PGWP, the last excluded from study costs.

Both US online MBAs studied from Lagos create **neither UK Graduate nor Canadian PGWP eligibility**. Gies does not sponsor a US-stay visa. No overseas Student visa or IHS is needed to remain in Lagos; optional visits have separate entry rules. Recheck immigration rules before any 2027 application and again before graduation.

## Do not use salary marketing as your repayment plan

Birmingham and Sheffield have no public official MBA cohort employment rate or median salary established here. Careers support and individual alumni stories do not fill that gap.

The salary footnote concerns November 23, 2023 and June 13, 2024 conferrals. Reliable employment information covered 85%; usable salary information covered only 23% of graduates accepting jobs within six months. There is no median, base-versus-total-compensation definition or standard-non-co-op-only split. Its 28% financial-services destination figure belongs to a separate 2025 cohort, not a 2025 finance salary result.

[Gies' online outcomes](https://giesonline.illinois.edu/explore-programs/online-mba/career-impact) claim 25% average pay increase and 65% promotion, offer or new role during study. Cohort dates, sample, response rate, geographic mix and a Nigerian median are not supplied. Its [online career resources](https://onlinestudents.giesbusiness.illinois.edu/student-resources/career-services) include GiesConnect, Big Interview, Handshake and VMock, with VMock available for one year after graduation. They do not establish residential-style placement or individual coaching, nor a blanket exclusion from all campus services.

It does not identify mean/median or the sample, response rate and geography needed for this decision. Its 79% offer/promotion/pay-rise and 95% skills-use claims concern inaugural-class respondents, a different cohort. This is not a starting salary, incremental gain or Lagos forecast. Gies states AACSB accreditation on its university page; [AACSB lists Boston University](https://www.aacsb.edu/accredited/b/boston-university). - **November 2026:** establish degree equivalence, management evidence, English requirements and the target role. Discuss study flexibility and employer support. If DeGroote is genuinely fundable, prepare for November 25, 2026; do not rush into unaffordable study to meet a round.
- **December 2026–January 2027:** prepare UK applications early enough for funding. Birmingham needs an offer before its scholarship application. Gies' March alternative closes January 14, 2027, without reopening Spring funding.
- **February–March 2027:** BU closes rounds on February 3 and March 10, 2027; DeGroote's international deadline is March 14, 2027. Birmingham's final scholarship window is March 2027, day unknown. Retain a test fallback where a waiver is discretionary.
- **April–May 2027:** compare written awards, exact fees, live schedules and the whole cash plan. Verify Gies' Fall funding day rather than assuming early-April guidance is a fixed 2027 date. BU's final Fall round closes May 5, 2027.

Secure funding evidence, passport, medical/TB requirements and CAS/LOA; UK applications from abroad can begin up to six months before study. Confirm teaching location, housing and actual permission dates. For online study, test power backup, connectivity and assigned live-team times before a non-refundable payment.

Choose Gies over BU if confirmed funding, curriculum choices and workload fit. Choose a UK year only if a new labour market is central to your goal and the whole reserve-preserving gap is affordably funded. ## FAQ

### Does US$25,000 pay for an online MBA?

It matches BU's published Fall 2026 tuition, not its whole education budget or a confirmed 2027 bill. Keeping US$5,000 aside leaves a larger funding gap. Lagos rent, food and tax still come from earnings.

### Do six years in banking remove the tests and management checks?

No. Birmingham makes GMAT optional, Sheffield lists no GMAT/GRE requirement, and the online schools make scores optional with their own conditions. DeGroote's waiver is discretionary and academic-record based. Management evidence and English requirements remain separate.

### Is a 20-month Canadian MBA too short for a three-year work permit?

Not solely because of length under these rules. An eligible master's of at least eight months can qualify, subject to programme and personal conditions. That is permission to work, not employment or permanent residence.

### Should a scholarship offer make me choose abroad?

Only after the remaining cash gap, personal reserve and realistic repayment plan work. Even the illustrated maximum awards leave substantial overseas gaps. Do not fund those gaps with salary expectations the outcome evidence cannot support.
