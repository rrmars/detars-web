## Keep the marriage simple

The [WA government Registry route](https://www.wa.gov.au/service/justice/civil-law/marriages), updated September 18, 2026, costs **AUD 517**: AUD 205 notice, AUD 252 ceremony and AUD 60 certified certificate. Lodge notice and original identification at least one month before the Australian ceremony. Bring two witnesses aged 18+; the Registry does not provide them. Ceremonies are weekdays excluding public holidays. This is the government Perth Registry Office, not the similarly named private Perth Marriage Office. Future fees and appointments are not locked.

For an illustrative legal ceremony on September 1, 2027, lodge the ordinary NOIM by August 1, 2027 at the latest—and preferably earlier. Complete the legal marriage before travel. Tell Bali suppliers that the celebration is symbolic and use your Australian legal date. Do not apply as though still unmarried.

A lawful Bali marriage can be recognised in Australia. [DFAT's overseas-marriage guidance](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/planning-trip/advice-your-planned-activities/getting-married-overseas), updated August 18, 2026, says recognition normally depends on validity where the marriage occurred and under Australian law. You cannot re-register an overseas marriage in Australia; that does not mean it is unrecognised. Keep official marriage evidence and any necessary translation. A blessing certificate alone is not a legal marriage certificate.

### If the actual legal marriage must be in Bali

**Indonesian marriage-law text and a district-specific foreign–foreign checklist are not verified here.** The practical requirements below come from [Australian Embassy guidance](https://indonesia.embassy.gov.au/jakt/marriageind.html), not an Indonesian-issued legal text. Confirm the exact district, religious pathway, document acceptance and physical arrival requirements before any non-refundable legal-wedding commitment.

- Embassy guidance says both partners must hold the same religion. This is not a secular celebrant-only shortcut; do not treat conversion as paperwork. Your circumstances must fit the local registrar or KUA and religious officiant.
- Muslim marriages use KUA and Buku Nikah. Non-Muslim couples generally give Civil Registry notice at least **10 days before**, then register the religious marriage. This is **not proof of a mandatory 10-day stay** for both people. Remote notice, waivers, arrival timing and certificate delivery remain unresolved.
- Prepare passports, birth certificates, a Certificate of No Impediment (CNI) and four 4×6 cm couple photographs, with originals and copies as required. No divorce or death documents apply to this never-married couple. The generic Embassy list also says “marriage certificate”; ask what that means for you rather than inventing a document. Confirm status/religious evidence, witnesses, translations and legalisation.
- Use the locally acceptable CNI issuer—normally the Bali Consulate-General for Bali. [DFAT's CNI process](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/our-role/notarial-services/how-get-certificate-no-impediment-marriage), updated October 1, 2026, allows other issuing locations, but that does not establish Bali acceptance of a Perth-issued CNI. Sign only with an authorised witness. Incomplete documents or authenticity checks can delay processing; usual while-you-wait issue is not instant appointment availability.
- The Embassy gives no CNI expiry, but says registries normally reject one issued more than **3 months** earlier. For an illustrative September 25, 2027 wedding, that boundary is June 25, 2027. Non-Muslim notice would be by September 15, 2027, subject to local counting and acceptance—not a verified arrival deadline.
- Complete the applicable religious and official registration steps. Arrange the actual certificate or Buku Nikah and any translation before leaving. The Australian Embassy cannot conduct the wedding.

The [June 2026 consular schedule](https://bali.indonesia.embassy.gov.au/blli/Fees.html) lists CNI issue at **AUD 181 or IDR 2,232,000 per service**. Reserving two fees, **AUD 362**, is provisional; certificate count is unconfirmed. These official currency tariffs are not the budget's assumed FX. [Bali Celebrant's packages](https://balicelebrant.com/ceremony-packages) list Christian legal services at IDR 6.5m or IDR 7.5m nett, plus IDR 0.5m priest-meeting transport, excluding venue and consular charges. **They expire December 31, 2026**, as does its IDR 3m nett symbolic rate. Local fees, translations, extra travel/stays and future increases remain unconfirmed. DFAT fees rise each January. Do not add a second legal-marriage route to the Perth-first budget.

## FAQ

### Is the resort wedding safely under the ceiling?

Not yet. Its small margin depends on written future pricing, the covered seated backup, drinks, furniture/service/power and supplier compatibility. Accurate arithmetic cannot make an unquoted cap sufficient.

### Can guests paying their rooms make the villa budget cheaper?

Only with an explicit cost-sharing and shortfall agreement. Until then, compulsory stays remain the couple's wedding commitment, even if guests pay their ordinary holidays.

### Does a symbolic Bali ceremony mean the venue must accept us?

No. It simplifies the legal-marriage route, not the supplier's policies. Get acceptance of your circumstances and ceremony in writing before paying.
