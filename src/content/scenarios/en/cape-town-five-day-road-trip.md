**Want the coast, penguins and wine country without spending the holiday worrying about the car? Stay in Green Point, use rides for city hops and shared coaches for the Peninsula and Franschhoek. For two adults from Frankfurt, the working total is R29,004 / €1,574.01 excluding flights. That leaves only R478.88 / €25.99 below €1,600, so confirm the room before committing to extras.**

This plan assumes ordinary German passports: arrive on Saturday, February 13, 2027 in the morning and leave on Thursday, February 18, 2027 in the evening. It gives you five sightseeing days, five nights and a gentle departure day. Chinese translation makes the same trip accessible; it does not change passport eligibility. Nothing is booked or arranged.

Prices were checked on November 6, 2026, Asia/Singapore; retrievals were late November 5, 2026 UTC and in Cape Town. Published prices below are dated examples, not February offers. Transfers, driving times, food, app rides, rental and insurance are planning estimates. Add traffic, queues and stops. R means South African rand, ZAR. Conversion uses **€1 = R18.4268**, the [ECB reference rate](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/eurofxref/eurofxref-daily.xml) dated November 5, 2026, not a February forecast or card rate.

## Keep the mountain flexible, not the whole week

Use the first good Table Mountain operating window. Sunday and Wednesday can swap; wind may stop the cableway even when the city looks sunny. Do not leave your only attempt until departure day, or replace a closure with an unplanned hike. The [cableway weather information](https://www.tablemountain.net/plan-your-visit/ticket-prices) matters more than a fixed sightseeing order. Keep pre-arranged Monday and Tuesday tours fixed unless a change is actually available.

Published validity is July 1, 2026–June 30, 2027. Standard tickets still queue; no Fast Track is included.

At Camps Bay, bring sunscreen, water and only what you need. Take turns entering the water so bags are not abandoned. This is not a promise of warm or safe swimming: follow lifeguards and flags, and skip swimming in poor conditions. ## Peninsula day: separate the coach from the gates

The [City Sightseeing Cape Point & Boulders Beach Penguin Tour](https://citysightseeing.co.za/en/cape-town/cape-point-penguin-explorer-tour) travels via Ou Kaapse Weg, Kommetjie and Scarborough, then via Miller's Point to Boulders near Simon's Town. It includes an English-speaking guide, air-conditioned return coach and designated-stop pickups near selected hotels; do not assume door-to-door collection. Allow an estimated 1¾–2¼ hours from central Cape Town to Cape Point on this route, 40–55 minutes from its visitor area to Boulders, and 60–90 minutes back to Green Point, before extra stops and traffic. The operator allows over two hours at Cape Point and about one hour at Boulders.

[Cape Tourism's secondary report](https://www.capetourism.com/sanparks-conservation-fees-2026-27/), published October 11, 2026, instead gives R592 Cape Point and R282 Boulders for the same period. The [official SANParks tariff PDF](https://www.sanparks.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/daily_conservation_fees_2026_27.pdf) was blocked by a verification page and was not inspected. Budget the higher amount, but verify the applicable official gate tariff before payment; do not reuse older prices.

The operator also displayed a Cape Point-entry-included option at R1,167 per adult. Its R592 uplift over the base ticket does not independently verify the gate fee. Two such tickets plus the reported Boulders fee would be R2,898, versus R2,904 in this plan. **Choose one ticket basis: do not add Cape Point again to an entry-included ticket.** Boulders, funicular and food remain extra. [SANParks payment information](https://www.sanparks.org/parks/table-mountain/rates-entry-fees) confirms both gates are cash-free, accepting debit or credit cards. Resident and SADC discounts require qualifying proof; this budget uses international adults.

Skip the funicular in this budget. Use the uphill paved walk only if comfortable, or enjoy lower viewpoints; the longer guided hike is optional. Keep food packed away around baboons and give penguins space.

## Wine day: buy the return journey, not just a tram ride

Choose the [City Sightseeing Franschhoek Wine Tram package](https://citysightseeing.co.za/en/cape-town/cape-explorer-tram-tour): **R825 per adult, R1,650 for two (€89.54)**. Its published offer includes Cape Town return coach transport, tram/tram-bus line ticket, local guide and a tasting on the tram—not estate tastings or lunch. Advance booking is required and the ticket is valid only on the selected date. February prices, seats, line and estates remain unconfirmed.

Green Point to Franschhoek is an estimated 75–100 minutes each way, up to two hours with collection stops and traffic. Follow the actual included line, check the estate sequence and connect with the fixed return coach. The operator suggests four estates, but two relaxed estate tastings plus lunch fit this gentler plan. Stops are generally hourly and a large estate can warrant two hours or more; don't improvise a late return.

Allow **R800 for estate tastings**: two people × two tastings × R200, an estimate. [Wine Tram's standalone pricing](https://winetram.co.za/hop-on-hop-off/) describes R60–260 per person for estate tastings; premium pairings may cost more. Lunch comes from meals. Standalone tram is **R370 per adult, R740 for two (€40.16)**, without Cape Town transfers: do not add it on top of the package.

The [Wine Tram FAQ](https://winetram.co.za/faq/) warns that Uber gets difficult after 17:00. It describes transfer pickup normally 1½–2 hours before departure, depending on location; confirm your package timetable rather than treating that as your pickup. A [separate operator transfer](https://winetram.co.za/transfers/) exists but no public price was established. The inclusive coach is the chosen return plan. Stellenbosch is a good alternative for town wandering, but not an extra stop in this wine day.

## Why the chosen plan has no hire car

The transport/package total is **R5,800 (€314.76)**: airport transfers R1,200 + city rides R1,800 + Peninsula coach R1,150 + wine coach/tram R1,650. Cableway, gates and estate tastings stay separate. Tour fares are dated published prices; rides and transfers are allowances. You share stops and departure times, but neither traveller navigates after a long flight or has to skip wine to drive.

A small automatic is an alternative, not necessarily a saving: estimated R650–950/day, plus R1,000–1,500 fuel and R400–700 parking/tolls. Saturday morning collection to Thursday afternoon return normally spans **six 24-hour billing periods**, not five rental days. That gives R5,300–7,900 (€287.62–428.72) before extra insurance and deposit/excess; no rental supplier quote or availability was established. Left-side driving, parking and theft precautions become yours, and wine still needs a sober driver or paid transfer. The [German Foreign Office](https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/de/reiseundsicherheit/suedafrikasicherheit-208400) requires an international driving permit together with the German licence; verify rental card, deposit, cover and excess terms.

App rides suit short city trips, but fares surge and pickups fail. For the whole Peninsula, arrange a driver who waits or a priced full day—not remote one-way drop-offs. A private day driver needs a fresh quote and is not hidden in this budget. The airport transfer is a recommendation, not an arrangement made for you.

## Entry: German passports, not simply German residence

These notes are for adult tourists on ordinary German passports, not other nationalities living in Germany. The [South African Embassy in Berlin](https://www.suedafrika.org/en/visa-immigration/visas-short-stay-not-exceeding-3-months) confirms holiday visa exemption for up to **90 days**. Border admission and the permitted stay are still the officer's decision: check the stamp. This is not work permission; the embassy describes advance section 11(2) authorisation for work.

Bring passports valid at least **30 days after intended departure** with **two unused pages**, plus return flight and accommodation details. For departure February 18, 2027, minimum validity is March 20, 2027. [DIRCO's passport requirements](https://dirco.gov.za/washingtondc/visa-application/) are the relevant part of that page; its separate nationality advice is not the German exemption. German Foreign Office advice at the check said eTA was **not compulsory for German nationals**. Recheck the embassy, Foreign Office and airline about two weeks before travel; do not assume future changes already apply.

Customs is separate from visa or eTA. The [SARS announcement](https://www.sars.gov.za/latest-news/required-online-traveller-declarations-from-1-july-2026/), dated June 29, 2026, says online traveller declarations are required before entering **and leaving** from July 1, 2026, with limited paper exceptions. Each traveller should complete the applicable [traveller declaration](https://www.sars.gov.za/travellerdeclaration/) and retain confirmation; nothing has been submitted for you. If connecting through a yellow-fever-risk country, check health-document rules for that exact route. No connection details were supplied.

## Calm precautions, and a dated power picture

Official safety advice says violent crime can affect tourist areas, especially after dark. Keep precautions routine rather than treating any neighbourhood or tour as risk-free:

- Use a trusted airport transfer and discuss the main-road route first: N2 / M3 as applicable, Airport Approach Road via N2 exit 16. Avoid R300 and Borcherd's Quarry / Nyanga shortcuts.
- Book rides inside the official app, wait indoors, match driver and registration, and share the trip. Do not accept an airport tout's substitute.
- Ride after dark. Keep phones and jewellery discreet, leave no valuables in vehicles and avoid deserted beaches.
- Use the cableway, not an isolated couple's hike. If hiking is important, use a registered guide and group: FCDO advises four or more on busy marked trails.
- If robbed, do not resist. Save **112** for mobile emergencies, **10111** for police and the guesthouse number.

Do not build the holiday around old blackout headlines. [Eskom's October 30, 2026 statement](https://www.eskom.co.za/generation-recovery-plan-sustains-eskom-reaching-a-68-15-eaf-and-diesel-cost-reduction-of-r4-81-billion-year-on-year/) reported **532 consecutive days without national load-shedding since May 16, 2025**. It was the latest system statement located at that check; the next update was due November 6, 2026. That is not a current guarantee for February or your street. Local faults remain possible. Confirm whether guesthouse backup runs lights, Wi-Fi and sockets, not necessarily air-conditioning, and bring a charged power bank. Recheck Eskom and Cape Town electricity notices before flying.
