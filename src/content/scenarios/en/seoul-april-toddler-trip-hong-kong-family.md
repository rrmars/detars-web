You want a Seoul trip that does not fall apart at 14:00 because a tired two-year-old has no bed. **Stay in one place, plan one real outing a day, and treat cherry blossoms as a bonus.**

This plan is for **two Hong Kong parents, a 2.5-year-old and one familiar stroller**: four nights, three full sightseeing days, **8–12 April 2027** (Thursday to Monday), a budget of **HKD 18,000 excluding flights**, and a room nap at 13:00–15:00. It assumes your daughter is still under 36 months on the trip. Check her actual date of birth.

The source materials are not published. Prices below are public examples read on 7 November 2026, not availability for your dates. Rules, opening calendars and prices need rechecking closer to travel.

## Short answer: Jamsil, if the room fits the cap

**Choose one family room near Lotte World Mall and Seokchon Lake for all four nights.** LOTTE HOTEL WORLD is the strongest family lead we found: it has a real 3–4-person room promotion and a hotel-guests-only kids lounge for ages 2–7. It is a shortlist, not a booking recommendation. We did not check April availability. [H2](https://app.lottehotel.com/world-hotel/en/offers/packages/260101/family-world)

A short outing followed by a proper hotel nap matters more for you than being “central to everything”. Jamsil keeps the aquarium, indoor meals and the lake in one cluster. The cost is a longer, less straightforward rail ride from the airport and fewer central sights. **That is our planning judgment, not a claim that every pavement or mall connection is step-free.**

There are two decision gates:

- **Room price.** Keep the room at or below KRW 350,000 per night, including taxes and all three guests. No verified April quote was found. If you cannot, switch the whole stay to Myeongdong and redesign the days around central Seoul. Do not commute across the city from Jamsil every day.
- **K-ETA for the parents.** The adult K-ETA waiver currently ends on 31 December 2026. **April 2027 is not confirmed exempt.** [E1](https://www.k-eta.go.kr/portal/board/viewboarddetail.do?bbsSn=299707&locale=EN)

## Entry: three separate questions

**Check all three actual passports.** The currently published visa-free tourist allowance for HKSAR passports is 90 days. Your Hong Kong residence is not what gives you that right. Check passport validity, return or onward travel and accommodation details against the live official requirements; we assume no “six-month rule”. Admission stays discretionary. [E0](https://www.k-eta.go.kr/portal/guide/viewetaalification.do)

**Parents: plan for K-ETA unless the official rule changes.** Hong Kong is covered by the temporary waiver through 31 December 2026 only, and **a 2027 extension was unconfirmed on 7 November 2026.** Check official notices well before departure, and check the passport and expiry of any existing approval. Approvals issued after 3 July 2023 currently last three years or until passport expiry, whichever is sooner. Your own age exemptions, if any, are unknown. [E1](https://www.k-eta.go.kr/portal/board/viewboarddetail.do?bbsSn=299707&locale=EN)

**Your daughter has a separate K-ETA exemption.** Visa-free travellers aged 17 or younger are currently exempt, independently of the temporary country waiver. Do not buy an unnecessary paid application for her because the parents need one. [E2](https://www.k-eta.go.kr/portal/board/viewboarddetail.do?bbsSn=175270&locale=EN)

**The arrival declaration still applies to the child.** Current rules cover K-ETA-exempt travellers and exclude valid K-ETA holders. A parent submits the child's details. If the adult waiver is extended and you use it, expect to file declarations for the parents too. Filing is electronic and free, opens three days before arrival (Korea time) and expires 72 hours after submission, so use the official portal close to travel. Do not assume a paper alternative will exist in April 2027. [E3](https://www.e-arrivalcard.go.kr/portal/guide/eacTargetGuide.do)

Have ready, but do not submit now: passports, accommodation address and contact, itinerary and return details, insurance, and a copy of the child's birth-date evidence for age-based tickets. The K-ETA fee is currently KRW 10,000 per person plus processing of generally about 3%, and it is nonrefundable. Our conditional allowance for both adults is 2 × 10,000 × 1.03 = KRW 20,600. **That is not a 2027 fee promise.** [E5](https://www.k-eta.go.kr/portal/guide/viewetafeeinformation.do)

**Visa-free is not the same as K-ETA-free, and neither is the same as arrival-card-free.** Recheck in early 2027 and again shortly before you travel.

## Blossoms: a bonus, not the point of the trip

**8–12 April can be late.** The Korea Meteorological Administration recorded Seoul and Yeouido first bloom on 29 March 2026, and first bloom is not peak. Year-to-year variation is large, and no 2027 flowering forecast or festival calendar was verified. Warm weather, rain and wind can leave little by these dates. Keep your hotel and activity choices worthwhile without flowers. [B1](https://www.kma.go.kr/kma/news/press.jsp?bid=press&mode=view&num=1194625)

**Seokchon Lake, Jamsil.** The best low-commitment attempt from this base: a short lakeside segment, not an obligatory full loop. Official indexed tourism text lists free, year-round, 24-hour access and accessible paths, but **its live page returned a 404, so current access is only partly verified and the route is not audited.** Expect pinch points and busy festival and photo areas. Turn back rather than fight a stroller through.

**Yeouiseo-ro, Yeouido.** The classic blossom road beside the National Assembly. Treat it as an optional cross-city substitute, not an addition to a Jamsil day with fixed hotel naps. If you go, go early on a weekday; that manages crowd risk but does not guarantee quiet paths. Drop it if the flowers are over or access controls make the stroller impractical.

The official 2026 precedent was Yeouido 3–7 April and Seokchon 3–11 April. **These are not 2027 dates.** Event stages, food and photo zones justify a crowd warning, not a numerical crowd prediction. As a planning judgment, Friday 9 April is better than the weekend for a first attempt. Check live bloom observations and official 2027 notices in late March, and do not chase blossoms across Seoul at the expense of naps. [B2](https://scpm.seoul.go.kr/seoul-policy/evt0254)

## Five days, three full days, one nap anchor

The suggested rhythm: breakfast 08:00, morning outing 09:00–11:15, lunch 11:30, back in the room by 12:30, nap 13:00–15:00, one short afternoon outing, dinner 17:30 and normal bedtime. Follow your daughter's usual sleep rather than forcing this clock. Korea is one hour ahead of Hong Kong. **All timings are planning allowances, not checked transit timetables.**

### Thursday 8 April: arrive and recover

Airport, Jamsil, check-in, a simple meal and supplies. No paid attraction and no blossom deadline. WORLD publishes 15:00 check-in and 11:00 check-out; **early room access is not guaranteed.** If you land early, protect sleep with your familiar stroller in a suitable supervised setting. An early check-in or day-room charge would need a new quote and a budget adjustment. Keep the day flexible around the actual flight. [H2](https://app.lottehotel.com/world-hotel/en/offers/packages/260101/family-world)

### Friday 9 April: lake and aquarium, with a real break

09:00–10:15 a short Seokchon blossom look if conditions are good. Lunch nearby and back in the room by 12:30. 13:00–15:00 nap. 15:30–17:00 Lotte World Aquarium, **only after its prices, toddler terms and actual hours are verified later;** leave if she tires. Otherwise replace the aquarium with COEX, preferably 10:00–11:30 with an early start from Jamsil, a return-to-nap buffer and an exit solution for the stroller, and drop the lake that morning. COEX has no re-entry. There is no “must see every tank”, and do not assume Lotte allows re-entry. In rain, choose the one indoor outing over the lake.

### Saturday 10 April: a deliberately light local day

09:00–11:00 another short local outdoor walk, or the hotel kids lounge if you are staying at WORLD; no special event is promised. Lunch, then a 13:00–15:00 room nap. In the afternoon, hotel play or a quiet mall browse and an early dinner. A private play café is an optional replacement once its branch, toddler rules and price are checked, not a second compulsory ticket. Avoid the busiest lake hours.

### Sunday 11 April: one museum outing, only if access is secured

10:00–11:15 Seoul Children's Museum toddler area; aim to arrive earlier for entry procedures. Plan the rail route and leave Jamsil with plenty of lift and transfer slack. Leave the museum at 11:15, aim to be at the hotel by 12:30 and nap 13:00–15:00. **This is a test of the route, not a guaranteed travel time:** if the route does not fit, keep the day local. Secure timed access later if available, because limited walk-up places can sell out. The afternoon stays local. The free National Museum children's museum is not an interchangeable walk-in backup.

### Monday 12 April: depart, not a museum day

Pack, have breakfast and check out by the applicable hotel time; leave airport margin based on your flight, terminal and mode. No Monday museum plan. A late departure needs a realistic supervised stroller nap or a separately costed late room; **luggage storage is not a sleeping room.** Do not use up the airport buffer for one last blossom stop.

**Optional adult-centred swap:** if Yeouido or central Seoul is a priority, replace the whole Sunday museum outing, check the return-to-nap route and abandon the swap if timing fails. Do not stitch Myeongdong, palaces, Yeouido and Jamsil into one day.

## A full budget, with room for uncertainty

The budget convention is **HKD 1 = KRW 170**. It is not a live exchange rate or a forecast. HKD 18,000 × 170 = KRW 3,060,000. Divide any KRW figure by 170 for HKD, shown to two decimals. Bank and card spreads and April rates can differ.

**All lines except the conditional K-ETA calculation are planning allowances, not quoted prices.** No breakfast inclusion or free child supplement is assumed. **Admissions.** Take the evidenced COEX normal two-adult example, KRW 70,000, plus the conditional museum family figure, KRW 8,000, and you get KRW 78,000. That leaves KRW 42,000 inside the KRW 120,000 envelope. Lotte's family ticket must be repriced later; it is not verified at 70,000. Add a Pororo family visit at KRW 45,000 and the current examples total KRW 123,000, which is KRW 3,000 above the envelope: replace another activity or use headroom on purpose. With the current COEX two-adult offer, if you qualify, the total would be 53,000 + 8,000 + 45,000 = KRW 106,000. **No discount is assumed in the baseline.** If the museum rejects foreign age proof, its actual child charge is unresolved; use the reserve rather than promise free entry.

**Stress tests.** If the four room nights average KRW 400,000, add KRW 200,000: the total becomes KRW 3,080,600, which is KRW 20,600 over your limit. If the exchange rate is only KRW 160 per HKD, the same plan costs HKD 18,003.75 (2,880,600 ÷ 160), slightly over. **Do not treat the contingency as permission to ignore exchange risk.**

The budget excludes flights, as you asked. No shopping, special dining splurge, extra hotel day, late checkout or mini bed is silently included. Any of those has to fit the reserves or replace something else. Keep the contingency unspent where you can. Adding the rounded HKD lines can differ by a cent from dividing the exact KRW total; the total is converted once from KRW.
