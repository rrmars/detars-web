You can plan an Osaka move without assuming you must already have a Japanese bank account to rent. **Start by looking for a landlord-approved no-bank payment route, not by rushing to open an account with a temporary address.** For Linh, a Vietnamese software engineer with N4 Japanese, an Umeda office and an April 1, 2027 job start, the sensible default is a 1K in Juso, Awaji or Tenjinbashisuji 6-chome, usually called Tenroku. If a separate living room matters, screen Awaji first for a 1LDK.

Set aside **¥1,250,000 for the 1K model or ¥1,450,000 for the 1LDK model** in accessible relocation funds. These are rounded planning targets, not quotes or approval thresholds. The model assumes arrival on March 18, 2027, ten temporary nights and permanent move-in on March 28, 2027. If housing or immigration is not ready, extend suitable accommodation and recost the delay rather than force a lease.

Linh's stated route is Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services. Her actual visa, landing permission and rental approval have not been inspected. The Chinese version explains the same Vietnamese applicant; it does not change nationality or eligibility. All prices below are JPY examples inspected on November 7, 2026, not refreshed offers for the move. No VND exchange rate is assumed.

## Choose the layout before ranking the neighbourhoods

Search around **¥60,000–80,000 base rent for a 1K** in Juso, Awaji or Tenroku. For an Awaji 1LDK, start around **¥80,000–100,000**. These are suggested search bands, not measured market ranges or confirmed stock. Esaka gives you a direct Midosuji-line alternative, but has the highest 1K benchmark of these four.

## Break the paperwork loop in a workable order

### Before departure: get permission and the payment plan straight

Ask the employer to coordinate the COE/work-status process. Prepare passport and valid visa/landing documents, signed employment contract or offer with pay and start date, employer contact, funds evidence, paper/digital copies and two consenting reachable emergency contacts. **A job start date is not permission to enter or work.** This guide does not verify Linh's COE or permission to enter/work, and her processing time and approval remain unknown. Confirm these with the employer and current official guidance before making nonrefundable commitments.

Secure either a GTN-accepted lease with usable interim transfers or suitable residential housing with confirmed card payment. GTN's published screening can precede residence-card issuance. If an owner insists on an existing registered address/account, use a genuinely suitable bridge rather than inventing an address or assuming a hotel can register you.

### Arrival: residence card and a personal phone can come before the bank

The [Immigration Services Agency](https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/publications/faq/newimmiact_4_port-city.html) lists Kansai among ports issuing cards to qualifying mid/long-term arrivals. At a non-issuing port, the passport is marked for later issuance; take it to the municipal procedure and follow delivery instructions. Check the status granted at landing.

A personal voice/SMS number can be a parallel task. [GTN Eazy](https://www.gtn-mobile.com/s/eazy?language=en_US) advertised 3GB at ¥1,200/month + ¥3,300 initial, tax included, before extras. The ¥650 teaser requires ¥550 of bundle discounts; do not budget it automatically. Domestic calls were ¥22/30 seconds and outgoing domestic SMS ¥3.3/message, with service and other extras separate. [Convenience-store cash billing](https://www.gtn-mobile.com/s/support/paying-bills?language=en_US) avoids a Japanese auto-debit requirement. [GTN features](https://www.gtn-mobile.com/s/features?language=en_US) advertise passport/residence-card routes and overseas/airport receipt, not guaranteed activation. The exact ID and address requirements, handset compatibility and screening for your chosen application route still need checking. Choose voice/SMS rather than a data-only travel eSIM. The ¥5,000 setup allowance already includes the ¥3,300 initial fee; do not add it again.

### Register where you really live, then use actual employment evidence for banking

Notify the correct municipality **within 14 days of establishing the residence**, not unconditionally within 14 days of landing. A later move needs a new notification within 14 days of moving. Bring the residence card or passport marked for later issuance, plus passport; confirm the local checklist and request a jūminhyō/certificate of residence when needed. Juso belongs to Yodogawa ward, Awaji to Higashiyodogawa, Tenroku to Kita, and Esaka to Suita City. Exact Suita documents and counter hours were not checked. [Osaka's registration guidance](https://www.city.osaka.lg.jp/contents/wdu020/enjoy/en/content_d.html) says an arrival from abroad does not require a moving-out certificate. Registration triggers My Number notification by post; the plastic My Number card is a separate application, not a prerequisite for starting housing arrangements.

**There is no universal six-month bank-account ban.** [PRESTIA's requirements](https://www.smbctb.co.jp/en/allied/openaccount/application.html) allow six months in Japan OR employment in Japan, for applicants age 18+, with resident registration, matching actual/ID address and at least three months left on the residence card. Online opening was suspended effective October 23, 2026. The branch-appointment route generally takes several business days; allow longer for review and check maintenance fees. A March offer is not proven equivalent to actual employment from April.

Ask the employer about its payroll bank first. [Japan Post's foreign-customer route](https://www.jp-bank.japanpost.jp/kaisetu/kat_gaikokujin.html) requires a residence card and rejects applications if its validity expires within three months of application. Vietnamese app support is available; a Japanese-speaking companion may be requested for branch explanations, useful to plan around N4 Japanese. Screening can take time or reject the application. [Card delivery](https://www.jp-bank.japanpost.jp/app/tetsuzuki/app_tz_ja-caution.html) is registered and non-forwardable; do not apply with an address you are about to leave.

Japan Post's listed under-six-month foreign-national nonresident category also requires **not working at an office in Japan**; it does not automatically cover every newcomer. Its [transfer page](https://www.jp-bank.japanpost.jp/kojin/sokin/gaitame/kj_sk_gt_index.html) announces changes from January 4, 2027. Recheck classification and payment restrictions for the move; current fees are not assumed to be April fees. A job offer alone does not prove the employment exception or unrestricted transfers.

After approval, give payroll the account and register rent debit. Confirm the first debit month and keep paying interim instructions until the switch is confirmed. Update the bank, phone provider and landlord after any address change. If a bridge cannot lawfully support registration and mail, it is lodging only: keep the no-bank rent-payment arrangement until you establish a genuine residence. No bank, phone or accommodation approval is promised.
