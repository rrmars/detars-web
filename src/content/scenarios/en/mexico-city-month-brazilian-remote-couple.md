It models **March 1–April 1, 2027: 31 nights**, subject to flight schedules. The aim is a furnished whole apartment, usable workstations for both, reliable internet and a few modest weekend outings—not a room in a shared apartment or a month of café-based work.

## Settle the entry route before relying on remote work

For Brazilian citizens arriving **by air**, the [Mexican Consulate in São Paulo](https://consulmex.sre.gob.mx/saopaulo/index.php/visto-menu/visto-para-turista) says electronic visas have been available since **February 5, 2026**. The [official eVisa guide](https://consulmex.sre.gob.mx/saopaulo/images/visaelectronica/guia_visa_electronica_v260210.pdf) gives **USD 10 per application**, **one entry**, and **180-day visa validity** if approved. Two applications cost **USD 20**. This is not the old free SAE authorisation. Use the [official eVisa portal](https://visaelectronica.sre.gob.mx/).

**Visa validity is not an automatic 180-day authorised stay or a guarantee of admission.** The visitor maximum is 180 days, but officers decide admission and the actual duration. Request the month needed and check the recorded departure deadline before leaving the airport. A single-entry eVisa does not permit a later re-entry after a side trip outside Mexico. It is the air-arrival route; do not assume it covers land or sea entry.

### Check exemptions individually, not as a couple

Each traveller may independently qualify through a **valid visa** from the US, Canada, Japan, UK or a Schengen country, or qualifying **permanent residence** there. The [INM visa-required guidance](https://www.inm.gob.mx/gobmx/word/index.php/paises-requieren-visa-para-mexico/) also lists permanent residence in **Chile, Colombia and Peru**, which São Paulo's summary omits. Verify the actual document if relying on that difference. An existing valid Mexican visa is another route. One partner's visa or an arbitrary temporary residence card does not exempt both people.

Prepare genuine passport and visa/exemption documents, return or onward flight, accommodation confirmation, Mexican address, duration and itinerary, usual residence, and employer/foreign-pay evidence. Officers may ask about purpose, funds, activities at home and in Mexico and departure plans. A Spanish employer letter is listed for relevant business travel. Funds evidence is prudent preparation, not a fabricated universal minimum bank balance. Passport validity must cover travel; the consular-application six-month condition is not established here as a universal entry rule. INM's [visa-exempt guidance](https://www.inm.gob.mx/gobmx/word/index.php/paises-no-requieren-visa-para-mexico/) also describes purpose checks. Under the air-arrival guidance read, you do not need to prefill or carry a paper FMM for an international-airport arrival.

### Being paid in Brazil does not settle permission

The [SRE eVisa announcement](https://www.gob.mx/sre/prensa/electronic-visa-for-brazilian-nationals?idiom=en) defines the category as **visitor without permission to perform paid activities**. [General visitor guidance](https://www.gob.mx/sre/acciones-y-programas/visa-de-visitante-sin-permiso-para-realizar-actividades-remuneradas) recognises certain business and technical activities without payment in Mexico. But it does **not expressly clear a month of routine online work for Brazilian employers on this eVisa**.

The express foreign-salary work provision in [temporary-residence guidance](https://www.gob.mx/sre/acciones-y-programas/visa-de-residencia-temporal) belongs to a different status; it cannot simply be transferred to visitors. That does not establish either blanket permission or blanket prohibition for your arrangement, nor does it prove temporary residence is suitable for a month.

Before relying on this plan, obtain competent classification confirmation using your actual duties, employer, payment source and local-market involvement. Do not hide the work or describe the eVisa as a digital-nomad permit. Employer approval and a truthful letter document facts; they do not replace correct status. Tax and employer payroll/security compliance are also unresolved. Brazilian-government Itamaraty pages could not be read substantively, so no corroboration from them is claimed. No authority or adviser was contacted.

## Keep arrival, water and emergency plans simple

### Ease into the altitude and budget safe water

[CDC's Mexico guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/yellow-book/hcp/americas-caribbean/mexico.html) describes Mexico City at **2,250 m**, with acclimatisation considerations, particularly for people with heart or lung conditions. Plan an easy arrival period, not a strenuous excursion or critical call immediately after landing. Seek pre-travel medical advice for relevant conditions.

CDC advises that Mexican tap water is unsafe to consume. Use sealed bottled or reliably purified water, check the apartment's drinking-water arrangement and follow food/water precautions; a sink filter alone is not verified protection. This is general US public-health guidance, not an assessment of your health or a test of the apartment's water. Obtain insurance for both people and the full travel dates; MXN 1,500 is only an allowance, not confirmed cover.

### Learn the building's earthquake plan at check-in

The [Mexico City Protección Civil guide](https://www.proteccioncivil.cdmx.gob.mx/storage/app/media/guia-para-saber-que-hacer-en-caso-de-sismo.pdf), also available from [Coyoacán government](https://coyoacan.cdmx.gob.mx/docs/guia_sismo.pdf), distinguishes an alert from shaking already underway. On an alert, evacuation may be possible using the pre-established route; high-floor occupants without time should use the identified lower-risk zone.
