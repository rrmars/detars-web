If you have IDR 95,000,000 for Korean-language study in Seoul, **it is not enough for this worked Yonsei KLI plan**. The modest outside-window goshiwon budget needs **KRW 12,283,000 / IDR 166,680,310**, leaving an **IDR 71,680,310 shortfall**. Before paying anything non-refundable, find real additional support or rebuild the plan around your actual school's written fees. A parent who supplies financial evidence does not automatically supply the money you will spend.

The example is Putri, 22, an Indonesian citizen with admission to an unspecified Seoul language institute and a visa still pending. She wants two 10-week terms, not a full academic year. The living-cost horizon is March–August 2027: arrival March 1, 2027 and checkout September 1, 2027, or 184 nights. Nationality-specific rules here apply to the Indonesian applicant; check the rules for your own nationality.

**Yonsei University Korean Language Institute (KLI), Regular Program, Sinchon is a named cost example—not Putri's confirmed institute or an all-school tariff.** The information below was read on November 7, 2026; future prices, rules and places still need confirmation. No part-time wages, scholarship, deposit refund or extra family funding are assumed.

## Separate spending money from financial proof

You need to pass both tests, not add a proof threshold as another expense.

| Money test | Amount or requirement | What it means for you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Putri's total resources | IDR 95,000,000 ÷ 13.57 = KRW 7,000,737, rounded | Before exchange and transfer charges; costs already paid from this pot remain part of it |
| Ordinary Seoul embassy financial proof | KRW 10,000,000, equivalent to IDR 135,700,000 at the model rate | Evidence of resources, potentially through an eligible parent sponsor; not a visa deposit or spending fee |
| Yonsei KLI admission proof | USD 10,000 or more | Separate school requirement; do not equate it with the embassy's KRW threshold |
| Worked cash, holds and reserves | KRW 12,283,000 / IDR 166,680,310 | Actual funding envelope, including tuition, flights and the full stay |

The [Korean embassy in Indonesia's D-4 guidance](https://sites.google.com/view/koreanembassy/장기-비자/일반연수d-4) asks ordinary Seoul applicants for the last 3 months' bank transactions; certain institutions require 6 months. **Transaction history is not proof of a continuously maintained minimum balance.** No continuous balance-holding period was established. School rules may be stricter.

[Yonsei KLI](https://www.yskli.com/course.php?mid=E01_01) asks for one original bank-balance certificate of at least USD 10,000, in the applicant's or a parent's name, issued within 30 days of document arrival; local-currency equivalent is accepted. Multiple-account aggregation or statements alone are not accepted. Indonesia is Group B: at least 2 terms, with transcript and family/birth evidence as well as common documents. Follow the institute's translation and authentication rules; original and translated Indonesian-language documents need authentication under its stated requirements.

My recommendation is roughly **IDR 167,000,000 of baseline funding capacity**, about **IDR 72,000,000 more**, plus independently adequate documented financial capacity. A sponsor may solve proof; only committed, accessible support solves the cash gap. If your actual school or scholarship changes the rows, recalculate rather than applying this total blindly.

All conversions use **KRW 1 = IDR 13.57**. This provisional rate comes from an indexed [Bank Indonesia table](https://www.bi.go.id/id/statistik/informasi-kurs/transaksi-bi/Default.aspx) updated November 6, 2026; its full page could not be verified. It is not an executable March exchange quote. Transfer charges and spreads are not separately quoted.

## Keep the whole six-month budget on one page

Yonsei's published 2027 regular fee is KRW 1,960,000 per term plus KRW 130,000 application, with books excluded. Each term is 10 weeks / 200 hours; two terms give 20 instruction weeks, not six months of complete living coverage. Tuition and application remain inside Putri's original resource envelope even if already paid. Do not count the same money again as remaining savings.

Monthly recurring costs are KRW 990,000 = 480,000 housing + 400,000 food + 80,000 transport + 30,000 phone. Six months gives KRW 5,940,000; other items, holds and reserves give KRW 6,343,000. Together: KRW 5,940,000 + 6,343,000 = 12,283,000. IDR conversions are rounded to whole rupiah.

The shortfall is IDR 166,680,310 − 95,000,000 = **IDR 71,680,310**, or KRW 5,282,263 rounded. Removing only the uncertain KRW 100,000 reservation hold still leaves IDR 165,323,310. Removing that hold, the NHIS boundary reserve and the whole KRW 500,000 emergency reserve leaves KRW 11,583,000 / IDR 157,181,310. That stripped-down version is still unaffordable and is not recommended.

## Get the Indonesian entry route right before paying

For eligible university-affiliated Korean-language study lasting 91 days or more with high-school graduation or higher, D-4-1 is the normal route. Private academies or excluded institutions do not automatically qualify. Admission is not visa approval. The embassy describes 3-month entry validity and a stay within 6 months; your actual grant and expiry control.

| Ordinary-case preparation | What to have ready | What not to assume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personal file | Signed form, passport with at least 6 months remaining at application, colour 3.5 × 4.5 cm photo, Kartu Keluarga, current student/employment status; latest graduation evidence if unemployed | [Common documents](https://sites.google.com/view/koreanembassy/참고사항/공통서류-안내) are a base list, not guaranteed approval |
| Institute file | Standard Certificate of Admission, school registration/unique-number certificate, detailed training plan | Admission certificate must be within 3-month validity when filed |
| Academic file | Latest education certificate, original and copy, issued within 6 months under the visa checklist; high-school evidence apostilled or Korean-consular authenticated | School transcript, translation and authentication rules still apply separately |
| Ordinary financial file | Applicant/parent sponsor identity, employment certificate/business registration, last 3 months' bank transactions; 6 months for certain institutions | Neither continuous balance-holding duration nor an individual waiver is established |
| TB confirmation | Embassy-designated hospital and current form; certificate valid 3 months; revised form from June 11, 2026 | [TB guidance](https://sites.google.com/view/koreanembassy/참고사항/결핵진단서-제출-안내) needs a current hospital/form check; test price unknown |
| Visa/KVAC charge | IDR 1,020,000 consular + IDR 240,000 administration = IDR 1,260,000; cashless; effective July 1, 2026 | [Fee schedule](https://www.visaforkorea-in.com/en-US/visa-center/fee) excludes optional services; recheck before filing; fees are not refunded after acceptance |

The embassy's [43-university list](https://sites.google.com/view/koreanembassy/참고사항/43개-대학-안내) gives named invitations financial-document exemptions; Yonsei is not named on the list read. The separate [special-institution list](https://sites.google.com/view/koreanembassy/참고사항/일정한-대학-안내) covered March 2026–February 2027, not the upcoming March intake. Putri's actual university is unknown. **Do not assume a waiver or infer eligibility merely from a Seoul address.**

Use November–December 2026 to prepare funding and documents. Aim for Jakarta KVAC filing in January or early February 2027 once the admission package is valid and complete. This is a buffer recommendation, not an official filing-window rule. The [general processing notice](https://sites.google.com/view/koreanembassy/공통사항-안내), effective April 10, 2023, refers to collection on the tenth counted day excluding public holidays; weekend treatment is unclear. Checks, interviews or more documents can delay it. Ordinary applications normally require attendance, with specified representation exceptions. Match certificate validity and visa entry validity to travel; do not buy non-refundable flights on a guaranteed turnaround assumption.

### Do not fund these terms with hoped-for work

**D-4-1 work eligibility starts only after 6 months from entry or status change, with school confirmation and immigration permission before work.** For March 1, 2027 entry, the point is around September 1, 2027, after these summer classes finish; lawful continuing enrollment/status still matters.

[Government residence guidance](https://www.studyinkorea.go.kr/cmm/life/residenceAndStayInfo.do) and the [employment table](https://www.studyinkorea.go.kr/en/work/aboutForeignerEmploymentSystem.do) show language-trainee weekly limits of 10 hours without the language standard, 20 with it, and a conditional enhanced weekday column of 25 hours. These are not degree-student 30/35-hour rights or unlimited vacation work. TOPIK 2 is a consistent route; the pages conflict on Sejong Elementary versus Intermediate 1, so do not rely on the easier option as settled. The actual permission controls job and hours. Prepare contract, school confirmation, passport/card, attendance/academic/language evidence and employer registration as required; approval must precede work.

## Make the arrival sequence work even if the card takes longer

Start with passport-based temporary connectivity and a payment backup. Then arrange residence/enrollment evidence and registration, and upgrade banking and resident-linked phone services only when accepted. **Receiving SMS does not mean you have resident identity verification or full banking access.**

That handbook is institutional advice, not selected bank/carrier authority for Putri; the operational sequence remains product-specific.

### Cover the health-insurance gap, not just the premium

Ordinary language-training D-4 is automatically enrolled in NHIS **after 6 months from entry**, unlike D-2 or D-4-3 initial-registration treatment. With March 1, 2027 entry that is around September 1, 2027; February entry could bring it toward August 2027. The actual eligibility record controls, not simply the date your residence card arrives.

Yonsei says it enrolls students in group insurance, but coverage dates, exclusions and separate premium remain unknown. Verify protection for the entire arrival-to-NHIS gap. KRW 200,000 private/top-up allowance is not a quote for adequate coverage, and KRW 100,000 boundary reserve is not a future NHIS bill.

The [NHIS-produced student leaflet](https://media.hallym.ac.kr/bbs/media/275/227379/download.do) gives **KRW 79,320/month for 2026**, not 2027. The 50% student reduction is conditional on monthly income at most KRW 300,000 and property-tax base at most KRW 135,000,000. [NHIS guidance](https://www.nhis.or.kr/english/wbheaa02900m01.do) also describes the reduction. General advance payment is due by the 25th of the prior month; first-bill handling remains unknown. Keep the registered address current and follow the actual bill. No Indonesian-specific exemption or exemption simply for holding private insurance was established.
