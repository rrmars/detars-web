You want your son to start school smoothly. You do not want to pay more rent for an admission advantage that does not exist. For this new Permanent Resident (PR) family, **Phase 2C is the normal route, not Phase 3**. Start with a home you can genuinely live in, then compare the last group admitted—not just the total vacancies.

This plan is for parents who became PRs in 2025, with no stated qualifying school links, considering Beauty World and the wider west of Singapore. The goal is registration in 2027 for school entry in 2028. **Exact 2027 phase dates are not yet announced in the retained MOE material.** The displayed calendar is for the earlier exercise; do not use it to book travel or set an application reminder.

Check your son's exact birthday first. The expected cohort is January 2, 2021–January 1, 2022 inclusive. This is an annual-cohort inference from the [MOE overview](https://www.moe.gov.sg/primary/p1-registration), not a newly published calendar. A January 1, 2021 birthday belongs to the earlier cohort and needs a different plan. Information below was retained on November 6, 2026–November 7, 2026, without a new source check for this guide.

## Use the phase you actually qualify for

Becoming PRs does not create an alumni, volunteer or community link. Check all genuine connections, but do not build your plan around acquiring one late.

| Phase | Eligibility | What it means for this family |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A sibling currently attends the school. | No qualifying sibling was stated. All eligible applicants receive a place. |
| 2A | A parent or sibling is a former pupil; a parent is on the School Advisory or Management Committee or is school staff; or the child attends the MOE Kindergarten in that school. | Neither parent being an alumnus rules out that route, not every possible link. No other qualifying link was stated. |
| 2B | A qualifying parent volunteer, directly connected church or clan endorsement, or endorsement as an active grassroots leader. | No present entitlement. Volunteering is not an admission guarantee. |
| 2C | An SC or PR child not yet registered in a primary school. | The baseline application phase. |
| 2C Supplementary | An SC or PR child still unregistered after the main round. | Use remaining vacancies. An unsuccessful child is then posted by MOE to a school with vacancies, not necessarily the preferred school or within 2 km. |
| 3 | International students who are neither SC nor PR, through a separate process. | Not your son's route and not a PR backup round. |

SC means Singapore Citizen. Follow [MOE's phase rules](https://www.moe.gov.sg/primary/p1-registration/registration-phases-key-dates) and [registration instructions](https://www.moe.gov.sg/primary/p1-registration/how-to-register). Register for only one school in a phase; MOE takes the latest submission in that phase. Applying early does not improve ballot priority. Registration is ordinarily online. Ordinary portal applicants need not submit documents unless using the online-form route, but schools can ask for clarification or additional documents. Keep identity and relationship records ready and follow the exercise-year instructions.

The ordinary volunteer route requires joining by July 1, 2026 and completing at least 40 hours by June 30, 2027 for this exercise. Starting now is ordinarily too late. The hours are a service minimum, not the full travel and scheduling commitment. Even an eligible PR volunteer remains behind SC applicants when citizenship priority applies. Do not assume a donation or a newly claimed community link buys priority.

## A nearby home does not outrank citizenship

At ordinary schools, the oversubscription order is:

**SC ≤1 km → SC >1–2 km → SC >2 km → PR ≤1 km → PR >1–2 km → PR >2 km.**

A citizen beyond the outer band comes before a PR in the nearest band. Balloting happens within the first group with more applicants than places left; later groups are not admitted. MOE runs computerised ballots centrally, with equal chances within the required group. Balloting can occur from Phase 2A through Phase 2C Supplementary. See [distance priority](https://www.moe.gov.sg/primary/p1-registration/distance) and [how balloting works](https://www.moe.gov.sg/primary/p1-registration/understand-balloting). “No ballot” does not always mean every applicant received a place.

### The coming exercise changes the distance calculation at selected schools

MOE's [September 10, 2026 announcement](https://www.moe.gov.sg/news/press-releases/20260910-enhancing-the-primary-one-registration-framework-for-greater-access-and-inclusivity) applies to the 2027 exercise and 2028 intake. Most schools will reserve 60 Phase 2C places, up from 40; smaller-intake schools are an exception. Phase 2B keeps 20 reserved places. These come from the intake, not additional places reserved for PR children.

At the 12 schools in [Annex A](https://www.moe.gov.sg/media/newsroom/news/press/2026/b0d58e7a-5220-49b9-bc9c-a7f783a4f510.pdf), Phase 2C places are split equally into ≤2 km and >2 km tracks. There is no 1 km preference within a track. All SC applicants across both tracks are considered before PR applicants. Spare places flow to SC applicants in the other track first; only then are remaining places split equally between PR tracks. An odd extra place goes to the nearer track.

Moving from 1.8 km to 0.8 km therefore does not improve priority within the same track. In the school comparison below, Pei Hwa, Methodist Girls' and Raffles Girls' are on the announced list. The latter two are girls-only and remain unsuitable for your son. Other phases and schools retain their applicable rules.

Separately, [specified schools cap PR intake](https://www.moe.gov.sg/primary/p1-registration/cap-on-pr-intake) in the main and Supplementary rounds at about 25–30% of planned intake, allowing for earlier PR admissions. This is not a universal quota. **The 2027 cap list and phase limits remain unconfirmed.** Spare total seats need not mean spare PR places.

## Make the address work for your family

Use the registering parent's official NRIC residential address. Your child must actually live there. A rental is not automatically excluded, but a tenancy agreement alone does not prove residence. Check your actual home through the [official OneMap school-distance service](https://www.onemap.gov.sg/school) in the registration year—not walking distance, an agent's map or a station-centred radius.

If distance priority secures admission, the ordinary-address rule requires **at least 30 months from the start of the registration exercise**, with the registering parent's NRIC address maintained there. It is not counted from your submission day or normally from school entry. No official end date can be calculated until the exercise start is known. Keep the lease, occupation evidence and official records aligned. Saving bills is sensible preparation, not a universal MOE document checklist.

- **Before registration:** actually move, update official records and confirm the distance. Do not use an ordinary future rental you have not occupied. Qualifying new or resale purchases have separate documentary routes; they are not a future-rental exemption.
- **After registration:** disclose address changes promptly. MOE allows a move within the same home–school distance category to continue fulfilling the residence requirement. Moving beyond the original category before completing it can lead to a transfer chosen by MOE, with no parental choice of school. Check how this works under the new two-track implementation rather than assuming the old bands apply.
- **If the lease is too short:** seek an extension. Inability to extend is not an exemption. A later move also does not guarantee a transfer to a nearer school. If distance priority did not secure admission, check the actual admission conditions rather than assuming the same condition applies identically.

The family is expected to reside there during primary schooling; the minimum is not advice to move immediately afterward. False address information can be investigated and referred to the police. Read [MOE's home-address rules](https://www.moe.gov.sg/primary/p1-registration/home-address) and [moving and tenancy FAQs](https://www.moe.gov.sg/faq?categoryid=E3E39C0FD0F04504A444B1F600CC8430) before committing to a lease.

Add meals, uniforms, books, transport and student care separately.

Your son's sponsorship route was not supplied. Check his grant conditions. The obligation comes from status, not winning a school place; an acknowledgement does not improve admission chances or create an exemption.

## Your next steps

- **Now:** confirm the birthday, actual school links and PR grant conditions. Set a sustainable rent, school-fee, commute and student-care budget.
- **Before moving:** check the actual address, campus location, cap list and applicable track. Choose a home the family can genuinely occupy for the required commitment.
- **When the calendar is published:** record the exact opening, closing and results times for the main and Supplementary rounds. Do not copy the previous calendar.
- **During registration:** watch actual vacancies and the citizenship group reaching the cutoff. Apply to one school you would accept. Keep wider-commute options ready.
- **If unsuccessful:** use the Supplementary round, not the international-student phase. If still unsuccessful, follow MOE's vacancy-based posting. Private and international schools are separate admissions and fee decisions.
- **After acceptance:** follow orientation and student-care instructions, check the entry-year fees and maintain the residence conditions attached to admission.

## FAQ

### Can a PR child use the international-student round as a backup?

No. Use the Supplementary round. If that is unsuccessful, MOE posts the child to a school with vacancies; preference and proximity are not guaranteed.

### Will a nearer rental make us equal to citizen applicants?

No. Citizenship comes first. At selected two-track schools, moving closer within the same track does not improve its internal priority either. Confirm the actual band before signing.

### Does “no ballot” mean every PR applicant got in?

No. A lower-priority group may receive no places without a ballot. Dazhong's Supplementary result above shows why the admitted citizenship group matters.

### Can we move after admission?

A same-distance-category move can continue fulfilling the residence requirement. A move beyond it before completion can lead to an MOE-directed transfer. Confirm the applicable category, especially under the new scheme, before changing homes.
