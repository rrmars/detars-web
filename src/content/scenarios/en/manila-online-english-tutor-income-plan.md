This plan is for **Carla, 29, a Filipino tutor in Quezon City**, with TESOL certification, **3 years of online teaching** and **PHP 40,000** to invest. She wants Japanese and Korean working adults for a **January–December 2027** business. It assumes she already owns a usable computer and is a resident, purely self-employed, non-VAT individual. ## Recommendation: hybrid first, direct-led by midyear

**Keep a platform income floor while you build your own clients.** For Carla, a direct-led business is the more workable route to PHP 60,000 a month—but the plan reaches PHP 60,632 in April 2027, not January. January comes to only PHP 17,848. Those figures depend on paid bookings, not just opening a calendar.

**First platform to investigate: Bizmates**, because its public Philippine FAQ actually publishes starting pay and adult Japanese-business demand. Carla’s three years of work meet the stated duration, but her business know-how and interview performance still need assessment. Keep RareJob as a conditional alternative (check its other-platform restriction), and Engoo as an unconfirmed recruitment lead; do not mistake an application page for an offer or guaranteed bookings. Korean-specific current Filipino recruitment is less securely evidenced here: treat Korea as a separately validated direct-market experiment, not guaranteed platform demand. [P1–P6]

**The economic constraint:** at PHP 140 per platform “hour” (two PHP 70, 25-minute lessons), a PHP 60,000 spendable target plus PHP 5,000 recurring costs needs 65,000 ÷ (140 × 0.92) = **504.66 paid two-lesson units**, about 1,010 lessons / 420.55 live teaching hours monthly before gaps. Even the published PHP 230 maximum needs 307.18 paid units / 255.99 live hours. These use an intentionally cautious 8% reserve on full turnover, not the final annual tax. Platform-only is not a sustainable base plan.

**Define the target:** “take-home” here is business cash after modeled payment costs, PHP 5,000 operating costs and the tax reserve; before household expenses, SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG contributions, voluntary benefits and recovery of the startup outlay. Those personal or status-specific obligations need their own budget. She is modeled as a resident, purely self-employed, non-VAT individual, not a mixed-income employee.

## Independent offer and operating stack

### Sell one specific outcome before building a site

Proposed test offer: **50-minute English meetings and interview practice for Japanese/Korean working adults, PHP 600 equivalent per paid session**. That price is a planning assumption—not a surveyed market average. Start with a diagnostic, a four-week goal and reusable meeting/interview role-plays. Use honest TESOL and experience details; no invented testimonials or promised proficiency scores.

Proposed package: 8 × PHP 600 = **PHP 4,800**, prepaid, with a clearly disclosed cancellation/refund policy and invoice. Schedule one 50-minute lesson in a 60-minute calendar slot. Do not auto-convert JPY/KRW with an invented exchange rate: quote the current provider conversion and all-in payer total before payment.

Acquire learners independently through useful public content, permission-based professional/community participation and genuine referrals. Test Japan and Korea separately; translate a short landing page carefully. Never move a platform learner off-platform in breach of its terms. Plan to retain about 18 learners taking 8 lessons monthly (144 paid sessions); churn and cancellations mean inquiries must continue. This is a capacity target, not a conversion claim.

### Lean stack

Start with a free landing page and free booking tier; use one calendar, one lesson type, timezone-aware confirmations and manual payment confirmation. Add a domain only after paid validation. Use video software whose free-session limit supports 50 minutes; otherwise budget a suitable paid plan rather than assuming a free 40-minute meeting will work.

Collect only necessary learner data; explain recording consent and retention. Publish adult-only scope, currency, rescheduling cutoff, package expiry/refunds and contact details before accepting payment.

### Do not buy complexity

Defer LMS subscriptions, custom development and large ad campaigns. A marketplace can generate discovery, but deduct its commission *plus* withdrawal/FX costs. The income model below is direct selling, not Preply/italki/Cafetalk economics. Replace the 8% payment allowance if actual quotes are higher.

Use the tool prices above only if the free workflow stops meeting your needs. Paid subscriptions do not create demand.

## Philippine registration, invoices and tax checklist

**General information, not tax advice.** Do not wait for PHP 60,000 revenue to consider registration. Carla’s existing reduced-hours work may already be freelance business activity: first establish her TIN, current registration, employment-versus-contractor status, other income and prior filings. No personal tax records were accessed.

- **Identify the correct registration route.** Use the existing TIN; do not seek a second one. Form 1901 is for self-employed registration, Form 1905 for updates, and Form 2303 is the Certificate of Registration. Determine the RDO for the actual home/business address and obtain its current documentary checklist; prepare ID/address evidence and relevant business-name documents. ORUS/RDO procedures and exact QC home-tutor classification remain to be confirmed. [T1,T5,T8]

- **Separate brand, tax and local permissions.** DTI says a business name different from the proprietor’s true name needs registration; DTI registration is not a Mayor’s Permit or BIR registration. DTI territorial fees are PHP 200 barangay / 500 city-municipality / 1,000 regional / 2,000 national, plus PHP 30 documentary stamp. Do not automatically choose national scope just because clients are overseas. QC permit, barangay, zoning, lease/HOA and any professional/occupational-tax requirements and charges need an address-specific determination; no exemption is assumed. [D1]

- **Do not pay an obsolete BIR annual registration fee.** The PHP 500 fee ceased on **22 January 2024**, including new registration; that alone no longer requires Form 0605. Printing, books, help and local permits can still cost money. [T3]

- **Choose the tax regime correctly, each year.** The inspected BIR rules permit eligible non-VAT individuals within the PHP 3 million sales/receipts-plus-relevant-other-income ceiling to elect 8%. New registrants use the registration/initial-quarter process; existing taxpayers renew the election through the prescribed beginning-of-year/initial-quarter route. The election is annual and irrevocable for that year. Do not leave it until the annual return. VAT registrants cannot simply choose 8% because actual turnover is low. [T1]

- **Books and invoices still apply under 8%.** Record gross sales, platform statements, payment fees, refunds and invoices; retain supporting tax-credit certificates and filing/payment evidence. Gross direct sales are not just what arrives after PayPal fees. The platform portion here is the contracted tutor fee; customer retail price versus commission-agent treatment must follow the actual contract. [T1,T5]

- **Use registered invoices for services.** Under the inspected non-VAT rule, invoice services PHP 500 or more, any sale on buyer request, and qualifying daily aggregates. Carla’s PHP 600 lessons exceed that threshold. Secure the appropriate registered invoice/Authority to Print route before trading; an ordinary receipt or a PayPal email is not itself the required BIR sales invoice. A later collection acknowledgment is not a second sale. Recheck the threshold’s scheduled adjustment for 2027. [T4]

- **Include the 2026 invoice updates.** RR 6-2026 adds a separate creditable-withholding-tax item where applicable and the “Less: Withholding Tax” phrase. RMC 98-2026 requires covered non-micro taxpayers to issue compliant structured electronic invoices by **31 December 2026**; its mandate expressly exempts micro taxpayers. This model’s PHP 921,600 sales is below the PHP 3 million micro classification boundary, assuming no other turnover. Confirm Carla’s classification/system requirements; an emailed Word/Excel/PDF invoice is not automatically compliant electronic invoicing. [T5,T10–T11]

- **Reconcile withholding, not double-tax it.** Obtain applicable BIR Form 2307 certificates from Philippine clients/platforms and reconcile credits against income-tax due. Actual payer withholding rates and marketplace obligations were not established for Carla. A tax credit can still constrain cash timing; the model does not promise immediate refund or count a credit as extra revenue. [P1,T5]

### 8% versus graduated rates

### Eligible 8% election

Pure self-employment: 8% × annual gross sales/receipts plus relevant non-operating income **above PHP 250,000**. No fee/expense/OSD deduction on top. It replaces graduated tax on that business income and Section 116 percentage tax; it does not remove income-tax returns or records.

**Mixed income:** compensation is taxed under graduated rates; eligible 8% business income gets *no* second PHP 250,000 reduction. If the turnover ceiling is exceeded, the tax and VAT consequences change. [T1]

### Graduated + percentage tax

Income tax uses taxable profit after allowable itemization *or* 40% OSD. The relevant 2023-onward brackets: up to 250,000 nil; 250,000–400,000: 15% of excess; 400,000–800,000: 22,500 + 20% of excess over 400,000. Higher bands exist but are not needed for this model.

Ordinary non-VAT Section 116 percentage tax is **3%** of gross quarterly sales/receipts, restored 1 July 2023, subject to exemptions. Do not reuse the expired pandemic 1% rate. [T2,T5,T7]
