**Wondering whether MYR 18,000 really covers your family? The lean plan below allocates MYR 17,690 a month, leaving just MYR 310. School-year progression alone, even with the fee table held unchanged, takes it to MYR 18,385. This is possible to plan around, but not comfortably affordable on autopilot. Choose the school before the condo.**

This is for a Hong Kong family with two working parents, two children aged 6 and 9 at the time of research, and an August 2027 move in mind. Prices and rules are the samples inspected on November 6, 2026, not future quotes or vacancies. The adaptation date is November 6, 2026; no external source was refreshed. All amounts are MYR unless explicitly USD; no currency conversion is assumed. Treat the ceiling as spendable income after tax and payroll obligations. This is practical planning, not immigration, tax or legal advice.

Start by pairing an affordable school with a nearby home. Taylor's KL is in Taman Maluri; its other campus is in Puchong. Sri KDU Subang Jaya is worth comparing, but none of these is inside Mont Kiara or Desa ParkCity. A cheaper home across town can mean four paid journeys each school day. Also check the floor plan: with separate bedrooms for both children, a three-bedroom flat has no closed-door office. “Fully furnished” does not promise two useful desks.

## Put the school bill on the table first

Taylor's has a calendar-year **2027** fee schedule, not an August 2027–July 2028 quote. The latest located Sri KDU, HELP and IGB tables were **2026/27**; their **2027/28** fees were not found. No Taylor's **2028** increase is priced. British Years and IGB Grades are not interchangeable: birthdays, previous schooling and assessment decide placement. The children could be 6/9 or 7/10 on arrival; compare the ranges below, not assumed offers.

| School and schedule | Younger child: annual tuition | Older child: annual tuition | Two-child recurring equivalent |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Taylor's KL / Puchong](https://www.tis.edu.my/admissions/school-fees/), [2027 PDF](https://www.tis.edu.my/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/TIS-School-Fees-2027.pdf) | Y2 29,760; Y3 33,150 | Y5 39,360; Y6 42,630 | 69,120–75,780/year; 5,760–6,315/month. No two-child tuition discount assumed |
| [Sri KDU Subang Jaya](https://srikdu.edu.my/subang-jaya/fee-structure/), 2026/27 | Y2 41,600; Y3 43,270 | Y5 49,250; Y6 51,430 | 86,690–90,373/year; 7,224–7,531/month after 10% younger-child discount, subject to approval. Without it 7,571–7,892/month |
| [HELP, Subang Bestari](https://his.edu.my/admission-fee/), 2026/27 | Y2 41,460; Y3 43,830 | Y5 or Y6 50,490 | 91,950–94,320/year; 7,663–7,860/month plus compulsory technology fees, amount unknown |
| [IGB, Sierramas](https://igbis.edu.my/admissions/fee/), 2026/27 | G1 or G2 86,500 | G4 or G5 86,500 | 184,731.50/year; 15,394/month model including 5% younger-sibling discount, 5,600 annual IT and modelled 6% SST |

Monthly equivalents divide annual costs by 12; they are reserves, not monthly payment offers. IGB's model is (86,500 + 86,500 × 95% + 5,600) × 1.06. Taxing IT is conservative modelling; without IT tax, annual cost is 184,395.50. No advance-payment discount is assumed. [IGB placement guidance](https://igbis.edu.my/admissions/age-grade-placement/) and its [placement table](https://igbis.edu.my/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/grade-placement-2627.pdf) are useful for placement, not proof of a future fee or place.

| School extras | What the headline tuition leaves out |
|---|---|
| Taylor's | Meals, bus, uniforms, ECAs, trips, exams and devices. EAL about 10% extra tuition; EEP about 20%, with campus/year exceptions. Neither support surcharge is in the household base |
| Sri KDU | Meals, books, uniforms, transport and support. Academic English Pathway 4,000/term/child, or 12,000/year if needed |
| HELP | [Other fees](https://his.edu.my/other-fees/): technology every term, materials, meals and some activities. Technology and deposit amounts unknown—not zero |
| IGB | IT 1,400/semester/child, included in the model above. ESOL/learning support 5,000/semester if needed; meals, transport, uniforms and some activities extra; Grade 5 onward needs a personal laptop |

Keep tax beside the school bill. The [Customs education SST FAQ](https://mysst.customs.gov.my/faq-expansion-of-service-tax-scope-2025/) describes a 60,000 annual per-student threshold and 6% tax; that is not a household threshold. Taylor's fee basis includes entry/support fees. Sri KDU's older child may cross it after 11,500 entry charges: a provisional first-year tax reserve is 3,645–3,776 if that aggregate basis applies. HELP's older child reaches 63,490 before technology: provisional tax 3,809.40 plus any taxable technology. These are invoice-risk estimates, not confirmed school tax bills; genuine refundable deposits are not taxed as spending in this model.

|---|---|---|---|
| Taylor's | 2,000 + 12,000 = 14,000 | 23,040–25,260; one term each | Three terms; no ambiguous sibling upfront rebate assumed. Refund needs acknowledged advance term notice |
| Sri KDU | 3,000 + 20,000 = 23,000; international-student rates | 20,000 | Tuition 40% / 30% / 30%; full-term deposit notice; provisional tax extra |

| IGB | 2,000 + 50,000 = 52,000 before modelled SST | 30,000 after younger-sibling reduction | New first-semester tuition/deposit due within 14 days of invoice |

Hong Kong residence or employers do not establish citizenship or Malaysian fee eligibility. Places, English assessment and exact year groups are unconfirmed. [HELP's academic-year FAQ](https://his.edu.my/faq/) says September start; an August relocation need not mean August classes.

**Progression is a cost even without inflation.** If Y3 + Y6 advances to Y4 + Y7, frozen 2027 term rates give (11,730 + 16,310) × 3 = 84,120/year = 7,010/month. Household allocation becomes **MYR 18,385**, or 385 over the ceiling, with a 2,780 deposit top-up. This is conditional on placement/progression, not a published 2028 quote. Separately, a hypothetical 5% rise in 6,315 adds about 316/month, more than the 310 unallocated margin. That is a stress test, not a forecast; contingency or cuts would have to absorb it.

Excluded: personal taxes/payroll obligations, HK housing, flights home, major holidays, relocation/shipping, substantial medical/dental/chronic needs, new furniture/devices, car purchase/loan payments, school entry/deposits and live-in help. Do not read the total as every possible cost of moving abroad.

## Pick a smaller daily journey, not just a nicer address

These are lifestyle judgments, not safety rankings, measured commutes or rental medians. [Property Lab's commercial area comparison](https://property-laboratory.com/areas) gives orientation; [Desa ParkCity's developer](https://www.desaparkcity.com/aboutdpc.html) describes its township, not independently verified safety.

| Area | Why consider it | What to check for this family |
|---|---|---|
| Mont Kiara | Condo life, international-school cluster, expat conveniences | No operating in-area rail in the inspected guide; do not budget around a future line. Affordable schools above are elsewhere |
| Bangsar | Established neighbourhood, shops and Kelana Jaya LRT corridor | Hillier residential areas are not automatically walkable to rail; Bangsar South is another search. Sample three-bed rents 5,500–6,500 exceed the lean target |
| Desa ParkCity | Parks, lakeside shops and community school | No internal station; small Sofiya homes are not proxies for premium condos. No ParkCity-school package is priced here |
| Cyberjaya / Putrajaya | More space in the Cyberjaya sample; planned-suburb option for remote parents | Separate places; station access is unit-specific. No Putrajaya unit priced; a daily drive north to school can undo the saving |
| Petaling Jaya | Different value/transport choices around Kelana Jaya, Tropicana and Damansara Perdana | Large city, not one walkable district. Neither a PJ address nor a station proves a short run to HELP or Sri KDU |

My starting shortlist is school-near Maluri/Puchong for Taylor's, or western suburbs only with a revised Sri KDU/HELP budget. Test opening/pickup traffic, exact-unit fibre, lift reliability, building works, balcony/window safety, water billing, parking and maintenance inclusions. Inspect the third bedroom and both workstations rather than trusting photographs.

## Check each parent's work permission separately

These are dated references, not personal eligibility or legal conclusions. [MDEC's DE Rantau FAQ v11](https://www.mdec.my/api/media/file/DE-Rantau_Pass_FAQ_Version-11_%2029102026.pdf), dated November 1, 2026, is the basis here. The still-accessible [v10](https://www.mdec.my/api/media/file/DE-Rantau_Pass_FAQ_v10.pdf), dated August 28, 2026, has older USD24,000/24-month descriptions; do not mix them with this plan.

| Pass check | What the inspected v11 says / what to do |
|---|---|
| Each working parent | Tech/digital income minimum USD36,000/year; specified non-tech roles USD60,000/year. Individual criteria, not combined household income; checklist also uses “greater than”, so do not assume exact-boundary approval. Principal over 18; passport over 14 months valid on submission; the FAQ excludes Israeli citizens |
| Employer and evidence | Full-time remote employee of foreign-based company not registered in Malaysia; signed remote contract, duties, pay and foreign-employer details. Employment began at least three months earlier; three payslips with matching bank deposits; preferably six months left on contract. English/certified translations |
| Spouse and children | Partner and children under 18 may apply as dependants; FAQ says no spouse work permission, with no overseas-remote exception found. Planning inference: assess two principal applications or another independently work-authorising status; children's applications remain separately assessed |
| Duration/location | Initial 3–12 months; up to three 12-month renewals, maximum 48 months subject to eligibility/approval; Peninsular Malaysia and Labuan, not permanent residence |
| Timing and insurance | Complete-file guidance 6–8 weeks, not a guarantee; renewal encouraged three months early; Malaysia health cover for full pass and LHDN registration slip at endorsement. Avoid substantial non-refundable commitments on assumed approval |

| Published processing / other cash | Amount and caveat |
|---|---|
| Principal processing | 1,080 each, including 8% SST, non-refundable |
| Dependant processing | 540 each, including 8% SST, non-refundable |
| Two principals + two children | 2 × 1,080 + 2 × 540 = 3,240 processing only; do not tax it twice |
| Pass, visas, bonds | Principal 360/year or 90/three months; nationality-based visas extra. Hong Kong bond group 1,000; actual passports govern. Child endorsement, family bond treatment and distinct renewal tariff not fully itemised |

**Resolve the children's study route before committing.** MDEC v11 says Student Pass through the school; Taylor's page says a yearly Permission to Study stamp for Digital Nomad dependants and warns immigration may take three months or more. A dependant pass alone is not a settled classroom permission; obtain the applicable route, costs and timing from the school/Immigration.

For endorsement, the approval letter lasts six months, non-extendable. Use the normal immigration counter and first-entry stamp, not Autogate; complete new endorsement within one month of entry and remain in Malaysia until issued. Tourist/social visitors must exit/re-enter correctly; approval is not an endorsed pass. Most Malaysian banks do not accept the Professional Visit Pass for opening an account according to MDEC: keep a payment fallback. Foreign salary is not automatically tax-free; tax clearance is required for renewal. Employer/payroll and treaty outcomes are not determined here.

[MM2H Silver](https://www.mm2h.gov.my/category/silver) is capital-heavy: USD150,000 fixed deposit, compulsory home at least 600,000 and 1,000 principal participation fee, not an all-in fee list. Up to 50% deposit withdrawal is permitted after approval for stated purposes; do not assume all capital stays separately locked forever. The page describes a five-year renewable pass, 90-day annual stay wording and no career permission; that is not proof either remote job is authorised.

| Upfront item | MYR | Refundability / timing |

The identifiable commitment subtotal is **MYR 82,260**, not your sufficient cash ceiling. Add 720 for two adult annual principal pass fees before separately applicable tax/visas; add 2,000 adult bonds only if both actually hold Hong Kong passports. With estimated 6,000–7,200 annual insurance prepayment, this becomes 90,980–92,180. Ordinary non-prepaid first-month operating lines add 6,375, giving **97,355–98,555** before unresolved child endorsement/study/bond charges, temporary accommodation, flights/shipping, emergency liquidity or car capital. A rental-car deposit is also unpriced.

Annual insurance and first-term tuition prepay already budgeted spending; do not count them again as extra annual costs. The operating subtotal excludes rent, tuition, annual insurance and reserve lines. Refundable deposits are not freely spendable emergency money.

A calm order: check both adults' pass fit and employer arrangements; confirm birthdays, assessment, placement and full future school invoices; reconcile study permission; then shortlist nearby homes and test the route/internet. Align approvals, school start, tenancy notice and refund terms before non-refundable payment. Keep temporary housing and a delayed-start fallback. A shorter school day is worth more here than a prestigious address.
