You need a working number and reliable work calls before you need a spectacular download speed. **Start with Vodafone CallYa prepaid; use a phone hotspot only while the home line is pending. Then choose DSL with useful upload, or fibre that is already ready in the apartment.** Cable is the cheaper example here, but the building and evening performance must suit you.

This plan keeps Mei's profile: **29**, a data analyst starting work in **Friedrichshain, Berlin on 1 March 2027**, with Blue Card status, **two work-from-home days** and evening streaming, plus weekly family calls to China. The timetable assumes Anmeldung around **day 14** and a bank account in **weeks 2–3**; neither is an approval promise. Arrival and mobile activation on the job-start date are budget assumptions, not a confirmed arrival date.

Prices and rules were read on **7 November 2026**, not promised for March 2027. **No specific address was checked.** The underlying source file is not published.

## Choose the connection the flat can actually use

A telephone socket does not prove a free DSL port. A TV socket does not prove internet-capable cable. Fibre in the street does not mean an activatable fibre termination in the flat. Ask for the existing socket and equipment details, then check the actual address before ordering.

All speeds below are advertised maximum download/upload in Mbit/s, not measured performance. Prices include VAT and assume the selected rental equipment.

HomeSpot avoids a fixed-line technician, not delivery, credit approval or indoor-signal risk. It is tied to the residential address, not an unrestricted travel hotspot. Test employer VPN, work calls and evening reception near a window. If the stay may be short, its separate **Flex** example is **€39.99/month + €49.99 connection**, monthly cancellable, equipment extra. It is not the discounted long-term plan.

## Keep China calls separate from EU roaming

For weekly family calls, use an app already shared with family, such as WeChat, over home Wi-Fi or included data. The model assumes **no incremental telephone-call charge**, not free data or guaranteed app availability.

**Dialling +86 is different.** Domestic flats and CallYa's EU-destination units do not cover China. Carrier “Wi-Fi Calling” is still a telephone call, not an app call. Neither the [German Lebara China page](https://www.lebara.de/de/minutenpreis-auslandsanrufe/china-anrufen.html) nor the [English page](https://www.lebara.de/en/minutenpreis-auslandsanrufe/china-anrufen.html) yielded a verified current rate. China mobile/fixed-number prices, connection charges, rounding and bundle eligibility remain unknown and are excluded. Get those details before choosing a telephone-calling package.

[EU/EEA roaming](https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/DE/Vportal/TK/InternetTelefon/Roaming/start.html) is temporary use abroad, subject to fair use, including prepaid. It is not Germany-to-China calling. China travel is outside that zone; UK and Switzerland inclusion is tariff-specific, not automatic under EU law.

## Four first-year budgets—with the same bridge

**Assume mobile starts 1 March 2027 and home starts 1 April 2027.** The model counts full monthly charges without prorating. The year ends just before **1 March 2028**: **366 days**, including leap day. That gives **11 home charges**, April–February, and **14 prepaid periods**. Period **14** begins **28 February 2028**, leaving validity beyond the horizon. These billing and activation dates are model assumptions, not installation promises.

Every row uses **€79.99 Black once + 13 × €9.99 S = €209.86**. Black supplies unlimited domestic data for the first **28 days**; the remaining **three March days** use S data or employer-approved office access. Test VPN and calls before relying on tethering. A **25 GB** phone allowance does not promise enough for sustained WFH and nightly streaming. **Schedule Black → S yourself** or Black can renew at the higher price. A second Black period replacing S adds **€70.00**.

## Paperwork and cancellation: keep flexibility real

Prepare consistent passport-name spelling, a real service/delivery address, tenancy/address evidence, any issued residence card, later Anmeldung confirmation and payment details. That is preparation, **not a universal legal document checklist**. [O2's privacy notice](https://www.o2online.de/assets/blobs/pdfs/datenschutzmerkblatt-telefonica/) and [Vodafone's pre-contract notice](https://www.vodafone.de/de/datenschutz/datennutzung-vor-vertragsschluss) describe identity/address, credit and fraud checks. No SCHUFA history is not proven bad credit; a Blue Card does not guarantee acceptance. No universal German-only IBAN requirement, minimum credit-history period or deposit workaround was established. If declined, correct name/address mismatches and ask for a human explanation; keep prepaid funded.

Under the [contract rules](https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/DE/Vportal/TK/InternetTelefon/Vertrag/start.html), an initial consumer term cannot exceed **24 months**, and providers must also offer a contract of **no more than 12 months**, not necessarily at the same price. After the initial term expires and the contract tacitly continues, cancellation is possible at any time with **one month's notice**. This has applied since **1 December 2021**, not just since November 2026. It does not make the initial long term monthly cancellable.

[Moving home](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.de/wissen/digitale-welt/mobilfunk-und-festnetz/umzug-die-rechte-der-telefon-und-internetkunden-11034) is not an automatic exit: if contracted service is available at the new home, the term continues unchanged; inability to supply can permit special termination with one month's notice. Save the Vertragszusammenfassung and product sheet, including upload, fees, dates and equipment returns. Eligible remote contracts generally have **14-day withdrawal**; shop contracts normally lack a statutory cooling-off right. Online-contract providers must provide a cancellation button. For sustained underperformance, retain evidence before seeking reduction or extraordinary termination.

## Your first-month sequence

- **February 2027:** recheck offers, socket/ONT and lease length; arrange office days and Wi-Fi for ID. Check the exact phone and passport channel. Run an address check only once you have the actual flat and choose to proceed.
- **Days 1–3:** activate prepaid through a supported channel, using real details. Try physical SIM or in-person verification if appropriate; do not blame every ID failure on SCHUFA. Test family calls, hotspot and VPN. Use Black only if substantial bridging is needed.
- **Days 3–7:** compare confirmed-ready fibre, DSL and cable; test cellular if relevant. Check minimum upload, activation estimate, every fee and hardware waiver. Place a single deliberate order only when ready; acceptance before Anmeldung is possible but unconfirmed.
- **Around day 14:** keep address details consistent, put your name on mailbox/bell and arrange technician access. A booked appointment is not bank or credit approval.
- **Weeks 2–3:** supply accepted payment details when available; follow up on activation or rejection. Do not abandon working access for an advertised fibre rollout.
- **Days 25–28:** confirm Black → S, or budget the extra €70.00 for another Black period. Reduce streaming before S data runs out.
- **Days 30–31:** test the activated line by Ethernet and Wi-Fi at different times; compare the first bill with promised setup, rental and shipping terms. Reduce backup only after service works; record term-end, cancellation and rental-return reminders.

The remaining gates are March offers, apartment availability, approval, passport/channel and device compatibility, signal/VPN performance, equipment terms, building work and China telephone rates. No address checks, forms, accounts, orders or provider contacts were made for this guide.
