**Want a room ready when you land, without putting your savings at risk? Try the correct graduate dorm route first, keep a low-deposit goshiwon as a backup, and consider a studio only after independent document checks. No advert, agent, address-registration promise or fixed date guarantees that your deposit will come back.**

This example is for a 24-year-old Vietnamese master's student starting at Yonsei on March 1, 2027, staying from March 2027 to February 2028. The same comparisons can help other international students, but nationality, programme and housing eligibility still need their own checks. The planning information was checked on November 6, 2026; nothing here establishes a vacancy or price for your arrival.

## Decide what your monthly ceiling includes

Treat **₩800,000 as housing-only** here: rent, maintenance, utilities and internet. Food, transport, phone and insurance are separate. If it must cover your whole life, use the lean budget below instead. All money is KRW, without conversion. The deposit is extra upfront cash, not monthly spending and not emergency money you can freely use.

| Housing choice | What you gain | What to check before paying |
|---|---|---|
| University dorm | Furnished campus base; often a shared room | Admission is separate from university admission; a semester does not secure a full year |
| Goshiwon / goshitel | Small furnished room; usually less upfront cash | Basic planning estimate ₩350,000–600,000/month; premium rooms can cost ₩750,000+. Check bathroom, window, laundry and bill caps; the name is not a quality guarantee |
| One-room · 원룸 | Own bathroom and cooking space | Examples below charge ₩550,000–600,000 rent + ₩89,000 maintenance and ₩10,000,000 deposit; electricity is not listed as included. Do not transfer that deposit before independent checks |
| Share house | Shared kitchen and company | Hongdae example: ₩450,000–550,000 + ₩80,000 maintenance, ₩1,000,000 deposit. Women-only, semi-basement; a twin-bed price is not a single-room price |

Start with Sinchon for campus convenience, or Yeonhui if the relevant campus gate suits your department. Check Hongdae for late-night noise and Mangwon for the actual connection, not just cheaper rent. Allow roughly 10–30 minutes on foot from nearby Sinchon/Yeonhui addresses or 25–50 minutes door-to-door from Hongdae/Mangwon. These are planning estimates, not measured commutes: pin your department and exact room in a Korean map.

No spring 2027 notice or fee was found in the [Korean board](https://dorm.yonsei.ac.kr/board/?id=schedule&p=1) or [English board](https://dorm.yonsei.ac.kr/en/board/?id=schedule_en) checked on November 6, 2026. The regular route uses a computer lottery. New graduates are included, but special graduate schools are excluded in the current notice: confirm your programme category. Foreign nationals are exempt from application residence/family documents, not the check-in TB chest-X-ray report in Korean or English issued within two months. Recheck the new instructions.

Your 12-month stay crosses vacations and cleaning gaps. Extensions need separate applications and payments; continuous residence is not promised. The [winter extension notice](https://dorm.yonsei.ac.kr/board/?id=schedule&mode=V&idx=9168), for existing residents from December 22, 2026 to February 20, 2027, lists Beophyeon double ₩894,000 and Muak 4 double/single ₩1,014,000 / ₩1,332,000. It is not a spring admission offer. Keep temporary-stay and storage money.

## Slow down before the deposit leaves your account

[Jeonse and wolse](https://www.easylaw.go.kr/CSP/CnpClsMain.laf?popMenu=ov&csmSeq=629&ccfNo=1&cciNo=1&cnpClsNo=1) are different commitments: jeonse usually substitutes a large deposit for regular rent; wolse combines a deposit and monthly rent. For a first year, my recommendation is to avoid jeonse and minimise deposit exposure. Refundable does not mean escrowed or protected against earlier claims.

Korean shorthand “1000/55” uses ten-thousand-won units: **₩10,000,000 deposit + ₩550,000 rent**. 관리비 is maintenance, not necessarily all bills. Stayes K means ₩1,000; “10,000K” is ₩10,000,000, not ₩10,000.

| Check before signing or paying | What it can—and cannot—tell you |
|---|---|
| Building register · 건축물대장 | Compare legal address/unit, floor, area and use; investigate 위반건축물 violations. This is not a mortgage search |
| Rights register · 등기사항증명서 | Match property and owner; 표제부 identifies it, 갑구 shows ownership/restrictions, 을구 mortgages and other rights. Check relevant land too and recheck before final payment |
| Authority, taxes and other tenants | Match owner, signer and payee; verify proxy/sublet authority and ask about tax arrears and prior deposits. A clean register does not settle every claim |
| Contract and actual room | All charges/caps, return date, deductions and early exit in writing; inspect window, damp, heat, hot water, locks and escape route |

Use the [Government24 building-register service](https://www.gov.kr/mw/AA020InfoCappView.do?CappBizCD=15000000098), the [building-record explanation](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkProc.do?joNo=003800000&lsNm=%EA%B1%B4%EC%B6%95%EB%B2%95&mode=2), [IROS](https://www.iros.go.kr/) and the [official rights-check guide](https://www.easylaw.go.kr/CSP/CnpClsMain.laf?popMenu=ov&csmSeq=629&ccfNo=2&cciNo=1&cnpClsNo=1). Building-record online issue/viewing is listed as free; office issue/view is ₩500/₩300 per record. No individual property record has been ordered here. Use independent Korean-language help, even with a registered agent.

**Stop rather than transfer** if there is pressure for a holding deposit before inspection/documents; mismatched identity, address or payee; unclear trust authority, attachment/auction, heavy prior debt or building violations; unexplained earlier deposits/tax arrears; refusal of address paperwork or a written contract. A cheaper rent does not repair a deposit-fraud risk.

## Your address has more than one deadline

The rules below are the dated planning references, not a personal legal determination. Recheck for your arrival and exact lease; immigration accepting an address does not certify housing-law protection.

| Task | Dated rule and practical next step |
|---|---|
| First foreign registration | For this long stay, within 90 days of entry, not class start. Book early; a reservation is not completed registration |
| Moving after registration | Report the new residence within 15 days; do not substitute a generic 14-day rule |
| Qualifying lease-contract report | Generally within 30 days of conclusion in designated areas if deposit exceeds ₩60,000,000 or rent exceeds ₩300,000; foreign parties included. Ask how shared/goshiwon arrangements apply |
| Deposit priority | Possession plus the required registration gives qualifying third-party effect from the following day; fixed date adds a different priority condition. Neither is deposit insurance |

For the first task, see [registration law](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLawLinkInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsId=001707&lsJoLnkSeq=900532945&print=print), [MOJ process](https://www.immigration.go.kr/bbs/moj/93/559234/artclView.do) and [student checklist](https://www.easylaw.go.kr/CSP/CnpClsMain.laf?popMenu=ov&csmSeq=2853&ccfNo=2&cciNo=3&cnpClsNo=1). Reserve through [HiKorea](https://www.hikorea.go.kr/) for the office serving your home address. Prepare passport, application, photo (currently 3.5 × 4.5 cm), residence evidence, fee and current D-2 papers; confirm enrollment and health/TB requirements. Official help: 1345. Keep receipt, address and card-delivery instructions; ask immediately how to update an address while an application is pending.

A dorm/goshiwon may provide occupancy confirmation; an own-name lease should identify your exact unit. For another-name/shared accommodation, confirm provider identity, underlying residence evidence, permission and accommodation confirmation with the office before paying. [Dongjak's examples](https://www.dongjak.go.kr/portal/main/contents.do?menuNo=201136) are address-change examples, not a nationwide first-registration guarantee; its old deadline is not used.

For a later move, use [national law](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkProc.do?joNo=003600000&lsNm=%EC%B6%9C%EC%9E%85%EA%B5%AD%EA%B4%80%EB%A6%AC%EB%B2%95&mode=2) and [Government24 guidance](https://www.gov.kr/main?a=AA020InfoCappViewApp&HighCtgCD=A01010&CappBizCD=12700000026). Take card/passport, form and residence evidence to the competent office. Online HiKorea reporting is applicant-only, subject to authentication/eligibility, and does not add a handwritten address to the card. Keep confirmation. [Lease reporting](https://www.easylaw.go.kr/CSP/CnpClsMain.laf?popMenu=ov&csmSeq=629&ccfNo=2&cciNo=2&cnpClsNo=5) is separate.

For deposit protection, read the [housing registration rule](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkProc.do?joNo=000300000&lsNm=%EC%A3%BC%ED%83%9D%EC%9E%84%EB%8C%80%EC%B0%A8%EB%B3%B4%ED%98%B8%EB%B2%95&mode=2), [foreign-registration substitution](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkProc.do?joNo=008802000&lsNm=%EC%B6%9C%EC%9E%85%EA%B5%AD%EA%B4%80%EB%A6%AC%EB%B2%95&mode=2) and [fixed-date rule](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkProc.do?joNo=000302000&lsNm=%EC%A3%BC%ED%83%9D%EC%9E%84%EB%8C%80%EC%B0%A8%EB%B3%B4%ED%98%B8%EB%B2%95&mode=2). **Do not wait 90 days to think about priority. A fixed date does not erase an earlier mortgage.** If registration is pending, or coverage/guarantee eligibility is unclear, get case-specific help before a large transfer.

## Make arrival flexible, not expensive

Confirm Sinchon/programme eligibility, your housing ceiling and maximum deposit exposure first. Watch the real dorm notice, prepare the applicant login and keep low-deposit backups. Once dates are known, get a room-specific written quote, align arrival/key dates and spell out the full term, early exit, deductions and deposit-return timing. If you cannot inspect a studio safely, a short low-deposit stay can buy time. Do not prepay a year just to feel settled. After arrival, inspect, document, register promptly and calendar extension/notice dates.
