**Worried about paying a deposit from abroad, then discovering you cannot cook or the commute is too long? Start with a legal HDB master room near Buona Vista, Commonwealth or Clementi, and aim for S$1,700–1,900 including bills. That is a planning target, not a room quote. A well-run condo common room can also work if its house rules and actual journey suit you better.**

This example is for a 27-year-old Malaysian engineer earning S$7,000/month, holding an Employment Pass (EP), and starting work at one-north on March 1, 2027. Gender was not supplied, so women-only adverts are not universal recommendations. Chinese-speaking renters can use the same comparisons without assuming the same nationality or pass eligibility. All money is SGD, without conversion.

**No fresh self-contained studio was verified, including under S$2,000.** The older S$2,200 studio below is a flagged comparison, not a current candidate or confirmed vacancy. ## Decide whether your ceiling includes bills

“Common room” usually means a shared bathroom; “master room” usually means an attached bathroom. Neither promises your own kitchen, unrestricted guests or permission to cook. Get those terms in writing before paying.

| Choice | Asking rents in this sample | What to weigh |
|---|---|---|
| HDB common room | S$1,200–1,400 | Shared facilities; some bills included. A from-price is not your final quote |
| HDB master room | S$1,400–1,600 | Own bathroom usually, but still a shared home; bills and owner rules vary |
| Condo common room | S$1,490–2,000 | Includes an undated comparison at the top end; maintenance and bills vary |
| Condo master room | S$2,300 plus bills, undated comparison only | Over budget; attached bathroom not explicitly confirmed in that advert; not a studio |
| Self-contained studio | Fresh price not verified; older comparison S$2,200 | Own bathroom and kitchenette advertised, but stale availability and unknown bills |

If bills are extra, allow an estimated S$80–150/month for a room or S$150–250 for a studio; heavy aircon use can cost more. Keep base rent around S$1,800 if S$2,000 is a strict all-in limit. These are allowances, not market medians or guaranteed utility caps.

## Check the legal rental before committing

The dated rules below help you ask the right questions; recheck them for your pass, address and signing date. Use HDB's [open-market regulations](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/renting-a-flat/renting-from-open-market/regulations) and [tenant eligibility](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/renting-a-flat/renting-from-open-market/eligibility), the [CEA tenant guide](https://www.cea.gov.sg/consumers/transacting-on-your-own/renting-an-hdb-flat-or-bedroom/) and [government homeowner explainer](https://www.gov.sg/explainers/renting-out-your-hdb-flat/) as starting points. Before paying, confirm your pass eligibility, the approval and the permitted occupants for the exact address; check the later occupancy extension below too.

| Housing | What to confirm |
|---|---|
| HDB room | Minimum 6 months, even if someone offers a one-month trial. EP is an eligible pass but needs at least 6 months remaining at the owner's application; tourist status is not enough. If your pass is not issued yet, confirm the paperwork sequence |
| Owner-bedroom HDB rental | Owner/authorised occupiers must remain resident. Ask for HDB approval covering you, your authorised-tenant record and the approved bedroom—not a partition cubicle. “No registration needed” is a reason to stop |
| HDB bedroom occupancy | 3-room flat: one rentable bedroom, 6 total occupants. 4-room or larger: two rentable bedrooms, up to 8 total occupants with required approval through December 31, 2028. Owners count; one- and two-room flats cannot rent spare bedrooms |
| Nationality and approval | Malaysians are exempt from the whole-flat non-citizen quota; bedroom rentals are outside that quota anyway. Approval up to 3 years if all tenants are Singaporean/Malaysian; otherwise up to 2 years. These are approval limits, not required lease terms |
| Private room or studio | At least 3 consecutive months. Smaller homes: 6 unrelated people. Homes of at least 90 m² can register for 8 through December 31, 2028; the higher cap is not automatic. A landlord may still require a year |

For the room and approval checks, see [HDB bedroom regulations](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/home-ownership/renting-out-a-flat-or-bedrooms/renting-out-bedrooms/regulations) and [bedroom eligibility](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/home-ownership/renting-out-a-flat-or-bedrooms/renting-out-bedrooms/eligibility). The [HDB extension](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/hdb-pulse/news/2026/extension-of-temporary-relaxation-of-occupancy-cap-for-rental-of-hdb-flats-and-private-residential) and [URA rules](https://www.ura.gov.sg/guidelines/property-and-business-owners/property/renting-property/) extend the relevant higher caps beyond the government explainer's older end date. Do not copy its superseded footnote.

## Have move-in cash ready, not just the first rent

For an ordinary lease of 4 years or less, [IRAS duty](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/renting-a-property) is 0.4% of total lease rent, using market rent if higher, including relevant service/furnishing charges. Round down to whole dollars, minimum S$1. The examples assume contractual rent is at least market rent and no extra dutiable charges.

| Duty example | Amount |
|---|---:|
| S$1,800 × 12 months × 0.004, rounded down | S$86 |
| S$2,000 × 12 months × 0.004, rounded down | S$96 |
| S$2,000 × 24 months × 0.004, rounded down | S$192 |

Agree who pays and budget as tenant. [IRAS signing deadlines](https://ask.gov.sg/iras/questions/cm5nfk8ki002j48aoydvnxvn8) are 14 days after first signing in Singapore, or 30 days after receipt in Singapore when signed overseas. Keep the stamp certificate.

The [published deposit convention](https://www.99.co/singapore/insider/how-to-rent-a-place-in-singapore-a-guide/) is one month's rent for a one-year lease, two months for two years—not a fixed official tariff. Negotiate refund timing, deductions and inventory. If a checked, written holding deposit rolls into the security deposit, do not count or pay it twice. Refundable does not mean recovery is guaranteed.

[CEA says commission is negotiated](https://www.cea.gov.sg/consumers/engaging-a-property-agent/what-to-take-note-of-when-engaging-a-property-agent/), with no fixed rate; document it and clarify GST. If you did not appoint a tenant agent, dealing with the landlord's agent normally means no tenant-agent fee. If you appoint your own, the [commercial planning convention](https://www.99.co/singapore/insider/property-agent-commission/) is half a month's rent for one year or a month's rent for two years, with exceptions. No agent can represent and collect from both sides.

| Cash example: S$1,800 room, 12 months | Amount |
|---|---:|
| First rent | S$1,800 |
| Negotiated refundable deposit assumption | S$1,800 |
| Duty under assumptions above | S$86 |
| Total without own tenant agent | S$3,686 |
| Own-agent half-month convention, if agreed | S$900 |
| Total with own agent, before any agency GST | S$4,586 |
| Additional moving/bedding/setup allowance, estimated | S$300–600 |

Hotel nights are extra. The deposit is locked-up cash, not monthly spending or an emergency reserve. A diplomatic/job-loss/transfer exit clause is negotiated, not automatic; read its trigger, notice and repayment terms before signing.

## Pause before money leaves your account

- Open the real [CEA Public Register](https://eservices.cea.gov.sg/aceas/public-register/) yourself. Search the advert's actual phone number, then match name, photo, registration number, agency and current status using [CEA's check guidance](https://www.cea.gov.sg/consumers/engaging-a-property-agent/check-if-your-property-agent-is-registered/). No advertiser here has been certified through that register.
- Call the registered number yourself. Incoming caller ID can be spoofed. [CEA's scam examples](https://www.cea.gov.sg/consumers/rental-scams/) reject the claim that a new number needs 14 days to update: changes are immediate. A copied agent card or portal advert proves neither identity nor ownership.
- See the actual room before paying—not just a lobby or stock photos. Stop for pressure to pay for a viewing, a changed address, an unexplained bargain or an overseas-owner transfer demand. Verify landlord identity, authority to let, contract and payee; an owner-direct advert still needs those checks.
- Rent/deposit goes directly to the verified landlord; commission goes to the property agency, not the agent's personal account. HDB agents must not handle rental deposits or monthly rent.
- Check mould, AC, water pressure, desk, Wi-Fi, noise, resident count and escape route. Put cooking, guests, laundry, bill caps/overruns, aircon hours/servicing, cleaning, repairs and early termination in writing. Photograph defects and sign the inventory at handover.

## Leave room in the salary for life and tax

For this EP-holder example, no employee CPF is assumed; the [CPF FAQ](https://ask.gov.sg/cpf/questions/cm0f52l2w02zfpz7ahr1y5h85) distinguishes foreign employees from citizens/PRs. These are spending estimates, not official living-cost rates. Bonus, debt, dependants, remittances and Malaysian tax liability are excluded. Check employer medical cover; the buffer is not an insurance quote.

| Monthly cash plan, SGD | Comfortable room | At rent ceiling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Gross salary | 7,000 | 7,000 |
| Rent | 1,600 | 2,000 |
| Bills/Wi-Fi allowance | 120 | 150 |
| Food/groceries | 650 | 650 |
| MRT/bus | 130 | 130 |
| Mobile | 20 | 20 |
| Medical/insurance buffer | 150 | 150 |
| Social/household/personal | 400 | 400 |
| Trips home/travel sinking fund | 200 | 200 |
| Income-tax reserve | 320 | 320 |
| Total spent/set aside | 3,590 | 4,020 |
| Left for saving/unplanned costs | 3,410 | 2,980 |

Do not add bills twice if already included. The ceiling example actually costs S$2,150 all-in for housing, above a strict S$2,000 limit. A studio with S$250 bills instead leaves S$2,880. The comfortable example totals S$3,590 and leaves S$3,410; replace every allowance with your own commitments before deciding it is comfortable for you.

At the dated [resident tax rates](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/basics-of-individual-income-tax/tax-residency-and-tax-rates/individual-income-tax-rates), S$84,000 chargeable annual income gives S$3,810 before reliefs. March–December 2027 salary assumes 10 × S$7,000 = S$70,000, giving S$2,650 before reliefs, normally assessed in YA2028. S$320/month is a cautious full-year reserve, not a payroll deduction or assessment. Benefits, bonuses, reliefs and future rates can change it.

[Tax residence](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/basics-of-individual-income-tax/tax-residency-and-tax-rates/working-out-my-tax-residency) also matters: at least 183 days staying/working in 2027 supports resident treatment; leaving early can change it. Non-resident employment income is generally taxed at 15% or resident rates, whichever produces higher tax. A qualifying work-pass provisional treatment can be reviewed at departure. Recheck the rules and your cross-border situation before relying on the reserve.

## A calmer route to moving in

| When | Next step |
|---|---|
| November–December 2026 | Use prices to choose non-negotiables; do not pay months of rent merely to hold a room without a written deal you understand |
| January 2027 | Refresh the shortlist; ask for a late-February / March 1 start, single occupancy, EP timing, exact term and all-in quote |
| February | Arrange in-person viewings. Bridge gaps with a hotel or properly authorised serviced accommodation, not a week in an ordinary HDB/condo rental |
| Before signing | Confirm room, approved occupants, pass eligibility, written rules, deposit/exit terms, verified payee and duty responsibilities; save the advert and promises |
| Handover | Check keys/cards, defects, inventory and bills; keep lease, relevant HDB approval, payment receipts and stamp certificate together |

A comfortable legal room can make the first year easier than a “studio” that is really a partitioned bedroom. Choose the home whose rules you can live with, then test the real commute. No landlord was contacted, application made, payment sent or account created for this plan.
