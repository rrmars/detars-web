You are a Tokyo graphic designer with sales of ¥7,200,000 a year, about 70% of it from corporate clients. You are tax-exempt today, and some clients have asked you to register as a qualified invoice issuer or accept a lower price. You want to know whether to register, when, and what it will cost. **Short answer: register with an effective date of 1 January 2027, unless your main corporate clients agree in writing to keep working with you for a price cut of no more than about 3.6% of the tax-excluded price you charge those corporate clients (for 2027 and 2028). Use the 3割特例 (30% of output tax) in 2027 and 2028: about ¥183,600 a year in the base case, about 2.55% of sales. Then switch to simplified taxation from 2029, about ¥306,000 a year, which assumes graphic design counts as Type 5 (unconfirmed).**

This is a judgement about the risk of losing clients, not a result of the arithmetic: the price-cut table in the second section shows that a matching price cut can cost less than registering until 2030. Our suggested first action is to file the e-Tax registration application by 30 November 2026, with the desired registration date set to 1 January 2027. That date is our own recommended target, not an official deadline. This guide was written on 8 November 2026 for a blue-return filer. **It is general information, not tax advice; confirm the details with a 税理士 or your tax office.** "Unconfirmed" marks anything that is not stated on an official page, and "assumed" marks our own planning figure. The source materials are not published.

**Two things you may have heard are out of date.** First, the 2割特例 (20% of output tax) ends with eligible tax periods that include days up to 30 September 2026, not with transactions on that date: an individual's ordinary annual 2026 tax period can still qualify. For individuals, a 3割特例 applies to 2027 and 2028 only. Second, the older "80% then 50%" schedule for the transitional deduction was revised in the FY2026 tax reform: it is now 80% → 70% → 50% → 30% → 0%, with dates in the second section below. Both changes are on the National Tax Agency (NTA) page for the FY2026 tax reform and its leaflet.

## What you pay: consumption tax by method

Everything here is our assumption, not fact: sales of ¥7,200,000 tax-excluded in both years; taxable sales of ¥10,000,000 or less in 2025 and in January–June 2026 (the 特定期間 test); 70% corporate clients; 15% individuals (domestic, 10% tax) and 15% overseas (zero-rated); sales spread evenly through the year; purchases of ¥1,000,000 tax-included, all from registered suppliers; and payable tax rounded down to ¥100. The figures are the consumption tax payable to the tax office, assuming you keep your prices unchanged. The 2026 figures are conditional full-year illustrations, not a bill for registering now. We assume an existing business using the ordinary annual tax period; its registration cannot now be backdated to cover all of 2026 (see the timing section).

Base case (15% overseas):

| Method | 2026 (full-year what-if; eligibility conditional) | 2027 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2割特例 (20% of output tax) | ¥122,400 | not available (ended) |
| 3割特例 (30% of output tax) | not available (not in force) | **¥183,600** |
| Simplified, Type 5 (50% deemed purchases; assumes design is Type 5, unconfirmed) | ¥306,000 | ¥306,000 |
| General (¥1,000,000 purchases) | ¥521,000 | ¥521,000 |

Sensitivity to the share of sales that is overseas (zero-rated):

| Overseas share | 2026 2割 | 2027 3割 | Simplified (both years) | General (both years) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0% (all domestic, worst case) | ¥144,000 | ¥216,000 | ¥360,000 | ¥629,000 |
| 15% (base) | ¥122,400 | ¥183,600 | ¥306,000 | ¥521,000 |
| 30% | ¥100,800 | ¥151,200 | ¥252,000 | ¥413,000 |

With taxable purchases of ¥500,000 or ¥2,000,000 instead, the general method in the base case comes to ¥566,500 or ¥430,100. Simplified taxation (Type 5, 50%) beats the general method unless your taxable purchases are very large, and the 3割特例 beats both while it lasts. Graphic design counting as Type 5 (service business) is **unconfirmed**: the NTA classifies by industry division and does not name design.

**Rules behind the table.** The 3割特例 requires an individual, taxable sales of ¥10,000,000 or less in the base period (2 years earlier: 2025 for 2027), and registration; no advance filing is needed, only a note on the return. The 2割特例 ends with eligible tax periods including 30 September 2026; later registration alone does not exclude an ordinary annual individual tax period. Simplified taxation requires a 簡易課税制度選択届出書 (notification of election) and then a 2-year commitment.

The 2026 2割 illustration also requires checking the 2024 base period and January–June 2025 specified period, plus exclusions such as elected shortened tax periods and the applicable inheritance or asset rules. Those facts are not established here; the assumptions above address the 2027 sales tests instead.

## If you stay exempt: what your clients lose

A client can deduct only part of the consumption tax on invoices from sellers who are not registered. The share they may deduct under the revised schedule is:

| Period | Share the client may deduct | Client's undeductible tax (% of price) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Until 30 September 2026 | 80% | 2% |
| 1 October 2026 to 30 September 2028 | 70% | 3% |
| 1 October 2028 to 30 September 2030 | 50% | 5% |
| 1 October 2030 to 30 September 2031 | 30% | 7% |
| From 1 October 2031 | 0% | 10% |

The client's loss is the undeductible share of the 10% tax: 3% of the tax-excluded price now, then 5%, 7% and 10%. Averaged over each calendar year, the loss and your costs look like this (a "matching cut" is a price cut that exactly compensates the client; the cost is on ¥5,040,000 of corporate sales):

| Year | Client's undeductible tax (% of price) | Exempt: cost if you give a matching cut on ¥5,040,000 corporate sales | Registered: tax you pay (3割 to 2028, simplified after; assumes Type 5, unconfirmed) | Break-even cut (% of corporate price; the 2029–2032 figure assumes Type 5, unconfirmed) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2026 | 2.25% | ¥113,400 | — | — |
| 2027 | 3.0% | ¥151,200 | ¥183,600 | 3.64% |
| 2028 | 3.5% | ¥176,400 | ¥183,600 | 3.64% |
| 2029 | 5.0% | ¥252,000 | ¥306,000 | 6.07% |
| 2030 | 5.5% | ¥277,200 | ¥306,000 | 6.07% |
| 2031 | 7.75% | ¥390,600 | ¥306,000 | 6.07% |
| 2032 | 10.0% | ¥504,000 | ¥306,000 | 6.07% |

**Counterintuitive finding:** the usual advice is "register or lose the client", but the arithmetic says that until 2030 a cut that exactly compensates the client costs you less than registering, because a matching cut applies only to corporate sales (¥5,040,000), while registering puts tax on all sales, and the client's loss is only 30% to 50% of the 10% tax. Clients that are themselves exempt or simplified-taxation filers lose nothing and need no compensation. Registering wins from 2031. It also wins today if a client asks for more than about 3.6% off (2027 and 2028) or about 6.1% off (2029 and 2030), measured against the tax-excluded price you charge corporate clients, or if refusing to register could cost you more than about ¥184,000 of yearly revenue (about 2.6%). This ignores that clients may not accept a cut at all and may simply switch: the "matching cut" is our model, and the size of cuts clients actually ask for is **unconfirmed**.

## Income tax and resident tax

With tax-included bookkeeping (税込経理), the NTA's general explanation makes consumption tax you pay a deductible expense in the year you file. A 2027 payment, filed by 31 March 2028, therefore reduces your 2028 income under that explanation. The NTA also allows an individual to accrue it as 未払金 (an accrued payable) in 2027, which would move the deduction into 2027. Applying the general tax-excluded-bookkeeping explanation to the 3割特例 is **unconfirmed**: the NTA directs special-method users to separate guidance, whose application has not been checked here. Treat the after-tax illustration as conditional until a 税理士 confirms the applicable accounting treatment. Resident tax follows the same business income (**unconfirmed**, our inference; no official page was read).

As an illustration at a combined marginal rate of 15%, 20% or 30% (assumed), the 2027 base-case ¥183,600 becomes about ¥156,060, ¥146,880 or ¥128,520 after tax. Your blue-return deduction is unaffected by registration (no official source was read; **unconfirmed**).

## Timing and steps

1. Apply in **e-Tax Soft (WEB version)**; for domestic individuals, a phone works. You need a My Number card or an ID and the 16-digit user ID.
2. State a **desired registration date** (登録希望日) at least **15 days after** you submit. For 1 January 2027, do not rely on 17 December as the last day, because that is our own counting of the 15 days (**unconfirmed**). Use 15 December as an outer safety limit and aim for 30 November, which is our recommended target and not an NTA deadline. The registration takes effect from the desired date even though the number arrives later (the NTA says about 2 months by e-Tax). Invoices you issue before the number arrives can be completed afterwards by notifying the number (Q&A 問36).
3. Registering during the remainder of 2026 would add a 2026 return, covering taxable transactions from registration onward. It does not create a separate stub tax period: an individual's ordinary period is 1 January–31 December, so eligible 2割 can still apply after 30 September 2026. The annual table is not an estimate for that shorter transactions interval. Elected shortened periods and other exclusions still matter. The NTA's start-of-period registration exception is for genuinely new businesses, not this existing exempt designer. Our 1 January 2027 recommendation avoids an extra 2026 return; reassess if clients require registration sooner.
4. Once registered, you must file consumption tax for 2 years even if you cancel; for a 1 January 2027 date that runs through 2028, on our reading (**unconfirmed**). That matches the 3割特例 window exactly.
5. **Simplified-taxation notification:** it is not needed for 2027 or 2028, because the 3割特例 is cheaper. To use simplified taxation from 2029, file the 簡易課税制度選択届出書 by the filing deadline of your 2029 return, which falls in 2030 (the NTA's example gives 1 April 2030 for the 令和11年分); the 3割特例 must have been used in 2028. If you want simplified taxation already in 2027, the deadline is 31 December 2027. Once chosen, it binds you for 2 years.
6. The first 3割特例 return (for 2027) is due in spring 2028; note the 3割特例 on the return.

## Overseas clients

Design services to non-residents are zero-rated exports. A non-resident is an individual or a company with no Japan address or main office; Japanese branches of foreign firms count as residents. The exception is a service whose benefit is enjoyed in Japan. Zero-rated sales add no output tax and, as we understand the law, still count as taxable sales in the ¥10,000,000 test (**unconfirmed**: not stated on the pages read), which is why more overseas work lowers every figure above. Keep contracts for 7 years. That your deliverables qualify, and that overseas clients need no qualified invoice, are **unconfirmed** for each client.

## When to register and when to stay exempt

- **Register** if clients worth more than about ¥184,000 a year of your revenue (2.6%) would leave or demand a cut above about 3.6%; if you want new corporate clients; or if you will still be working after 2031.
- **Stay exempt** if written agreements keep your main clients at a cut below break-even, or your corporate share is small. Revisit before 1 October 2028, when the client's loss rises to 5%.
- **The 3割特例 is not available** if your 2025 taxable sales exceeded ¥10,000,000, if your January–June 2026 taxable sales exceeded ¥10,000,000 and the applicable 特定期間 rules remove exemption, or in the other cases listed in NTA Q&A 問115-2 (short tax periods, certain high-value asset purchases, inheritance). Sales alone do not settle the specified-period test: where permitted, you may use salary payments instead of sales. Confirm the applicable test before concluding that you are excluded. Your cost would then be the simplified or general figure in the tables.
