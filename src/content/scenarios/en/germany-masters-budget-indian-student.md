**If your family can contribute ₹15 lakh, keep Germany on your shortlist—but do not treat that as a funded degree. A tuition-free, 24-month plan comes to about ₹35.09 lakh, including a housing deposit and safety reserve, at an explicitly assumed ₹110 per €1. That is a planning rate, not a live bank quote.** Solve the gap before you commit, rather than relying on a job immediately after landing.

This example keeps the applicant profile specific: an Indian BTech Mechanical graduate in Pune, 7.8 CGPA, IELTS 7.0, aiming for October 2027 entry. Chinese-speaking students can use the comparisons too; the applicant's nationality, degree and eligibility do not change. The information below was researched on November 6, 2026, not refreshed for this draft. Future fees, rules and availability need checking before payment.

**The other urgent issue is timing:** if you need the newly announced dMAT before APS can finish, an early university deadline may be out of reach even when your academic profile fits. Check that dependency before spending application money.

## Make the family funding decision first

The base case is a single student without dependants, an existing laptop, no tuition, no job or scholarship income and no loan interest. It assumes October 2027 through September 2029, completed on time. Optional travel, a new computer and extra semesters are outside the plan.

Monthly living is an estimated **€1,000**: all-in rent €450 + food €250 + insurance €150 + phone €20 + study/clothing/personal €100 + household/broadcasting share €15 + extra local travel €15. Semester contributions are separate. Replace the rent and uncovered transport with actual offers; this is not a claim that every city costs the same.

|---|---:|---:|
| Living, including rent and insurance | €12,000 | €24,000 |
| Semester contributions, estimated | €700 | €1,400 |
| Blocked-account charges | €227 | €435 |
| APS, rounded euro equivalent | €164 | €164 |
| dMAT, assuming no exemption | €150 | €150 |
| Applications, copies, courier and translations, estimated | €250 | €250 |
| Visa, service and residence administration, estimated | €225 | €300 |
| Flights and airport travel, estimated | €600 | €1,000 |
| Arrival setup and temporary lodging, estimated | €700 | €700 |
| Spending subtotal | €15,016 / ₹16.52 lakh | €28,399 / ₹31.24 lakh |
| Housing deposit held separately, estimated and potentially refundable | €1,000 | €1,000 |
| Unspent safety reserve, not expected consumption | €1,500 | €2,500 |
| Funding envelope | €17,516 / ₹19.27 lakh | €31,899 / ₹35.09 lakh |
| Gap against the family's ₹15 lakh total | ≈₹4.27 lakh | ≈₹20.09 lakh |

The contribution allowance is €350 per semester. The account calculation uses Expatrio's dated tariff; the full-term amount includes a further €100 **estimated** extension/admin allowance, not a quoted renewal fee. APS costs ₹18,000; rounding it to €164 slightly raises the model. Application allowances include document costs: [uni-assist](https://www.uni-assist.de/en/how-to-apply/pay-all-fees/handling-fees/) lists €75 for the first selected course and €30 for each additional course in the same semester. Count courses, not simply universities. No agent fee is included.

**Do not add the blocked principal again:** it funds the living-cost row as it is released. Bank conversion margins, transfer charges and any Indian remittance tax collection can change the cash you need; ask the bank for a delivered-euro quote.

|---|---:|

| Base €31,899 at ₹105 / ₹110 / ₹115 per euro | ₹33.49 / ₹35.09 / ₹36.68 lakh |
| Stuttgart: replace €1,400 contributions with 4 × €184, add 4 × €1,500 tuition | €37,235 / ₹40.96 lakh, before extra rent |

A cheaper room helps, but does not close the whole gap. Even illustrative earnings of €400 net/month for 18 months yield €7,200 / ₹7.92 lakh—not a wage quote or job promise, and not deducted above. A six-month delay adds roughly €6,000 living costs plus another semester contribution. Search [DAAD's scholarship database](https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/stipendium/datenbank/en/21148-scholarship-database/), but count only a confirmed award. If the family ceiling is fixed and no further funding is secured, delaying is safer than depending on immediate work.

## Choose programmes by transcript fit, not just the fee

These are English-taught public-university MSc examples, normally four semesters—not admission predictions. **7.8 CGPA does not automatically mean 78% or a particular German grade.** Get the awarding university's maximum grade, minimum passing grade and official scale, then match modules, credits and teaching hours. IELTS overall 7.0 alone does not settle test mode, validity, components or visa evidence.

All charges below are dated published references, not guaranteed winter 2027/28 fees. Apart from Stuttgart's explicitly published window, the application dates project recurring rules into the target year: recheck the actual cycle.

## Put dMAT and APS on the critical path

The [APS graduate checklist](https://aps-india.de/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Leaflet_BA-Graduates_English-1.pdf), valid October 2026, lists a **non-refundable ₹18,000** fee. APS verifies Indian academic documents; it does not grant admission or a visa. The [APS FAQ](https://aps-india.de/faqs/) gives **no fixed processing time**. Allow several months for coordination as your own buffer, not a service promise.

The [dMAT notice](https://aps-india.de/dmat/) dated October 29, 2026 covers all BTech/BEng specialisations for master's intakes from summer 2027 onward, including winter 2027/28, unless exempt. Budget **€150 / ₹16,500**, separate from APS. Confirmed tests are **January 16, 2027 and March 13, 2027**. Registration is open in the dated information; check seats, registration closure and result-release dates before planning around a session.

An already-issued APS certificate, APS online registration before June 29, 2026, or complete documents shipped before that date may qualify for transitional exemption; confirmed partnership/exchange routes also have exemptions. Your prior APS status is unknown. Assume the test applies to a new ordinary application until your status is confirmed. APS can begin checking documents, but cannot finish an affected application without the required test certificate.

**A January test is not a promise of APS before Stuttgart's February 15, 2027 deadline. The March test is not a safe route to Dortmund's March 15, 2027 target either.** Check each university's completeness and late-document rules; do not assume an extension. Verify the awarding institution in [anabin](https://anabin.kmk.org/db/institutionen)—Pune alone does not identify it. Gather degree, semester marksheets, school records, passport and the current checklist; keep the digital APS PDF unchanged.

## Keep visa money separate from fees—and accessible cash

The dated [German Missions in India study guidance](https://india.diplo.de/in-en/service/2756350-2756350) lists **€11,904** first-year blocked principal and **€992/month** withdrawals. At the assumed rate, that is ₹13,09,440, leaving ₹1,90,560 from the family's starting fund for everything else. Recheck the required amount for your application. Formal German sponsorship and qualifying scholarships are listed alternatives; a family promise is not the same document.

[Expatrio](https://www.expatrio.com/blocked-account) showed €119 setup + €9/month, giving €227 fees and €12,131 including principal for the first year. [Fintiba's tariff notice](https://www.fintiba.com/news/more-value-for-you) lists €159 + €9.90/month, effective November 3, 2025: €277.80 in annual fees. Follow the actual funding invoice, allowing for transfer deductions and FX costs; do not assume old help-page fees or a universal fixed buffer. A bundled insurance product does not make premiums free.

For timing, the illustrative pre-travel cash need is **€16,470 / ₹18.12 lakh**, including principal, early fees, travel, deposit and accessible arrival cash. **This is not another total to add to the budget.** Keep cash available while the blocked account activates. The model's second-year spending is €13,383 / ₹14.72 lakh; arrange savings, an approved loan or a confirmed award for it. Continuing-funds evidence must be checked, rather than assuming every applicant must renew an identical block.

The mission's dated guidance generally asks for C1 in the study language for direct degree entry, even where a programme accepts B2. Check visa language evidence separately. Health cover must begin at intended arrival; travel cover is not a substitute for adequate degree-time insurance.

Your age is unknown. The budget allowance is not a future quote; get an individual quote if older or differently eligible.

The university finance guidance above describes 140 full days or 280 half-days annually, or up to 20 hours/week: alternative counting routes, not added allowances. Your permit and job category govern; freelance work needs separate permission. Start German now, but keep wages out of the funding decision until reliable.

## A manageable next sequence

Start with your transcript-to-programme comparison, APS status and dMAT schedule. Check early deadlines before paying. Aim to decide whether the full degree is fundable by May 31, 2027; that is a personal checkpoint, not an official deadline. For the later uni-assist route, aim at least six weeks before its cutoff as a buffer, not a processing guarantee.

After admission, follow the current visa instructions and keep several months of appointment/processing slack as a planning allowance. A July decision can make arrival tight. Plan September 2027 arrival only when authorised and supported by housing, insurance and cash. Avoid non-refundable travel before the visa outcome. Offer-specific enrolment and orientation dates control; after arrival, promptly handle address registration, insurance reporting, account activation and residence-permit steps.
