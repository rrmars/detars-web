**Worried that a year in Lisbon could cost more than your salary can cover? For a solo remote software developer renting a furnished one-bedroom from April 2027 through March 2028, start with €2,305.50/month lean, €3,179.50 base or €4,030 with more cushion, before income tax and social contributions. The base needs €11,679 for initial commitments, or €21,217.50 with an untouched three-month emergency fund. Flights and tax liabilities come on top. On USD7,000/month gross, setting aside 30% leaves room for this budget—but that is not a tax calculation. Set aside 50% and the base is slightly cash-negative. If you really plan to leave after a year, check the temporary-stay remote-work D8 before choosing the residence route.**

You have several decisions to make together: a furnished home, enough arrival cash, a desk open during your calls, and the right Portugal D8 and tax arrangements. The six rental leads below help you start comparing; none is a secured home.

Use the [temporary-stay checklist](https://vistos.mne.gov.pt/en/national-visas/necessary-documentation/temporary-stay) to prepare your signed form, photos, passport valid three months beyond your estimated return, applicable lawful-residence proof, legalized or apostilled criminal records, means evidence and return transport document. Your consular post can ask for more. Ask it what housing and bank documents you personally need: the national remote-work list does not set a universal twelve-month lease or fixed Portuguese bank balance. Do not treat an agent’s usual practice as law. If you choose the residence route, price the separate AIMA documents and fees; they are not automatically included in this capped-year temporary-stay budget.

**Your visa label does not decide your tax residence.** [AT CIRS16](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs16.aspx) looks at more than 183 days in a relevant twelve-month period **or** the habitual-home test. January does not simply reset the clock, and the first 183 days are not automatically tax-free. A year living and working here likely creates Portuguese tax residence. [CIRS15](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs15.aspx) covers residents’ worldwide income. Under [CIRS18](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs18.aspx), employment physically carried out in Portugal can be Portuguese-source even with a foreign payer. If you are a contractor, get a separate income-source assessment.

Do not build your budget around a tax concession you may not get. Old NHR repeal and transitional rules sit in [CIRS16](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs16.aspx); an ordinary first move in 2027 does not automatically qualify. [IFICI, EBF58-A](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/bf_rep/Pages/EBF58A.aspx), requires five prior nonresident years and qualifying activities and entities. **Remote development alone does not give you the benefit.** Before using an actual take-home figure, ask about your exact residence dates, 2027/2028 returns, treaty relief, employee or contractor registrations, social contributions, and your employer’s payroll and permanent-establishment exposure.

## Questions you may still have about IFICI

The Portuguese Tax Authority’s IFICI FAQ, read November 5, 2026, helps with the next questions. Use it alongside advice on your own circumstances, not as a personal eligibility decision.

**When would I need to apply, and what activities qualify?** [AT FAQ 5857](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/apoio_contribuinte/questoes_frequentes/pages/faqs-00643.aspx) explains the activity requirements and registration procedure. The usual deadline is January 15 of the year after becoming Portuguese tax-resident: **January 15, 2028 if you first become resident in 2027 and the rule still applies**. Check your activity and validating body first; meeting a deadline does not make you eligible.

**What goes in the activity and employer fields if I am self-employed?** [AT FAQ 5526](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/apoio_contribuinte/questoes_frequentes/pages/faqs-00643.aspx) points to the entity where the relevant IFICI activity takes place. Identify its NIF and the qualifying conditions. Being a freelancer is not enough: confirm your exact [statutory activity/entity pathway](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/bf_rep/Pages/EBF58A.aspx) before counting any benefit.
