On £34,000, a modest flatshare can work; the studios below are much harder to afford.** If you are 22 and starting in Canary Wharf in **September 2027**, begin with **£850–£1,000 including bills at November 2026 prices**. Do not build your budget around the cheapest advert. A cautious £1,000 room budget leaves **£328.77/month** after a 5% rise in costs. You would need **£4,851.53 accessible cash** to move in and keep a cushion. Replace the estimates with your own bills before committing: neither the rent nor the savings is promised for September 2027.

## Travel and council tax: avoid the automatic add-ons

**You may not need a Zone 1 Travelcard at all.** If your commute avoids Zone 1, compare pay-as-you-go first. The DLR allowance is **21 return commutes × £2.30 × 2 + £25 leisure = £121.60/month**, not a season-ticket price. Check your exact route. Westferry assumes walking plus £40 occasional travel; using £121.60 instead reduces the planned saving by **£85.68/month**, to **£418.02**. Maryland's £150 is an allowance: price the actual station pair before choosing.

Being 22 or unmarried does not exempt you from council tax. [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/council-tax/who-has-to-pay), checked November 5, says a qualifying sole counted adult can apply for 25% off. Working sharers do not each get that discount. Check your actual student status; having been a student is not enough.

With a joint tenancy, do not assume you must pay the council directly. The [2023 amendment](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2023/1175/regulation/3/made) and [owner-liability rules, Class C](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/551/regulation/2), checked November 5, put qualifying HMOs under owner liability. Not every flatshare is an HMO, so ask about the actual classification and assessment. If rent includes the tax, do not add it again. The permitted-payment guide separates tax within agreed rent from a prohibited extra council-tax payment directly to the landlord.

## Before committing to a September 2027 move

After April 2027, check the detailed payroll rules. Re-price housing, travel and bills 6–8 weeks before moving. Ask your employer about pension basis and method, first payday, tax-code setup and any postponement. Confirm the course end date and your actual loan plan.

Before signing, get written bill inclusions and caps, tenancy or lodger status, deposit terms, referencing and guarantor requirements, the exact address, studio tax band, heating and water arrangements. Walk the route to the office entrance. Advertised walking times are estimates; check neighbourhood safety, the landlord and property condition yourself.

The [tenant reform guide](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/renters-rights-act-overview-for-tenants), checked November 5, says most ordinary assured private tenancies became periodic on **May 1, 2026**. You normally need **two months' written notice**, and rent continues during notice. Do not wait for 2027 to use rules already in force. Lodgers, halls and social housing can differ, so confirm what applies to your agreement near the move.

## Questions you may still have

Here are the practical points to settle before choosing a home.

**Can I afford a studio on £34,000?** Not either of these without a bigger budget or more income. The monthly shortfalls are £510.06 and £541.56, before unknown furniture or heating costs. You can still look elsewhere, but calculate the full bill for each studio.

**Why not budget from £2,390/month take-home?** That is the first tax year's seven-month average, with unused allowance and no loan due. For ongoing commitments, use £2,206.80, or £2,240.80 before loan deductions begin.

**Will I spend all £4,851.53?** No. £1,211.53 is a refundable deposit tied up for the tenancy, and £2,200 stays as cash. The £1,050 first rent already belongs in living spending. Count the credited £242.30 holding payment only once, then check the actual payment dates and extra bills.
