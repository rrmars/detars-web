**Worried a month away will turn into unreliable calls and surprise bills? A home-first month can fit CNY 20,000 for both of you, excluding flights: the plan below is about CNY 19,547, including contingency. But it works only if both partners have the right permission to keep working. E33G eligibility is unconfirmed for each of you. A tourist visa is not an established substitute.**

This plan is for a Shanghai couple, one vegetarian, staying June 1–30, 2027. That is 30 calendar days and normally 29 accommodation nights; a monthly rental may still charge a full month. Prices and rules are the November 6, 2026 snapshot, not June 2027 quotations.

My pick is a walkable pocket of Penestanan, with an enclosed quiet home, two usable workspaces and a safe car pickup point. Keep Nyuh Kuning as the calmer alternative. Do not build the month around self-ridden scooters if you hold only mainland Chinese licences.

## First, can both of you keep working?

For ongoing overseas-company employment, investigate the explicitly designated E33G route before paying non-refundable rent. The B1 page does not affirmatively authorize that work. Being paid in China alone is not enough to assume permission. This is a conservative route recommendation, not a claim that every holiday email is unlawful.

### E33G: the budget reserves a fee, not an approval

The [official E33G guide](https://ngurahrai.imigrasi.go.id/web/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/e33g-visa-guide-en.pdf), labelled **2024 Edition**, describes a one-year limited stay, multiple re-entry and no local sponsor. It requires an overseas employment contract, annual income/salary of **USD 60,000**, and the last three months' bank statements with at least **USD 2,000** balance, plus passport, photo, CV and itinerary. Local paid employment is prohibited.

Its published estimate is **IDR 7,000,000 per applicant / IDR 14,000,000 for both**, not a checked June 2027 checkout price. Each partner's eligibility remains unknown. Do not assume a partner's status covers the other's work, or that freelance contracts meet the employment requirement. If either cannot qualify, resolve the right permission with immigration before committing, or make this a genuinely non-working holiday. Check employer approval, overseas login access and any employer/tax-compliance obligations separately.

### Tourism only: B1 e-VOA or VOA

[Ordinary PRC passports are VOA-eligible](https://kemlu.go.id/id/lima/pelayanan-perwakilan/visa-elektronik-saat-kedatangan-%28e-voa%29). Government cost is **IDR 500,000 each / IDR 1,000,000 for both**; bank or agent charges are extra. The [official B1 page](https://jakartapusat.imigrasi.go.id/index.php/layanan/warga-negara-asing-wna/visa-republik-indonesia) gives 30 days from arrival. June 1 is day 1; June 30 is day 30. Leave within the actual permit expiry. A delay into July 1 is not covered by that calculation.

Have at least six months' passport validity on arrival, an onward/return ticket and the documents requested by the [official eVisa portal](https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id/). For June 1, the six-month threshold is December 1, 2027; leave extra margin. e-VOA's 90-day entry validity is an entry window, not a 90-day stay.

One extension adds up to 30 days, for 60 total, at **IDR 500,000 each**. The [extension guidance](https://jogja.imigrasi.go.id/warga-negara-asing/perpanjangan-izin-tinggal/) allows application from 14 days before expiry, no later than the working day before expiry, at the office covering your residence. Start early. Since **May 29, 2025**, the [national procedure](https://ngurahrai.imigrasi.go.id/alleviating-immigration-violations-foreign-nationals-must-go-to-immigration-office-to-extend-stay-permit/) also requires an office photograph and interview, including VOA holders. Online submission does not mean a fully online extension.

The [Love Bali FAQ](https://lovebali.baliprov.go.id/faq) lists a separate levy of **IDR 150,000 each / IDR 300,000 for both**, once during Bali travel before leaving Indonesia. Pay cashlessly through the official system and keep the emailed QR voucher. KITAS/KITAP holders are listed as exempt on showing their card; other specified categories have application rules. For E33G, confirm activated ITAS/KITAS evidence and the exemption process. The budget keeps the levy reserve until that is settled.

The [official portal](https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id/) directs all travellers to complete the [All Indonesia arrival card](https://allindonesia.imigrasi.go.id/) within three days before arrival. Recheck rules in May 2027 and before departure. Do not apply so early that the entry window expires.

Shanghai and Bali use UTC+8. Your 09:00–18:00 China workday stays 09:00–18:00 locally. Keep sightseeing before work or at weekends.

A useful screening target is **50 Mbps down / 20 Mbps up for both people**, not a measured local service or guarantee. Test both laptops together during working hours; upload, latency and packet loss matter. Keep a charged hotspot and power bank. A router UPS helps only while the upstream connection works. Confirm company approval and login/2FA support abroad.

Try a coworking day before buying a month. Check chairs, AC, call privacy and booth allowances. Personal passes cannot be shared. These dated prices do not establish future capacity.

## Getting around without self-riding

The [consulate driving guide](https://denpasar.china-consulate.gov.cn/tztx/202407/P020240717647196049332.pdf) says short-term visitors need a valid IDP plus the original national licence. Motorcycle entitlement is required for motorcycles. Mainland Chinese licences cannot obtain a convention IDP; a translation or online “international licence” does not fix that. A genuine licence from another participating jurisdiction needs its proper category and IDP checked. KITAS/KITAP holders may apply for an Indonesian licence, not automatically; motorcycle category is SIM C. This is not a day-one solution. The [consulate safety notice](https://denpasar.china-consulate.gov.cn/tztx/202405/t20240529_11313775.htm) also advises against renting/riding motorcycles.

## Questions you may still have

### Can one E33G cover both partners' work?

Do not assume so. Each person's eligibility and work permission remain unresolved. Confirm both before a non-refundable commitment.

### Is “fiber Wi-Fi” enough for calls?

No. None of the rental leads states numeric speed. Test the actual desks, simultaneous calls and your company VPN; retain mobile and coworking backups.

### Can we stay without renting scooters?

Yes, that is the budget's approach: walkable meals, shared cars and a safe pickup point. A cheaper isolated villa can erase the rent saving through transport.

### Does the tourism-only saving let us keep working?

No such permission was established. Use that comparison only for a genuinely non-working trip; resolve ongoing employment with immigration separately.
