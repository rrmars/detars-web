You do not need to erase your retail career to start working with data. My recommendation is to keep your job, learn Excel/Power Query, SQL and one BI tool, then show how you use them to make a retail decision. Add practical statistics and enough Python to reproduce your work. This skills recommendation draws on only 7 usable non-student early-career ads out of 25 screened—not a 25-ad skills survey, and none of those seven explicitly offered a zero-experience opening.

This plan is for a 35-year-old Toronto retail manager earning CAD 62,000, with Canadian permanent residence, CAD 6,000 saved and 12 hours a week available. January–September 2027 gives you 468 hours for learning, projects and job searching together. A first analyst role by September is a goal, not a promised outcome. Start with retail reporting, merchandising, operations or commercial analysis rather than aiming straight at an intermediate title.

The job and course examples were checked on November 6, 2026. They are not promises of vacancies, seats or prices in 2027. Your education and computer access are unspecified: check degree screens and [Power BI Desktop's Windows requirements](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/fundamentals/desktop-get-the-desktop) before paying for anything. The CAD 0 core route assumes existing suitable hardware, internet and Excel access; it excludes paid credentials and any equipment you still need.

## Make the hours fit around a working life

January 1–September 30, 2027 is 273 days, or 39 weeks. At 12 hours/week, the total is 468 hours—not courses plus unlimited extra homework. Monthly blocks below are planning allocations, not exact calendar-week counts.

| Month | Hours | Main work and a useful finish line |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| January | 48 | Excel/Power Query, pivots, cleaning and basic SQL. Reconcile a messy file and explain five queries independently. |
| February | 48 | Joins, NULLs, CASE, CTEs, window functions, keys and table grain. Answer ten retail questions and check duplicate joins. Start the sales project. |
| March | 60 | Power BI modelling, DAX, filters and charts. Finish the sales dashboard and decision memo; start low-volume applications in the final two weeks. |
| April | 48 | Sampling, uncertainty, seasonality, correlation versus causation and experiment design. Start the retention project; propose a test without inventing its results. |
| May | 48 | Python/pandas cleaning and reproducible analysis. Finish retention work and reconcile it with SQL. Aim for about 3–5 well-matched applications/week within your hours. |
| June | 60 | Build the retail trading brief, test a simple forecast with held-out months, and practise SQL/business interviews. Consider PL-300 only if genuinely ready. |
| July | 48 | Applications, interviews and one targeted portfolio improvement. Explore internal reporting openings; practise a five-minute business presentation. |
| August | 48 | Timed SQL/spreadsheet tasks and better targeting. After 20–30 well-matched applications without a screen, revise your CV and role fit before adding volume—a planning rule of thumb, not a hiring benchmark. |
| September | 60 | Follow-ups and offer comparison. If no offer, keep earning and extend the search; consider reporting-coordinator or operations-analyst bridges rather than buying another broad credential. |
| Total | 468 | Includes projects, applications, interviews and catch-up. |

Initially try 4 hours learning, 5 hands-on, 2 review/CV/job scanning and 1 catch-up. From May, try 2 learning, 5 practice/projects, 4 applications/interviews and 1 catch-up. Both total 12. If life intervenes, narrow the project rather than making every retail shift end in another study session.

### Build three pieces of proof

These are proposed projects, not completed analyses or applications. For each, include a data dictionary, metric definitions, raw-to-clean steps, checks, useful findings and a short management recommendation. Use public data, not employer customer, staff or sales records.

1. Sales and cancellations. Use [UCI Online Retail](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/352/online+retail), historical UK transactions with prices in sterling, not CAD. Create SQL views and a Power BI dashboard for sales, orders and average order value. Explain cancellations, negative quantities and missing customer IDs; reconcile totals. Without cost, stock or labour fields, do not claim profit or staffing productivity.
2. Repeat customers and retention. Reuse UCI deliberately at customer level. Build cohorts and RFM segments with Python and SQL checks. Choose a fixed observation date, handle incomplete cohorts fairly and report excluded identifiers. Propose a retention experiment; a pattern is not proof a campaign works.
3. Ontario retail trading brief. Start with [Statistics Canada table 20-10-0056-01](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=2010005601). Its direct page was blocked during research; [the official catalogue](https://open.canada.ca/data/dataset/36515a02-cc2e-4f17-a1d8-6bcbfc92bce1) supplies a download lead, but a later repeat was also rejected. Recheck the actual CSV, geography, units, licence and adjustment flags when starting. Compare like-for-like Ontario/Canada trends and test a seasonal-naive forecast against held-out months. A sector trend does not predict your employer's store.

The UCI metadata was inspected; dataset files were not downloaded or validated. Olist was an optional alternative, but its download/schema/licence were not directly verified, so it is not needed for this plan.

The following funding notes describe the retained November 2026 guidance, not a refreshed eligibility decision:

- Canada Training Credit: the [CRA claim rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/line-45350-canada-training-credit.html) and [Income Tax Act](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/I-3.3/section-122.91.html) limit the claim to the smaller of your available assessed balance and 50% of eligible fees, subject to all conditions. Qualifying years may add CAD 250, with a CAD 5,000 lifetime cap—not an automatic balance or upfront grant. Residence, filing history and exact institution/fee eligibility matter. CAD 0 eligible fees means CAD 0 credit. For 2027 training, a claim would normally accompany the 2027 return filed in 2028 if the mechanism remains applicable; the available CRA pages described the 2025 return and 2026 limit, not verified 2027 thresholds. Ask for eligible tuition documentation before relying on it.
- Better Jobs Ontario: do not budget for it while continuing ordinary full-time work on the stated facts. The [overview](https://www.ontario.ca/page/better-jobs-ontario) and [January 27, 2026 guidelines](https://eopg.labour.gov.on.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/bjo-program-guidelines-en-2.pdf) require a qualifying employment pathway and assessment; PR status alone is insufficient. Approval must precede training. Do not quit or take leave expecting automatic or retroactive support; course/occupation restrictions also apply.
- Part-time OSAP: CAD 62,000 salary does not automatically rule it out. [OSAP eligibility](https://www.ontario.ca/page/learn-about-osap) depends on family resources, need, academic/loan history and the approved program. [Part-time course load](https://www.ontario.ca/page/osap-definitions) is 20–59% of full load, not simply evening study. Under the [federal 2026–27 table](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/education/student-aid/grants-loans/part-time.html), a one-person family's previous-year gross income cutoff is CAD 69,987 and maximum-grant threshold CAD 38,474. CAD 62,000 could fall in the partial band; the maximum CAD 2,520/year is capped by assessed need, not guaranteed. Recheck rules for study from August 2027. Loans are debt, and part-time aid is not general living-cost support.

## FAQ

### Do I need a degree before starting?

Not to begin this free learning plan. Employment and college admission are separate: two Konrad ads did not state a degree, other roles accepted different diplomas/equivalents, and exacare asked for a bachelor's. Seneca also has academic entry requirements. Check your actual targets rather than treating silence as a waiver.

### Should I finish the Google certificate before applying?

Not necessarily. This plan starts modest applications once you can explain a complete project and solve basic SQL independently. Google's approximately 240-hour certificate is an alternative foundation, not a compulsory addition to the 468-hour plan or a Toronto placement guarantee.

### Is the free Microsoft route the same as PL-300 certification?

No. Free Learn modules are training; the credential requires the separate exam. Its Canada/CAD fee was not verified. Budget it only after confirming the amount and your readiness.

### Can I rely on September for a job or a pay rise?

No. Even the seven usable early-career examples requested experience, and some posted pay can be below CAD 62,000. Keep working, track responses and extend the search if needed. A relevant reporting bridge may be better than another expensive course.
