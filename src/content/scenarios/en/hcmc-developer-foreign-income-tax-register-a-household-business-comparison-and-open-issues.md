You are paid in US dollars by clients abroad, the money lands in a Vietnamese bank account, and you have never declared it. You want to know what you owe, what happens if you wait, and what to do first. **Short answer: first obtain written tax-office confirmation of your income classification and how pre-registration receipts are treated. The base figures illustrate about VND 3,209,920 of personal income tax for 2026 under a business scenario versus about VND 198,984,000 under a wage scenario, about 62 times apart. These are not established classifications or outcomes you can secure simply by registering a household business (hộ kinh doanh). The lower figure rests on three assumptions below; "unregistered means wages" is our reading of two articles, not an official statement about freelancers.**

This guide follows Minh, a resident of Ho Chi Minh City (HCMC), single, who supports his mother and works as a freelance software developer for a US client and a Singapore client. He earns about USD 4,000 a month (USD 48,000 a year), paid in USD into a Vietnamese bank account, never declared. It was written on 8 November 2026 from public legal texts and government pages; nobody was contacted and no account was created. "Unconfirmed" marks anything that is not settled by an official text, and "assumed" marks our own planning figure. **It is general information, not tax advice. Check with a Vietnamese tax adviser or the tax office before you file.** The source materials are not published.

## The recommendation and why

**First obtain written tax-office confirmation of your income classification and treatment of your 2026 receipts before registration. If the household-business route is confirmed applicable, then register at the ward-level People's Committee; registration alone does not guarantee the lower tax.**

- **The two scenarios.** Decree 253/2026, art 8(2)(c), includes fees for services received by an individual who has no business registration and no tax registration for business activity in wage-type income (progressive rates from 5% to 35%). The business scenario uses Law 109/2025, art 7 (2% of the excess for services; the nothing-taxed band is VND 1 billion of yearly revenue under Decree 68 as amended by Decree 141, but VND 500 million in the law text, see the next bullet). **Applying these provisions to you as "wages until registration, business afterwards" is our own reading, not an established classification. We found no official statement about freelancers. Obtain the tax office's written answer before committing to either route.**
- **Where the VND 1 billion comes from.** It comes from Decree 68/2026 as amended by Decree 141/2026 (read through official government portals, not the decree itself). The Law 109 text we read says VND 500 million, and the law that may be the statutory basis for the change (Law 09/2026/QH16) was not read.
- **Household business or "individual business"?** The tax rules are the same for both (Decree 68, art 4(1), covers both: same thresholds, forms and deadlines). The difference is paperwork. The only documented route we found to a business tax status is the household-business certificate, which also serves as the tax registration. If the tax office confirms that route applies, registering a hộ kinh doanh is the practical paperwork route, not proof of how earlier receipts are taxed.
- **Illustrative scenarios on the base figures (revenue about VND 1,229,280,000 a year):** about VND 3,209,920 a year under the business scenario after the 30% relief, against about VND 198,984,000 under the wage scenario (VND 176,664,000 if your mother counts as a dependant). Neither classification is established for your receipts.
- **How firm is the VND 3,209,920? Not very.** It rests on three inputs: (1) the VND 1 billion threshold under Decree 141/2026; (2) software work counting as a 2% "service"; (3) VAT being nil. If only the VND 500 million threshold applied, the figure would be about VND 10,209,920 a year. **Unconfirmed.**
- **Illustrative tax for 2026 under the business scenario:** about VND 3,209,920, all of it arising in Q4, with the return due 31 January 2027, only if the tax office accepts business treatment for the whole year and the lower-tax assumptions hold. If it does not, the treatment and amount need confirmation; the wage figure is not an automatic fallback. Nothing would yet be late for 2026 under that business scenario only if the tax office accepts business treatment for income received before registration. **Whether registering now covers income received earlier in 2026 remains unconfirmed.**
- **Next step.** This week, export your 2026 bank credits into one sheet and obtain the HCMC tax office's written classification and pre-registration-treatment answer before committing; a licensed adviser can help prepare the question in the last section.

## Illustrative tax scenarios, in VND, for a full year at about USD 4,000 a month

**Base case (assumed):** USD 48,000 × 25,610 VND per USD = VND 1,229,280,000 a year (VND 102,440,000 a month). The rate is the Vietcombank USD buying rate quoted at the close of 30 October 2026, as printed in the State Bank of Vietnam weekly report. It is a proxy: the law uses your own bank's buying rate on the day each payment arises (Decree 253, art 17(1)). Every VND figure was recomputed once by the author, not by a tax professional; this checks the sums, not the assumptions.

| Illustrative scenario and method | Tax base (VND) | Tax before 30% relief (VND) | Illustrative tax payable (VND a year) | Share of revenue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Business scenario, revenue method (2% × revenue above VND 1 billion) | 229,280,000 | 4,585,600 | 3,209,920 | 0.261% |
| Business scenario, profit method, costs 0% (assumed) | 1,229,280,000 | 184,392,000 | 129,074,400 | 10.5% |
| Business scenario, profit method, costs 10% (assumed) | 1,106,352,000 | 165,952,800 | 116,166,960 | 9.45% |
| Business scenario, profit method, costs 30% (assumed) | 860,496,000 | 129,074,400 | 90,352,080 | 7.35% |
| Wage scenario, no dependant (VND 186 million deduction) | 1,043,280,000 | — | 198,984,000 | 16.19% |
| Wage scenario, mother as dependant (VND 186 million + VND 74.4 million deductions) | 968,880,000 | — | 176,664,000 | 14.37% |

Within the assumed business treatment, the revenue method gives the lower result unless your documented costs exceed roughly 97.5% of revenue, which is not realistic; this comparison does not establish eligibility for that method. If you choose the profit method, Decree 68, art 4(5)(đ), says you must keep it for 2 years. Household or individual makes no difference to these modelled numbers, but neither label settles classification. The 30% relief needs form 01 of Decree 398 filed with the return, or an amended return.

- **VAT is assumed to be VND 0.** An HCMC tax-office reply to a household-business programmer with foreign clients says software services are not subject to VAT, and that a 0% rate applies to exported services if the business holds a service contract with the foreign party and non-cash payment evidence. **That reply is guidance to one taxpayer, and it does not decide between "not subject" and "0%". Whether it applies to an individual with these two clients is unconfirmed, and so is the VAT rate if tax were charged.** If VAT were charged, the total would rise.
- **Mother as a dependant** counts only on the wage route. Her average income must be at most VND 3 million a month (Circular 87, art 3) and, if she is of working age, she must be incapable of work (loss of at least 81%); the dossier is filed by 31 December. Under the business scenario, the dependant gives no deduction; that does not establish which scenario applies.
- **Insurance:** no health or social-insurance deduction is modelled. You would pay voluntarily, and we did not find the 2026 premium on an official page.

Under the assumed business treatment and threshold, below it no PIT or VAT is modelled, but the annual revenue notice is still required. Above it, each extra VND 100 million of revenue adds VND 1.4 million of tax (2% × 70%), or 1.4% of each extra dong. Above VND 3 billion the method switches to profit-based (Decree 68, art 4(5)). On the same conditional inputs, 2027 gives the same illustrative figures, because the rules and the 30% relief both run through 2027. Registration alone does not establish these outcomes.

## Do you owe anything for 2026?

- **Illustrative business scenario (if accepted for all of 2026 and the lower-tax assumptions hold):** VND 3,209,920. Quarterly revenue is VND 307,320,000, so the VND 1 billion line is crossed in October and only Q4 carries tax: VND 4,585,600 before relief, VND 3,209,920 after. The Q4 return and payment fall due on the last day of the first month of the next quarter, which is 31 January 2027, a Sunday (Decree 68, art 8(3)). Whether a Sunday date rolls over to Monday 1 February 2027 is not confirmed. Only within that conditional scenario, because that date is still ahead, you would not be late for 2026 on this route, **provided the tax office accepts business treatment for income received before you register.**
- **Illustrative wage scenario (only if the tax office confirms wage treatment for the modelled income):** about VND 198,984,000 for 2026, finalised annually (Decree 253, art 51). **We could not confirm the due dates for self-declaring foreign-paid wages, nor whether any monthly or quarterly instalments are already overdue.** Ask the tax office.
- **Years before 2026:** you said you never declared. We did not analyse 2025 and earlier, because different rules applied, but the look-back rules below apply to them. **This is the biggest unanalysed exposure.**

## How and when to declare and pay

- **Get your records in order and obtain written tax-office confirmation first** (see the bank section below): bank statements, client contracts and invoices, and a sheet listing each credit with the VND credited. Confirm classification and treatment of receipts before registration before committing to the steps below.
- **Register the household business only if that route is confirmed applicable** with the ward-level People's Committee registrar (Decree 168/2025). The registration fee and processing time were not found. The certificate number is the household business's tax code (art 91). Check at the ward counter whether your existing personal tax code is reused or a new one is issued; that is not confirmed.
- **Tell the tax office your bank account(s)** with form 01/BK-STK, sent with the first revenue notice or return (Circular 18, art 4). No text we found requires a separate business account, but using one keeps records clean.
- **File online** through the National Public Service Portal or the national ID app (VNeID), as the official procedure page names them. The older eTax portal and eTax Mobile were not named in the texts we read, so log-in specifics are unconfirmed. Paper is for special cases only.
- **Cadence once registered with revenue above VND 1 billion:** quarterly return 01/CNKD (PIT and VAT lines) by the last day of the month after each quarter: 30 April, 31 July, 31 October and 31 January, with payment by the same date. If revenue stays at or below VND 1 billion: annual notice 01/TKN-CNKD by 31 January. The forms were re-issued by Circular 50/2026/TT-BTC (13 May 2026), which was not read.
- **E-invoices:** with annual revenue above VND 1 billion you must use e-invoices with the tax-authority code (Decree 141, art 1, via official summaries). How invoices work for exported services (content, 0% proof) was not confirmed.
- **Claim the 30% relief** with form 01 of Decree 398 attached to the return.
- **2027, if the business route applies:** same cycle; the notice or returns for Q1 2027 fall due on 30 April 2027.

## Bank and foreign-currency receipts

- **A gap, stated plainly:** we could not retrieve a State Bank of Vietnam text on USD paid to an individual (conversion duty, limits, documents banks request). Do not rely on this guide for that. **Next step:** ask your bank what it needs for each remittance, and keep what you hand over.
- **Exchange rate for tax:** convert each receipt at your own bank's buying rate on the day it arises. If the payer has no Vietnamese account, use the State Bank central or cross rate that day (Decree 253, art 17(1)). The proxy used here is the Vietcombank buying rate of 25,610 on 30 October 2026; using it for the whole year is our assumption.
- **Tax-office access to bank data:** the tax office may require information from third parties where there are signs of evasion (Law 108/2025, art 23). We found no official text making banks report foreign credits automatically. Unconfirmed.
- **Keep for each client:** a signed service agreement, an invoice and a transfer confirmation. That is also the proof set the HCMC tax office named for the 0% VAT treatment.
