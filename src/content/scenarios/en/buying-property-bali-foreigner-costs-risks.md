With USD300,000 total cash, start with a completed, independently checked contractual lease and a demonstrably lawful rental operator.** Do not use nominee freehold or assume a PT PMA solves everything. ## Start with how you want to use the home

This example is for a **Singapore citizen** looking for a one- or two-bedroom home in **Canggu/Pererenan or Uluwatu/Bukit**, with about three months of personal use and short stays for the rest. The calculation allows **90 owner nights**; it does not promise a nine-month trading season. Your citizenship does not establish your tax residence. Before relying on the figures, arrange a site visit, title and permit checks, seller enquiries and checks of actual booking history. Any financing needs a separate budget.

Use the [editable budget workbook](/scenarios/buying-property-bali-foreigner-costs-risks/bali_buy_2027.xlsx) and purchase and rental notes (original supporting file is not published; use the information in this guide) to see the assumptions, not as a valuation. Their figures have not been updated. Dollar amounts are **USD unless another currency is stated**. The [Bank Indonesia JISDOR](https://www.bi.go.id/en/statistik/informasi-kurs/jisdor/default.aspx) reference is **November 4, 2026: USD1 = IDR18,140**, accessed November 5. Ask for the actual settlement rate before paying; this is neither a 2027 forecast nor a rate you can execute.

## Be clear about the right you would acquire

| Route | What you would hold | What to check for your plan |
|---|---|---|
| Contractual lease / marketed Hak Sewa | Time-limited contractual use, not freehold land | Start here only after checking your capacity to lease, the underlying title, remaining term, assignment, subletting and lawful operation. An extension option is not tenure you have already paid for. |
| Personal Hak Pakai home | Registered, finite right of use on eligible land | Treat this as a separate legal route, not permission to host tourists. On state or management land, terms can be up to 30 years, then 20 extension and 30 renewal, subject to conditions. On Hak Milik, renewal needs a new deed. You are not guaranteed 80 years upfront. |
| Genuine foreign-eligible strata unit | Eligible individual apartment-unit ownership over qualifying underlying land | You do not personally own Hak Milik land. Check the project, unit certificate, underlying expiry and use restrictions. The apartment below is advertised as **leasehold**; ask for a separate certificate before considering strata ownership. |
| PT PMA holding eligible title such as HGB | Shares in an Indonesian company; the company holds the asset | Do not treat this as a routine way to buy a single villa. Check investment, capital, eligible activity and Bali's new-application restrictions separately. |
| Indonesian nominee holding Hak Milik for you | Local person's title plus side arrangements | **Rule this out.** Side letters, loans and powers of attorney do not give you lawful foreign freehold ownership. |

For your legal review, use the PP18 sources accessed November 5: [PP18/2021](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Home/Download/154522/PP%20Nomor%2018%20Tahun%202021.pdf), Articles 52 and 67–73, and [Basic Agrarian Law 5/1960](https://jdih.pkp.go.id/produk-hukum/file/uu-nomor-5-tahun-1960.pdf), Articles 21, 26 and 44–45. Ask counsel how the foreigner-domicile wording applies to your nonresident land/building lease. **Article 26(2) voids arrangements intended to transfer Hak Milik directly or indirectly to a foreigner. You can lose money as well as title.**

**Two details are worth taking to your lawyer.** PP18's Explanation to Article 69 accepts a **visa, passport or stay permit** as immigration documentation; a KITAS/KITAP-only summary is too categorical. Still confirm registration and continuing eligibility. Buying property does not grant residence, employment or hosting permission. The [ATR/BPN Decision 1241/2022](https://jdih.atrbpn.go.id/peraturan/download/1109/Kepmen%20ATR%20KBPN%20Nomor%201241_SK-HK.02_IX_2022.pdf), page 3, gives Bali's ordinary floors as **IDR5bn for landed homes and IDR2bn for strata units**, not the IDR3bn apartment figure found in a professional summary. Ask counsel to check the operative table and amendments before a 2027 purchase; do not settle this from a summary.

At the dated exchange rate, those floors are about **$275,634** and **$110,254**. A minimum-price Hak Pakai example comes to **$321,199 all-in**, beyond this budget; the eligible-strata example is **$137,107**. These are ways to compare route costs, **not properties found for sale**. Unconfirmed VAT and shifted seller tax sit outside them, and the apartment example has no quoted service or sinking charge. Do not automatically apply title-purchase minimums to a genuine contractual lease.

## Ask who pays each tax—and whether you can rent at all

Use these dated distinctions to ask for a written tax opinion, not to calculate your personal bill. Check the relevant local rules: Badung rules are not automatically the same across Bali.

| Payment or layer | Dated rule to discuss with your adviser | What you need in writing |
|---|---|---|
| Eligible title transfer | Badung BPHTB 5% of acquisition base after eligible allowance; ordinary first-acquisition allowance IDR80m. NJOP substitutes when acquisition value is unknown/below NJOP. | Get the actual assessment, allowance eligibility and legal object confirmed. Do not assume an ordinary contractual lease transfers title. |
| Seller's ordinary title-transfer income | PP34/2016 ordinary rate 2.5% of gross transfer value, not profit. | The seller owes the tax, but your contract may pass the cost to you. Do not automatically apply this to resale or assignment of a contractual lease. |
| Genuine land/building rent | PP34/2017 final 10% gross regime; linked service/facility charges are included. Article 2(3) excludes accommodation-service income. | Confirm the recipient, withholding and any contractual gross-up. Selling a guest room and paying master rent to an owner are different payments. |
| Nonresident individual's rent | DGT Article26 lists 20% gross, subject to treaty treatment. Singapore treaty Article6 allows Indonesian taxation of Indonesian property income without a 10%/15% rent ceiling. | Get a written opinion on residence, recipient, tax base and the conflicting 10%/20% practice. The 20% of **guest gross** here is a simplified stress, not your assessed liability. |
| Taxable supply / VAT | PMK131/2024 uses statutory 12% × 11/12 base for ordinary non-luxury taxable supplies, effectively 11%. | Confirm the seller's PKP status, taxable object and base, whether the price includes tax and any luxury classification. Do not count on a 2027 housing VAT incentive. |
| Badung guest accommodation | PBJT hotel services 10%; villas/private residences functioning as hotels are included. The calculation charges PBJT to guests on top of ADR. | Confirm registration, taxable base and payment duties. If ADR includes tax, divide by 1.10. Do not automatically deduct OTA fees from the tax base. |
| Annual property tax | Badung PBB uses assessed NJOP and applicable fraction/relief, not simply a percentage of the USD purchase price. | Obtain the actual SPPT assessment. Replace the $200 reimbursement estimate with the agreed cost, and ask about PBJT on your own stays. |

Keep these sources with your adviser's answers: [Badung Perda7/2023](https://jdih.badungkab.go.id/storage/produk-hukum/peraturan-perundang-undangan/2023/perda/2023perda5103007.pdf), Articles 7–9, 12–14, 22 and 25–28; the [2025 amendment](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/408192/PERDA%20NO%208%20TAHUN%202025%20OCR.pdf), whose relevant changes need checking against the operative rules; [seller transfer tax](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/pajak-penghasilan-atas-penghasilan-dari-pengalihan-hak-atas-tanah-danatau-bangunan-dan); [land/building rent](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/pajak-penghasilan-atas-penghasilan-dari-persewaan-tanah-danatau-bangunan); [Article26](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/pph-pasal-26); [IRAS treaty PDF, Article6](https://www.iras.gov.sg/media/docs/default-source/dtas/singapore-indonesia-dta-(ratified)(23-july-2021).pdf?sfvrsn=9be93475_12); and [VAT regulation](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/ad276b82-94bd-4197-b409-af33e2842cd6/2024pmkeuangan131.pdf). Selected national-tax provisions were checked November 5. Have your adviser apply the treaty's Article6 to your residence and payment arrangements.

A hospitality company's taxable profit and a shareholder's distributions are separate layers, not another automatic tax on the same guest receipts. Do not assume a new PT PMA qualifies for a 0.5% turnover-tax shortcut. The [DGT-hosted PP20/2026 commentary](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/node/119950) describes restrictions but is expressly its employee author's view; ask for the signed transition provisions. [IRAS](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/basics-of-individual-income-tax/what-is-taxable-what-is-not/income-received-from-overseas), accessed November 5, generally exempts individuals' overseas income received in Singapore, with partnership and business-linked exceptions. That does not remove Indonesian tax.

**A PT PMA is not a licence shortcut.** [BKPM Regulation 5/2025, Article 26](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/394633/005%20th%202025.pdf) distinguishes **IDR2.5bn paid capital per company** from **more than IDR10bn investment** per applicable activity or project. Property-unit rules affect whether land and buildings count. Capital is business funding, not a government fee to add twice. The [Bali government announcement](https://www.baliprov.go.id/web/gubernur-koster-batasi-akses-oss-untuk-pma-di-sejumlah-kbli-lindungi-umkm-lokal-dari-persaingan-tidak-sehat), listed as published July 24 and referring to a July 22, 2026 release, says 18 categories were closed to **new PMA OSS applications from the third week of May 2026**, pending further policy. Existing entities still have reporting duties. Check the actual activity and operator; this is neither a ban on every rental nor permission for every existing business.

The [Tourism Ministry May 26 release](https://api.kemenpar.go.id/storage/app/uploads/public/6a1/69a/4f7/6a169a4f77a67636581355.pdf) targeted automated platform verification for **June 2027**. Check whether it actually launches and what applies to your operator. A target date is not a grace period making unlicensed operation lawful. Before closing, obtain answers on current foreign eligibility, the full investment annex, the accommodation classification and site approvals. Ownership, a visa, NIB alone or a manager's assurance does not settle these questions.

## See what rental income might leave in your pocket

| Upside | $210 / 70% | 182.7 / $38,367 | $21,463 | $16,904 / 8.5% | $9,230 / 4.6% | $2,230 |

Management **20%**, OTA/distribution **15%** and repair/capex reserve **4%** take 39% of room revenue before PBJT. Fixed yearly allowances total **$6,500**: utilities/internet/water $1,800; housekeeping/pool/garden $2,400; insurance $600; community/waste $300; PBB reimbursement $200; simple accounts/compliance $1,200. Replace these estimates with bills and quotes. Ask what the manager includes so you do not count OTA or staff twice. The cash used to calculate yield includes liquidity kept aside; yearly expenses are deducted in cash flow, not charged again on acquisition.

For context, the dated [AirDNA Bali overview](https://www.airdna.co/vacation-rental-data/app/id/default/bali/overview) gives 58% available-night occupancy and $126 ADR; a [manager publishes an 18% fee](https://balimanagement.villas/). Neither is a fresh comparison for a similar one-bedroom home in your neighbourhood. Get comparable booking records before relying on the $150 ADR/55% occupancy assumption.

**Cash yield does not mean your capital is intact.** Allowing $175,000 ÷ 25 gives **$7,000 annual lease depletion**, with no extension or residual proceeds. The twenty-five-year term is an illustration, not this home's confirmed remaining term or tax depreciation. That is why positive base cash after the tax stress still leaves a negative economic surplus. No appreciation, financing, inflation, exit proceeds, SGD movement or savings on your holidays are credited. Figures are rounded independently to whole dollars; calculations use unrounded amounts.

If you cannot rent lawfully, allow **$0 rent and −$6,500 annual fixed cash**, before extraordinary repairs, financing or penalties. Ask separately about tax on your own stays: **90 × $150 × 10% = $1,350** if the Badung no-payment market-value rule applies to your structure. That is a conditional check, not a conclusion that every owner's stay is taxable. Apartment service and sinking charges, plus full company compliance, can make the result worse.

## Pause before non-refundable payments until these are clear

1. **Know the legal object and counterparty.** Have independent counsel check the certificate, searches, seller or lessor authority, mortgages, disputes, access, boundaries and required consents. Obtain the signed lease start and expiry, exact remaining years at completion, assignment, subletting and succession terms, extension price mechanism and who owns improvements at expiry.
2. **Confirm your actual rental route.** Name the operator. Check current activity and foreign-investment eligibility, licence and location coverage, parcel-specific zoning and spatial compatibility, approved building use, PBG or applicable legacy permit, SLF and environmental and sector approvals. A lawful operator is something to investigate, not a cleared workaround. Apartment management must also permit the intended short stays.
3. **Check the building and delivery protection.** Arrange an independent technical inspection and water, waste, drainage and insurance quotes. For off-plan, require secured milestone releases, checks on developer rights and solvency, a long-stop date, refunds and defect remedies. Resolve 99680's permit-in-process label, CUV-220's 101% payment schedule and BTR-1497's size discrepancy first.
4. **Price the money flows and the exit.** Get a written tax opinion, IDR settlement terms and FX quote, service and management contracts, verifiable operating records, current SPPT and reserve funding. Set out resale and assignment consent, fees, expiry and succession. Recheck 2027 laws before committing and operating; do not assume incentives continue or restricted activities reopen.

## Questions worth asking before you choose a route

The commercial [FAQ from Bali Real Estate Consultants](https://balirealestateconsultants.com/faq/), accessed November 5, 2026, raises useful questions. Its answers include simplified or outdated claims, so use them to frame questions for your lawyer—not as government guidance.

**What does Leasehold mean for you?** Here, it means contractual use for a limited term, not permanent ownership of land. Read the deed, check the lessor's title and confirm the remaining term. An advertised extension is separate. The FAQ's general 1–25-year description is not a universal legal maximum under Law5/1960's lease provisions.

**Could you use a company to buy?** A company can hold eligible rights, but you hold shares and must meet investment and licensing rules. Do not take the FAQ's unqualified “safest route” recommendation at face value. The BKPM capital/investment distinction and Bali new-application restrictions above can change whether a $300,000 single-property plan is feasible.

Before acting on the November 5, 2026 figures, read the [source notes and questions to resolve](#sources-and-access-dates) with your advisers.
