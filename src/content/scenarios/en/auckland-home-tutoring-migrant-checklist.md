You can build a small maths tutoring practice without renting a centre or becoming an agency. **Start solo, prove demand and keep fixed costs low.** For Priya, a former teacher from India in Mount Roskill, the proposed February 2027 launch is home-based and online, using an existing laptop, broadband and room, with **NZ$3,000** available. Offer one-to-one NCEA maths and Cambridge IGCSE first; add AS/A Level only when you can confidently teach the actual syllabus and exam technique.

Test **NZ$55–NZ$65 per 60 minutes**, with a planning average of **NZ$60**. Start with parent referrals and local community channels, supported by one marketplace profile—not several paid lead subscriptions. The aim of **20 paid hours/week** is a target, not guaranteed demand. The first operating-year base case produces **NZ$43,200 revenue** and **NZ$31,081.60 operating-year cash surplus after estimated tax, a provisional ACC reserve and capital spending**. That surplus excludes the original seed balance; it is not a salary, closing bank balance or spending promise.

**Before taking bookings, confirm the actual visa, private-tutor registration position and home-use permission.** Home visits remain conditional; online-only delivery is the fallback if the property cannot be cleared. This guide reflects information read on **7 November 2026**, not refreshed rules or February 2027 availability. Recheck in January 2027. The underlying source file is **not published**; the practical plan and important limits are explained here. All amounts below are NZD.

## Clear the visa and registration gates first

The [Immigration New Zealand announcement](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/about-us/news-centre/upcoming-changes-to-employment-conditions-for-open-work-visa-holders/), published **24 February 2026**, puts Partner of a Worker Work Visas in the open-work category allowing sole trading from **20 April 2026**. **This does not permit hiring:** open-work holders are prohibited from employing people directly or indirectly through an owned business. Check Priya's actual eVisa category, conditions and expiry; her husband's AEWV is a different visa. Do not grow this plan into an agency employing other tutors.

**Private-tutor registration is still unconfirmed at regulator level.** The best reading is that ordinary parent-paid supplementary tuition does not require school-teacher registration, but the available [Wintec career profile](https://www.wintec.ac.nz/future-you/explore/jobs/education/private-teacher-tutor) is secondary career guidance, not a current Teaching Council ruling. It says there are no specific tutor entry requirements, but some terminology is old. Seek a current regulator-supported answer before launch rather than advertising a blanket exemption. Indian teaching experience is relevant; it does not establish New Zealand registered-teacher status. School employment or contracting, replacing schooling, or awarding official credits needs a separate check.

## Make child safety and privacy part of the service

There is no universal personal “Police-vet certificate” to obtain. [Police vetting registration](https://www.police.govt.nz/advice-services/businesses-and-organisations/nz-police-vetting-service/register-new-zealand-police) is for authorised agencies, not individual personal requests. A genuine engaging agency may submit a consented request for an eligible role; the [vetting process](https://www.police.govt.nz/advice-services/businesses-and-organisations/nz-police-vetting-service/information-about-vetting) does not certify suitability. An [MOJ conviction-history record](https://www.justice.govt.nz/criminal-records/police-clearance/) is separate and narrower, not a Police vet or safety guarantee.

[Children's Act mandatory workforce checks](https://www.orangatamariki.govt.nz/working-with-children/childrens-act-requirements/safety-checking/) have a funding and service boundary: privately family-funded tutoring is not automatically covered simply because pupils are minors. Government-funded regulated-service contracts can change the position, including for sole practitioners. That distinction is not a reason to skip safeguarding.

Put these practical controls in place; they are recommendations, not a claimed statutory checklist for every tutor:

- Contract with the parent/guardian and include the learner in expectations. Record emergency contacts, necessary safety support and authorised pickup arrangements.
- Teach in a visible professional area, not an isolated bedroom. Let parents observe, keep household members separate, stagger arrivals and agree late-pickup procedures.
- Avoid routine transport, private social-media messaging and undisclosed contact outside teaching channels. Copy parents into ordinary scheduling, with age-appropriate learner participation.
- Use professional online accounts, controlled invitations, waiting rooms/passwords and no default recording. Keep attendance and factual incident notes.
- Check identity, qualifications and references. Have a concern/escalation procedure and do not promise secrecy about harm.

The [Privacy Act obligations](https://www.privacy.org.nz/responsibilities/your-obligations/) apply to sole traders. Designate a privacy officer—you can do it yourself—and give parent and learner a short notice explaining what you collect, why, recipients, contact details and access/correction arrangements.

Collect only information needed for teaching, payment and safety, not whole medical or school files by default. Since **1 May 2026**, [IPP3A indirect-collection notification](https://www.privacy.org.nz/resources-and-learning/a-z-topics/protecting-children-and-young-peoples-privacy/childrens-privacy-guidance-for-the-education-sector/chapter-5-collecting-information/) applies subject to exceptions; information from parents is not automatically outside a teenager's rights. A parent does not automatically have unrestricted access to every record: follow [children's information-request guidance](https://www.privacy.org.nz/responsibilities/responding-to-requests-for-personal-information-about-children-and-young-people/) on identity, authority, age/maturity and lawful grounds, while explaining normal progress sharing.

Use strong security, private storage and restricted household access. Assess cloud/overseas handling and delete information when no longer needed, keeping required tax records separately. The [privacy principles](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/) underpin these controls. Do not publish pupil photos, results, testimonials or recordings without appropriate specific authorisation, or upload identifiable pupil work to an AI tool by default. Assess breaches promptly; **where the notifiable serious-harm threshold is met, notify the Commissioner and affected people as soon as practicable, subject to exceptions**. The [NotifyUs guidance](https://www.privacy.org.nz/responsibilities/privacy-breaches/notify-us/) was supported by official indexed text, not independently confirmed full-page evidence; no fixed hour deadline is asserted here.

## Price a sustainable week, including the unpaid work

| Proposed service | Per 60 minutes |

| NCEA Levels 1–2 | NZ$55 |
| NCEA Level 3 and Cambridge IGCSE | NZ$60 |

Use the same price online and at home; do not offer travel at launch. The assumed mix is **25% × NZ$55 + 50% × NZ$60 + 25% × NZ$65 = NZ$60**. These are proposed prices, not quoted market facts. Offer a free **15-minute parent call**, not an unpaid full lesson. Invoice weekly, seek payment before lessons, issue receipts and agree fair cancellation terms—suggest **24 hours' notice** with a defined illness/make-up policy. Do not count cancelled slots as paid or guarantee grade improvements. Review pricing after the first **six teaching weeks**.

**24 available slots × 83⅓% paid occupancy = 20 paid hours.** Add about **8 hours preparation/marking + 3 hours administration = 31 working hours**, before unused-slot availability. Fifteen-minute breaks protect handovers and child pickups. Keep Sunday free and do not indefinitely fill all 24 slots plus extra make-ups. For ACC, those 31 total hours matter more than the 20 paid teaching hours.

The ramp assumes **6 teaching weeks at 8 paid hours**, then **8 at 14**, then **28 at 20**: **42 paid teaching weeks and 10 non-teaching weeks** under a 52-week planning convention. Reaching 20 hours after the **14th teaching week** is a mid-2027 ambition, not promised enrolment. Put the high-load weeks around actual school/exam demand and allow for January/post-exam cancellations. At only **12 paid hours/week × NZ$55 × 42 weeks**, revenue is **NZ$27,720**, before costs and tax—NZ$15,480 below the base case, not a calculated downside take-home amount.

## Keep the four money clocks separate

The operating year is **1 February 2027–31 January 2028**. The standard NZ income-tax year is **1 April–31 March**; GST tests rolling turnover; ACC billing follows earnings information. Treating all four as one annual salary calculation gives misleading results.

That leaves **NZ$3,000 of annual operating costs beyond the non-capital launch allocation**. Claim only actual [allowable business expenses](https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/income-tax-for-businesses-and-organisations/types-of-business-expenses), fairly [apportion home use](https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/income-tax-for-businesses-and-organisations/types-of-business-expenses/using-your-home-for-your-business), and consider [capital/low-value asset treatment](https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/income-tax-for-businesses-and-organisations/types-of-business-expenses/depreciation/claiming-depreciation) separately. Pre-commencement costs need individual advice. This model allocates NZ$2,000 expenses to February–March and NZ$2,800 to April–January, without claiming capital depreciation/deductions or an ACC deduction. That simplification does not mean NZ$600 furniture can never be deductible.

### Base case: conditional cash, not a salary

| Operating-year item | Calculation | NZD |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Paid hours | 6 × 8 + 8 × 14 + 28 × 20 = 48 + 112 + 560 | 720 hours |
| Revenue | 720 × NZ$60 | 43,200.00 |
| Assumed allowable operating expenses | Evidenced business expenses; no capital deduction | −4,800.00 |
| Profit before ACC deductions | 43,200 − 4,800 | 38,400.00 |
| Illustrative income tax across two tax periods | 92.40 + 5,474.00 | −5,566.40 |
| ACC reserve, not assessed levy | 3% × 38,400 | −1,152.00 |
| Separate capital cash paid | Furniture/equipment | −600.00 |
| **Operating-year cash surplus after these allocations** | 43,200 − 4,800 − 5,566.40 − 1,152 − 600 | **31,081.60** |

The original seed balance is not additional revenue. These tax figures assume Priya is an NZ tax resident, with **no other taxable income, losses, student-loan deductions or tax credits**, no GST registration and unchanged tax rates from 7 November 2026. They exclude possible Independent Earner Tax Credit and ACC deductions. Tax is personal to her, not combined with her husband's salary. [Tax residence](https://www.ird.govt.nz/international-tax/individuals/tax-residency-status-for-individuals) is not established by a work visa; arrival, permanent place of abode, the more-than-183-day test, overseas income and transitional-resident treatment need assessment. Non-resident NZ self-employment may require an [IR3NR return](https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/income-tax-for-individuals/what-happens-at-the-end-of-the-tax-year/individual-income-tax-return---ir3/non-resident-individual-income-tax-return---ir3nr).

[Individual marginal rates](https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/income-tax-for-individuals/tax-codes-and-tax-rates-for-individuals/tax-rates-for-individuals), effective from **1 April 2025**, are **10.5% to NZ$15,600; 17.5% on the next portion to NZ$53,500; 30% to NZ$78,100; 33% to NZ$180,000; and 39% above that**. All relevant income affects the calculation.

| Tax period | Revenue − expenses = profit | Illustrative tax |
| --- | --- | --- |
| February–March 2027, year ending 31 March 2027 | 48 × NZ$60 = NZ$2,880; less NZ$2,000 = NZ$880 | 880 × 10.5% = **NZ$92.40** |
| April 2027–January 2028, part of year ending 31 March 2028 | 672 × NZ$60 = NZ$40,320; less NZ$2,800 = NZ$37,520 | 15,600 × 10.5% + 21,920 × 17.5% = 1,638 + 3,836 = **NZ$5,474** |

**NZ$5,474 is not the final 2027/28 bill.** It assumes no further February–March 2028 income solely to isolate the operating year. If those months add **6 teaching weeks × 20 hours × NZ$60 = NZ$7,200 revenue**, less NZ$800 costs, extra profit is **NZ$6,400**. Full 2027/28 profit becomes **NZ$43,920** and tax **1,638 + (43,920 − 15,600) × 17.5% = NZ$6,594**, another **NZ$1,120**. That continuation is outside the first-year cash total. Do not simply apply one annual bracket schedule to NZ$38,400.

### Filing, provisional tax, GST and ACC checklist

| Obligation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Returns and terminal tax | Under ordinary rules without an extension, the February–March stub generally needs filing by **7 July 2027**. Terminal tax is nominally **7 February 2028**, projected to move to **8 February 2028** because 7 February is the observed Waitangi holiday. The next full return is generally due **7 July 2028**, terminal tax nominally **7 February 2029**. These are current-rule projections, not personal notices; tax-agent extensions and residency can change obligations. |
| Provisional tax | Generally triggered by previous-return residual income tax **more than NZ$5,000**, not revenue or profit. It is advance income tax, not extra tax. Usually none under standard methods in the first business tax year, but still save for terminal tax. The stub's NZ$92.40 does not trigger 2027/28 provisional tax; a 2027/28 liability above NZ$5,000 can trigger 2028/29 instalments alongside terminal tax. Other income/adjustments change this. Standard March-balance nominal dates: **28 August, 15 January and 7 May**; method, GST frequency and working-day rules matter. |
| GST | Register if taxable turnover is **at least NZ$60,000 in the past 12 months or expected to be at least NZ$60,000 in the next 12 months**; adding GST to taxable prices also triggers registration. Voluntary registration is possible below that. Ordinary commercial tuition is not automatically exempt because it is education; generally **15% GST** applies when registration is required. Online delivery alone does not establish export zero-rating. The model stays unregistered, adds no GST and uses GST-inclusive expense cash. |
| ACC | Self-employment normally brings automatic CoverPlus; classification, IRD earnings and working status determine the bill, including Work, Earners' and Working Safer levies. Invoices are generally around September after return information is available. **More than 30 hours/week from all employment can mean full-time:** the proposed 31 total hours matter. First-year part-time relief may apply, but a short stub alone is not an exemption. Confirm classification, all work hours, part-year treatment and minimum/maximum liable earnings. **The 3% / NZ$1,152 reserve is not an official rate or quote and may be insufficient.** |

For dates, use [IR3 guidance](https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/income-tax-for-individuals/what-happens-at-the-end-of-the-tax-year/individual-income-tax-return---ir3), [first-year payment guidance](https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/provisional-tax/paying-tax-in-your-first-year-in-business), the [working-day rule](https://www.ird.govt.nz/managing-my-tax/make-a-payment/when-to-pay), [provisional payment dates](https://www.ird.govt.nz/provpaymentdates) and the [school holiday calendar](https://www.education.govt.nz/term-dates-and-holidays). Check the [provisional-tax trigger](https://www.ird.govt.nz/provisional-tax) separately from your profit forecast.

For turnover, read [GST registration](https://www.ird.govt.nz/gst/registering-for-gst), [the standard rate](https://www.ird.govt.nz/gst/what-gst-is) and [IRD's education-centres guide](https://www.ird.govt.nz/-/media/project/ir/home/documents/forms-and-guides/ir200---ir299/ir253/ir253.pdf%3Fmodified%3D20250320193924%26modified%3D20250320193924). At **20 hours × 42 weeks × NZ$60**, mature target revenue is **NZ$50,400**; at **20 × 50 × NZ$60 = NZ$60,000**, the threshold is reached. Monitor rolling turnover, not just a tax-year total.

For ACC, check [self-employed cover](https://www.acc.co.nz/for-business/understanding-your-cover-options/types-of-cover-for-self-employed), the [CoverPlus invoice](https://www.acc.co.nz/for-business/received-an-invoice/explaining-your-invoice/coverplus-invoice) and [levy calculation inputs](https://www.acc.co.nz/for-business/received-an-invoice/calculate-your-levies), then seek a personal estimate through the [levy estimator](https://www.acc.co.nz/for-business/received-an-invoice/estimate-your-levy) or [CoverPlus calculator](https://www.levycalculators.acc.co.nz/cp.jsp). No personal quote has been obtained. Published [2027/28 settings](https://www.acc.co.nz/about-us/our-levies-2/levy-results) include minimum liable earnings **NZ$51,632**, maximum **NZ$160,244** and Earners' levy **NZ$1.59 per NZ$100 excluding GST**. These do not settle the tutoring Work classification, personal charge or reserve adequacy.

**Cash habit:** initially ring-fence **25% of receipts** for income tax and ACC: **NZ$43,200 × 25% = NZ$10,800**. That is a savings policy, not a tax rate. The model's tax plus ACC reserve is **NZ$6,718.40**, leaving **NZ$4,081.60** inside that ring-fence as a buffer, not extra spending or an extra deduction. Keeping the whole reserve intact leaves **NZ$27,000** of receipts after NZ$4,800 operating costs and NZ$600 capital. Adjust the policy when tax and ACC are assessed.

## A launch sequence you can actually follow

- **November–December 2026:** verify eVisa conditions and expiry, obtain a current regulator-supported tutor-registration answer, and check actual home-use/tenancy conditions. Map syllabus readiness, prepare a diagnostic, safeguarding procedure, parent terms and plain privacy notice. Seek permission before using former-pupil testimonials.
- **January 2027:** recheck rules, platform operation/charges and rate examples. Confirm appropriate insurance and get individual tax/ACC advice. If home visits cannot be cleared, launch online only. Use Indian credentials honestly and a sample explanation rather than invented local experience.
- **February–March 2027:** aim for 8 paid hours per teaching week. Track enquiries, conversion, attendance, cash collected, preparation time and learning goals. Give short parent progress updates.
- **April–mid-2027:** grow to 14 then 20 paid hours only as repeat bookings support it. Keep room for exams/make-ups and stop buying leads that do not pay back.

The remaining decisions need actual documents: eVisa wording/expiry, address/zone and tenancy, tax residence and other income, expense timing, ACC classification/assessment, insurance exclusions/quotes, syllabus competence and platform terms. Publication-dated individual rate evidence and an exact Mount Roskill rate/duration comparison are still missing. Some tax deadlines, expense rules and serious-breach guidance still need checking against current official guidance; this plan does not clear your personal position. No accounts, messages, registrations, applications or payments are part of this guide.
