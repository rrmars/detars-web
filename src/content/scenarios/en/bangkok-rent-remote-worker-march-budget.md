**Want a comfortable home without spending your whole budget on rent? Start with On Nut for value or Ari for a neighbourhood routine. Compare Ekkamai if you need more space. THB 25,000–35,000 is the housing budget, not your total living budget. Allow THB 49,200 a month in the middle example, and THB 90,000 at move-in on the rent and deposit terms below.**

**Before you pay, ask about your actual March 2027 dates. No vacancy, unit condition, internet speed or facility reliability has been confirmed. An advert or broadband package is not a landlord-confirmed offer or a tested connection. This is planning help, not legal or immigration advice.**

## Start with the stay you actually want

This example is for a single UK software contractor arriving on 1 March 2027 for 6–12 months, working from home for overseas clients. It assumes a one-bedroom condo and everyday journeys by BTS or MRT. Adjust the plan if your clients, work or preferred transport are different.

Use the rental prices dated 5 November 2026 to narrow your search, not to reserve a future unit. The policy information is also dated 5 November 2026, so check it again before committing. Area preferences are suggestions, not measured noise, safety or internet ratings. Keep THB living costs and the GBP visa fee in their own currencies; no conversion is built in.

At THB 30,000 rent, 2 months of deposit + 1 month in advance means THB 90,000 at signing or move-in. That already includes your first rent. THB 60,000 stays tied up as security; it is not another monthly bill or automatically your final rent. Allow roughly THB 5,000–10,000 for setup and temporary accommodation, then replace that estimate with actual prices.

Keep insurance, healthcare, visa, extension-related travel, flights, tax, coworking, leisure and a reserve separate: none is in these monthly totals. If THB 25,000–35,000 is all you can spend on living, rather than housing alone, look for lower rent before committing.

AIS lists 500/500Mbps packages around THB 500/599/699 a month, but the allowance above is not an ordered package. Ask about VAT, installation, building access, cancellation and the minimum contract. A 12-month broadband commitment can outlast a 6-month lease. At your actual desk, test upload, download, latency, a video call and mobile backup. Package speed alone cannot tell you whether calls will work.

## Put the important promises in the lease

Ask for the exact unit and owner, the agent’s authority, and an English-readable agreement checked against the Thai text. Write down dates, rent due day, payment account, inventory, condition, meters and the deposit receipt. Agree normal wear, deductions, refund timing and the evidence needed. Make reservation refunds and rent credits clear. Put break notice, penalties and any replacement tenant or sublet permission in writing. A visa problem or leaving Thailand does not itself give you a refund right.

Check who you are renting from before assuming deposit protections apply. The 2025 OCPB notification, published 6 June 2025 and effective after 90 days, covers rental businesses operating at least 3 residential units across one or more buildings for natural-person residential tenants. Statutory hotels and dormitories are excluded. It is the operator’s portfolio that counts, not the tower’s total apartments. A smaller individual owner is not automatically covered; other laws may still apply.

For covered leases up to 3 years, including 6–12 months, advance rent and deposit together cannot exceed 3 months of rent; utility charges cannot exceed what suppliers charge the operator. This is not a universal 1-month deposit rule. Start with pages 1–3, clauses 3–6.1, then check the appendices and the detailed refund and termination terms too. Those details still need confirming; get help if the agreement is disputed.

For electricity, ask to see the actual MEA bill or a transparent reimbursement at the supplier’s rate. Usage bands, the Ft adjustment, service fees and VAT all matter; there is no universal all-in kWh rate here. The calculator link gave a 403 error, so do not rely on it for a verified tariff or March 2027 price. Get the latest bill and write down the meter and any markup.

MWA’s published residential base starts at THB 8.50/m³ for the first 30 m³, before VAT and extra levies or service charges. Your condo can bill differently because of its bulk account, allocation or minimum charge. Ask for its own water bill and written tariff. Also agree who pays common fees, internet installation and termination, cleaning and air-conditioner servicing; aim for owner-paid common fees.

Arrange TM30 with the owner or authorised agent, or clarify who may file as the authorised possessor. Work to the government guidance and form’s 24-hour deadline after arrival at the residence, and get the acknowledgement. The building office is not automatically responsible. Clause 2.1 has wording that can read differently; confirm it rather than missing the stricter 24-hour timing. Do not assume a conditional same-address return exception covers your new condo. TM30 is separate from the arrival card and any required 90-day stay reporting.

## Check whether DTV fits your work and stay

For overseas-client contracting, start by checking London’s DTV workcation route. It lists £300, 5-year multiple-entry validity, 180 days per entry and one extension of up to 180 days per entry. Visa validity is not permission to live continuously for that whole period. Budapest’s official guidance excludes Thai-company or Thai-client work; use it for that boundary, not as a replacement London application checklist.

London’s checklist updated 8 September 2026 asks for a passport, recent photo, permanent-residence evidence, at least £12,000/THB 500,000 in financial evidence, home-country employment evidence or a professional remote-work portfolio, and police clearance issued within 6 months. As a contractor, prepare contracts and a portfolio rather than counting on an employment letter you cannot get. Clarify any additional non-UK business-registration wording that affects you. Keep proof-of-funds requirements separate from what you plan to spend.

You must remain physically within London’s jurisdiction throughout processing and show suitable residence evidence; a UK passport alone does not settle that. Use the official Thai e-Visa link from the embassy. Applications open no earlier than 90 days before travel, with 6–8 weeks recommended. For 1 March 2027 travel, the calculated opening is 1 December 2026; aim for early-to-mid January 2027 and leave time for documents. This is not a promised decision date.

**6 calendar months are longer than 180 days, and 12 calendar months are longer than 360 days.** From 1 March 2027, the calendar endpoints are 1 September 2027 and 1 March 2028. Apply for an extension before your actual stamp expires. A full-year stay also needs lawful departure and re-entry, or a shorter plan; an extension is not automatic. London describes free TDAC submission within 3 days before each arrival. Check the current procedure before travelling.

Sort out tax separately from the visa. The Revenue Department explains the 180-day calendar-year residence threshold and that work done in Thailand may be Thai-source even if paid abroad. DTV is not a blanket tax exemption. Ask a UK/Thai cross-border adviser about your own work and treaty position before moving; these figures do not calculate your tax.

## A calmer move starts before the deposit

- Check the owner’s ID, ownership and agent authority with the building office for the exact unit. A portal badge is not proof. Pause for copied photos, bait prices, rushed payment, an unrelated payee or changed bank details. Do not send the full deposit after only a video call. Agree reservation conditions, keep signed inventory and receipts, and watermark necessary ID copies with purpose and date.
- Visit during working hours and late evening. Listen for traffic, lifts, neighbours and building work. Try the station walk in heat or rain, including crossings and last-mile costs. Check the desk, chair, sockets, air conditioning, mould, leaks, hot water, drainage, appliances, pests and locks.
- Test private internet and mobile backup. Then check pool and gym rights, hours, fees and closures, plus lifts, parcels and outages. Take dated photos or video together, read the meters and sign for the keys and cards. Shared Wi-Fi alone is not enough for a reliable working day.
- November–December 2026: check jurisdiction, overseas-client eligibility and funds, then prepare police clearance and a portfolio. Shortlist buildings, not a non-refundable future unit. January 2027: submit within the allowed window and refresh lease terms. February 2027: arrange a cancellable initial stay and independent viewings. March 2027: inspect, sign, document handover, obtain TM30 acknowledgement and turn on tested internet and backup.
