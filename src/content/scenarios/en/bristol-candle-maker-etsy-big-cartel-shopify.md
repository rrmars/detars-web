You have customers at Bristol markets. The worry is whether taking orders online will grow the business—or quietly spend your margin on fees and delivery. **My pick: test a small Etsy range for new buyers, opt out of Offsite Ads while eligible, and use Big Cartel Gold for independently earned customers if five product listings are enough. Choose Shopify when its tools earn their keep, not simply when you reach 120 orders.**

First fix the basket: **£18 × 1.4 candles = £25.20**, so collecting **£26 per order leaves only 80p for delivery**. Against £3.75 postage, you absorb **£2.95 per order before protective packaging**. Recovering that postage in full would make the average basket **£28.95**.

This guide is for a non-VAT-registered Bristol soy-candle maker planning a January 2027 launch, with existing market sales of 60 candles a month at £18 each. Prices and rules below are dated **November 6, 2026**, not confirmed January tariffs. The baseline is selling to Great Britain (GB); Northern Ireland (NI) and EU sales need separate preparation.

## Pick the channel for the customers you actually have

- **Etsy:** useful as a discovery test. Its higher fees may be worthwhile for buyers you would not otherwise reach. Keep it where those additional customers justify the cost.
- **Big Cartel Gold:** the cheapest checkout here for your own market followers, provided its five-product limit and simpler features fit. A free shop does not bring buyers by itself.
- **Shopify Basic:** a fuller direct shop when catalogue, inventory or other needed tools justify a fixed bill. Do not assume Etsy buyers will follow you to it.

Track where each order came from. Keep independently earned market traffic separate from Etsy-started transactions; do not move an Etsy transaction off-platform to avoid its fees.

## Etsy Offsite Ads can change the margin sharply

Under [Etsy's Offsite Ads rules](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360000338367-How-Etsy-s-Offsite-Ads-Work), shops are automatically enrolled:

- Below **US$10,000 in any consecutive 365-day period**, you can opt out. If you stay in, attributed orders cost **15%**.
- Once you reach **US$10,000**, participation becomes compulsory for the shop's lifetime and the rate becomes **12%**, even if later sales fall. This is not the UK VAT threshold.
- The threshold counts item sales, not separately charged delivery, tax or gift wrap. Etsy's exchange rates apply; there is no permanent GBP equivalent. Monitor its counter rather than guessing a crossing month from an unspecified sales ramp.
- The ad fee includes charged delivery and gift wrap. A purchase within **30 days** of an ad click can qualify, and one click can lead to several attributed orders. A final Etsy Ads click takes precedence. The fee cap is **US$100 per order**.

At £26, an attributed order adds **£4.68** at 15% including fee VAT, or **£3.744** at 12%. The tables assume **20% of order value attributed at 12%**, adding **74.88p per average order**. That is a sensitivity, not a prediction: compulsory participation does not mean every order pays the fee. With all orders attributed, total Etsy fees would reach **33.07%** at 15% or **29.47%** at 12% under these inputs. The [fees policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/) explains the different threshold and fee bases.

## Before the first sale: make the candle safe, then list it

These duties matter at markets too. A platform listing is not safety approval, and making candles at home is not an exemption.

### Assess each finished fragrance formula

Gather supplier safety data sheets and assess the finished wax, fragrance and colourant mixture at its actual concentrations. Do not copy the undiluted fragrance oil's label. Even a mixture not classified as hazardous can need supplemental sensitiser wording. Your exact allergens, symbols and wording depend on the formulation, which is not supplied here. Start with [Trading Standards candle guidance](https://www.anglesey.gov.wales/en/business/trading-standards/trading-standards-guidance/safety/331331).

Where required, [GB CLP label elements](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2008/1272/article/17) include the product identifier, supplier name/address/telephone, nominal quantity unless elsewhere on the pack, applicable pictograms, signal word, hazard and precautionary statements, and supplemental information in English. Burn and fire warnings are another layer.

**Put applicable hazards on the online product page before purchase.** Under [GB CLP Article 48(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2008/1272/article/48), an advertisement allowing consumers to buy a hazardous mixture—or one covered by Article 25(6)—without first seeing its label must mention the hazard types on that label. A label hidden inside the delivered parcel is not enough. Apply this to Etsy, Shopify and your own checkout.

### Test the actual candle, not just its label

The [General Product Safety Regulations 2005 guidance for GB](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/general-product-safety-regulations-2005/general-product-safety-regulations-2005-great-britain) calls for a safe product, relevant warnings, producer and batch identification, proportionate risk/testing records, complaint monitoring and corrective action or notification if unsafe. Assess the actual wax–wick–fragrance–container combination for flame behaviour, sooting, stability and secondary ignition.

Useful benchmarks are [BS EN 15426:2018 for sooting](https://knowledge.bsigroup.com/products/candles-specification-for-sooting-behaviour), [BS EN 15493:2026 for fire safety](https://knowledge.bsigroup.com/products/candles-specification-for-fire-safety-2), published **September 30, 2026**, and [BS EN 15494:2019 for safety labels](https://knowledge.bsigroup.com/products/candles-product-safety-labels). The full paid standards were not available here, so this is not a test protocol or certification. Do not assume leaflet-only labelling meets the standard without checking it.

There is an important conflict: local guidance calls the standards designated, but the [government referenced-standards notice dated October 8, 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/standards-general-product-safety) did not list this trio. Treat them as useful safety benchmarks, **not an automatic statutory presumption or a certificate**. Ask Trading Standards to clarify any claimed designation before relying on it.

### Decide whether you really mean GB-only shipping

[EU Regulation 2023/988 guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/general-product-safety-regulations-northern-ireland/eu-regulation-2023988-on-general-product-safety-detailed-guidance) applies in NI/EU from **December 13, 2024**; it does not replace GB's 2005 regime for GB-only sales. NI/EU sales need an appropriately established responsible economic operator, technical/safety documentation and the required online identity, contact and warning information. A Bristol address alone does not supply that operator.

For a simpler GB launch, explicitly exclude NI and EU destinations: “UK shipping” may otherwise include NI. [Etsy has manufacturer/operator and safety fields plus an EEA/NI opt-out](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/28211364687383-What-is-the-General-Product-Safety-Regulation-GPSR). [Shopify explains website disclosures](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/international/gpsr), but does not provide your responsible person. [HSE describes GB poison-centre submission as voluntary](https://www.hse.gov.uk/chemical-classification/classification/poison-centres.htm); EU/NI CLP, UFI and poison-centre duties need their own check before expanding destinations.

Also provide [business identity/contact address, full price, delivery and cancellation information and a saveable order confirmation](https://www.gov.uk/online-and-distance-selling-for-businesses/online-selling). Ordinary non-personalised candles normally have [14-day online cancellation rights](https://www.gov.uk/accepting-returns-and-giving-refunds); handmade does not automatically mean bespoke. Budget competent testing and product-liability cover separately. No universal platform-specific certificate or insurance fee is established here; that is not permission to skip product or platform requirements.

## Watch total turnover, not the platform payout

The dated [HMRC registration threshold](https://www.gov.uk/register-for-vat) is **£90,000 taxable turnover**. Combine markets, Etsy, your own site and other taxable business sales **before fees**, including delivery charges. Applicable reverse-charged overseas services can also count. Check the rolling total each month, not just the calendar year.

[HMRC Notice 700/1](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/vat-notice-7001-should-i-be-registered-for-vat/vat-notice-7001-should-i-be-registered-for-vat) separates two triggers:

- **Past turnover:** if the rolling 12-month total exceeds £90,000, register within 30 days after the month-end. Registration takes effect on the first day of the second month after crossing.
- **Forward test:** if you expect more than £90,000 in the **next 30 days alone**, register by the end of that period; the effective date is when you realised. An annual forecast above £90,000 is not this test.

With markets unchanged at 60 × £18 = **£1,080/month**, a steady 120 online orders at £26 gives **£50,400/year** annualised. That is below the dated threshold, not a forecast of actual 2027 turnover. A constant 247 online orders/month would give **£90,024/year**; 246 gives **£89,712**. Those shortcuts help planning, but the rolling test controls.

At the [standard 20% VAT rate](https://www.gov.uk/vat-rates), holding an £18 sticker price gives **£15 net + £3 VAT**. A £26 order gives **£21.67 net + £4.33 VAT**. To preserve £18 net, charge **£21.60 gross**; to preserve £26 net, **£31.20 gross**. These are output-VAT illustrations, not a complete repricing plan.

[Eligible input-VAT recovery](https://www.gov.uk/charge-reclaim-record-vat) can soften the effect. Etsy fee billing/reverse charge and the VAT already inside Tracked 48 postage also need different treatment after registration. Do not simply add 20% to every fee or subtract 20% from profit. Until registered, do not separately charge output VAT. The workbook's comparison remains a non-registered model; its VAT sheet is illustrative.

## A practical launch rule

1. **January 2027:** start the small Etsy discovery test; opt out of Offsite Ads while allowed. Send independently earned market traffic to Gold if five products and its features fit.
2. **Look for 20 independently sourced orders/month repeating for 3 months** before adding a paid direct channel. This is a cautious planning checkpoint, not a platform rule. Free Gold can start earlier.
3. **Choose Shopify for needed tools, with acquisition included.** Etsy base costs £3.91776/order against Shopify's £0.77 variable fee: a £3.14776 difference. A £30 fixed monthly budget first produces a strict fee saving at 10 same-demand direct orders. With £1 extra acquisition cost/order it takes 14; with £3 it takes 204. At extra acquisition cost of £3.14776/order or more, there is no fee-only break-even. Add domains, apps and your time to the fixed bill.
4. **Gold has no fee-only crossover with Shopify under these inputs.** Shopify costs £30 + 18p/order more: £40.80/month at 60 orders and £51.60 at 120. Move because the features matter, not because volume alone makes Shopify cheaper.
5. **Do not buy Grow for card savings at this scale.** Its 0.3-percentage-point saving costs £40/month more before tax. It needs more than £13,333 monthly card revenue—513 whole £26 orders—to cover that difference. At 120 orders, it saves only £9.36 in card fees.

Before committing, check December 2026 platform prices, fee-tax billing, actual packaging, packed weights and postage. Etsy can bring discovery; a direct shop can serve repeat customers. You can keep both without pretending all marketplace demand transfers.

## Questions you may still have

### Is Big Cartel completely free?

Gold has no ongoing platform charge, but Stripe or PayPal still charges for payments. This comparison uses Stripe standard UK cards, and Gold must fit within five product listings. It does not include paid marketing or optional services.

### Is £12.24 per Etsy candle my take-home pay?

No. It is what remains after the stated fees, protective shipping packaging and postage only. Pay for the candle itself, your labour and the rest of the business before calling anything profit.

### Should I switch to Shopify at 120 orders?

Not automatically. The fee savings assume the same demand without extra acquisition cost. Gold is still cheaper here; choose Shopify when its features justify the extra bill.

### Does choosing Etsy settle candle safety for me?

No. You remain responsible for the finished formulation, safety testing, labels and pre-purchase hazard information. NI/EU destinations bring additional duties; set shipping destinations deliberately.
