**Worried your savings will cover tuition but not the whole degree? With ₹35 lakh for the entire degree, start with German public master's programs that fit your academic background, then Memorial's course-based Computer Science route.** For a final-year B.Tech student in Pune, even the cheapest base case, Saarland, comes to **₹39.4 lakh**, leaving **₹4.4 lakh unfunded**. Only the frugal German scenarios fit ₹35 lakh; none of these ten base cases does. Do not fill the gap with hoped-for part-time work, an unawarded scholarship or a promised overseas job.

You need more than a tuition comparison: put full CS and data-science master's costs, funding gaps and loan repayments beside your 2027 application dates before committing a ₹35 lakh family budget.

If you are looking for a master's under 35 lakh, compare Germany, Canada and the UK by the whole degree, not the country label. Your 2027 application plan from India should start with what you can fund and repay.

The figures are dated planning amounts, not live quotes. Some official pages were checked again on **November 5, 2026**, but you still need to confirm each fee, 2027 entry requirement and the rules likely to apply at 2028–29 graduation. ### Know what your money must cover

- **Already included:** living costs for the full planned course, tuition and compulsory fees, health and immigration-health allowances, the selected application fee, visa and administration, flights with a return reserve, arrival costs, books, equipment and India compliance. Shared preparation is ₹60,000, with ₹30,000 set aside for other applications. Adjust that to your shortlist. No wages or scholarships have been deducted.
- **Room for changes:** allow 2% above reference FX for remittance spread, then 10% contingency on completion spending. The 5% entry uplift applies only where tuition or living costs are unpublished, not to published 2027 UK tuition or Australian city budgets that already include buffers. Some upper figures allow extra fee changes. Treat them as cautious budgets, not a prediction of how likely each outcome is.
- **Ask your bank for the rate you can actually buy:** the assumptions are EUR ₹106.6318; CAD ₹67.9140; GBP ₹127.7640; AUD ₹66.3918; USD ₹94.6408. The [central-bank reference](https://cbcg.me/en/core-functions/financial-and-banking-operations/fx-reference-rates) was accessed November 5 for November 4, 2026. Get a fresh settlement quote before sending money.
- **Keep separate cash pots:** loan interest and fees, plus optional post-study permit and job-search living costs, sit outside completion costs. Refundable housing bonds and temporary remittance-tax-credit cash lock still tie up cash, even though they are not permanent expenses. Tuition deposits credited to your bill and proof-of-funds principal pay for tuition and living already counted here. Do not add them twice; check actual invoices and refund terms.

**A blank price is not a free service.** Confirm Saarland and Leicester application fees before relying on the provisional zeros. Australia's AUD100 application reserves are estimates. German health costs assume under-30 student eligibility; get a quote for your actual age and status. Australian whole-stay OSHC is estimated too. Replace flights, setup and city living with real quotes. Saarland's India-compliance allowance includes ₹30,000 for GRE if you lack an acceptable GATE result; do not add the general optional-GRE allowance again.

## Check the loan against life after graduation

| Lower-cost candidate | Base gap ₹L | Illustrated monthly EMI | Completion plus loan interest/fee ₹L | Separate three-month post-study extension ₹L |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Saarland | 4.4 | ₹7,076 | 43.6 | 4.7 |
| Dortmund | 5.3 | ₹8,524 | 45.3 | 4.8 |
| Memorial | 11.8 | ₹18,172 | 56.9 | 5.8 |

The loan examples use the workbook's `Loan!B5:I7` and course `C46`: **10.5% assumed annual interest, an average 65% course draw, a six-month post-course moratorium and ten-year repayment**. Processing fees are examples, paid separately rather than borrowed. Ask a bank for your actual terms; a small gap may not qualify for the [SBI product](https://sbi.bank.in/web/personal-banking/loans/education-loans/global-ed-vantage-scheme). The extension column allows for permits, health and living, and is **outside both completion columns**. Check future permit fees before setting that cash aside.

Try keeping EMI within 20% of conservative take-home pay, including if you return to India. The examples would need roughly **₹35,379 / ₹42,618 / ₹90,858 monthly take-home** respectively. This is a budgeting choice, not a legal affordability rule or salary forecast. The part-time illustration uses 12 hours × 36 paid weeks with a 20% reserve, **not a calculated after-tax wage**. No wages reduce the funding gaps. Check your permit before working: countries use different time units and authorization conditions.

Do not borrow on the assumption that a post-study route will stay available. The [UK guidance](https://www.gov.uk/graduate-visa) gives eligible Graduate applications on or after January 1, 2027 an 18-month period, not the older two years for a 2028 applicant. Check eligibility and the applicable duration again for Canada PGWP, German job-search permission and Australian graduate routes. Ask your remitting bank and a qualified adviser about remittance tax and loan exceptions; the workbook's TCS cash-lock example is not your tax bill.

## Questions you may have about APS

The **APS India dMAT FAQ**, accessed November 5, 2026, is a useful place to check your own status. Start with these questions, then read the conditions that apply to you.

**Do you need dMAT?** The [APS FAQ](https://aps-india.de/dmat/) covers affected B.Tech/B.Eng fields from summer 2027, but includes semester-stage, existing-certificate and transitional exemptions. Check your exact status; saying you are in your final year is not enough. Taking or passing the test does not guarantee admission.

**Can you send APS documents before the dMAT result?** The [official FAQ](https://aps-india.de/dmat/) allows affected applicants to send documents before the test certificate, but required results must be checked before APS issuance. This exception is for dMAT only. Get other required academic documents ready; do not assume missing or updated qualifications can be added later in the same procedure.

For the fee assumptions and questions still to resolve, keep the [source notes](#sources-and-access-dates) handy.
