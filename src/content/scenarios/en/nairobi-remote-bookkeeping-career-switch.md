You can want more control over your work without gambling your savings on the first remote-bookkeeping offer. **Stay employed for now. Do not resign earlier than November 2027, and only then if every income, cash, client and compliance condition below is met.** Your contractual notice may move the actual exit later. If the conditions are not met, keep the salary and reset the date.

This example follows Wanjiru, a 29-year-old CPA(K) in Nairobi, earning KES120,000 a month with KES250,000 saved. Replacing that salary within 12 months is an ambitious goal, not something the job examples establish. The safer route is free software learning, a synthetic portfolio and tightly scoped paid work alongside lawful employment—then a decision based on **money collected**, not invoices sent.

The working hurdle is **US$2,200 collected each month for three consecutive months**, with diversified repeat clients and at least **KES510,000 of unrestricted cash** left for six months of living and business costs. Those are planning judgments, not legal requirements or income predictions. Prices, rules and listing details below reflect research dated November 7, 2026; they have not been refreshed for your eventual launch. Several individual tax, practice, data and account-access questions must be resolved before paid work.

## Set up Kenyan obligations before the first paid engagement

**A foreign payer does not make work performed in Nairobi tax-free.** Nor is professional bookkeeping automatically eligible for the small-business turnover-tax shortcut. The [KRA source-of-income FAQ](https://www.kra.go.ke/component/kra_faq/faq/309) and [income-tax explanation](https://www.kra.go.ke/individual/filing-paying/types-of-taxes/individual-income-tax/473-what-is-income-tax) are the starting point; applying them to your actual services requires individual assessment.

### Ordinary income tax, not a flat reserve percentage

A sole proprietor normally uses an individual KRA PIN. Keep contracts, invoices, expenses, bank/platform statements and KES conversion records. Declare salary and business income appropriately; having two income streams does not create a second personal relief.

[KRA's individual income-tax page](https://www.kra.go.ke/individual/filing-paying/types-of-taxes/individual-income-tax) gives the following annual marginal bands, effective July 1, 2023. Confirm the applicable rules before acting in 2027.

| Annual taxable-income slice | Rate |
| --- | ---: |
| First KES288,000 | 10% |
| Next KES100,000 | 25% |
| Next KES5,612,000 | 30% |
| Next KES3,600,000 | 32.5% |
| Excess over KES9,600,000 | 35% |

Resident personal relief is KES28,800/year. Apply allowable deductions and bands to taxable income, not simply gross dollars. While employed, additional freelance profit can land in bands already reached by salary. The model's **35% of gross reserve is not a statutory flat tax rate**: it also buffers AHL, health and uncertainty, and may still be insufficient.

The [KRA turnover-tax brochure](https://www.kra.go.ke/images/publications/Everything-About-Turnover-Tax.pdf) describes 1.5% of gross sales for resident businesses with annual turnover above KES1m and at most KES25m, but excludes management, professional and training fees. Calling accounting work “freelancing” does not resolve the professional-fee exclusion. The brochure's March 19, 2024 effective date and the [HTML page's older date wording](https://www.kra.go.ke/individual/filing-paying/types-of-taxes/turnover-tax-tot) are inconsistent. Confirm the current rule and your scope rather than electing TOT on assumption.

### VAT and eTIMS need their own decisions

[KRA VAT guidance](https://www.kra.go.ke/individual/filing-paying/types-of-taxes/value-added-tax) gives mandatory registration at **KES5m or more in annual taxable supplies**, including expected supplies; conditional voluntary registration exists. Standard VAT is 16%; specified zero-rated supplies are 0%.

A foreign address or USD payment alone does not prove an export. The [VAT Regulations' overseas-use test](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2017/54/eng%402022-12-31/source) requires evidence of recipient location and use, consumption or enjoyment abroad. Preserve the contract, deliverables/use and payment trail. This consolidation is from 2022, not proof of the current export schedule. **The exact current consolidated export-zero-rating schedule and your eligibility remain unresolved.** Exported taxable services may be zero-rated rather than exempt; zero-rated turnover is not automatically outside the registration threshold. Resolve this before quoting “VAT-free.”

### Kenyan duties and ODPC registration

The [Kenyan Data Protection Act](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Data-Protection-Act.pdf) can cover a Nairobi-established processor even with overseas clients. Client-instructed bookkeeping commonly resembles processing; your own billing and marketing can involve controller responsibilities. These are role inferences, not automatic classifications. Set written instructions, controller–processor terms, safeguards, retention/deletion and lawful onward-transfer arrangements for each activity.

The [registration regulations](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/THE-DATA-PROTECTION-REGISTRATION-OF-DATA-CONTROLLERS-AND-DATA-PROCESSORS-REGULATIONS-2021.pdf) describe a small-entity exemption requiring **both turnover below KES5m and fewer than 10 employees**. Listed purposes can override it, including financial services/advice and debt activities. Have your precise bookkeeping scope classified. Exemption from registration is not exemption from data-protection duties.

The [ODPC FAQ](https://www.odpc.go.ke/faqs/) lists small/micro registration at KES4,000, renewal KES2,000 and validity of 24 months, with renewal 30 days before expiry. Acting as both controller and processor can mean two applications and two fees. Category and personal applicability remain unresolved; these are not a personalized registration total.

Prepare an incident process. The preliminary Act reading distinguishes a controller's qualifying regulator notification within **72 hours after awareness** from a processor notifying the controller **without delay and, where reasonably practicable, within 48 hours**. That is not a universal 48-hour regulator-filing rule for processors. Agree faster internal escalation, and confirm the operative text. Onward cloud or subprocessor use also needs a lawful transfer assessment.

### UK-hosted software does not make overseas access disappear

[ICO international-transfer guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/international-transfers/a-guide-to-international-transfers/) treats access by a separate overseas organisation, including remote access, as a potential restricted transfer when its test is met. UK-hosted QBO/Xero, a VPN and an NDA do not alone fix that. The UK exporter should determine and document the route before access.

Where appropriate, that may mean an [IDTA or UK Addendum to EU SCCs](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/international-transfers/international-data-transfer-agreement-and-guidance/), the required [transfer-risk assessment/data protection test](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/international-transfers/a-guide-to-international-transfers/transfer-risk-assessments/) and an assessment of [appropriate safeguards](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/international-transfers/a-guide-to-international-transfers/what-are-the-rules-on-international-transfers-of-personal-data/how-do-you-make-a-restricted-transfer-in-accordance-with-the-uk-gdpr/what-are-the-appropriate-safeguards/). EU SCCs alone are not the UK-specific answer. Kenya adequacy and the right route for a particular engagement were not verified; do not assume either.

[Article 28 processor-contract guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/controllers-and-processors/contracts-and-liabilities-between-controllers-and-processors-multi/what-needs-to-be-included-in-the-contract/) covers subject, duration and purpose; data types and subjects; instructions, confidentiality and security; subprocessors; help with rights and incidents; deletion/return; and audit information. Processing terms and a transfer safeguard are distinct. Whether UK GDPR applies directly to you territorially is a separate analysis; having a UK client alone does not settle it.

### US onboarding and everyday controls

For US work, identify sector and state requirements for the actual data. Payroll, tax, health-related or financial-institution records can require specialist review and outsourcing controls. This guide does not clear every US privacy rule. Start with ordinary bookkeeping; exclude regulated tax-return preparation, audit, payment authority and custody of funds unless separately authorized and competent. Do not present CPA(K) as US CPA status.

A US payer may request an individual's [W-8BEN](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw8ben) to document foreign status. Provide it securely to the requesting payer/agent, not directly to the IRS; do not substitute a W-9 claiming US-person status. The IRS generally [sources personal-service income where the work is performed](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/source-of-income-personal-service-income), rather than where the payer or payment is located. Wholly Kenya-performed work does not automatically become US-source because the client is American. That does not settle every US tax circumstance; no treaty entitlement is assumed.

For a practical starting setup:

- Use client-owned software, named least-privilege access, MFA, a patched encrypted device, password manager and tested backup internet. Avoid shared bank passwords and default exports to personal devices.
- Use approved secure portals/storage; restrict local downloads, set retention/deletion and test recovery. Obtain permission before AI tools, subprocessors or onward-country transfers see client data.
- Separate preparation from client approval of payroll, supplier changes and payments. Verify changes through an independently known channel; keep reconciliation and audit trails.
- Put scope, transaction/accounts caps, reporting dates, client record deadlines, approval duties and exclusions in the engagement letter. Add currency, due dates, collection/FX fees, changes, liability/insurance, dispute law, termination and records handover. These are recommended controls, not one universally mandatory template.

Do not begin with identifiable payroll or sensitive records on the strength of a badge, an NDA or “the data stays in the UK.” Service terms, processing terms, lawful transfers and real security controls need to work together.
