Moving countries is enough to organise without discovering that your dog cannot travel on the same timetable. **Start now, and treat December 1, 2026 as the latest sensible planning start for a June 2027 move.** That date is a buffer judgment, not an AVS deadline or a guarantee of quarantine space. Starting earlier gives you more room for a failed test, paperwork delay or unavailable kennel.

This plan is for a couple relocating on an Employment Pass with a 4-year-old, 18 kg microchipped mixed-breed dog vaccinated in Shanghai. My recommendation is an experienced relocation coordinator working with an **AVS-recognised Singapore clearance agent**. A lower-cost hybrid is possible: you manage vet visits and records, while professionals handle export/freight logistics and Singapore clearance. Pure DIY airport clearance is not an option for either checked baggage or cargo.

Plan for a pet-permitted private condo, a nonstop PVG–SIN candidate and **at least 30 days at the Animal Quarantine Centre (AQC)**. A passing rabies titre does not remove quarantine. Do not assume HDB eligibility or home quarantine. Your dog may not join your household until approximately mid-July, even if you arrive in June.

Rules and prices below reflect November 7, 2026, not a fresh quote or confirmed June arrangement. Flight acceptance, housing consent, breed classification, the lab route and several Customs steps remain open. **This is a planning guide, not an overall compliance pass, approved import file or guaranteed total.** Nothing was booked or submitted, and no provider was contacted.

## Settle the veterinary clocks before buying travel

Mainland China falls under **Schedule III**, not Hong Kong SAR’s Schedule II. Use the [AVS import overview](https://avs.nparks.gov.sg/pets/importing-exporting-a-pet/import/dogs-and-cats/) and the [detailed Schedule III conditions dated April 1, 2026](https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/30/09b135c8-00b2-4859-8d90-9ee700d2b67b/DOGCAT%2083-3%20(w_feeattd1)%2001042026.pdf), rather than older letter-category guides.

- Keep rabies vaccination valid under the manufacturer’s primary/booster schedule, using an acceptable inactivated or recombinant vaccine.
- Draw blood at least 28 days after the qualifying vaccination. The sample must be at least 90 days and no more than 12 months before export, with a result ≥0.5 IU/ml.
- Use a WOAH rabies reference laboratory, or a competent-authority-approved laboratory in a Schedule I/II country or region. A routine clinic antibody screen, or an arbitrary China-recognised lab, is not automatically eligible. Establish the receiving lab’s eligibility before paying.
- Scan the existing chip and match its number across vaccination, treatment, lab and certificate records. ISO 11784 / 11785 Annex A is the standard route. A non-ISO implanted chip can be accepted if the owner or agent supplies a compatible reader at entry; check its type before considering replacement and preserve record identity.
- Existing valid Shanghai rabies vaccination may support the draw if timing and documentation qualify. Do not repeat it merely because it was given in Shanghai. Current canine distemper, adenovirus type 1 and parvovirus type 2 vaccinations must follow the manufacturer’s schedule and be completed at least 14 days before export. The minimum import age is 12 weeks; this adult dog meets that age assumption.

The final clinical examination, chip verification and certificate must be within 7 days before export. A **government-approved veterinarian** completes certificate Sections I–III, and an official government veterinarian endorses Section IV. Record non-pregnancy and fitness to travel where applicable. External flea/tick and internal worm treatments must be 2–7 days before export, with ingredients and dates recorded. An ordinary private-clinic note is not enough. Carry original vaccination and titre records. No captain’s declaration is required for Schedule III.

## Check breed and housing as separate decisions

“No prohibited ancestry that we know of” is not clearance for a mixed-breed dog. AVS needs records, photographs and its own assessment. Import-prohibited breeds and their crosses include the Pit Bull group—American Pit Bull Terrier, American Staffordshire Terrier, Staffordshire Bull Terrier and American Bulldog—plus Akita, Boerboel, Dogo Argentino, Fila Brasileiro, Neapolitan Mastiff, Tosa and Perro De Presa Canario.

Other [Part 2 Specified dogs and crosses](https://avs.nparks.gov.sg/pets/licensing-a-pet/information-on-dog-and-cat-licences/specified-dogs/), including Bull Terrier, Doberman, Rottweiler, German Shepherd and related shepherds, and specified mastiffs such as Bull Mastiff, Cane Corso and Dogue de Bordeaux, can face public leash/muzzle rules, applicable training, ≥S$100,000 insurance cover and a S$2,000 bank guarantee. These are coverage/security amounts, **not premiums or bank fees**; a guarantee can still tie up cash or security. The budget assumes a non-Specified dog, but classification is unresolved.

No general 18 kg Singapore import ceiling was found in the inspected conditions. That does not settle housing or airline acceptance, and dog weight is not dog-plus-crate weight.

**Private housing is the practical starting point, not unconditional permission.** [AVS ownership limits](https://avs.nparks.gov.sg/pets/licensing-a-pet/information-on-dog-and-cat-licences/limits-of-ownership/) generally allow up to three cats/dogs combined in private premises, at most one Specified dog. Obtain the estate’s actual pet by-laws and written landlord permission for this particular dog before signing. Check lift/common-area arrangements, size and nuisance conditions. [BCA by-law material](https://www1.bca.gov.sg/docs/default-source/docs-corp-regulatory/building-maintenance-and-strata-management/smg10-by-laws.pdf) is supplementary, unconfirmed context—not a legal ruling on a restriction.

HDB generally allows one approved-small-breed dog per flat, not an unrestricted medium mixed-breed dog. [Project ADORE](https://avs.nparks.gov.sg/community-animals/population-management/project-adore/) concerns local mixed-breed rehoming through partner groups, up to 55 cm tall, with licensing, microchipping, vaccination, sterilisation and compulsory training. Its former 15 kg limit was removed. **18 kg alone neither grants nor defeats eligibility**, and ADORE is not an established route for an existing imported Shanghai dog. Do not sign an HDB lease on that assumption; obtain specific official clearance if pursuing an exception. The [HDB page](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/home-ownership/keeping-pets) did not yield its detailed accordion text; the key restriction here is corroborated by AVS, not a claimed full reading of HDB’s breed list.

## Reserve quarantine, then align the licences and arrival

[AQC](https://avs.nparks.gov.sg/about-us/our-centres/animal-quarantine-centre/) is at 2 Jalan Lekar, Singapore 698919. CAPQ at Changi is the border inspection/holding station, not the month-long kennel. Schedule III dogs receive rabies vaccination on arrival at AQC.

- Once the passing titre report is ready, reserve space through [QMS](https://avs.nparks.gov.sg/pets/importing-exporting-a-pet/import/quarantine-management-system/) with chip and rabies records. Obtain booking acceptance before applying for import. The deposit is 10 days’ accommodation plus transfer, credited to the total—not an additional fee.
- A reservation is held only up to 3 days after the allocated date. The first amendment more than 30 days ahead is free; subsequent amendments cost S$65, and all amendments within 30 days cost S$65. Cancellation forfeits the deposit. A long flight delay does not guarantee a preserved kennel.
- Get the Singapore dog licence using a compliant residence, then the personal import licence with AQC acceptance and a colour photo showing the mixed-breed dog’s face and body. **Portal instructions differ:** the detailed PDF directs both applications to PALS; the overview directs import licensing to GoBusiness. Check the current route with your recognised agent at filing; neither portal is declared unusable here.
- Personal import licence: S$50 normal or S$100 express; normally 2 or 1 working days after complete documents, valid 90 days. Those processing times are not sensible planning lead times.
- [Singapore Customs relocation relief](https://www.customs.gov.sg/personal-shipment/moving-to-singapore/importing-personal-pets-as-part-of-a-change-of-residence/) is separate. For owner and pet on the same transport, apply ≥5 working days before arrival with passport, AVS licence and EP/DP or MOM IPA evidence; carry approval. An EP does not automatically mean zero tax. If unsuccessful, an In-Payment GST permit is needed. The Customs GST permit is valid 10 working days from approval, distinct from the AVS licence; do not assume that duration also governs a relief approval. Unaccompanied-cargo relief, valuation and charges remain unconfirmed.
- Since April 1, 2026, an AVS-recognised agent must clear both baggage and cargo arrivals. Book inspection ≥5 working days before arrival, following the stricter PDF requirement. Unappointed inspection costs S$133/hour or part. A routine appointment-inspection fee was not established; that is not proof it is free. The [recognised-agent register](https://avs.nparks.gov.sg/outreach/resources/avs-recognised-pet-agents/) was not successfully retrieved, so the example provider’s recognition is unchecked.

**Counterintuitive arrival trap: landing before inspection closes may still mean overnight holding.** [CAPQ](https://avs.nparks.gov.sg/about-us/our-centres/changi-animal-plant-quarantine-station/) opens Monday/Tuesday 09:00–17:00, Wednesday–Friday 09:00–20:00, with lunch 13:00–14:00; weekends/public holidays are closed. Yet AQC transfers run only weekdays 10:00–10:30. Allow ≥3 hours before CAPQ closure and ask the agent to map aircraft → ground handler → inspection → AVS-appointed AQC transport, including missed-window costs. Late-Friday/weekend arrivals can prolong holding. Airline-handler holding is separately priced and unknown; S$35/day or part at CAPQ is not its tariff.

Baggage arrivals require declaration/handover through Lost & Found and the handler; cargo uses an airway bill (AWB) and handler transfer to CAPQ. You cannot simply collect the dog and leave the passenger terminal. CAPQ is at 113A Airport Cargo Road, Changi Airfreight Centre, Singapore 819985. Crates exceeding 82 cm L × 64 cm H × 58 cm W need owner/agent collection at CAPQ/Tuas or separate recovery from the private transporter at extra cost. This recovery trigger is separate from airline crate limits.

## Shanghai export and flights: confirm the whole chain

The [direct GACC accompanied-items guide](https://online.customs.gov.cn/static/pages/guides/000629014001/000629014001.html) provides destination-required export quarantine/certification. Its published administrative inspection service is CNY0, not a free clinic, lab, agent or freight service. Do not use its older inbound-country wording to plan outbound travel.

The [Shanghai government explanation attributed to Customs](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-KeepingAPetInShanghai/20250909/323071e741b8414aadb8f040e98e9bf4.html) describes an Animal Health Certificate (动物卫生证书), Internet+Customs non-commercial pet-export application, rabies records, photo, titre report and Shanghai dog registration. Bring the dog, passport and originals. It gives inspection at 617 Sanmen Road, Baoshan and collection at 7F, 8 Middle Huaihai Road, Huangpu. ## Before committing money, resolve these go/no-go items

Keep originals and offline copies of owner passports, EP/DP/IPA and move evidence; dog/import licences; QMS acceptance; inspection appointment; relief approval or applicable Customs permit; chip-linked vaccine/titre records; government-endorsed AVS certificate and China Animal Health Certificate; dated parasite treatments; airline acceptance/carrier paperwork and AWB if cargo; agent handover, housing permission and release transport details.

Then check what could still stop travel: actual breed classification, sterilisation and crate measurements; eligible lab, passing result and continuous vaccine validity; confirmed QMS space and a compatible flight/arrival chain; written private-housing consent or individual HDB clearance; Shanghai final-exam/endorsement and cargo-export workflow; June aircraft/pet approval; actual handling/freight prices; Customs tax/relief decision and agent recognition. Recheck official requirements before financial commitments and again before final paperwork.
