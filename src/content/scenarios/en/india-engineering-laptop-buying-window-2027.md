### A practical shopping order

- **Platform first:** Intel/AMD x86 Windows 11 with a supported edition. Do not choose a Mac or Windows ARM machine for this mixed stack because one application happens to support it.
- **RAM before branding:** start at 16 GB and verify an upgrade route to 32 GB for later assemblies/meshes. Do not buy 8 GB without pricing the upgrade. SO-DIMM does not prove a spare slot; two 8 GB modules may need replacement. No exact candidate’s 32 GB route or upgrade price is established.
- **CPU:** look for responsive modeling and sustained multi-core work, not just “i7” or “Ryzen 7.” H/HS/HX candidates are practical shortlist choices, not benchmark rankings. Cooling, noise and long simulations remain untested.
- **GPU:** RTX 3050 4 GB is a budget compromise; 6 GB means more memory, not guaranteed speed. None meets Fusion’s 8 GB discrete recommendation. No shortlisted configuration has been established as SOLIDWORKS-certified.
- **Storage:** 512 GB is a practical starting point; 1 TB is useful if affordable. These are planning judgments, not universal vendor minimums. Leave room for Windows, projects and solver results, and keep backups. Ordinary Python coursework does not justify spending the budget on a top GPU.
