## Use the right fare for each age

Adults and the older teen each need a separate contactless card/device. Use exactly the same token all day: phone and physical card are different. Touch in/out on rail, only in on buses. Canadian bank cards may add FX fees; if rejected, use Oyster and take additional card fees from the reserve. See [TfL's visitor payment guidance](https://tfl.gov.uk/travel-information/visiting-london/getting-around-london/best-ways-for-visitors-to-pay).

**Age 14 does not automatically mean free travel.** Buy standard Oyster, with credit, and ask staff to add the [Young Visitor discount](https://tfl.gov.uk/fares/free-and-discounted-travel/11-15-zip-oyster-photocard), child present: 50% off adult pay-as-you-go fares and daily caps for up to 14 days. It works on Oyster/Visitor Oyster, not a bank card. Staff can add it at Tube/Overground, selected Elizabeth line stations and Visitor Centres. Budget the higher **£10.50 Oyster purchase fee**, before credit, from [the specific buying page](https://tfl.gov.uk/fares/ways-to-pay/where-to-buy-tickets-and-oyster); the general visitor page still says £10. Do not assume this fee is refundable.

Age 16 has no Young Visitor discount. A visiting 16–17-year-old outside the UK can apply for a [16+ Zip photocard](https://tfl.gov.uk/fares/free-and-discounted-travel/16-plus-zip-oyster-photocard); a parent applies under 18. Eligible PAYG fares are half adult rate, but the £22 fee, required age documents and at least 28 days for overseas collection make it poor value here. At this capped pattern, £25.20 saved less £22 is only £3.20 net. The plan deliberately uses adult contactless, with no application needed.

The travel day ends at 04:29; weekly capping is Monday–Sunday, so Friday–Monday crosses two weeks. Do not buy a weekly pass for this plan. Watford Junction is outside the central and Zones 1–6 allowance; Heathrow Express is not included.

The family ceiling model is 3 × (£16.30 + £8.90 + £8.90 + £16.30) = £151.20; one adult's four-day caps are £50.40, the child half £25.20, plus one Oyster £10.50: **£186.90 total**. Correct touching, listed routes/zones and the discount applied before travel are essential. Actual use can cost less. ## Canadian passports: approval before departure

For ordinary Canadian tourists, [GOV.UK's dated guidance](https://www.gov.uk/eta) points to an electronic travel authorisation (ETA), not a visitor visa. Each family member, including both teens, needs one: £20 each, £80 total. Exceptions include British/Irish passport holders and existing UK immigration permission. **An ETA permits travel, not guaranteed entry.**

Use GOV.UK or the official UK ETA app. [Application guidance](https://www.gov.uk/eta/apply) says usually within a day, but allow up to 3 working days, apply well ahead and wait for approval. Parents can apply for children. Validity is 2 years or linked-passport expiry, whichever comes first; travel on that passport, and get a new ETA for a new passport.

Passports must be valid for the whole stay—not an invented blanket six-month remaining-validity requirement. Be ready to show return travel, accommodation, support for yourselves/dependants and intention to leave. [Standard Visitor guidance](https://www.gov.uk/standard-visitor) normally allows up to six months; the short holiday fits that purpose. Recheck fees and rules before July 2027, especially with dual nationality or a different status. This is not immigration or legal advice.

Before spending, confirm flights/airport, a cancellable room accepting all travellers, then city Potter versus studio. Next check the stadium's event restrictions, free museum slots and a show within your ceiling. Before leaving, confirm ETA approvals, fares, works and opening hours. Pack water, comfortable shoes and a light rain layer.
