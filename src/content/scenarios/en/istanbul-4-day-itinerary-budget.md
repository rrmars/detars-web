## Check entry rules for your passport, then travel with care

**Check your own entry conditions before departure; this dated guidance is not legal or immigration advice, or a guarantee of admission.** The [German Foreign Office](https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/de/service/laender/tuerkei-node/tuerkeisicherheit-201962) and [Turkish MFA](https://www.mfa.gov.tr/visa-information-for-foreigners.en.mfa) describe visa-free tourism for German citizens for up to 90 days in any 180-day period, counting earlier Türkiye stays. Living in Germany is not enough: nationality matters. Take a valid German passport with at least one blank page; German national ID is also accepted under this guidance. If you hold another passport, check its rules separately.

Do not confuse general six-month validity advice with a blanket German legal minimum. The [MFA’s national-ID and expiry exceptions](https://www.mfa.gov.tr/countries-whose-citizens-are-allowed-to-enter-T%C3%BCrkiye-with-their-national-id_s.en.mfa) exempt eligible nationalities, including Germany, from the general 60-days-beyond-authorised-stay rule. The guidance also describes German documents expired no more than one year. Even so, use a valid passport rather than relying on an exception: airlines, transit and exit checks may create complications. Recheck official entry and safety advice, keep onward travel and accommodation details handy, and check your entry stamp.

If a stranger invites you to a bar or a “special place”, it is fine to say no. Inflated-bill and extortion scams are a particular warning for Beyoğlu. Keep your phone and passport secure in tram, market and ferry queues, and keep a separate document copy. Use official ticket desks and turnstiles, agree food prices and weights, and check the bill and change. If you need a taxi, insist on the meter and remember it is outside the public-transport core allowance. Avoid demonstrations and follow local closure notices.

## Before you go, settle the few things that could change the plan

- When choosing a room, ask for your exact dates, two adults in one double, the full tax total, breakfast, cancellation, access and noise details. You still need a September quote before treating the hotel as affordable.
- 2–4 weeks before travel, check all prices, Cistern hours and operator, hammam slots, Sunday ordinary-ferry calls both ways, Marmaray/M11 initial charges and airport options. Buy when the details fit; nothing is bought for you here.
- Before visiting historic sights, check the daily prayer and site notices, weather, security and queues. If time slips, move the Cistern rather than rush your rest or changing time.
- Before leaving for the airport, check the airport, direction-specific stop, current first and last services, and your terminal buffer. Add your actual flight time before fixing the departure plan.
