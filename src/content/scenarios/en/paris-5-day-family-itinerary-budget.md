**Worried that Paris will become a rush between queues? For 2 adults and a child aged 5, July 8–12, 2027, plan 4 nights near western Luxembourg Garden and one main outing each day. Keep 14:00–16:00 for hotel rest on the main outing days. The €2,200 family budget excludes flights: €1,906.40 planned spending and €293.60 spare, provided a suitable all-in room costs no more than €1,100.**

**Ask for your actual dates before booking. Hotel prices below are starting rates from 5 November 2026, not confirmed July rooms or family quotes. There is no confirmed July 2027 closure or staging timetable here for Champ de Mars, Concorde or summer-event areas. No announcement found does not mean access is guaranteed; keep a free outdoor alternative.**

## Count the travel days, not just the attractions

Arriving Thursday 8 July 2027 and leaving Monday 12 July 2027 gives 4 sightseeing days including arrival, not 4 full days plus travel. The first afternoon assumes you reach the hotel by mid-afternoon. If you arrive later, skip the park rather than move a paid visit. Add your airport and flight times before choosing transfers; they are not yet specified.

Times, meals, the hotel uplift, playground spending and transfers are estimates, not reservations or quotes. Keep all costs in EUR and replace the allowances with your own prices before paying. The plan should make the trip easier, not require every outing to happen.

14 July falls after you leave, so do not plan for the parade or fireworks. Earlier barriers are possible, not a confirmed closure. School holidays start 3 July 2027; allow for family demand, without expecting a particular queue. Paris Plages says Summer 2027 but has no exact dates. The Tuileries fair page still gives 20 June 2026–23 August 2026; its 2027 dates and the cauldron’s daily access and footprint remain unconfirmed. Treat these as possible extras, not the reason a day works.

Start near western Luxembourg Garden in the Latin Quarter. You can enjoy the park without a transport expedition and still return to your room. The routes below start from Hôtel Jean Bart. Hôtel du Levant is farther east in the same Left Bank area, adding about 10–15 minutes to western Luxembourg. Book a real third sleeping place for the child; do not assume a double room allows an extra guest.

## Day 1 — Thursday 8 July: arrive, unpack, then play

Arrival–15:30: transfer, lunch and rest. Keep this afternoon free of prepaid sightseeing. If the room is not ready, leave bags and sit in the garden. 16:00–17:15: try Luxembourg Garden and LudoJardin on the western side, allowing 45–60 minutes to play. 17:30–19:00: eat nearby and settle in for bedtime. The Tower sparkle can wait; tomorrow will feel easier.

From Jean Bart, allow about 5–10 minutes to the western gates and another 5–10 inside. Use main paths, not terrace stairs. The usual 1–15 July garden hours are 07:30–21:15, but can change. The playground opens from 10:00 and closes one hour before the garden. Its exact price still needs checking; put aside €10 for this family visit, or enjoy the free garden instead.

## Day 2 — Friday 9 July: enjoy the Eiffel view, skip the summit rush

08:00–09:30: have an early breakfast and travel by bus. Aim to reach the entrance around 09:30 for a 10:00 second-floor lift slot. 10:00–11:45: enjoy the view and spot boats on the river. Take the lift, skip the extra summit transfer and bring a folding stroller. Timed tickets still involve two security checks. These are target times, not tickets already booked.

11:45–12:30: early lunch and a quick Tower photo. Do not rely on a Champ de Mars picnic or playground while barriers are uncertain. 12:30–13:45: return, with room for traffic delays. **14:00–16:00: keep the hotel rest.** 16:15–17:15: optional Luxembourg play or pond watching, then dinner around 18:00. If the morning ran long, doing nothing more is a good choice.

Bus 82: walk 10–15 min to Luxembourg, take direction Neuilly–Hôpital Américain to Champ de Mars, then walk 10–15 min. Allow 25–40 min riding and 5–15 waiting: 50–85 min door to door. Return toward Luxembourg. Check the live direction and the curb before boarding; exact stop-side access is not confirmed. Be ready to fold the stroller or use spare money for a short taxi.

No lift tickets? Keep the outside view, avoid the walk-up queue and leave the €54.40 unspent. If barriers prevent a close approach, choose a permitted upper riverside pavement. Never cross a fence to save the plan.

## Day 3 — Saturday 10 July: find the animals, then take a break

08:50–09:50: travel with time for a toilet and snack. 10:00–11:15: visit only the Grande Galerie de l’Évolution permanent collection. Find an elephant, giraffe and a favourite sea creature, then leave while it is still fun. 11:15–12:30: main garden paths and lunch; skip the zoo, another attraction and the hilly labyrinth. 12:30–13:45: return. **14:00–16:00: hotel rest.** Later, a local snack or a short garden revisit is plenty.

At 36 rue Geoffroy Saint-Hilaire, the street entrance has a small threshold; the garden approach is level and the gallery floors have lifts. Ask staff about stroller admission or storage, as the general policy is not confirmed here. **Galerie des Enfants is permanently closed. Choose the main Grande Galerie, not that former children’s gallery.**

Bus 89: Musée du Luxembourg → Jardin des Plantes, direction Porte de France. Return from Buffon–La Mosquée toward Gare de Vanves–Malakoff, getting off at Musée du Luxembourg. Allow 15–25 min riding, 5–15 waiting and 15–25 total walking: 40–65 min door to door. Check the return stop live; the different Jardin des Plantes stops are not interchangeable, and curb access is not confirmed. Walking all the way with the stroller takes about 45–60 min; save that energy for the museum.

## Day 4 — Sunday 11 July: Louvre outside and Tuileries play

08:30–09:30: take the bus toward the Louvre and spend 20–30 minutes outside the Pyramid. No museum ticket or Mona Lisa queue today. 10:00–11:30: Tuileries main paths and the free playground by the Castiglione entrance on Rue de Rivoli. Optional sailboat rental is currently €6; check future opening and price. 11:30–13:30: lunch and return. Avoid Concorde staging and any fenced fair or cauldron area. **14:00–16:00: hotel rest.**

Bus 27 outward: Luxembourg → Pyramides toward Gare Saint-Lazare, then walk 10–15 min to the Pyramid. Allow 15–25 riding + 5–15 waiting + 20–30 walking: 40–70 min door to door. After the garden, cross Pont des Arts to Pont des Arts–Quai de Conti and take bus 27 toward Porte d’Ivry to Luxembourg. Allow 45–65 min including the playground walk. Check each direction’s curb and steps rather than assuming the return stop is the same.

Optional 16:00–18:00 Notre-Dame: that morning, look for a free 16:45–17:00 slot and spend 20–30 minutes inside. If there is no slot or the queue looks longer than about 20 minutes, enjoy the façade and river instead. Worship can change access; skip towers and treasury. Jean Bart is about 30–40 min on foot each way by ordinary pavements and upper quays. Or take bus 27 Luxembourg → Les Écoles: 5–10 min riding, waiting and 15–20 total walking, about 25–45 min overall. If another outing feels too much, leave it out.

## Day 5 — Monday 12 July: keep departure simple

Keep the morning free of timed sightseeing. Allow the airport’s check-in requirement, the transfer and a traffic buffer. Use official airport taxi ranks on arrival; for the return, arrange a suitable child seat or bring your own travel seat. €150 covers the family’s estimated round trip, not a seat-service quote. Current 2026 Left Bank flat fares are CDG €65 each way or Orly €36. Check 2027 fares and extras. Allow about 60–90 min by road for CDG or 35–60 min for Orly, longer if disrupted.

From CDG, direct RER B to Luxembourg is another option: about 45–55 min on the train, 15–30 for terminals, waiting and lifts, then 10–15 walking. Luxembourg is listed as lift-accessible, but check the entrance and whether the lift works that day; mind the train gap. Use rail instead of, not on top of, the taxi allowance. Beauvais or a non-Paris arrival needs a different transfer plan.

## A folding stroller makes changes easier

Start with buses, but check the stop as well as the vehicle. An accessible bus does not make every curb step-free, and exact stop-side layouts remain uncertain. Keep the stroller compact, leave wheelchair space free and follow the driver. If full or difficult to board, fold it and let the child walk aboard, or take a short taxi from the spare money. Never carry an occupied stroller on stairs.

Luxembourg RER B is listed as accessible since March 2019, but you still need the right street entrance and a working lift. Line 14 platforms at Pyramides and Châtelet have street-to-platform lifts; connecting corridors are a separate question. Avoid Rennes (12), Cluny–La Sorbonne (10), Cité (4), Jussieu (7/10), École Militaire (8) and Tuileries (1) for stroller routes unless a current official check shows a suitable route. That does not mean no lift or escalator exists anywhere in those stations.

Current 2026 Bus–Tram singles are €2.05 per adult and €1.05 per child; reduced fares apply from age 4 to under 10. These fares were checked on 5 November 2026. A family boarding costs €5.15. For 10 family one-way journeys, allow €51.50 + 3 Easy cards at €2 = €57.50. The €70 allowance leaves €12.50 spare. Each traveller needs a separate valid medium, and a return needs a new ticket. Bus–Tram does not cover Métro or RER; current non-airport Métro–Train–RER fares are €2.55 / €1.30. Check 2027 prices before loading tickets.

All walking and journey times are estimates, so allow extra time for booked visits. Check RATP or IDFM accessible planning, traffic and lifts a week before and each morning. July works are not confirmed absent; November disruption notices cannot tell you what will happen in July.

## Before you go, settle the things that could change the day

- Get the all-in room price, cancellation terms, cooling, lift size, entrance steps and stroller storage first. Hold off on non-refundable outings until the accommodation works. Once flights are known, arrange transfers and the child seat.
- Check the official Eiffel office around the calculated release window, then the museum’s July sale opening and free child ticket requirements. Recheck Notre-Dame’s same-day and worship rules. No suitable ticket means a free outside visit, not a reason to pay a queue seller.
- Near travel, check 2027 transport, taxi and tax rates, garden hours, Tower notices, Champ de Mars and Concorde barriers, and Tuileries or Paris Plages event areas. Check again 48 hours before ticketed visits and each morning for bus or lift problems. No announcement found is not a promise that nothing will close.
- Pack water, hats and light rain protection. In heat or rain, keep shade and the room break. Swap the museum and garden mornings only if tickets allow. Otherwise shorten the park, not the nap, and do not automatically buy another indoor attraction. A good family day can include dropping an outing.
