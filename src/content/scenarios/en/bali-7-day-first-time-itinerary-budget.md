**Worried about fitting Bali in without rushing? Keep the route simple: settle into Ubud, then slow down by the sea in Sanur. Give Nyepi its own quiet hotel day, skip transfers, and confirm in-house meals before choosing where to stay.** The hotel prices below are November examples, not a promise of rooms on your dates.

For 2 adults traveling from Singapore on 2027-03-06–2027-03-13, try 4 Ubud nights + 3 Sanur nights, with USD 2,500 excluding flights. The date to plan around is Nyepi on 2027-03-08.

## Check your dates and passports first

This plan uses information checked on 2026-11-05 and assumes 2 tourists with ordinary Singapore passports. Flying from Singapore does not mean the same entry rules apply to your passport. You'll stay 7 nights across 8 calendar dates: days 1–7 cover the trip, followed by departure. Flight times are still needed; the arrival plan assumes you land by about 14:00. Bali WITA is UTC+8, so your clock stays on Singapore time.

The [official holiday calendar](https://setkab.go.id/en/govt-annnounces-2027-national-holidays-and-collective-leave-days/) lists Nyepi on 2027-03-08, Eid al-Fitr on 2027-03-10–2027-03-11 and collective leave on 2027-03-09 and 2027-03-12 during your stay. Islamic dates still depend on the Religious Affairs Minister's determination. Leave room for holiday disruption; this isn't an ordinary sightseeing week.

Following [Bali's general Nyepi guidance](https://ceraken.baliprov.go.id/detail/hari-suci-nyepi-1744696661), keep 06:00 on 2027-03-08 through 06:00 on 2027-03-09 entirely inside your accommodation. That means no road travel, sightseeing, beach walks or airport transfers. The general guidance includes airport/port closures, but the exact 2027 operating notice still needs confirming before you rely on it. Don't change hotels during 2027-03-07–2027-03-09.

## The route: fewer transfers, room to rest

Sanur makes a gentle beach stop. [Indonesia Travel](https://www.indonesia.travel/id/en/destination/bali-nusa-tenggara/bali/pantai-sanur) describes calmer water, a pedestrian beachfront route and an outlook toward sunrise. Still, check conditions before swimming; calmer water isn't a daily safety guarantee. Uluwatu's cliffs, Canggu nightlife and a Penida boat trip are left out to keep the pace easy, not because they're less worthwhile. There are 0 required very early starts and 1 optional sunrise.

| Day / date | Your plan | Where you'll stay / what to check |
|---|---|---|
| 1 / 2027-03-06 | Arrive, clear entry and collect bags, then drive straight to Ubud; have dinner at the hotel | Ubud; allow 60–120 minutes for entry and 1.5–2.5 hours driving, possibly 3 hours |
| 2 / 2027-03-07 | Take an easy rice-field walk; visit central Ubud/Saraswati if open; return by 14:00–15:00 | Same Ubud hotel; processions/closures are possible, so don't depend on taxis for the evening |
| 3 / 2027-03-08 | Spend Nyepi at the hotel: read, rest and eat in-house | Same Ubud hotel; use the pool/internet only if expressly permitted |
| 4 / 2027-03-09 | Leave around 08:30 for Ceking/Tegallalang, then Tirta Empul; return around 16:00–17:00 | Same Ubud hotel; check reopening first; no purification package included |
| 5 / 2027-03-10 | Check out around 10:00 and drive straight to Sanur, without sightseeing detours | Sanur; allow an estimated 1.5–2.5 hours, possibly 3 hours; early check-in isn't guaranteed |
| 6 / 2027-03-11 | Wake at 05:45 for sunrise if you like, then enjoy the beach/pool and a couples massage | Same Sanur hotel; check the actual sunrise time and swimming conditions |
| 7 / 2027-03-12 | Try an optional cooking class around 10:00–13:00, then take the afternoon slowly before your final dinner | Same Sanur hotel; confirm the class is running during the holiday |
| Departure / 2027-03-13 | Travel from Sanur to the airport, then home | No overnight; allow an estimated 45–90 minutes driving and reach the terminal 3 hours before take-off |

Treat road times as planning buffers, not live traffic predictions. On the countryside day, allow 45–75 minutes to the terraces, 30–45 minutes onward to Tirta Empul and 60–90 minutes back to Ubud. If a place hasn't reopened or the weather turns, shorten the route rather than chasing a distant replacement. Wet paths or lightning are a good reason for café or hotel time. A midday/afternoon return flight will help if you'd rather avoid another early start.

## Entry checklist: passport, visa, levy and declaration are separate

Use this checklist, based on information checked on 2026-11-05, to prepare rather than assume you're ready to travel. Recheck the rules before departure. No applications, declarations or payments have been made for you; you'll still need to handle them yourself.

- With ordinary Singapore passports, the [A1 exemption](https://www.imigrasi.go.id/wna/daftar-visa-indonesia/A1) allows a free stay of up to 30 days, with no extension; [Singapore MFA](https://www.mfa.gov.sg/travelling-overseas/travel-advisories-notices-and-visa-information/indonesia/) also supports this guidance. Each passport needs at least 6 months' validity on arrival, and you'll need a return/onward ticket. For arrival on 2027-03-06, check that expiry is 2027-09-06 or later. Check temporary/emergency documents separately.
- If either passport differs, check eligibility for that nationality first. The [B1 VOA/e-VOA](https://www.imigrasi.go.id/wna/daftar-visa-indonesia/B1) costs IDR 500,000 per eligible person for an initial 30 days. Not every Bali visitor needs it. Departing from or living in Singapore does not itself give you the exemption.
- The [Love Bali FAQ](https://lovebali.baliprov.go.id/faq) lists a separate tourist levy of IDR 150,000 per person, or IDR 300,000 for the couple. Visa exemption doesn't waive it. Use the official payment route and keep the emailed QR voucher. Check payment and FX fees too; they aren't confirmed here.
- The [official All Indonesia FAQ](https://kemlu.go.id/files/publikasi/additional_file/175648407968b1d1ef0a46b_FAQ_All_Indonesia__Trilingual_.pdf) says the declaration is free and mandatory. Complete it from D-3 through arrival day: 2027-03-03–2027-03-06 for this trip. Use [the official portal](https://allindonesia.imigrasi.go.id/) and save the QR. Even in a group submission, enter each traveler's own passport details. This doesn't replace the visa or levy requirements.

## Before you book

Confirm both passports and flight times, then ask for exact-date hotel totals including taxes and Nyepi packages. Recheck airport notices, entry rules and hotel restrictions 2–4 weeks before travel. Confirm drivers, temple reopening and class operation before paying for activities. March weather, parade schedules and provider availability still need checking. Pack temple-appropriate clothing, non-slip shoes and offline reading. You don't need a scooter or boat day for this route.
