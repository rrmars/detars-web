You want your children settled and your nursing career moving forward, without watching your savings disappear while paperwork catches up. **Start CRNA recognition before the move, keep the first home modest, and plan for the first household income without assuming an RN start date.** With CAD $28,000 available before travel and setup, this family does not have six income-free months.

Achieng is **34**, with a BScN and **eight years' nursing experience**. Her husband is an accountant; their children are currently **5 and 9**. They plan to land as permanent residents **by July 2027**. The exact COPR/visa expiry has not been inspected: check it now, and do not miss the actual landing deadline while waiting for professional recognition.

The two-bedroom model costs **$4,017/month** with no car or paid childcare. After **$13,200** of travel, setup and earmarked licensing cash, **$14,800** remains: **3.68 months**, or about **3.7 income-free months**. It runs short during **month 4** and is **$9,302 short over six months** without earnings or benefits. Aim for a credible first job/start plan before flying, not a hoped-for RN salary.

## Your nursing route need not start with NNAS and a full bridge

**Counterintuitive finding:** “NNAS → mandatory bridge → RN” is not Alberta's universal sequence. CRNA accepts approved education assessments or qualifying NNAS reports, and bridging is not required for every applicant. Your experience matters, but neither a Kenyan licence nor eight years of work is an Alberta practice permit. Start with the [CRNA competency options](https://nurses.ab.ca/nursing-in-alberta/international-applicants/options-for-confirming-competency/) and map education, recent practice and English separately.

### Collect the right evidence before buying another assessment

Inventory passport/name-change documents, BScN award and transcript, first/current Kenyan nursing licences, dated employer role/hour records, English-language practice or education evidence, and any immigration ECA. Keep Kenyan registration and practice current where feasible. Arrange issuer-to-regulator records when the chosen route requests them.

The [education-verification rules](https://nurses.ab.ca/nursing-in-alberta/registration-requirements/post-secondary-education-verification/) accept IQAS, WES, ICAS, ICES/BCIT and CES/University of Toronto assessments. A suitable accepted-provider immigration report can be reused; age alone does not invalidate it. The issuer sends it directly. Alternatively, use a qualifying NNAS report. A non-equivalent result needs CRNA review—not self-declared equivalence. The [education requirement](https://nurses.ab.ca/nursing-in-alberta/registration-requirements/post-secondary-education-requirement/) still applies to Achieng's particular qualification.

Before purchasing a **new CES assessment**, check service availability. NNAS displays a [CES wind-down notice](https://www.nnas.ca/news/update-for-applicants-ces-wind-down-and-your-nnas-application/), but its detailed instructions could not be read. That does **not** establish that an existing CES report is invalid.

### Recent hours and English are separate gates

- [RN currency](https://nurses.ab.ca/nursing-in-alberta/registration-requirements/currency-of-practice/) includes **450 RN/NP hours in the preceding two practice years**, or **1,125 in five**; approved assessment/education alternatives also exist. CRNA practice years run **October 1–September 30**. Keep five practice years of records. NCLEX and LPN work do not establish RN currency.
- [English proficiency](https://nurses.ab.ca/nursing-in-alberta/registration-requirements/english-language-proficiency/) may be met through qualifying English-language nursing practice of **250 hours within five years**, or qualifying English theory/clinical nursing education abroad lasting at least **two years**, completed within **five years**. Kenya or an English-taught degree alone is not automatic proof. Submit requested evidence through the issuer when prompted.
- The ordinary test route is **IELTS Academic L7, W6.5, R6.5, S7, overall 7**, or **CELBAN L9, W7, R8, S8**, normally within **two years**. Immigration IELTS General and NCLEX are not substitutes for this English requirement.

### Get exam eligibility, then finish the permit conditions

If she has not passed NCLEX-RN, request eligibility through CRNA and follow its authorization-to-test and Pearson instructions before booking. The [exam guidance](https://nurses.ab.ca/nursing-in-alberta/registration-requirements/registration-exams/) allows global testing; actual seats, location, travel and ATT validity still need checking. International applicants have **two years from CRNA's exam-eligible decision** to pass, subject to Pearson scheduling rules. Passing is not final permission to work.

Under [CRNA's permit steps](https://nurses.ab.ca/nursing-in-alberta/international-applicants/five-steps-to-a-permit/), complete identity verification, criminal check, truth/character/fitness declarations, Alberta jurisprudence and protecting-patients courses, professional liability protection/core services, payment and approval. Its **“up to two business days”** refers to competency-information review after receipt—not end-to-end licensing. Activate around an approved start, verify active status before an RN shift or using the Alberta RN title, and remember that a July permit ends **September 30** before renewal.

### Where NNAS fits

The [NNAS fee page](https://www.nnas.ca/fees/) lists **CAD $750** for Expedited RN/LPN service, nonrefundable—not a universal **US$650** RN charge. The latter belongs to a different psychiatric-nurse pathway. Its [expedited requirements](https://www.nnas.ca/expedited-service/) include two notarized IDs, a course-by-course ECA from an IRCC-approved organization and first/current licensing evidence, with third-party documents sent as specified. The report target is **five business days after all documents arrive**, subject to clarification, not five days after payment. The order lasts **12 months**. ECA, notarization and document costs can be additional; NNAS does not issue the Alberta permit.

### Only fund a bridge or assessment that resolves an actual gap

[CRNA bridging guidance](https://nurses.ab.ca/nursing-in-alberta/registration-requirements/bridging-programs/) lists Bow Valley, Keyano, MacEwan and Mount Royal. Successful completion can address education/currency gaps; enrolment or a waitlist cannot. An appropriate nursing-focused three/four-year equivalent ECA can permit direct bridge/competence-assessment application, but institution admissions rules still apply. Neither RNCCAP nor a bridge replaces NCLEX.

## Find first income without assuming another clinical title is automatic

### Achieng's parallel options

**GN, LPN and HCA are not automatic fallbacks.** The [CRNA provisional register](https://nurses.ab.ca/nursing-in-alberta/application-support/provisional-register/) needs specific approval and supervision; some substantial-equivalence routes are excluded. [International LPN registration](https://www.clha.com/lpn/apply-to-be-an-lpn/applicants/internationally-educated-nurses/) is a separate CLHA route and permit.

[HCA regulation began February 2, 2026](https://www.clha.com/hca/hcas/hca-faqs). The [international substantial-equivalence route](https://www.clha.com/hca/apply-to-be-an-hca/applicants/substantially-equivalent-hcas) requires CLHA registration and a valid permit and may require AHRE. Approved provisional work is supervised. An overseas RN qualification—or an employer calling clinical work “caregiving”—does not establish an exemption.

The [HCA fee schedule](https://www.clha.com/hca/apply-to-be-an-hca/registration-information/fee-schedule) gives an illustrative **$100 application + $315 AHRE + $125 partial permit = $540**, excluding other requirements. AHRE already includes administration. This is not a guaranteed July 2027 tariff or all-in cost.

Search in parallel for hospital administration/reception, housekeeping, food service, retail or customer support where duties and hiring requirements fit. These are search directions, not verified vacancies or wages.

### Her husband's accounting route

Establish his degree, professional body, active membership and good standing first. [CPA Alberta](https://www.cpaalberta.ca/Become-a-CPA/Internationally-Trained-Professional) describes a Core 1 entry route after transcript assessment for qualifying active IFAC-body members without an MOU. [IFAC lists ICPAK as a member](https://www.ifac.org/about-ifac/membership/members/institute-certified-public-accountants-kenya). That does not prove he personally qualifies, confer Canadian CPA status or grant public-practice rights. Without the membership, investigate academic/transcript assessment instead. Recheck 2027 transition/intake rules and costs.

Search for **accounts payable/receivable clerk, accounting assistant, billing clerk and junior bookkeeping**; junior accountant where requirements fit. [Federal Job Bank](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/requirements/14076/geo25411) identifies accounting-related clerk work as nonregulated. Demonstrate Excel, reconciliations, invoicing and bookkeeping skills; learn relevant Canadian payroll/tax workflows. No salary or time-to-job promise is used here.

These specialist regulator/issuer pages are primary for their own rules; applicant-specific routes, future prices and jobs remain unconfirmed. **One parent must cover school pickups, summer days and exams until paid care and shift-compatible work are arranged.** Judge any second income after care and transport costs.

### No Canadian credit history does not remove the deposit cap

[Alberta tenancy guidance](https://www.alberta.ca/starting-a-tenancy) caps security deposits at **one month's starting rent**, with no later increase. Multiple months of security are not a lawful credit-history workaround. Use a written lease naming occupants, utilities, parking, fees and insurance; record move-in condition and keep receipts.

[IRCC rental guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/settle-canada/housing/renting.html) explains credit/income/reference checks. Prepare Kenyan rental receipts/references, employer references, PR/identity evidence, redacted savings proof and an offer if one exists. [CMHC](https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/consumers/renting-a-home/i-want-to-rent/credit-checks-and-bad-credit) describes alternatives such as a guarantor and prior rent receipts; acceptance is discretionary. Verify the property and manager before payment or sensitive-document sharing. Advance rent is distinct from a deposit; do not assume it lifts the cap or commit your whole runway upfront.

## Schools, health cover and benefits: apply correctly, budget cautiously

### School placement depends on birthdays, address and capacity

PR children use the [CBE Welcome Centre](https://cbe.ab.ca/welcome): begin SchoolEngage registration, upload documents, then staff arrange completion. [Required documentation](https://cbe.ab.ca/registration-documentation) includes child age/legal-name/status evidence—COPR is listed—parent-named address proof such as bank, utility or renters-insurance documents, and a parent/guardian selfie holding government photo ID. Bring school reports and immunization records as useful preparation. A lease is **not explicitly listed** in that inspected CBE address-evidence list; do not confuse it with AHCIP's accepted lease evidence.

[CBE age rules](https://cbe.ab.ca/new-students) require age **five by December 31, 2027** for fall 2027 kindergarten and **six by then** for Grade 1. The younger child may already be six at arrival. Use exact birthdays and records; do not assign the older child's grade from age alone. Grades **1–12** noncitizens who speak another language at home receive an English assessment; kindergarten assessment is at school. Interpretation is free.

Most [kindergarten](https://cbe.ab.ca/kindergarten) is half-day or alternating full-days—not free full-time childcare. July 2027 Welcome Centre hours, 2027–28 placement and [school charges](https://cbe.ab.ca/fees) still need checking. Supplies, lunch supervision, trips, transport and care can cost extra. The $100 budget row is an allowance, not paid childcare or a quoted fee.

### Confirm AHCIP's effective date; do not impose the interprovincial wait

For eligible residents [arriving from outside Canada](https://www.alberta.ca/ahcip-moving-to-alberta), coverage may start when Alberta residency is established. The interprovincial third-month rule is not this family's rule. [Apply within three months](https://www.alberta.ca/ahcip-how-to-apply), with residency evidence—a rental agreement is accepted here—identity and legal entitlement. Registry application service is free; published processing is up to **five days**, plus card mailing. [Eligibility](https://www.alberta.ca/ahcip-eligibility) includes a permanent Alberta home and commitment to physical presence of at least **183 days in a 12-month period**.

Keep temporary travel/emergency cover until the effective date is clear. [Public coverage has limits](https://www.alberta.ca/ahcip-what-is-covered): do not assume every dental, prescription, optical or travel expense is paid. The **$300 temporary-insurance** and **$100/month uncovered-health** allowances are unconfirmed, not policy quotes.

### CCB eligibility is not an approved payment

Under [CRA's conditions](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/child-family-benefits/canada-child-benefit/who-apply.html), PR status meets the immigration-status condition once Canadian tax residence and care/residence conditions are met: **no 18-month temporary-resident wait** applies to these PRs. Normally Achieng applies as the female parent living with the children; if the father is primarily responsible, follow the signed-letter procedure.

[Newcomer applications](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/child-family-benefits/canada-child-benefit/how-apply.html) use **RC66 with RC66SCH** and child birth evidence; **CTB9** may apply for the spouse's nonresident periods. Give both spouses' requested pre-arrival worldwide income. [CRA newcomer guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/international-non-residents/individuals-leaving-entering-canada-non-residents/newcomers-canada-immigrants.html) allows eligible benefit applications before the first tax return; both should file annually to maintain calculation.

For **July 2026–June 2027 only**, [published CCB maximums](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/child-family-benefits/canada-child-benefit/how-much.html) are **$8,157/year = $679.75/month under six** and **$6,883/year, published $573.58/month for ages six–17**, with a **$38,237** adjusted-family-net-income maximum-payment threshold for that period. One child in each band gives **$1,253.33/month**; both older gives **$1,147.16**. These are not this family's award or July 2027 rates. Birthdays and worldwide income matter. **Budget $0 until approved and received**, with no guaranteed first-payment date.

## Prepare for winter, but let real commutes decide the car

Before the cold spell, arrange insulated waterproof grippy boots, coats, windproof/thermal layers, hats, mitts/gloves, neck protection and warm socks. Children need outdoor school gear and dry spares. Try fit in Canada rather than guessing in Nairobi. The **$1,200 = $300 × four people** reserve assumes a mix of sale/new/used purchases; it is not a priced retail basket. [Alberta cold-weather guidance](https://www.alberta.ca/blizzards-freezing-rain-ice-storms-and-extreme-cold) supports warm windproof layers and limiting exposure in extreme cold.

[Calgary Transit fares](https://www.calgarytransit.com/fares---passes.html), including the [2026 change notice](https://www.calgarytransit.com/news/calgary-transit-fares-changing-for-2026.html), give **$126 per adult calendar-month pass**, **$252 for two**, and **$4 singles valid 90 minutes**. Children **12 and under** ride free. Compare passes with actual trips while unemployed. Low-income passes need approval and are not budgeted. Recheck 2027 rates; no airport route/fare is promised.

Defer the car until workplaces show whether transit works. If necessary, price purchase/inspection, newcomer insurance, fuel, parking, registration, repairs and winter tires first; none is quoted here. Under [Alberta licence-exchange guidance](https://www.alberta.ca/exchange-non-alberta-licences), permanent residents must change a non-Alberta licence within **90 days**. Kenya is not reciprocal: knowledge/road tests and validation of experience may be needed, not a simple swap. Bring the original licence/history and insurance records; follow learner-supervision conditions during the process.

## A practical departure and first-90-days plan

1. **Before departure:** check the actual landing expiry and any provincial-nomination conditions. Keep identity, birth, marriage, school and immunization records accessible. Start CRNA document/ECA/hour/English mapping; clarify a gap before paying a bridge. Establish the husband's credentials and prepare evidence-led CVs. Re-price flights, rentals, insurance, care and fares; check 2027–28 school designation/overflow and summer registration arrangements. Do not remotely pay an unverified manager.
2. **Days 1–7:** complete landing and arrange SINs, banking/payroll readiness and general job searches. [PR work rights](https://ircc.canada.ca/english/helpcentre/answer.asp?qnum=1113&top=23) are separate from professional permission; a [SIN](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/services/sin.html) is needed for work/program access and is private. View housing, verify manager/occupancy/terms, record condition, map daily routes and obtain address evidence.
3. **Days 8–30:** submit AHCIP documents and confirm effective cover; begin CBE registration/assessment when address evidence is ready; confirm actual school/care hours. Apply for CCB when eligible with newcomer income evidence, but retain $0 in the budget until received. Continue licensing and both job searches; check any HCA offer against CLHA permit requirements.
4. **Days 31–60:** replace allowances with real bills. Without credible first income, trigger the warning and revise funding/spending. Revisit licensing delays, tuition and possible support without assuming an award or paid placement. Arrange shift-compatible care before both accept work; prepare winter gear early.
5. **Days 61–90:** complete relevant licence-change/testing steps within the driving boundary. Buy a car only after a full quote. Confirm RN activation/renewal or a funded gap plan, follow CCB/address updates, plan both adults' annual tax filing and refresh the next three months of cash.

The open decisions are individual CRNA equivalence, recent hours and English evidence; the husband's exact membership/credentials; children's birthdays and care needs; future housing occupancy/availability, school placement, fares and CCB rates; college seats/total tuition; and third-party licensing, flight, insurance and retail prices. No applications, contacts, accounts or bookings were made for this guide.
