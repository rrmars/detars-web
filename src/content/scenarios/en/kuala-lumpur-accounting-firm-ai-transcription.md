If your accountants spend too long writing up meetings, AI notes are worth evaluating—but client audio is not suitable test material. **For Farah's 12-person Kuala Lumpur accounting and tax firm, evaluate three named Fireflies Business hosts on monthly billing, using fictional information first. Do not use it for confidential client meetings until the contract, cross-border transfer, professional-secrecy, Malay-language and DPA-purpose questions are cleared.** If any gate fails, keep human notes rather than rush the planned January 2027 start.

The budget is about **MYR 600/month**. At the planning assumption **USD 1 = MYR 4.50**, **3 × USD 29 = USD 87 = MYR 391.50/month**, leaving **MYR 208.50** before taxes, exchange costs and extras. This is a recommendation to evaluate, not a finding that the product is compliant or accurate.

Prices and documentation were read on **7 November 2026**. They are public USD references, not Malaysian checkout quotations or guaranteed January prices. Product capabilities, storage and security statements below remain **vendor claims**, not performance tests. The underlying source file is not published.

## Why three hosts—not twelve licences

A firm needs enough named capture users for its actual meeting schedule, not automatically a seat for every employee. Three hosts are a working assumption: check simultaneous meetings, who attends and whether the firm's agreed export/use rights let colleagues receive reviewed notes through restricted client files. The other nine staff would not be Fireflies workspace members. **Do not share host logins:** members and administrators are billable, and the [terms prohibit credential sharing](https://fireflies.ai/terms-of-service).

The reason to shortlist Business is specific. Its [Multi-Language Mode](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/2585231364) explicitly lists English, Malay `ms` and Chinese `zh`, and claims mid-sentence switching across 60+ languages. It is **beta**, restricted to Business and Enterprise, and untested here for Malaysian accents, Mandarin, accounting vocabulary or overlapping speakers. Select **Multi-Language (Auto-Detect)**, not plain Auto-Detect, which chooses the dominant language. Summaries follow the dominant language; an English summary does not prove the Malay speech was captured correctly.

[Business features](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/2063312779-fireflies-business-tier-pricing-and-features) include Teams/Zoom capture, mobile/in-person recordings and a **3-hour meeting limit**. Base transcription and summaries are included; advanced AI features cost extra credits. Verify your intended workflow and purchased-tier controls before relying on either.

## Five options: language fit is only half the decision

Transcription recognises the original speech. Translation, captions and summaries are different outputs. A vendor's “Chinese” label does not certify every Mandarin accent or dialect, and Simplified/Traditional text choices are not spoken-language accuracy tests.

The [billing guide](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/9606045468-fireflies-billing-terms) lists **USD 29/seat monthly**, or **USD 19/month on annual billing = USD 228/seat/year**. Every workspace member/admin counts. Do not assume that a host-only design permits shared accounts or free workspace access.

[Storage guidance](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/9596505232) says default storage **and processing are in the US**, using AWS/GCP. Enterprise private/EU storage is not proof of EU-only processing; the vendor still notes US processing. The full current subprocessor chain remains unverified.

The [safety policy](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/2154538358-policy-on-keeping-information-safe) claims no meeting-content AI training internally or by partners; no opt-out is needed under that promise. However, the terms distinguish prohibited generative-model training from permitted derived statistical/usage analytics. “Zero retention” at an AI provider does **not** mean Fireflies keeps no recordings or transcripts.

The [organisational DPA](https://fireflies.ai/data-processing-agreement) is incorporated into the terms, but it is not Malaysian transfer permission. **Attachment 1 includes analytics, advertising/marketing and ad-impression auditing purposes alongside meeting-content/transcript categories. Clarify their applicability and restrict any inappropriate use in the governing agreement before client approval.** This breadth is not itself proof of model training. Resolve service/deidentified-data carve-outs, backup/legal deletion exceptions and the full processing chain. Its incident wording is “without undue delay,” not a fixed immediate deadline; advance consultation before notices naming the vendor must never delay the firm's legal reporting. Automated retention should not be assumed at Business tier.

## Clear the Malaysia privacy and secrecy gates before client audio

This is an operating checklist, **not legal advice**. The legal basis reviewed was the [official Act 709 reprint as at 15 September 2026](https://www.pdp.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Akta-709-cetakan-semula-2026-BM.pdf), [Amendment Act A1727](https://www.pdp.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Act-A1727.pdf) and [commencement gazette](https://www.pdp.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/PENETAPAN-TARIKH-PERMULAAN-KUAT-KUASA.pdf), with final Commissioner guidance. Processor/security and revised cross-border provisions took effect **1 April 2025**; DPO and breach-notification provisions **1 June 2025**. They are already operative, not January proposals.

**Map the whole transfer, not just storage.** Under revised section 129, the old destination-list mechanism is removed. A destination may qualify through substantially similar law or at least equivalent protection; specified alternative routes include consent or reasonable precautions/due diligence. Routine convenience does not automatically establish contractual necessity. The [final cross-border guideline](https://www.pdp.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/GP_CBPDT_EN-1.pdf) calls for written overseas-transfer information and retained evidence of the chosen route. Record recipients, purposes, countries, speech/LLM processing, support, backups and onward transfers. A transfer impact assessment can support the relevant protection finding; it is not mandatory for every possible route. Relevant findings must be reviewed on changes and be no more than **three years old** under the guidance. EU clauses, an overseas DPA and security badges alone do not establish Malaysian permission.

**Contract for oversight and immediate escalation.** Processors now have direct Security Principle duties, but controllers retain all seven principles and processor oversight. Own-purpose reuse may not be solely processor activity. Require restricted instructions, confidentiality, safeguards, subprocessor controls, rights assistance and return/deletion, including backup exceptions. A vendor waiting on its own incident timetable can consume the firm's reporting window.

**Prepare a breach response before recording.** The [final breach guideline](https://www.pdp.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/GP_DBN_BM.pdf?read=20261107) and [Commissioner's circular](https://www.pdp.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Pekeliling-DBN.pdf) operationalise notice as soon as practicable within **72 hours** of the relevant awareness/confirmation trigger. Log the earliest reliable trigger, including processor notice or the firm's clear evidence, whichever is earlier; do not wait for completed forensics. Significant harm or **more than 1,000 affected subjects** can trigger Commissioner reporting. A small financial-data leak can qualify. For significant harm—not scale alone—notify affected people without unnecessary delay, no later than **7 days after Commissioner notification**. Supplement initial information within **30 days after initial notification**; late reports need reasons. Obtain acknowledgement. Keep a register including non-notifiable incidents and reasons. The instruments use different two-year retention starting points; retain through the later applicable endpoint and set a defensible schedule for non-notified incidents. Require immediate processor escalation and assistance contractually.

**Assess DPO need across the firm.** Under the [final DPO guideline](https://www.pdp.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/GP_DPO_BM.pdf), any criterion can trigger appointment: more than **20,000 data subjects**; more than **10,000 subjects' sensitive data including financial information**; or activities requiring regular and systematic monitoring. Twelve employees is not an exemption. Financial information here is a DPO-threshold term, not a universal new statutory sensitive-data category. Routine meetings neither automatically meet nor avoid the monitoring test. A qualifying controller notifies/registers its DPO and business contacts within **21 days of appointment**; relevant new-appointment updates within **14 days**. A competent, supported, conflict-managed employee or outsourced officer may qualify. If criteria do not apply, document why and nominate a privacy lead anyway. Controller-category registration is a separate unresolved question.

**Check professional secrecy separately.** A PDPA basis does not override engagement terms or professional confidentiality. Current applicable MIA/other secrecy rules and client authority need professional review; a current MIA text was not successfully retrieved, so no specific exception is asserted. Sensitive tax matters may need exclusion altogether.

## Give people a real choice—not just a bot announcement

Section 7 and the [Personal Data Protection Regulations 2013](https://www.pdp.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/PERATURAN-PERLINDUGAN-DATA-PERIBADI-2013.pdf) underpin the notice/consent work. Prepare a written notice in **Bahasa Malaysia and English**; a Mandarin explanation may help participants but replaces neither required language. This English guide is not the final statutory notice.

Cover the firm/controller and privacy contact; audio/transcript/summary, identities and metadata; purposes and source information; access/correction rights; recipient classes and overseas routes; who can access the material; retention; choices and consequences of voluntary/compulsory provision. Give it before recording and retain its version and timestamped permissions, including late joiners. A company's agreement is not automatically every participant's personal-data consent.

Identify the applicable ordinary-processing basis under section 6; do not import a general GDPR “legitimate interests” shortcut. Where consent is relied on, make it recordable and provable—paper signatures are not universally required. Record informed transfer consent distinctly where that is the section 129 route. Sensitive data needs an appropriate section 40 route; explicit consent is one, not the only statutory route. Assess speaker-recognition/voiceprints specifically: not every audio recording is automatically biometric data. Health, beliefs or offence information can matter independently.

Offer human notes with no AI recording if anyone refuses. Obtain permission **before** starting the recorder, not by recording the consent request itself. Minimise third-party identifiers, pause for excluded topics, and handle written withdrawal through the legal workflow. Do not promise irreversible model “unlearning” or deletion of records that must lawfully be retained.

After advance notice, a meeting opening could be:

> With your permission, we would like to use [service] to prepare draft meeting notes. Processing involves [countries and recipient classes in our notice]. Authorised staff will check the draft, and recordings follow [retention policy]. You can choose human notes without AI recording. Has each participant received the notice and agreed to recording and the stated processing? [Record distinct transfer permission where relied on.]

Have this adapted and professionally reviewed. A yes does not cure an incomplete notice, unlawful transfer, third-party disclosure or secrecy restriction.

- **November 2026: Farah owns the decision.** Inventory meeting types, existing Teams/Zoom licences and simultaneous capture needs. Test the three-host scheduling assumption. Assess firm-wide DPO criteria and excluded matters, including sensitive disputes, identity-document discussions and any refusal.
- **Before client material: finish procurement.** Retain the governing DPA/version, full destinations/subprocessors, no-training scope, permissible purposes, deletion/backup terms, incident escalation and available controls. Document the transfer route, notices and professional/engagement authority. Missing evidence means no client deployment.
- **December 2026: separately authorise a fictional-data evaluation.** No signup or audio test was performed for this guide. If approved later, use consenting staff with invented names and figures. Test English, Malay, Mandarin, mixed Malay–English, amounts, dates and negations; compare a quiet room with shared microphones and Teams/Zoom capture. Check source speech against both transcript and summary. Measure correction time and missed obligations, not vendor accuracy percentages. Require no unresolved material amount/deadline errors after human review; stop if the work is not reduced.
- **Configure minimum access.** Named accounts, MFA where available, manual bot invitations, private visibility and no calendar-wide auto-joining. No public links or automatic external recaps. Disable unnecessary integrations, speaker learning, analytics and feedback sharing where supported. Select the correct multilingual mode. Keep video/screen capture off unless justified. Verify each control at the purchased tier; do not assume Enterprise features exist on Business.
- **Set retention and billing.** A proposed starting policy is deletion of raw recordings and drafts within **30 days after notes are approved**, unless a justified shorter period or legal hold applies. This is not a Malaysian fixed retention rule. Approved notes follow the firm's records schedule. Keep a manual deletion register if needed, covering exports and backup expiry. Avoid advanced Fireflies credit features; disable Auto-Upgrade in Billing and verify/cancel any AI-credit subscription separately. Base transcription/basic summaries need no credits.
- **January 2027: limited consenting-client use only after clearance.** Recheck law, price and actual invoice before purchase. Give the bilingual notice and obtain permissions before bot invitation or room recording; check late joiners. A named accountant verifies names, amounts, currencies, dates, negations, obligations and speaker attribution, records corrections and approves the file note. Never send clients raw AI tax advice or unreviewed notes.
- **Weekly during the first month:** review correction time, material errors, objections, missed meetings, access/deletion compliance and invoice. Rehearse incident escalation and the reporting clock. Expand only when privacy and utility both pass; otherwise stop capture and return to human notes.
