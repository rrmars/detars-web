**Worried about spending your Lisbon break rushing uphill or watching every euro? If you are travelling solo in your 20s, allow €277.40 for the plan plus €50 for surprises: €327.40 in all, leaving €122.60 within a €450 limit, excluding flights. Stay in Baixa, take Alfama slowly and choose Cascais rather than squeezing in Sintra too. Keep the final afternoon clear enough to reach the airport comfortably.**

**The prices below are from 2026-11-05, not offers for 2027-05-21–2027-05-23. The transport budget uses published 2026 fares; beds, meals, walking, queues and travel buffers are estimates. Check fares, rooms, opening hours and closures before booking: nothing here reserves a bed or ticket for you.**

## Start with your flight times and your energy

This plan runs from Friday 2027-05-21 to Sunday 2027-05-23, for 1 adult staying 2 nights in a dorm. All times are local Lisbon time. It works best if you land at Lisbon Airport (LIS) early enough to reach Baixa around 11:00 and fly home on Sunday around 21:00 or later. Your actual flight times and terminal still need to be checked; adjust the sightseeing around them, not the other way round.

Leave room to sit down. Lisbon's hills, cobblestones and stairs make short map distances slower than they look. Wear grippy shoes, carry water and take the tram uphill. Estimated walking, with rests, is 5–7 km on Friday, 6–8 km on Saturday and 4–6 km on Sunday. Choose Cascais for a change of pace: sea views and a walkable town, without palace tickets or hill transfers. Sintra can be affordable too, but combining both would take away the breathing room. Keep the coast day away from your flight home.

Ask for the full stay total, the bed or room type, cancellation terms, breakfast, towels, lockers and deposits. If you want a private room, price it separately rather than assuming this allowance will cover it.

You may be able to drop your bag before your room is ready. Lisbon Lounge lists check-in from 15:00; Home's house rules say 16:00, although its description differs. Plan to return after sightseeing and before the stated 00:00 cutoff. Confirm bag storage for departure day too, and keep cash available for refundable deposits: it is tied up temporarily, not money you have spent on the trip.

Check CP the week before travel and again that morning. If daytime replacement buses are announced, allow an estimated extra 30–60 minutes each way and shorten the coastal walk. If that makes the day feel rushed, stay in Lisbon for neighbourhood walks and cafés. Check the weather, beach access and swimming conditions locally; use sheltered streets and follow any barriers rather than pushing through the plan.

Aim to be central by 15:30–16:00. | Allow €0 for these public-space views, not entry to the monuments. No rooftop or interior ticket is included; follow works barriers. If late, skip the tower walk first. |
| 16:00 onward | Collect your bag and have an early dinner or takeaway. Allow 60–75 minutes from hostel to airport terminal, plus any Terminal 2 transfer your airline requires. | Allow €15 for dinner; your Sunday pass includes Metro. Work back from your own flight time, not just this example. |

**Do not plan the church for Sunday morning.** It has different hours from the cloister: the published Sunday church hours were 14:00–17:00. Do not assume free Sunday admission as a foreign visitor. Cloister tickets can sell out; if you decide to buy ahead, use the [official seller linked by the operator](https://mmp.bymeoblueticket.pt/pt/event/14759/mosteiro-dos-jeronimos-claustro). You still need to buy your own ticket. If sold out or the queue is too long, enjoy the exterior for €0 and keep the €18 rather than relying on a reseller.

**Give yourself an unhurried trip to the airport.** Aim to arrive at least 3 hours before departure, or earlier if your airline requires. Leave central Lisbon at least 4¼ hours before the flight; add 30 minutes for Terminal 2 or disruption. For a 21:00 flight, leave by 16:45, or 16:15 with that extra margin. If your flight is earlier, shorten Belém. Keep the time buffer even if you have money left for a taxi.

## Choose passes that leave room to change your mind

Use these standard adult prices from 2026-11-05 to plan, then check what you will pay in 2027. You can compare the [Metro fare page](https://www.metrolisboa.pt/en/buy/), [card guidance](https://www.metrolisboa.pt/en/buy/viva-viagem-card/), [CP conditions](https://www.cp.pt/info/w/tickets-lisbon) and [CP tariff effective 2026-01-01](https://www.cp.pt/info/documents/d/cp/precos-comboios-urbanos-lisboa). Being a tourist in your 20s does not automatically qualify you for local youth transport passes.

| Ticket or card | Price to plan from | How you would use it |
|---|---|---|
| Navegante occasional support card | €0.50 | Card is separate from fare; reusable. |
| Carris/Metro ticket, 24 hours | €7.25 | Friday and Sunday; regular buses/trams and Metro, including airport Metro. |
| Carris/Metro/CP ticket, 24 hours | €11.40 | Saturday adds CP Lisbon urban lines including Cascais, not all Portugal trains. |
| Carris/Metro single | €1.90 | Valid 60 minutes under conditions; not consecutive separate Metro journeys. |
| Metro zapping / bank-card gate payment | €1.72 / €1.92 | Alternatives, not additions to a valid pass; zapping is operator-specific. |
| Onboard Carris tram / bus | €3.30 / €2.30 | Costlier fallback per [Carris fare sheet effective 2026-01-01](https://www.carris.pt/media/zwth41fn/a4_prices_vertical_025.pdf); do not pay again with a valid loaded pass. |
| CP Lisbon–Cascais return / CP zapping journey | €5.10 / €2.05 | CP-only return does not include Lisbon Metro/Carris. |

Set aside €7.25 + €11.40 + €7.25 + 3 × €0.50 for cards = €27.40. A separate card each day keeps loading simple, but it is not the cheapest choice: reusing empty or expired cards can save up to €1. CP allows only 1 ticket type on a card at a time; do not load a different product over an unexpired ticket or zapping balance. Buy or load at Metro machines or offices, or CP machines or offices for the combined ticket. Keep receipts and validate when boarding and at gates as required. Passes last 24 hours from first validation, not until midnight; CP's final 30 minutes allow exit only, not entry.

Want to spend less? Walk to and from Cais do Sodré and use the CP-only Saturday return. Transport then costs €21.10, saving €6.30 with the same card allowance. The main budget keeps the combined ticket so you can take local transport when your legs need a break. You do not need a Lisboa Card; sightseeing buses, ferries, taxis and private tours are not included.

Any personal insurance or phone purchases must fit the remaining €122.60; keep extra available cash for refundable deposits.

## Before you book, settle the things that could change your day

In 2027-04–2027-05, before travelling, get actual room totals and cancellation terms. Check fares, 12E/15E diversions, CP works, Jerónimos opening and ticket eligibility, and your airline's terminal and cutoff. The prices above do not reserve anything. If you need to shorten a day, drop the final Friday hill, the Saturday coastal extension or the Sunday tower walk. A sold-out cloister can be an exterior visit; major coastal disruption can be a Lisbon day instead. You are here to enjoy the city, not finish a checklist.
