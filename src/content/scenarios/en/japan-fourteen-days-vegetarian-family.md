You can give the children a varied Japan holiday without changing hotels every other night—or hoping that vegetable noodles contain no fish stock. For this Mumbai family of two parents and children aged 9 and 13, I would **arrive in Tokyo, leave from Osaka/Kansai and use four bases: Tokyo 4 nights → Hakone 2 → Kyoto 4 → Osaka 3**. Nara is a day trip from Kyoto. Keep Hiroshima optional.

The dates are **April 2, 2027 to April 15, 2027**, assuming those are arrival and departure dates in Japan, not flight dates out of Mumbai. The ground plan is **¥1,216,700 / ₹790,855 including 15% contingency**, excluding international flights and airfare-bundled taxes. Against ₹900,000, that leaves ₹109,145 at the assumed ¥1 = ₹0.65. These are planning allowances, not a holiday you can already buy at that price.

**Do not buy a nationwide JR Pass for this route.** Separate intercity tickets are substantially cheaper. If you take the Hakone transport loop, the three-day Odawara Hakone Freepass fits the two-night stay; a Kansai–Hiroshima pass makes sense only if you keep the Hiroshima return trip.

Hotel configurations, menus and rules below were read on **November 7, 2026**. They are not April availability, current quotes or assurances of future eligibility. Leave one main outing per day, choose food before sightseeing, and protect the afternoon rest.

## Set up the Mumbai visa applications before you commit

For **Indian ordinary-passport residents**, India is not on the ordinary short-stay exemption list. Eligible tourism eVisas use an **agency/VFS submission route**, not independent self-service online filing. Each family member needs an individual visa. Residence jurisdiction controls, not the departure airport: this Mumbai-resident example uses Mumbai's sightseeing-without-guarantor checklist. Ordinary direct Consulate intake ended on April 20, 2026. Check the [MOFA exemption list](https://www.mofa.go.jp/j_info/visit/visa/short/novisa.html), [online-visa route](https://www.mofa.go.jp/j_info/visit/visa/visaonline.html), [mission jurisdiction table](https://www.bengaluru.in.emb-japan.go.jp/itpr_ja/11_000001_00032.html) and [Mumbai visa information](https://www.mumbai.in.emb-japan.go.jp/itpr_en/visa_information.html) before filing. Translating this guide does not change this family's Indian-passport eligibility.

Use the [Mumbai VFS information](https://www.vfsglobal.com/one-pager/japan/india/mumbai/index.html) and its [CL-01 checklist](https://www.vfsglobal.com/one-pager/japan/india/mumbai/pdf/CL-01-Visitor-Visa-Without-Guarantor.pdf), whose body is labelled November 2025:

- Current and old passports; copies of first and last pages.
- An individual completed, signed application and recent colour photo.
- Covering letter, detailed itinerary, confirmed hotel and flight reservations.
- Latest personal ITR and the last three months' personal bank statements; employment leave/NOC or business-ownership evidence where applicable.
- For dependant/student children: student IDs, parental consent, sponsoring parent's finances and relationship proof.
- Requested travel history over the last two years to Australia, Canada, Europe, USA and Japan.

Use A4, no staples and checklist order. One common family-document set does not replace individual passports, forms and photos. Birth certificates and a joint sponsorship/consent letter are practical ways to show relationships and consent, not universally prescribed extra templates. School leave and documents linking differing names are sensible preparations. **Confirm the exact minor-signature method**; the generic parent-consent FAQ does not settle Mumbai's signing instructions. No fixed bank balance, notarisation requirement or three-year ITR rule is established here.

Local VFS guidance says six months' passport validity from entry: for April 2, use **October 2, 2027 or later** as the expiry benchmark. The [general eVisa FAQ](https://www.evisa.mofa.go.jp/faq) is less restrictive, but use the stricter local intake benchmark and check airline rules. A renewed passport needs a new visa; use the same passport as the issuance notice.

For the photo, local guidance says **2 × 2 inches**, white background, no more than 6 months old, face 70–80%. The [Mumbai-linked form](https://www.vfsglobal.com/one-pager/japan/india/mumbai/pdf/Visa-Application-Form.pdf) has 45 × 45 mm or 2 × 2 inches; the [generic MOFA form](https://www.mofa.go.jp/mofaj/files/000124525.pdf) instead has 45 × 35 mm or 2 × 1.4 inches. Use the common local 2 × 2 inch option and recheck rather than pretending the forms agree.

## Buy the journeys you need, not a pass for the trip's length

Rail calculations use **3 adults + 1 child**: the 13-year-old pays adult fares; the 9-year-old pays child fares. [JR rules](https://www.jr-odekake.net/ticket/guide/assets/pdf/stipulation/2026/covenant2-03.pdf) treat age 12+ as adult and 6–11 as child. Do not simply halve a combined adult fare: components and special child grids have different rounding. Odawara–Kyoto below is ¥6,040 child, not ¥6,050.

The [reserved smartEX fare sheets](https://smart-ex.jp/en/product/plan/service/pdf/service_fares_reserved.pdf) and [non-reserved sheets](https://smart-ex.jp/en/product/plan/service/pdf/service_fares_non_reserved.pdf) were linked by the operator but labelled **as of April 1, 2025**. These are regular-season ordinary-class smartEX Service examples, not April 2027 station-counter quotes. The exact 2027 tariff and seasonal dates remain unverified. [smartEX Service](https://smart-ex.jp/en/product/plan/service/) does not include city feeders or an overnight stopover on one through ticket: buy separate tickets around Hakone. The retrieved [seasonal calendar](https://smart-ex.jp/en/product/set_dates/) showed 2025/2026, which does not prove no 2027 calendar exists elsewhere.

## Make the vegetarian plan specific enough to trust

**Plan vegan by default**, meeting no-meat/no-fish-stock needs without assuming this family eats eggs. Add dairy only if wanted. [JNTO's vegetarian guidance](https://www.japan.travel/en/guide/vegetarian-guide/) warns about hidden bonito and animal ingredients. Tofu, miso, curry, soba sauce, vegetable dishes and plain-looking rice snacks do not establish stock-free preparation.

Show this Japanese card, an author translation rather than an allergy certificate:

> 肉・魚・魚介類を食べません。かつおだし、煮干し、魚醤、肉のスープ、ゼラチンも食べません。動物性の材料を使わないヴィーガン料理をお願いします。野菜だしや昆布・しいたけのだしは大丈夫です。

It says no meat, fish, seafood, bonito/dried-fish stock, fish sauce, meat broth or gelatin; please use no animal ingredients, with vegetable/kombu/shiitake stock acceptable. If staff cannot confirm, choose another meal. This does not promise no cross-contact.

Use a kitchen/grocery breakfast, then decide lunch and dinner before leaving. Check operators' menus and closure notices the evening before. Carry a complete shelf-stable meal for Hakone, Nara and long trains: plain rice, fruit, nuts and labelled beans/tofu can help, but sauces and fillings need checking. Do not presume kombu/ume onigiri, inari or tempura is fish-free. Prefer dedicated vegan venues; mixed kitchens need dish-and-stock confirmation. Allergies require a separate equipment/cross-contact discussion.

### Check tax inclusions rather than assuming “all in”

[Kyoto's bands from March 1, 2026](https://kyoto.travel/en/news/tax-change/) are per person/night: below ¥6,000 → ¥200; ¥6,000–19,999 → ¥400; ¥20,000–49,999 → ¥1,000; ¥50,000–99,999 → ¥4,000; ¥100,000+ → ¥10,000. Illustratively, ¥45,000 ÷ 4 = ¥11,250, giving 4 people × 4 nights × ¥400 = **¥6,400**.

[Osaka's bands from September 1, 2025](https://osaka-info.jp/en/information/to-tourist/news-lodging-tax/index.html) use room-only per-person/night amounts: below ¥5,000 exempt; ¥5,000–14,999 → ¥200; ¥15,000–19,999 → ¥400; ¥20,000+ → ¥500. Mandatory service/cleaning is included, meals excluded. Illustratively, ¥35,000 ÷ 4 = ¥8,750; 4 × 3 × ¥200 = **¥2,400**. Combined ¥8,800 leaves **¥3,200** of the ¥12,000 tax allowance for Tokyo/Hakone/bathing tax. Actual invoice allocation and inclusions control; this is not a verified tax bill or Tokyo/Hakone statutory-rate claim.
