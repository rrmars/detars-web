## Start with weight and support, not the biggest battery

The [Galaxy M07's official specifications](https://www.samsung.com/in/smartphones/galaxy-m/galaxy-m07-black-64gb-sm-m075fzkdins/) give its weight as 184 g, the lightest here. Samsung advertises six OS generations and six years of security support, with an explicit security end date of August 31, 2031. That does not mean six years after your purchase. Its compromises are an HD+ screen, mono sound, 64 GB storage and no optical image stabilisation. It is a reasoned fit, not a camera or display test winner.

The moto g37 is the backup if its stereo speakers and claimed brighter screen make a useful difference to him. It costs ₹14,499 in the dated example, leaving only ₹501 under the cap before accessories or extra charges. Its model-specific OS-upgrade count and security-support duration were not established. Ask for that information before treating it as a long-term payment phone.

**Counterintuitive finding:** a bigger battery can make the phone harder to use. The 7,000 mAh alternatives weigh 215–220 g. For mildly shaky hands, I would favour a lighter handset and a stand over maximum battery capacity. A good home connection matters more for these tasks than choosing 5G just for its label; try calls on his actual Wi-Fi and mobile network.

### Practise the things he will actually do

For WhatsApp, start with the family chat and its video-camera button. For photos, clean the lens, use good light and 1× rather than digital zoom. Rest elbows or the phone on something solid; try a short timer for posed pictures. Agree a backup routine if he wants one. Check storage monthly: 64 GB needs care with downloads and WhatsApp media. Review large files together instead of deleting memories blindly.

## Make UPI safety easy to remember

**To receive money, do not enter your UPI PIN.** Do not scan a stranger's QR code or approve a collect request to “receive a refund”. [NPCI's fraud guidance](https://www.npci.org.in/fraud-awareness) supports the pay-not-receive warning. Read the recipient name and amount before authorising a payment. Never share a PIN, OTP or screen with someone claiming to be a bank, courier, police officer or support agent.

Start with one official-store payment app and a small supervised payment. Let him enter his own UPI PIN privately. Check the successful transaction record; if it says pending, do not immediately retry. Ask his bank or app whether a lower daily or transaction limit is available. There is no universal Android switch. A separate low-balance account is an option only if he is comfortable managing it.

Initially leave UPI Lite, AutoPay/mandates and biometric payment authorisation off unless he understands that specific flow. Some legitimate payments can happen without a PIN. Teach **“only approve payments I started”**, not “no PIN means safe”. The one-app layout and these initial choices are practical recommendations, not NPCI requirements.

Rehearse a short response to KYC-expiry, refund, prize, parcel or “digital arrest” threats: **hang up, do not tap, call saved family**. A familiar photo, logo or displayed number is not proof. Find bank support in its app or on the card, not through the caller.

If money goes missing, contact the bank/payment app's official fraud support and call **1930 immediately**. Report through the [National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal](https://cybercrime.gov.in/) and keep transaction IDs and messages. Do not wait for family to investigate or pay a recovery fee. Follow [official police guidance](https://navimumbaipolice.gov.in/en/citizen/cyber-security) to report promptly and preserve evidence.

## Help from Bengaluru without taking control away

Keep his real birth date and adult account. [Google's Family Link documentation](https://support.google.com/families/answer/7103338?hl=en) concerns child supervision; do not turn him into a child account to offer remote help. Start with an ordinary call and the paper guide.

If he wants attended screen help, [TeamViewer's Android instructions](https://www.teamviewer.com/en/global/support/knowledge-base/teamviewer-classic/mobile/android/remote-control-an-android-device-via-attended-access/) describe QuickSupport on his phone and Remote Control on yours. Set it up together, never at an unexpected caller's request. Account/licence rules, add-ons and viewing versus full control vary; this has not been tested on the M07.

1. Call through the saved family number. Ask him to open QuickSupport and share its connection ID with you, not a stranger.
2. He must approve Allow and any screen-capture prompt. Explain overlay or accessibility permissions before granting them. Do not enable unattended access.
3. Finish, disconnect and close the app. Revoke optional control permissions if no longer needed.
4. **End every screen-sharing session before banking, UPI, PINs or OTPs.** [Google Pay's warning](https://support.google.com/pay/india/answer/10768310?hl=en-IN) explains why screen sharing and payments must stay separate.

If approval steps confuse him, skip remote control. Voice coaching or trusted in-person help is better than a permanently open support app. Agree a weekly check-in and tell him before making changes.

## Set up emergency help without making a test emergency call

Search Settings for Emergency SOS or Safety and emergency. Read this exact handset's instructions before enabling anything. Add two agreed family emergency contacts and essential medical information where supported. Explain that information shown on the locked screen can be read by someone holding the phone. Location sharing needs consent, permissions and connectivity; it is not guaranteed.

Review the emergency number, trigger, confirmation or countdown, and contact-sharing options. Prefer a confirmation step if it is offered and usable for his hands. **Write the SOS shortcut on the paper guide only after checking the phone.** [Generic Android help](https://support.google.com/android/answer/9319337?hl=en) describes five or more power-button presses; [Android's India guidance](https://source.android.com/docs/core/connect/emergency-affordance) allows manufacturer-specific triggers, including triple press. Neither establishes this M07's or g37's actual gesture. Do not teach a guessed count.

For a real emergency, show Phone → keypad → **112** → call, and where Emergency call appears on the locked screen. Practise finding the controls without placing a call. Use a demonstration only if it explicitly says no emergency call will be made. If a call connects accidentally, stay on the line and explain the mistake. Test ordinary calls to family separately. SOS does not replace a nearby person who can help.

## FAQ

### Is 5G worth a heavier phone for these tasks?

Not by itself. The recommendation favours the lighter M07 if calls and videos work on his actual connection. The g37 is the backup if its screen and stereo sound genuinely help him, but its support duration still needs checking.

### Can I rely on Easy Mode and Tamil menus?

No. Exact M07 Easy Mode support and the shortlisted Indian variants' Tamil language lists were not established. Keep the built-in home screen sparse, enlarge text and check Tamil menus, messages and keyboard separately on the phone.

### Can a WhatsApp shortcut make every video call one tap?

Do not assume so. A chat shortcut may only open the chat. Opening the familiar family chat and tapping its camera button is the fallback; test any direct call shortcut before teaching it.

### Should I use Family Link or leave remote support connected?

Neither is the plan. Keep his adult account and consent. Start with voice coaching; use attended help only if he can approve it. Disconnect before any payment or secret entry.
