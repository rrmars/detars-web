**Worried that moving abroad will use up your ₹18 lakh before you find work? My pick is to keep earning in Bengaluru while pursuing an offer-first German EU Blue Card. Plan about ₹5.24 lakh–₹8.30 lakh for that move through the initial payroll runway. Canada becomes more convincing with French, a genuine qualifying offer or a real provincial nomination—not an English-only profile and a hoped-for invitation.**

This is for an Indian citizen, age 29, single, with a BTech in computer science, six years of Java/Go/cloud backend experience, no German and no language test yet. Rules, fees and market examples are dated November 6, 2026 for a possible 2027 move. If your passport or qualifications differ, check your own eligibility and costs before using these figures.

Amounts marked ₹ are Indian rupees; 1 lakh = 100,000 rupees. Keep the Indian savings profile when using this comparison.

## First, a Canadian change that affects every comparison

The [IRCC reform notice](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/changes.html) says that November 3, 2026 introduced a Labour Market Integration factor and qualifying high-wage-offer points. Existing profiles were being recalculated, potentially for up to two weeks, with invitation rounds paused. Do not read that dated pause as a live status update.

With no Canadian work or offer, your model still gets zero for this factor. A genuine qualifying software offer could add **70 CRS points** under the [occupation list](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/check-score/high-wage.html). **Every draw shown below predates November 3, 2026. Those cutoffs cannot predict rankings after the offer-points change.**

## Which gate can you actually open?

| Decision | Germany: offer-first Blue Card | Germany: Opportunity Card | Canada: offshore Express Entry |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entry gate | Eligible offer for at least 6 months, salary floor and degree or IT-experience evidence | Recognised-skilled-worker branch, or qualification, language and 6 points; funding | Eligible FSW profile, then invitation; eligibility is not selection |
| Model cash capacity | ₹5.24 lakh–₹8.30 lakh, including refundable deposit and initial runway | ₹16.71 lakh–₹19.15 lakh for 12-month funding, setup and reserve; tight against ₹18 lakh | ₹12.65 lakh–₹13.28 lakh base; preferably ₹14.15 lakh–₹15.78 lakh with contingency |
| First full-time salary from the snapshot | Planning: 4–10 months if an offer arrives in the next few months | Planning: 4–12+ months; a full-time job may never arrive | Planning: 12–24+ months only with an early invitation; pool wait can be indefinite |
| Main risk | Hiring, employer support, documents and visa processing | Same hiring risk while spending savings abroad | Invitation, category changes, processing and then job search; PR does not supply a job |
| Permanent status | Settlement eligibility after 21 months with B1 or 27 months with A1, qualifying work/contributions and other conditions | First obtain a qualifying work title; job-search time does not start the Blue Card clock | PR on approved landing/confirmation, not several years of work first |
| Ordinary citizenship eligibility | Normally 5 years lawful habitual residence and B1; processing extra | Same law, with qualifying status at application | Normally 1,095 physical-presence days within 5 years, with PR and other conditions; processing/oath extra |

These are planning ranges, not official processing times or success probabilities. A Blue Card is temporary work residence; Express Entry selects for PR. See [Blue Card law](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__18g.html), [Opportunity Card law](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__20a.html), [settlement law](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__18c.html), [German citizenship law](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stag/__10.html), [EE pool guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/create-profile.html) and [Canadian citizenship conditions](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/canadian-citizenship/adult-minor/who.html).

## Germany: secure the job before spending the runway

### Salary thresholds are dated, not a promise for your move

| Blue Card route | 2026 annual gross floor | What you need |
|---|---:|---|
| Standard academic | €50,700; €4,225/month | Comparable qualification and work appropriate to it |
| Shortage/IT degree | €45,934.20; €3,827.85/month | Eligible occupation and Federal Employment Agency consent |
| IT experience instead of degree | €45,934.20 | At least 3 relevant years in the last 7, tertiary-equivalent level, eligible ICT duties and Agency consent |

All require an offer lasting at least 6 months. Six years on a CV is promising, not proof: get dated letters describing duties and seniority. The floors follow the [2026 pension ceiling](https://www.bmas.de/DE/Service/Gesetze-und-Gesetzesvorhaben/sozialversicherungs-rechengroessenverordnung-2026.html) of €101,400, multiplied by 50% or 45.3% in [§18g](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__18g.html).

**Do not carry those floors into 2027.** The [BMAS proposal](https://www.bmas.de/DE/Service/Gesetze-und-Gesetzesvorhaben/sozialversicherungs-rechengroessenverordnung-2027.html), with Cabinet decision October 7, 2026, proposed a €106,200 ceiling. Applying the existing multipliers gives €53,100 / €48,108.60. These are conditional calculations, **not verified final 2027 Blue Card thresholds**. The annual notice is due by December 31, 2026; check the final notice before contracting or applying.

A separate [experienced-IT employment route](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/beschv_2013/__6.html) uses 2 years in the last 5 and a 2026 salary formula of €45,630, with a collective-pay exception. It is not the Blue Card; do not transfer its accelerated settlement timetable to that route.

### Check the awarding university, not just “AICTE approved”

For the academic route, check both the exact awarding university's anabin H+ result and the degree's equivalent/corresponding result. AICTE approval is not German comparability. If those entries are insufficient, a ZAB Statement of Comparability costs **€208**. Regular processing is 3 months after complete documents and payment. The Blue Card service targets 2 weeks with employer evidence, including a signed contract or accepted employer letter of intent. That is not visa processing. Opportunity Card cases do not get this expedition.

Use the [India employment checklist](https://india.diplo.de/in-en/service/2539264-2539264), [ZAB FAQ](https://zab.kmk.org/en/statement-of-comparability/faq), [application process](https://zab.kmk.org/en/statement-comparability/application) and [Blue Card service](https://zab.kmk.org/en/eu-blue-card). The mission checklist retains 2025 salary figures; use it for document scope, not current floors. No institution-specific result is established because the university is missing.

[APS India](https://aps-india.de/faqs/) principally describes student-visa verification. It is not listed as routine Blue Card evidence in the employment checklist and does not replace anabin/ZAB. A mission can still request additional verification. If degree comparability fails, assess the experience-based Blue Card rather than assuming Germany is closed.

### Opportunity Card: a fallback, not a cure for hiring risk

The recognised-skilled-worker branch has no minimum points or points-branch language minimum. Otherwise, a state-recognised foreign qualification, German A1 **or** English B2, secured living costs and at least 6 points are needed. An English-medium degree should not be assumed sufficient language proof.

| Potential component | Points | Condition |
|---|---:|---|
| Age 29 | 2 | Age at application |
| At least 5 related post-qualification years in last 7 | 3 | Verify dates and duties |
| CS qualification in shortage group | 1 | Verify qualification mapping |
| No German; English B2 prerequisite | 0 | B2 opens the branch, not a bonus |
| Conditional total | 6 | Without the shortage point, only 5; proven English C1 can add 1 |

The initial card lasts up to 1 year. Work averages at most 20 hours/week in total, with qualifying trials up to 2 weeks per employer. It is not unrestricted full-time or freelance permission. Change to an appropriate work title for a normal full-time job. A follow-up card is not an automatic extension for unsuccessful search. See the [Foreign Office portal](https://digital.diplo.de/chancenkarte), [§20a](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__20a.html), [§20b](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__20b.html) and [points annex](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/anlage.html).

The dated funding benchmark is €1,091/month × 12 = **€13,092**, about ₹13.88 lakh. This is living capital, not a fee. Insurance, account costs, deposit and cash before the first release are separate; the release may not cover Munich housing. Recheck the amount for 2027. For an offer-first Blue Card, adequate salary normally supplies the subsistence basis. Do not automatically add the Opportunity Card's full blocked account; cover any employment gap and mission-specific request instead. [Subsistence law](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__2.html) includes health cover.

### Compare the offer with rent, not the city's reputation

| City | Employer-posted backend data, all levels | Suggested search target, not a guaranteed offer |
|---|---|---|
| Berlin | Median €65,000; interquartile €60,000–75,000; top-decile boundary €80,000 | Roughly €65,000–80,000; €80k+ is a stronger/senior outcome |

The [Berlin](https://germantechjobs.de/en/salaries/Backend/Berlin/all) and [Munich](https://germantechjobs.de/en/salaries/Backend/Munich/all) data cover employer-posted salary ranges. The stated 2,000+ jobs are across the dataset, not each city's sample of engineers like you. Counterintuitively, Munich does not automatically pay more: Berlin's median is higher here. Compare actual offers and housing.

The Blue Card has no German admission test. English-only hiring still narrows your options. Start A1 and aim for B1: settlement needs 27 qualifying work/contribution months with A1 or 21 with B1, plus livelihood, housing and other conditions. Zero German does not qualify. Ordinary citizenship normally needs 5 lawful habitual-residence years, B1, qualifying status and other conditions. A 2027 residence start points to 2032 earliest ordinary eligibility, not a passport date. The [BAMF summary dated June 30, 2025](https://www.bamf.de/EN/Themen/Integration/ZugewanderteTeilnehmende/Einbuergerung/einbuergerung-node.html) still mentions a deleted 3-year exception; the current statute controls.

## Canada: language scores open two different gates

### An overall IELTS score is not a CRS score

Assume age remains 29, ECA awards one Canadian bachelor's-equivalent credential and your work qualifies. There is no Canadian work/study, sibling, nomination or offer. BTech equivalence is not established yet. Use IELTS **General Training**, not Academic; One Skill Retake is not accepted for EE. See [language rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/documents/language-test.html) and [ECA rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/documents/education-assessment.html).

| All CLB8 | 7.5 / 6.5 / 6.5 / 6.5 | 8 / 8 / 8 / 8 |
| 7.5 in every ability | 7.5 / 7.5 / 7.5 / 7.5 | 8 / 9 / 10 / 10 |

These use the [dated CRS rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/check-score/crs-criteria.html). Foreign work reaches its bracket at 3 years; 6 years does not double it. One bachelor's earns 25, not 50, education-transfer points at CLB9. Moving CLB8 → CLB9 adds 32 language + 12 education-transfer + 25 work-transfer = 69 points. CLB9 → CLB10 adds only 12. At age 30 subtract 5 CRS from every scenario. **These scores use the November 3, 2026 framework; the historical draws below use the earlier pool and are not directly predictive.**

FSW's separate 67/100 gate is not a CRS invitation cutoff. Age 12 + bachelor's 21 + six qualifying years 15 = 48. CLB8 adds 20 → 68; 7.5 each adds 23 → 71; CLB9/10 adds 24 → 72. All-CLB7 alone gives 64 and fails without another factor. You also need a continuous paid qualifying year in TEER0–3 within 10 years, matching duties, funds, admissibility and intent outside Quebec. Without test/ECA, eligibility is not established. ### Money and paperwork come before a landing plan

[IRCC's funds table](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/documents/proof-funds.html) displayed **CAD15,263** for one person, about ₹10.33 lakh, still labelled July 7, 2025. A separate 2026 revision was not confirmed. Recheck for your application. Funds must be unborrowed and accessible at application and visa issuance. Bank letters include debts and 6-month average balances. This is neither a fee nor a blocked account. An overseas offer alone does not exempt you; Canadian work authorisation is also required for that exemption.

The [fee list](https://ircc.canada.ca/english/information/fees/fees.asp), modified September 21, 2026, gives CAD990 processing + CAD600 right of PR + CAD85 biometrics = **CAD1,675**, about ₹1.13 lakh. EE profile creation has no government application fee. PNP fees are excluded. [After-application guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/after-apply.html) requires biometrics for each PR application for the applicable age group.

[WES](https://www.wes.org/eca/) is one designated ECA option, not compulsory. Its [immigration ECA fee](https://www.wes.org/eca-2/) is CAD264 plus tax/delivery: standard CAD14 or international courier CAD97. That gives CAD278 or CAD361 before tax; the high budget models CAD407.93 with 13% tax, subject to actual checkout. Its [published timing](https://www.wes.org/current-processing/) is typically 2 weeks document review plus 1 week evaluation, or 2 for complex cases, after required documents arrive. University collection and verification holds add time. Plan 1–3+ months end-to-end, not a guaranteed 3 weeks.

Language results must be under 2 years old and ECA under 5 at profile and PR submission. A pool profile lasts 1 year. After invitation, normally 60 days remain for the complete PR application. The route is preparation → profile → potentially indefinite invitation wait → application/processing → landing → job search. The [live processing tool](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/check-processing-times.html) did not yield a confirmed program-specific month figure; a historic 6-month standard is not the whole journey.

For a direct overseas PR newcomer, citizenship normally needs about 3 physical years in Canada: 1,095 days within 5 years, PR and other tax/language/test conditions. Processing and oath add time; travel extends it. A hypothetical 2027 landing suggests around 2030 presence eligibility, not citizenship that year. The later CAD653 citizenship fee is excluded from the PR budget.

## Questions you may still have

### Can I get a Blue Card without a German-comparable degree?

Possibly, through the IT-experience exception. You still need the eligible offer, salary, documented qualifying experience and consent. A CV alone is not approval.

### Does IELTS 7.5 mean Canada is within reach?

Not by itself. Scores are per ability, ECA is unresolved and FSW eligibility is separate from invitation ranking. Get the diagnostic vector before paying for a move.

### Is the Opportunity Card funding lost money?

No. It is living capital released for your stay, not a government fee. But spending it while job hunting reduces your return cushion, and part-time work is not assured.

### Would French make Canada a sure thing?

No. It raises the model score and can open the French category. Test attainment, eligible profile and future post-reform draws still matter.
