**Worried about heat, traffic and feeding a vegetarian child? Keep the temples early, make lunch a cool-down stop and leave the afternoons loose. For two adults and children aged 8 and 12, this plan sets aside THB 42,400 for spending and THB 8,800 as reserve: THB 51,200 / USD 1,600, excluding international flights. The assumed exchange rate is USD 1 = THB 32, not a live quote.**

The example runs Thursday, June 24, 2027 to Sunday, June 27, 2027, with three hotel nights. All times are Bangkok time, the same as Jakarta. Prices and rules retain their November 6, 2026 research date; no sources were refreshed for this draft. June rooms, tickets, closures and schedules are not confirmed. Nothing was booked, submitted or paid for, no account was created and nobody was contacted.

## Put the room decision before the sightseeing

I would start with Centre Point Plus Hotel Silom near BTS Saphan Taksin and Sathorn Pier. Allow a child-paced 5–10 minute walk to the station and 5–10 minutes to the pier. Kitchen space helps with breakfast and a vegetarian backup meal. All routes below start here; walking times are estimates.

These are advertised room/night ranges seen on November 6, 2026, not offers for your dates. ## Feed the vegetarian child before everyone gets hungry

Tofu is not proof of a vegetarian dish. Ask about fish sauce, oyster sauce, shrimp paste and meat stock, plus eggs and dairy according to your child's preferences. Vegetarian does not automatically mean vegan.

Ginger Farm Kitchen, ICONSIAM 6F, is a year-round mixed restaurant in the mall's vegetarian-friendly directory: THB 250–400/main estimated. Same-mall backup Masala Art, 6F, has vegetable dal, rice or paneer possibilities if dairy is accepted: THB 250–450/main estimated, potentially plus service/tax. The guide is dated October 16, 2025, not an assurance of a current dish or an October-only festival stall. Neither kitchen is guaranteed allergy-safe.

Everyday estimated prices: rice/tofu/vegetables THB 80–150; mall meal THB 100–180; fruit THB 40–80; bottled water THB 10–25. If no counter can explain ingredients, buy bread, fruit, yogurt if accepted and a labelled vegetarian ready meal. Use the room's kitchen space and carry snacks for both temple mornings and Chatuchak.

Save this dietary request on your phone:

> เด็กคนนี้กินมังสวิรัติ ไม่ใส่เนื้อสัตว์ ปลา อาหารทะเล น้ำปลา น้ำมันหอย กะปิ หรือน้ำซุปเนื้อสัตว์

“This child is vegetarian. No meat, fish, seafood, fish sauce, oyster sauce, shrimp paste, or meat stock.” Add **mai phet** for not spicy. **เจ (jay)** is a useful strict-vegetarian cue, but still ask. This is a dietary request, not an allergy statement.

The estimated **THB 8,000 family food envelope** includes roughly THB 1,000 for three breakfasts, THB 2,800 for four lunches, THB 2,700 for three dinners and THB 1,500 for water, snacks and a departure-airport bite. Thursday breakfast is before/on the flight. Hotel breakfast is excluded from the room rate. You cannot choose every example's highest price and assume the same envelope holds.

## Heat, rain and airport transfers: make the escape easy

June climate planning uses roughly 33–34°C afternoons and 26–27°C nights, not a forecast. The underlying [weather source](https://www.weather-atlas.com/en/thailand/bangkok-weather-june) was available only as a search excerpt, giving 33.6°C / 26.1°C; its full page was blocked. A wet morning or whole afternoon is possible—there is no guaranteed dry-morning rule.

Leave the market by 11:00 and keep lunch/early afternoons indoors. Bring hats, sunscreen, refillable bottles, raincoats, grippy shoes, spare socks, a waterproof pouch and a thin air-conditioning layer. Pause for water/shade every 45–60 minutes outdoors. If a child feels unwell, stop and cool down. During thunder, get inside a building rather than waiting on an exposed pier or using the pool. Boats can pause and Grab prices/waits can rise. Keep THB 500–1,000 of reserve handy for a larger vehicle or rain detour.

For four people and bags I would use the official taxi queue or a suitably large Grab. A sedan may not take four suitcases. Use seat belts; a child seat/booster is not guaranteed. Check vehicle capacity and bring an appropriate travel booster if needed. The following totals/times are estimates, not official through fares.

[AOT BKK](https://suvarnabhumi.airportthai.co.th/service/way-to-airport/detail/Transportation_BKK) and [AOT DMK](https://donmueang.airportthai.co.th/service/facility/detail/211) support pickup/surcharge rules, not hotel-specific road totals. Airport allowance is THB 2,000 return, not unlimited surge protection. Use the flight-minus-five-hours rule on Sunday. Car estimates are per vehicle, rail per person; both children are costed at full rail fare.

## Indonesian ordinary passports only: entry is a separate decision

**This section is not advice on Chinese-passport eligibility.** The Chinese version translates this same Indonesian profile; other passport holders need their own checks. The following is a dated publication snapshot, not a promise for June 2027 or a fresh immigration check.

As published on November 6, 2026, [Indonesia was listed](https://helsinki.thaiembassy.org/en/publicservice/new-visa-exemption-and-visa-on-arrival-voa-to-thailand?menu=5d80876d15e39c3354007bb1&page=5f49f4a199a85e260f4278de) for up to **30 days** of visa-exempt tourism. The [effective-date notice](https://washingtondc.thaiembassy.org/en/page/visaexempt-voajul2024), updated September 17, 2026, says the scheme began September 15, 2026. Older 60-day guidance from July 2024 is not this snapshot's rule. The four-day trip fits; no visa-on-arrival purchase is budgeted. Admission remains immigration's decision; extensions are irrelevant here and not automatic.

All four travellers, including children aged 8 and 12, need a **free TDAC**, not a visa or guarantee of entry. Use only [the official form](https://tdac.immigration.go.th/). The [Immigration FAQ](https://tdac.immigration.go.th/manual/en/faq.html) allows a parent/family submission, up to 10 travellers. It describes both a three-day window including arrival day and “up to 72 hours”. For June 24, 2027 arrival, complete it on Tuesday, June 22, 2027 or Wednesday, June 23, 2027 if the rule is unchanged. It does **not** mean at least three days ahead. The [embassy guidance](https://london.thaiembassy.org/en/page/exemp-visa) confirms that TDAC is free; avoid paid third-party forms.

Have passport details, flight number/date, Thai hotel address, purpose, health declaration and email ready. Save each person's confirmation/QR offline. Correct changed flights/hotels before entry; uneditable identity changes require a new submission. No forms have been submitted for you.

Carry each person's own passport. [General entry guidance](https://mexico.thaiembassy.org/en/publicservice/visa?cate=641d1920928a6a39d7426b63) says at least six months' validity on arrival: June 24, 2027 means at least December 24, 2027. Leave more margin and check the airline. Carry June 27 return flights and June 24–27 accommodation confirmation. The dated funds guidance says THB 20,000 per person or **THB 40,000 per family**, with Washington specifying cash/equivalent cash. It is proof of means, not a fee or extra cost to add. Check accepted evidence and each passport's permitted-until stamp. Do not import the separate visa-on-arrival fee, 15-day limit or blank-page rules into this exemption profile. Recheck eligibility, documents and TDAC in June before flying.
