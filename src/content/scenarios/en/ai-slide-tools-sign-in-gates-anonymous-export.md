**Need a deck quickly, without opening another account? Among 10 presentation products tried on 2026-11-05, only FreeSlide produced an anonymous PPTX. But it returned 7 slides instead of 6 and invented business figures.** You can use it for a draft, not an unchecked investor pitch. For the tools stopped by sign-in, output quality is untested—not zero.

## Know what you are asking the tool to make

The example business, Brewline, delivers coffee every 2 weeks to offices with 5–50 staff. Plans start at $89/month, with assumed traction of 40 paying offices after 6 months. The brief asked for problem, solution, process, pricing, traction and ask. The valid no-account attempts involved no signup, payment or imported sign-ins. An accidental blank Google deck created in an existing signed-in session was excluded; a separate logged-out attempt reached sign-in instead.

| Tool and entry | What happened when creating | What you can conclude |
|---|---|---|
| [Gamma](https://gamma.app/products/presentations), [Canva](https://www.canva.com/create/ai-presentations/), [Beautiful.ai](https://www.beautiful.ai/ai-presentations) | Create or start led to signup before a prompt could be entered | No deck; quality, export and editing remain untested |
| [Google Slides](https://slides.google.com/create), [PowerPoint web / built-in Copilot](https://powerpoint.cloud.microsoft/) | Creating a presentation led to sign-in before the editor | Built-in AI was not reached; quality and plan eligibility remain untested |
| [SlidesAI](https://www.slidesai.io/), [AiPPT](https://www.aippt.com/), [VibeSlide](https://vibeslide.app/) | A prompt could be entered, but submitting required sign-in before generation | No deck; quality and export remain untested |
| [SlideMaker.app](https://slidemaker.app/ai-presentation-maker) | Creating a presentation opened a login-required dialog before prompt entry | No deck; other conversion routes were not tried |
| [FreeSlide](https://freeslide.app/) | Anonymous generation and PPTX download worked | 7 slides with a watermark; business claims need correcting |
| [Standalone Microsoft Copilot](https://copilot.microsoft.com/) | Opening and retrying returned ERR_CONNECTION_CLOSED | A connection problem, not proof that login is required |

Do not confuse a login requirement with poor output. VibeSlide and SlideMaker advertised creation without signup or login, yet their creation buttons required authentication here. For your own deadline, check the actual creation path rather than relying on the homepage promise.

## Check the business facts before sending the deck

FreeSlide added $149/month and $219/month plans, claimed ~$4.5k in monthly recurring revenue, and proposed a $500k raise, 200 offices and a 12-month deadline. None was supplied or marked as a placeholder. Assumed traction became a statement of business performance. The calculation 40 × $89 = $3,560/month is only an illustration with everyone on the starting price, not actual revenue.

The PPTX contains 36 text boxes and a native pricing table, so those parts are not just flat slide images. Opening, editing and saving through a GUI was not tested. Every slide carries a watermark. Only PPTX export was tried; quotas were not pushed to their limits. Request durations do not tell you how long it takes to reach a usable pitch. Check the downloaded file in your own presentation app before relying on its editing features.

## If the deadline is close, keep the claims simple

A separately authored 6-slide example keeps traction labeled as an assumption and leaves the funding ask blank. That is a useful approach to copy, not proof of a website's free tier. Confirm your real metrics and fundraising terms before presenting. Treat the unchanged FreeSlide deck as something to repair, not send to investors. Anonymous use does not mean your data will not be stored.

These results do not promise the same behavior today, on every URL or in every region. They say nothing about paid-plan quality or export conditions after signing in. If you are willing to use an account, check those conditions separately before spending time on a full deck.
