**Want the night markets without spending your weekend rushing between sights? Stay near Zhongshan, eat at Ningxia on Friday and Raohe on Saturday, and save Sunday morning for Beitou only if the weather suits. Plan NT$14,070 per person, including contingency, within a US$700 budget excluding flights.** Skip Jiufen and Yehliu this time: that is a pacing choice, not a claim that they are impossible.

This plan is for two friends travelling from Kuala Lumpur on 22 October 2027–24 October 2027, sharing a twin room for two nights. All times are Taipei local time, the same clock as Kuala Lumpur. Information was checked on 5 November 2026; menus, entry rules and room prices have not been refreshed for your trip. **Monday 25 October 2027 is a public holiday on the [government calendar](https://www.dgpa.gov.tw/information?uid=353&pid=13028).** The 23–25 October long weekend could mean longer queues and dearer rooms; that is a planning inference, not a demand forecast.

## Keep the route compact

| Day | A comfortable route | If things do not go to plan |
|---|---|---|
| Friday · 22 October 2027 | Airport arrival; Sihai brunch 11:30–13:00; optional Dihua Street walk; hotel rest 15:00–17:15; Ningxia 17:30–20:30 | A late flight means late lunch and no Dihua walk. Room access before check-in is not assumed. |
| Saturday · 23 October 2027 | Fu Hang 06:15–08:30; Yong Kang noodles 11:00–12:30; rest; Raohe 17:00–20:30 | Allow 45–90 minutes for the breakfast queue, possibly more. Return to Sihai rather than lose the morning. |
| Sunday · 24 October 2027 | Breakfast 08:00–09:00; Beitou 09:00–12:30; town lunch and bags 13:00–16:00 | Heavy rain or a valley closure: longer breakfast and Zhongshan browsing. No distant substitute and no evening market before the flight. |

[Ningxia's official listing](https://www.travel.taipei/en/attraction/details/1689) gives daily 17:00–23:30; [Raohe's](https://www.travel.taipei/en/attraction/details/1691) gives daily 17:00–00:00. These support the chosen days, not a guarantee that every stall opens. At either market, check the menu and move on if a queue looks longer than 30–40 minutes. Share snacks, but get your own noodle bowl.

### Friday: land, settle, graze

Assuming Taoyuan Airport (TPE), allow 90–120 minutes after landing for immigration, bags and reaching the train. The [Airport MRT fare matrix](https://www.tymetro.com.tw/tymetro-new/en/_images/document/travel-guide/price.pdf) lists NT$160 each way to A1 Taipei Main. The [official city guide](https://www.travel.taipei/en/information/taoyuanmetro/) gives express rides of 35 minutes from T1 and 38 from T2. These train times do not include your airport or hotel walks.

Allow another 25–40 minutes from A1 to the hotel, including the long station walk and the red line to Zhongshan, one stop taking about 2 minutes aboard. With light luggage, walking may be simpler. Ask about bag storage; the hotel listings show 15:00 check-in, not free early room access. After brunch, Dihua Street is roughly a 20–30 minute walk from this area. Rest properly, then walk about 15–25 minutes to Ningxia; the return walk is similar. All these door-to-door ranges are estimates.

### Saturday: breakfast, beef noodles, pepper buns

For Fu Hang, take red from Zhongshan to Taipei Main, then blue to Shandao Temple. Allow 10–15 minutes through stations and 2–5 minutes on foot to Huashan Market, 2F, 108 Zhongxiao East Road Sec. 1. After breakfast, browse Huashan without committing to a paid exhibition.

For Yong Kang, take blue back to Taipei Main and red to Dongmen: allow 15–20 minutes including transfer, then a 5-minute walk. After lunch and Yongkang Street, Dongmen–Zhongshan is roughly 10–12 minutes aboard; allow 20–25 minutes including waits and the hotel walk. Later, green runs directly Zhongshan–Songshan: about 15–20 minutes aboard, or 25–35 minutes hotel-to-market, with about a 5-minute walk toward Ciyou Temple. Reverse the route afterward. These are planning estimates, not future timetables.

### Sunday: a nature walk, not a spa booking

Check out and ask to store bags before leaving. Red to Beitou takes about 25–30 minutes, then the Xinbeitou branch about 3–5 minutes; allow 40–50 minutes station-to-station with waiting and transfers, and the same coming back. [Thermal Valley](https://www.travel.taipei/en/attraction/details/536) is free, around 14 minutes' walk from Xinbeitou Exit 1, and listed open Tuesday–Sunday 09:00–17:00, closed Monday. **This is a landscape walk, not bathing: do not enter or touch the hot water.** Rain, slippery paths or closure are good reasons to stay in Zhongshan instead. Bring an umbrella and grippy shoes.

Keep lunch near the hotel flexible at NT$250–350 each, an estimate rather than a named menu price. Leave the hotel 4½–5 hours before departure: allow 75–90 minutes hotel-to-terminal, including the 35–38-minute express ride, and aim for check-in three hours before an international flight. [Taoyuan Metro's advice](https://www.tymetro.com.tw/tymetro-new/en/_pages/travel-guide/timetable.php) is at least 2–3 hours. For a 21:00 flight, leave around 16:00–16:30; an earlier flight needs an earlier plan. Flight number and terminal are unknown. If you land at Songshan (TSA), replace the Taoyuan route and budget.

## Cards and cash without counting rides twice

Buy a standard adult [EasyCard](https://www.easycard.com.tw/en/easycard?cls=1521769569&id=1521769904) each: NT$100 for the card, no balance. Load NT$400 initially as an estimate and top up when needed. The NT$350 local-ride allowance below is what you spend; loading money is not an additional expense. [Adult stored-value fares](https://english.metro.taipei/cp.aspx?n=BECC2E7AC426F659&s=98639C1CC8FAE752) match single-ticket fares, so do not assume a tourist discount. Tap in and out separately. Keep airport tickets separate: NT$160 each way, NT$320 return. Recheck journeys in the [official fare/time planner](https://english.metro.taipei/cp.aspx?n=46A82F0C116392CB).

## Malaysian passports: check each traveller, not just the itinerary

As checked on 5 November 2026, [BOCA](https://www.boca.gov.tw/cp-149-4486-7785a-2.html), updated 17 July 2026, lists ordinary Malaysian passport holders for visa-exempt stays up to 30 days. Conditions include six months' passport validity on entry and confirmed return/onward travel with a departure seat. For 22 October 2027, aim for validity beyond 22 April 2028. Admission remains an immigration decision; recheck before travel rather than assume these rules will last.

Each friend should use the free [official Taiwan Arrival Card portal](https://twac.immigration.gov.tw/). It is not a visa. BOCA states submission within seven days before arrival; older guidance may say three. Set a reminder for 20 October 2027, within both windows, then follow the live instructions. Have passport, flight and accommodation details ready and save confirmation. No paid intermediary is needed, and no forms have been submitted for you.
