**You can sleep on the bay and still enjoy Hanoi. For 10 April 2027–14 April 2027, keep three hotel nights near the Old Quarter and one cruise night, returning to Hanoi before flight day. Start with AUD 900 each, excluding international flights. A mid-range Lan Ha twin cabin is the better fit here; Ninh Binh is the first thing to drop if money or energy gets tight.**

This plan is for two friends from Sydney sharing a twin hotel room and cruise cabin. All times are Vietnam local time. Prices and rules were checked on 6 November 2026, not secured for April. No bookings or supplier contact have been made. A boat's star label, higher price or “luxury” wording does not certify safety or buy quiet water.

## Give each transfer the time it actually needs

| Date | Plan and timing | Keep this escape route |
|---|---|---|
| Saturday · 10 April 2027 | Hanoi arrival: allow 60–90 minutes after landing, then 45–75 minutes by car, possibly 90 in traffic. An 08:00 landing suggests bag-drop 10:00–11:00, not a 09:00 tour. Normal check-in roughly 14:00, subject to hotel terms. | Early room access is not included. Eat, walk the Old Quarter and Hoan Kiem Lake, then rest. Weekend street activity is optional, not guaranteed. |
| Sunday · 11 April 2027 | Breakfast before Old Quarter pickup around 08:00–08:30. Allow 3–4 hours door-to-boat; board around noon for lunch, afternoon activity and dinner aboard. | Road time alone omits collections, port check-in and tenders. Activities depend on weather and the booked route. |
| Monday · 12 April 2027 | Early breakfast and a short activity; cabin checkout may be 09:30–10:00, disembarkation 11:00–12:00. Target Hanoi hotel return 15:00–16:30. | Return can be late. Do not book a timed city attraction before 17:00. Keep dinner easy. |
| Tuesday · 13 April 2027 | Optional Hoa Lu–Trang An–Mua Cave shared tour: estimated pickup 07:00–08:00, 2–2.5 hours each way, Trang An boat 2–3 hours, Hanoi return 19:00–20:00. | Another 4–5 hours on the road may be too much. Stay in Hanoi for breakfast, the Temple of Literature and coffee instead. |
| Wednesday · 14 April 2027 | Breakfast, checkout around noon under hotel terms, luggage storage if available. Leave 4–4.5 hours before the international flight: 60–90 minutes on the road plus around three hours at the airport. | For a 19:00 departure, leave 14:30–15:00. Adjust to your actual flight. No bay or Ninh Binh transfer today. |

The transfer ranges are planning allowances, not guarantees. Ask to store large bags during the cruise and take a small overnight bag; free storage is not assumed. Carry your original passport for registration. Confirm twin beds in writing: “twin/double on request” is not a twin guarantee. A typical 2-day/1-night package includes lunch, dinner, breakfast and brunch/lunch, but check yours.

For [Ninh Binh](https://vietnam.travel/places-to-go/northern-vietnam/ninh-binh), allow AUD 85 each for a shared day tour including guide, lunch, boat/entrance tickets and return Hanoi transport. **That is an estimate, not a verified supplier quote or private-car price.** Drinks and tips are extra. Skip Mua Cave's steep steps when slippery and do not add another boat ride. Staying in Hanoi saves roughly AUD 85 before replacement food, transport and admissions.

## Buy the boat route and the final total, not the stars

Ha Long fits the classic cave and island-viewpoint wish list, but headline stops and anchorages can feel busy. Lan Ha is my slight preference for scenery and kayaking without the same classic circuit, not a promise of solitude. Boats share activity spots; a Tuan Chau departure and tender can still mean a long transfer. Pay for an identified ship, confirmed twin cabin, air-conditioning, a decent bathroom, food and clear inclusions—not a huge suite for about 24 hours aboard.

**Peony is the working choice only if its final twin-cabin total fits.** Both Peony and Orchid visit shared Tra Bau/Trung Trang areas. Their reseller pages advertise a USD 40 return shuttle beside old 2017/2018 policies, so that amount is not used as a current transport quote. Contradictory tax wording and a “free cancellation” headline are reasons to request written, dated terms—not reasons to assume the cheapest interpretation.

Ask for the actual ship, cabin, route, taxes, port/entry fees, kayaking and return shuttle on one quote. Peony's operator asks for passenger details at least 24 hours ahead and dietary requests 48 hours ahead; confirm your seller's process. Weather cancellation can leave charges for transport or meals already used: **a full refund is not automatic.** Port restrictions can change stops or cancel sailing. Use Sunday in Hanoi if cancelled, then consider Monday's cruise only if it works; that may replace Tuesday's Ninh Binh outing. Use life jackets on tenders and kayaks, check safety equipment and the emergency briefing, and never assume safe swimming or empty water.

## Airport, cash and useful small precautions

Use [Grab's airport pickup guidance](https://www.grab.com/vn/en/transport/airports/hanoi-airport/), activate data first, and match the plate and driver to your app. Otherwise use an authorised taxi rank or desk, agree an all-in total or clarify meter/tolls, and avoid terminal touts. Airport cars are estimated at VND 300,000–450,000 per car each way, about AUD 18–26 for both travellers; the budget allows AUD 28 each for both directions combined. Compare the app or desk if the fare is much higher.

[Noi Bai Airport's](https://www.noibaiairport.vn/) search-indexed reference fare for bus 86 was VND 50,000 each, about AUD 2.95, seen on 6 November 2026—not a counter check. Allow 60–90 minutes plus the last walk or ride, and ask the airport desk about current stops and hours. For tired friends with bags, the car is the simpler choice. In town, walk where practical and use GrabCar or an official metered taxi for longer hops. Skip self-drive scooters; cross roads predictably while checking every direction.

For [small purchases](https://vietnam.travel/things-to-do/currency-and-payments-vietnam), start with roughly VND 1–1.5 million cash each, AUD 59–88 as an estimate, then withdraw more if needed. This is a payment method, not extra budget. Keep small notes and distinguish VND 20,000 from 500,000. Use bank ATMs, inspect local and home-bank fees, and choose VND rather than conversion to AUD where offered. Split cash and spare cards. April can be warm, humid and showery, with haze; pack rain protection and grippy shoes, but check an actual forecast close to travel. Check insurance for the planned activities and keep offline maps, visa copies and confirmations.

## Australian passports: plan for an e-visa

As checked on 6 November 2026, ordinary Australian passport holders need a visa for Hanoi; do not borrow another nationality's exemption or the special Phu Quoc arrangements. Use the [official e-visa portal](https://evisa.gov.vn/). The [tourism authority](https://vietnam.travel/plan-your-trip/visa-requirements) lists up to 90 days, single or multiple entry: USD 25 single entry, about AUD 39 with AUD 40 budgeted; USD 50 multiple entry is unnecessary here.

Published processing is 3–5 working days, not guaranteed. Apply at least 3–4 weeks before departure as a planning buffer once passport and flights are settled. Match full names, passport number and dates exactly, choose Noi Bai International Airport and the correct exit details, and check the approval before flying. Have at least six months' validity from arrival—through at least 10 October 2027 for entry on 10 April 2027—and two blank pages. Keep printed and offline approved visa copies, accommodation and onward/return details. Approval is not guaranteed admission.

[Smartraveller](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/asia/vietnam) encourages a Digital Pre-Arrival Form within 72 hours of arrival. **This is not presented as an established mandatory Hanoi requirement.** Check the airport-specific rule and official link near departure; avoid paid lookalikes. Recheck visa and passport guidance before paying and again before flying. These are dated rules for ordinary Australian passports, not a promise for April or advice for unusual status cases.

## A budget that leaves the general buffer separate

Use fixed planning assumptions AUD 1 = VND 17,000 and USD 1 = AUD 1.55, not live rates. All amounts below are per person sharing hotel and cruise twins, not a package quote.

| Item | Basis | AUD each |
|---|---|---:|
| Hanoi hotel | 3 nights × AUD 90 per room ÷ 2; tax margin, no assumed breakfast | 135 |
| Peony cabin and meals | USD 300 ÷ 2 × 1.55, rounded up | 233 |
| Return Hanoi–bay shuttle | Separate estimate | 70 |
| Cruise tax/payment margin | Unresolved tax wording and possible fees, not a tax quote | 35 |
| Airport cars | Two directions × up to VND 450,000 per car ÷ 2, rounded up | 28 |
| Food, coffee and water | Off-boat meals; no duplicate package lunches | 95 |
| City rides | Shared rides and plenty of walking | 20 |
| Ninh Binh day tour | Shared tour with transport, lunch, guide and tickets | 85 |
| Single-entry e-visa | USD 25 × 1.55 rounded up | 40 |
| Hanoi admissions | Optional estimate | 20 |
| Mobile data | Plan not selected | 10 |
| Travel insurance | Allowance; personal cover can cost more | 40 |
| Tips and a few drinks | Modest allowance | 30 |
| Planned spending | Before general contingency | 841 |
| General contingency | Separate from cruise fee margin | 59 |
| Full budget | AUD 1,800 for both; flights excluded | 900 |

Peony's working cruise allowance is AUD 338 each: 233 + 70 + 35. The AUD 59 general buffer is separate. Orchid adds about AUD 116 at base; remove the AUD 85 Ninh Binh tour and planned spending becomes about AUD 872 each, leaving roughly AUD 28 within AUD 900 before replacement meals or larger fees. That is a thin margin, not a free upgrade.

Flights, shopping, separate rooms/cabins, paid early check-in and suite upgrades are excluded. Insurance is only an allowance; age, health and cover affect a real quote. **If prices rise, protect confirmed twin beds and the flight buffer, then drop Ninh Binh.** Choose a simpler identified boat rather than hide shuttle costs or remove safety checks. Spend contingency only after the hotel and cruise totals are clear.
