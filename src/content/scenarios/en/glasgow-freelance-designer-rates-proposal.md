**Worried that your first freelance price will scare the client away—or leave you doing the work for too little? For this Glasgow designer, I would start at £350 per 7-hour day and lead with the £5,005 identity package. You are new to freelancing, not new to design.**

This example is for a 28-year-old designer with four years in-house, starting a sole trade in January 2027 and quoting a whisky start-up. The rate supports a £36,000 annual personal take-home target under the assumptions below. It does not guarantee bookings, include a separate pension or emergency fund, or predict your first tax bill.

The tax model uses 2026/27 Scottish income tax and UK Class 4 National Insurance as recorded on November 6, 2026. **The 2027/28 Scottish bands were not confirmed in these materials. Check them and NI before April 2027; do not carry this table forward as approved rates.**

Use the editable day-rate calculator (original supporting file is not published; use the information in this guide) to change your costs and capacity. The brand-identity contract (original supporting file is not published; use the information in this guide) is a **starting template, not legal advice or a solicitor-approved agreement**. Complete every bracket and have a Scottish solicitor check it before use. It is for business-to-business work, not an unchanged consumer contract.

## Build your rate from what you need to keep

Here, take-home means money left after business costs, income tax and Class 4 NI. The £36,000 target is £3,000 a month for living costs and whatever pension or savings you choose to fund yourself.

If you meant £36,000 profit before personal tax instead, £42,000 turnover would cover the £6,000 costs. But you would keep only £29,883.13 after tax and NI. That interpretation gives a £270.97/day floor, not enough for the personal cash target above.

[HMRC's expenses guidance](https://www.gov.uk/expenses-if-youre-self-employed) explains the distinction; drawings for yourself are not deductible salary.

Assume Scottish taxpayer status, the standard Personal Allowance and sole-trade income only. There is no pension relief, student loan, employment income or other tax adjustment in this annual model.

The [Scottish income-tax table](https://www.gov.uk/scottish-income-tax) assumes a full £12,570 allowance. The [allowance taper](https://www.gov.uk/income-tax-rates/income-over-100000) removes £1 for each £2 of adjusted net income above £100,000. The workbook includes that taper. Under the recorded [NI rules](https://www.gov.uk/self-employed-national-insurance-rates), Class 2 is treated as paid at profits of £7,105 or more in 2026/27; this model adds no compulsory cash Class 2 payment.

| Annual calculation | Amount |
|---|---:|

| Scottish income tax | −£6,743.01 |

In this band, net = 0.52 × profit + £12,772.15. Required profit = (£36,000 − £12,772.15) ÷ 0.52 = £44,668.9423. Rounding each displayed component separately can leave an apparent penny difference; the calculation keeps its precision before rounding.

At **£350/day for 155 days**, turnover is £54,250 and profit is £48,250. Income tax of £8,247.05 and NI of £2,140.80 leave **£37,862.15 take-home**. The £1,862.15 margin above target is modest, not spare money you can count on before the work is booked.

If you sell only 130 days, the floor rises to £389.76/day. At 180 days it falls to £281.49/day. At the proposed rate you need about 145 whole billable days to reach the target. Check utilisation each quarter rather than discounting now on the hope of future work.

**Your first tax year needs a separate calculation.** January–April 5, 2027 belongs to 2026/27. Employment salary from April–December 2026 shares that year's allowance and bands. January does not reset them. Take your P45/P60, actual freelance receipts and allowable costs to an accountant; do not divide this annual tax bill by four.

## Make payment and changes easy to understand

Ask for **40% to book, 30% on selected-identity approval and 30% on final approval before final-file release**. The deposit must clear before work starts. Later invoices are due within 14 calendar days.

For Better, that is **£2,002 + £1,501.50 + £1,501.50 = £5,005**. The deposit counts towards the fee. It is not extra or automatically forfeited. Payment does not depend on the client's next investment round.

No VAT is charged while unregistered. If registered, applicable VAT is additional and must be clear in the signed quote. “Not VAT registered” is not the same as “0% VAT”.

One revision round means one consolidated list from one decision-maker and one response. New concepts, a changed brief, extra sizes or extra rounds need written approval of a change order. Price extras at £350/day or £50/hour, or agree another fixed fee before starting. Correcting your failure to meet the agreed scope is not a paid revision.

A friendly reply is enough: “Happy to add that. It is outside the agreed template and one-size brief. I'll send the extra fee and revised date for written approval before I start.”

## Put the boundaries in the contract

Start with the editable agreement (original supporting file is not published; use the information in this guide). **It is a starting template, not legal advice or a solicitor-approved contract.** Have a Scottish solicitor review cancellation, liability and IP before relying on it. Downloading it does not form an agreement.

- **Identify the parties.** Fill legal names, addresses, company number where relevant, authorised contacts, notices and signatures. Attach the chosen package and complete Schedule A.
- **Separate feedback from acceptance.** The template allows 3 working days for creative feedback and 5 working days to approve a milestone or identify specific scope defects. Read-only or watermarked proofs allow checking before finals are released. Silence is not acceptance. Written reminders, pause and breach procedures apply if the project stalls; a client-caused pause of 30 calendar days is not automatic deposit forfeiture.
- **Be clear about usage.** Under the template, the signed written assignment of copyright you own in final approved bespoke work takes effect only after the entire project design fee and approved expenses are paid. Milestones do not transfer separate rights unless a signed variation says so. Until then, permission is for internal evaluation only; an early public launch needs a separate written licence.
- **Keep other rights separate.** Rejected concepts, drafts and pre-existing tools stay yours. Embedded pre-existing material gets the stated licence with the final work after full payment. Third-party assets, fonts and platform tools keep their own licences. There is no blanket moral-rights waiver. [Copyright ownership guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/ownership-of-copyright-works) and [the signed-writing requirement](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/48/section/90) explain why paying an invoice alone is not an assignment. Payment-conditioned transfer is negotiated drafting, not automatic protection under law.
- **Make cancellation fair and specific.** The draft charges recorded completed work plus approved non-cancellable costs. A reserved-capacity fee is up to 20% of unperformed design fee, limited to evidenced reasonable loss after mitigation, avoided costs and replacement bookings. Total design charges cannot exceed the fixed fee. Credit the deposit, refund excess and avoid double charging. No kill fee applies for your unremedied breach. This is a negotiable term, not a statutory entitlement or an assured enforceable percentage. Cancellation preserves rights already vested; paying cancellation charges alone does not buy usage rights to unfinished work.
- **Agree portfolio use.** Get express permission for the final public work after launch. Honour embargoes, NDAs and confidentiality. Non-public metrics and behind-the-scenes material need prior approval.
- **Check exposure before signing.** The draft cap is the design fee paid or payable, only insofar as lawful and reasonable. It does not exclude fraud, negligent death or personal injury, or other non-excludable liability. Scottish [breach-of-duty](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1977/50/section/16) and [standard-term](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1977/50/section/17) rules mean a cap is not guaranteed protection. Ask the solicitor and insurer how IP and confidentiality claims fit. The template uses Scots law and Scottish courts.

### If a business client pays late

For qualifying B2B debts, the recorded [statutory-interest rule](https://www.gov.uk/late-commercial-payments-interest-debt-recovery/charging-interest-commercial-debt) is **8 percentage points above the applicable Bank of England reference rate**. Do not invent a current rate for a future overdue invoice.

Under the [Scottish reference-rate rule](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2002/336/article/4/made), use the preceding December 31 rate where interest starts in January–June, or June 30 where it starts in July–December. Simple interest is overdue principal × annual statutory rate × days overdue ÷ 365. The template preserves statutory rights rather than substituting a lower contractual rate.

[Fixed recovery compensation](https://www.gov.uk/late-commercial-payments-interest-debt-recovery/claim-debt-recovery-costs) is £40 below £1,000 of debt, £70 from £1,000 to £9,999.99, and £100 from £10,000. It is per qualifying late debt, not per day. Additional reasonable recovery costs may be available without double counting. The underlying framework is the [Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/20/contents).

Before signing in January 2027, check commencement and transition of any [Commercial Payments Bill changes](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/commercial-payments-bill-factsheets/commercial-payments-bill-overview). These materials do not establish future commencement; proposals are not treated as operative rules here.

## Set up the quiet admin before you start

Keep a separate business-money account or pot, numbered invoices, receipts and a tax reserve from day one. As a [sole trader](https://www.gov.uk/become-sole-trader), you remain personally responsible for business debts. [Keep records](https://www.gov.uk/self-employed-records/how-long-to-keep-your-records) for at least five years after the relevant January 31 submission deadline. Keep employment records for the transition too.

For a January 2027 start, the first period ends April 5, 2027. If gross trading income exceeds £1,000, or another filing reason applies, the ordinary [Self Assessment deadlines](https://www.gov.uk/self-assessment-tax-returns/deadlines) give:

| Action | Date under the ordinary annual rules |
|---|---|
| Notify or register with HMRC | October 5, 2027 |
| Normal paper filing | October 31, 2027 |
| Online filing and payment | January 31, 2028 |

Register early. These dates apply the annual rules to this example; they are not a personalised HMRC filing notice.

**Budget for payments on account as well as the balance.** Under the recorded [rules](https://www.gov.uk/understand-self-assessment-bill/payments-on-account), they normally apply unless the relevant previous bill is below £1,000 or more than 80% was paid outside Self Assessment. Each advance is usually half the previous relevant bill. If applicable, January 31, 2028 brings the 2026/27 balance plus the first 2027/28 advance; the second advance is due July 31, 2028. Your PAYE history may change whether this applies.

**Watch turnover for VAT.** The model's £50,668.94 minimum turnover and £54,250 at the chosen rate are below the recorded £90,000 compulsory threshold. But the [registration tests](https://www.gov.uk/register-for-vat) use taxable turnover over rolling 12 months, not take-home, and expected turnover over £90,000 in the next 30 days alone. Voluntary registration is possible. Recheck the rules when trading begins.

**Do not assume immediate Making Tax Digital entry from your run-rate.** The recorded [Income Tax phases](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/find-out-if-and-when-you-need-to-use-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax) are:

| Qualifying income | Relevant tax year | Entry from |
|---|---|---|
| Over £50,000 | 2024/25 | April 2026 |
| Over £30,000 | 2025/26 | April 2027 |
| Over £20,000 | 2026/27 | April 2028 |

A genuinely new January 2027 trade is not automatically in April 2027 merely because annualised turnover is £54k. [Qualifying income](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/work-out-your-qualifying-income-for-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax) combines gross self-employment and property income, not PAYE earnings. Short sole-trade periods can be annualised. Review the first return, prior income and exemptions for possible April 2028 entry.

At the proposed rate, annual model tax plus NI is £10,387.85, or 21.53% of £48,250 profit. Moving around 30% of profit into a tax pot is a starting cash habit, not a guaranteed sufficient reserve. ## A few questions before you send the quote

### Should I cut the rate because this is my first freelance job?

Not simply for that reason. You already have design experience, and the personal-cost floor leaves little room for a large discount. Reduce or stage the scope if the budget is lower.

### Does paying the deposit let the client launch the logo?

Not under this template. The deposit books the work; pre-payment permission is internal evaluation only. Early public use needs a separate written licence, and the signed assignment has its stated full-payment condition.

### Can I charge for every revision that takes longer than planned?

No. The fixed fee includes agreed outputs and rounds, even if you underestimated them. Extra concepts, sizes, rounds or a changed brief need an approved fee-and-date change order. Genuine scope defects are corrected without charging a revision.

### Can the annual model tell me my first January tax bill?

No. The short first trading period shares a tax year with prior employment. Use actual receipts, costs and employment records with an accountant, including any payments on account.
