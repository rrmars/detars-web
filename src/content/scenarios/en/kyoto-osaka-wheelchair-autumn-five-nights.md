You should not have to choose between autumn colour and a bathroom you can actually use. **Plan 3 nights in Kyoto and 2 in Osaka, with no more than 1 main outing per half-day.** But do not make non-refundable commitments yet: no exact room has a guaranteed roll-in shower, verified transfer fit and a November 2027 quote. A step-free bathroom doorway is not a roll-in shower.

This plan follows **Helen, 58 when researched, from Melbourne**, travelling with her husband from **18 November 2027 to 23 November 2027**: **6 days and 5 nights**, with **AUD 7,500 for both excluding Melbourne–KIX international flights**. She uses a manual wheelchair full-time and can briefly stand for transfers. That does not mean she can climb stairs, step over a bath rim or transfer into a low sedan. Her citizenship/passport is not established; no travel eligibility is inferred from living in Melbourne.

At an assumed **AUD 1 = ¥95**, the plan is **¥667,000 / AUD 7,021.05 including contingency**, leaving **¥45,500 / AUD 478.95** beyond that contingency. These are spending allowances—not proof that suitable rooms and medically accepted insurance can be bought within them. Evidence was read **7 November 2026**; no price, policy or availability has been refreshed. The underlying source file is not published.

Confirm the step-free street-to-room and restaurant routes. Compare those answers with Helen's actual chair dimensions, preferred transfer height and toilet-transfer side; those personal measurements are still unknown.

Request Kyoto **18 November 2027 to 21 November 2027** and Osaka **21 November 2027 to 23 November 2027**, for both guests. Ask for the full price including service, consumption and accommodation taxes, cancellation terms and guaranteed room allocation—not a request noted against an ordinary booking. Keep refundable alternatives until access and price are settled. If a true roll-in configuration cannot be confirmed, change the hotel, not the bathing requirement. No hotel has been contacted.

Living in Australia does not establish Helen's eligibility. The [JR accessibility policy](https://www.westjr.co.jp/travel-information/en/train-usage-guide/accessibility/) gives a wheelchair envelope of **120 cm maximum length/height and 70 cm width**. Explain manual/self-propelled use, whether Helen remains seated, party size, train, segment and contact/collection arrangements; obtain operator guidance if outside that envelope.

The inspected [Japanese wheelchair-reservation policy](https://www.jr-odekake.net/railroad/service/barrierfree/wheelchair/index.html) lists HARUKA wheelchair accommodation in **car 3**. Use a staffed station or its wheelchair reservation telephone channel; **an ordinary online tourist ticket does not secure wheelchair accommodation**, and the Shinkansen web process must not be assumed to cover HARUKA. Station/telephone applications open at **10:00 JST, 1 month before travel through travel day**, subject to inventory and possible callback. If unchanged, the airport journeys open **18 October 2027** and **23 October 2027**, at **10:00 JST**. Reconcile the discount ticket and wheelchair allocation with staff before paying.

[Boarding/alighting assistance](https://www.jr-odekake.net/railroad/service/barrierfree/boarding-alighting/) is separate unless explicitly arranged together. For the local Kyoto–Osaka service, arrange ramp/boarding help, not an unnecessary limited-express reservation. JR West lists **0570-00-8989, 08:00–20:00 JST**; overseas reachability and English service are unconfirmed, and general email does not arrange reservations or assistance. If it cannot be reached from Australia, resolve a usable official channel; a staffed station on arrival or later hotel help with permission is a fallback, not a same-day space guarantee.

Allow **30–45 extra minutes** at unfamiliar stations as a planning buffer, not a JR minimum or guarantee. Add more after immigration, luggage and airport assistance; do not lock in an optimistic flight-to-train connection.

## Anchor the food day with a seated meal

Eat early, around **11:00 or 17:00** if operating. Prefer movable-chair tables over fixed stools, high counters or tatami, and locate an accessible toilet before settling in. Short Dotonbori browsing or takeaway is a bonus, not a substitute for a comfortable meal. Dietary/allergy needs remain unknown. No restaurant has been contacted or reserved.
