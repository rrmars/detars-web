You may be able to spend less in Tbilisi without giving up your remote contract. But make this a **conditional move, not an automatic tax-saving strategy**. Resolve your contract, work-right or exemption route, Polish and Ukrainian obligations, full-stay insurance and cat documents before signing a year-long lease.

This example follows Oksana, 31, a Ukrainian backend contractor living in Warsaw, invoicing **EUR 4,500/month**, moving for a year in 2027 with one cat. Treat that income as gross contractor receipts, not take-home pay. The model puts recurring Tbilisi costs at **GEL 4,266.92 / EUR 1,471.45**, including conditional Georgian 1% income tax. The **EUR 502.03/month** living-cost reduction is against an assumed Warsaw budget—not her actual savings.

Start the housing search in Saburtalo, but compare the pet-accepting Vake example too. These few advertisements do not establish that one district is always cheaper. Keep the move reversible until the important gates are clear.

## Keep entry, work permission and residence separate

For a new Ukrainian arrival in 2027, the current baseline is **one full year visa-free**, not three years. The [April 2025 amendment](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/6463219) created the one-year rule alongside an older-arrival extension. The [February 2026 amendment](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/6793257) extends only Ukrainians who entered and were present before **24 February 2025** until **24 February 2027**. It is not a new-arrival offer. Check your actual lawful-stay end date and do not rely on border runs guaranteeing readmission.

| Route | Current conditions | Fees and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visa-free Ukrainian entry | One full year for a new arrival | Not a residence permit, tax-residence determination or automatic work authorization. |
| IT temporary residence | At least two years of IT experience; at least USD 25,000-equivalent annual IT remuneration; designated activity and evidence. Qualifying small-business individual entrepreneurs can apply. | First grant three years. GEL 500 / 750 / 1,000 for 30 / 20 / 10 calendar days. Prior-year remuneration evidence includes at least two receipts at least 30 days apart. Work-right or exemption evidence depends on the case. |
| Ordinary work residence | Work/enterprise evidence; income at least five times average-consumer subsistence minimum; ordinarily GEL 50,000 annual enterprise turnover per foreign applicant | Initial six months to one year; GEL 300 / 450 / 600 for 30 / 20 / 10 calendar days. Later rules allow excluded-activity evidence and a narrow new-enterprise turnover-certificate exception, not automatic approval for every new IE. |
| Property-based short-term residence | Assessed non-agricultural real estate exceeding USD 150,000 equivalent | Renting does not qualify. Buying solely for this cost-saving year is not recommended; fee unverified. |
| Study or family residence | Genuine qualifying study or family grounds | No such basis is established for this single-contractor example; not an artificial workaround. |

Read the [Aliens Law](https://matsne.gov.ge/en/document/view/2278806), [SDA checklist and fees](https://sda.gov.ge/en/products/migration-residence-permits/) and [April residence documents](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/6831591). The [July residence amendment](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/6939703) permits a first-issuance turnover-certificate exception for an enterprise registered no more than three months earlier with no more than three foreign employees; that case receives a six-month permit. Do not translate this exception into personal eligibility. Foreign documents may need legalization or apostille; residence-card, translation and apostille fees are not verified here.

### Why neither blanket work-permit answer is safe

The [Labour Migration Law](https://matsne.gov.ge/en/document/view/2806732) includes self-employed independent contractors, but excludes qualifying services to a non-resident relating to that non-resident's activities outside Georgia. A Polish payer alone does not prove the full test. The [April statutory change](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/6839931), [July work-rule qualification](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/6940189) and [later residence amendment](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/6986724), effective **1 September 2026**, must be considered together. The latter expressly provides exemption-evidence paths for qualifying IT workers and small-business IEs.

**Neither “every remote freelancer needs a work permit” nor “foreign clients always exempt you” is a safe answer.** Ask a Georgian adviser to classify the contract and intended residence route together. Establish the client, relevant overseas activities, actual work and any Georgian engagements.

If you are in scope, self-employed applicants apply personally; the July process includes a video interview in Georgian or English, with an interpreter allowed. Work-right fees are separate from residence: **GEL 200 / 30 calendar days**, **GEL 400 / 10 working days**, renewal **GEL 200 / up to 30 calendar days**. The [base fee rule](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/6791218), [February amendment](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/6796896), [March amendment](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/6813582) and July amendment support that dated schedule; recheck before applying.

After a work-right grant, applicants in Georgia generally have **10 calendar days** to apply for work/IT residence; those abroad generally have **30 calendar days** for D1, subject to exceptions. The **1 January 2027** transition concerns workers actively registered on **1 March 2026**, not a newcomer grace period. The general residence-application rule is at least **40 days** before lawful stay expires, with an express IT exception. Prepare early anyway. SDA's ordinary-work introduction uses older registration wording; later legislation governs.

## IE registration does not automatically give you 1% tax

Genuine backend development performed in Tbilisi for a foreign business is a plausible candidate for individual-entrepreneur registration plus separately granted Small Business Status (SBS). **Your actual activity and independence matter.** Salary is outside SBS; consulting is on the prohibited list, ordinary software development is not explicitly listed. Relabeling consultancy invoices does not make them development.

Use the [current Georgian Tax Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717?publication=248), the [English translation](https://matsne.gov.ge/en/document/view/1043717) and [Revenue Service guidance](https://voice.rs.ge/Default.aspx?id=4236). The English text inspected pointed to publication 247, dated **15 October 2026**; the [latest amendment](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/7030851), enacted **28 October 2026** and published **30 October 2026**, concerns gambling-tournament provisions rather than the SBS/source/VAT provisions used here. Georgian passages are research translations, not certified legal translations. Reconfirm the operative rules before your first invoice.

1. **Register the IE.** [NAPR's fee schedule](https://napr.gov.ge/en/page/fees-and-terms) lists GEL 26 in one working day or GEL 75 on application day. An English extract adds GEL 26; signature certification is GEL 7 if needed. These are separate services, not universally mandatory extras. Prepare passport, lawful registration-address evidence and necessary translations; the exact foreign-applicant checklist remains unverified.
2. **Apply separately for SBS.** RS says status starts on the application date. A service-centre application costs GEL 100; application through the authorized-user page is free. Confirm the certificate, accepted activity and effective date. Do not backdate pre-status receipts into 1% treatment.
3. **Keep the special accounting journal and evidence.** Retain contracts, invoices and statements; record where work is done, client business status and service type. Agree the accounting and transaction-date GEL conversion method with your accountant. The fixed FX below is not a tax-filing method.
4. **File and pay by the 15th of the following month.** Missing a return does not count as filing a zero return. Additional ordinary-tax activity may require an annual return by **1 April**; VAT and reverse-charge duties can be separate.

| SBS condition | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Qualifying Georgian-source turnover income | Normal rate 1%; not 1% of profit after expenses. Salary and excluded categories remain outside SBS. |
| Economic-activity gross income exceeds GEL 500,000 | 3% from the beginning of the crossing month through calendar-year end, not just on the excess. |
| Threshold exceeded in each of two calendar years | Cancellation from the following year under Article 89. RS has an additional qualification where SBS existed for only one of those years; monitor relevant income with your accountant. |
| Prohibited activity | Can trigger cancellation back to the start of the relevant year. Obtain classification before contracting. |

### Foreign payment does not mean foreign-source income—or Georgian VAT

**Counterintuitive finding:** coding physically in Georgia can be Georgian-source income even if the client and receiving bank are abroad (Article 104). Yet ordinary B2B services to a genuinely foreign business normally have the customer's foreign VAT place of supply (Article 162¹), absent a receiving Georgian fixed establishment or relevant exception. The same receipts can therefore qualify for 1% income tax while falling outside Georgian output-VAT scope. This is not a blanket “zero-rated export” rule.

Mandatory VAT registration generally concerns **more than GEL 100,000 of VAT-taxable supplies in any 12 consecutive calendar months**. Apply within **two business days** of crossing; liability starts with the crossing transaction. Do not count every foreign-client receipt automatically as Georgian VAT-taxable turnover. Track domestic, B2C and differently classified services separately. Foreign SaaS/cloud purchases can create reverse-charge VAT even without VAT registration; ask about purchases as well as sales.

At the illustrative FX, EUR 4,500 × 2.8998 = **GEL 13,049.10/month**; annual receipts are **EUR 54,000 / GEL 156,589.20**, below GEL 500,000. If every receipt qualifies, 1% is **GEL 130.491**, displayed as **GEL 130.49 / EUR 45.00/month**; annual tax is **GEL 1,565.892**, displayed as **GEL 1,565.89 / EUR 540.00**. Actual liability uses real timing, classification and applicable conversion rules.

## Ask a cross-border adviser before calling the difference a saving

Your actual Warsaw spending, Polish tax/ZUS and whether EUR 4,500 is gross or net are unknown. Replace the proxy budget with statements before deciding. **Unknown continuing liabilities are excluded, not assumed zero.** Visa-free entry, tax residence, residence permission and IE registration answer different questions.

Which treaty, tie-breaker, split-year treatment, certificates, returns, address/business/VAT changes and ZUS/health contributions apply? Does leaving affect temporary protection or Polish residence rights you rely on?
- **Your client:** is permanent Georgian performance permitted? Must business/invoicing details change? Is the relationship genuinely independent development rather than employment, consulting or staff supply? Could withholding, payroll, fixed-establishment or permanent-establishment duties arise? Obtain the company's position before moving.

- **Ukraine:** does domestic law or a treaty leave you resident? If a Ukrainian FOP remains, what filings, contributions, closure/suspension steps or foreign-income reporting continue? Citizenship alone does not decide residence, and Georgian 1% does not extinguish Ukrainian obligations.

Bring a daily travel log, registrations, leases, earlier returns, contribution records, contract, invoices and bank statements. No treaty rate, exemption or personal liability conclusion is established here.

It can be from a Georgian or authorized foreign insurer, in English or Georgian, paper or electronic. Exemptions exist, but Ukrainian nationality alone does not establish one. ## Bring the cat—and protect the return to Poland

One accompanying cat is non-commercial movement, not automatically commercial import. Start with a Polish official veterinarian before buying travel. The [Georgian MFA baseline](https://newyork.mfa.gov.ge/en/veterinary-control), [Georgian veterinary law](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/4367880) and [Polish return guidance](https://www.wetgiw.gov.pl/nadzor-weterynaryjny/podroz-ze-zwierzetami-towarzyszacymi-z-panstw-trzecich-do-polski/printpage) answer different parts of the journey.

1. **Identification and rabies:** ISO 11784/11785-compatible microchip, matching passport/certificate and valid rabies vaccination after identification. Primary-course immunity interval is at least **21 days**. Keep originals and uninterrupted boosters.
2. **Documents:** confirm the appropriate official health certificate or accepted EU-passport route, owner-signed non-commercial declaration and documented internal/external parasite treatment. A Ukrainian booklet is not automatically an EU passport. **Exact certificate issue/validity windows and treatment timing remain unverified for this itinerary.** Ask the official vet and NFA before departure.
3. **Titre, if required:** Georgian law has country/route exemptions. Where applicable, sample at least **30 days** after vaccination, obtain at least **0.5 IU/ml** from an accredited laboratory, and allow the conditional **three-month** pre-movement/transit interval under Article 24. Prior satisfactory tests and continuous boosters matter. The Polish-origin exemption and transit conditions were not conclusively established; neither “no titre needed” nor “every cat needs one” is justified.
4. **EU return:** Polish official guidance identifies Georgia as an origin requiring antibody evidence. Complete satisfactory serology in Poland and record it in an EU passport **before departure**, maintaining boosters. This is return protection, not proof of a universal Georgian import requirement. A subsequent booster outside the EU can require an additional official third-country certificate; use current forms.
5. **Itinerary and home:** obtain airline pet-space approval, carrier dimensions and total cabin/hold weight rules, transit-country clearance and written landlord cat consent. Flights, veterinary services and carriers have not been priced or booked.

The NFA route-specific country list, document windows, parasite timing and transit/titre exemptions remain decision gates. The transport allowance below does not individually price them. No authority or airline has been contacted.

## Make the decision in a reversible order

1. **Before commitments:** obtain contract-specific Georgian classification and Poland–Georgia–Ukraine advice; verify the actual 2027 entry/work/residence rules and client consent. Replace Warsaw assumptions with spending and contribution records.
2. **Before travel:** obtain compliant full-stay health/accident cover and an official-vet cat plan, including EU-return serology where appropriate. Choose reversible accommodation rather than relying on these advertisements.
3. **After arrival, if proceeding:** inspect homes, retain working banking, and complete IE, SBS, work-right/exemption and residence steps in the legally correct order. None has been executed for this example.
4. **Each month:** retain evidence, track SBS/VAT thresholds, file/pay on time, record travel days and compare actual spending. Reprice rent and insurance before renewing.

Individual eligibility, historic IT remuneration, cross-border residence/tax/ZUS, bank requirements and approval, personalized full-year insurance, cat import documents/timing and future rules remain open. Treat those as gates to clear—not evidence that no obligation exists.
