You can spend AUD120,000 opening a lovely café and still earn very little from it. **Change the plan: validate paid demand before committing to 40 seats in Ho Chi Minh City.** At the proposed ticket and costs, you need **130/day** to cover operating costs including founder pay, and **147/day** to cover depreciation and a conservative tax reserve too. Neither threshold repays your investment.

This plan follows Minh, 34, a Vietnamese-Australian returning from Melbourne for a mid-2027 opening. Start with coffee, tea and bought-in pastries—not alcohol, roasting or live entertainment. Your first job is to test a VND60,000 realised ticket and repeat local trade, not to spend the entire budget on décor.

Prices and rules below are the November 7, 2026 snapshot, not refreshed quotes or legal clearance. **This is a conditional planning guide, not compliance clearance or a validated demand forecast.**

## Prove the ticket and repeat trade before taking a lease

For the full café, favour a domestic single-member limited liability company (LLC) if Minh’s current Vietnamese nationality and election of domestic-investor treatment are documented; screen both legacy District 3 and Bình Thạnh on site economics, not district reputation. Keep the concept simple: coffee, tea and bought-in pastries, no alcohol, roasting or live entertainment at launch.

Do not sign a long lease on the strength of these estimates. First validate a VND60,000 realized ticket and a credible path to **at least 150 paid transactions/day**, preferably with headroom towards 180. If demand cannot support that, reduce area, seats and staffing rather than spend the full budget on décor. These are decision hurdles, not forecasts of customer demand.

Use **VND32m/month rent** as a screening assumption, **VND35m** as a provisional ceiling, and **VND1,080m** as the opening cash allocation. Reject a site whose fire/access/food-safety works break that cap. Preserve the remaining **VND960m** for the café, separate from relocation and private living costs.

Operating break-even: **130/day** — 129.37 rounded up. After owner economic pay, before tax, depreciation and capital replacement.

Base monthly surplus: **VND22.96m** — At 150 transactions/day; only VND3.37m after depreciation and conservative tax reserve.

Zero-sales reserve: **6.67 months** — 960 ÷ 143.95 fixed burn; not a promise of runway after further capex or private withdrawals.

## Settle your citizenship route before choosing the business structure

**Vietnamese citizenship materially changes the route.** The Investment Law 143/2025 baseline defines a domestic investor to include a Vietnamese national and a foreign investor to include a foreign national. A dual national overlaps those definitions: Australian citizenship alone is not a sound reason to force Minh into a foreign-invested structure. Conversely, Vietnamese ancestry without current citizenship documents would not establish domestic status. [Investment Law baseline](https://en.baochinhphu.vn/print/viet-nams-law-on-investment-2025-111260731145214387.htm)

**Best planning route:** document his continuing Vietnamese citizenship and seek domestic-investor treatment consistently at incorporation and capital remittance. Domestic projects are exempt from investment registration under the Article 26 baseline. The current dual-national election provision in Decree 96/2026 and the September 2026 consolidated Investment Law were **not successfully verified from current operative law**; therefore this is a conditional recommendation, not a conclusion that an IRC is definitely unnecessary for this individual. Do not use a relative as a nominee to avoid settling that issue. ### If the foreign-investor route is required or chosen

The 2025 Investment Law baseline allows an entity to be established before IRC procedures; **An enterprise registration certificate (ERC) first does not abolish the investment registration certificate (IRC)**. Foreign-project requirements, market access for the exact activities, capital-account/remittance process and registration sequencing must then be checked. Do not plan on “15 days and done” for foreign-investment formalities. The domestic cost model below does not include a priced foreign-investment legal package. [Investment Law baseline](https://en.baochinhphu.vn/print/viet-nams-law-on-investment-2025-111260731145214387.htm)

For either route, register the precise beverage/food activities under the **current** Vietnam industry classification, not an unverified old code copied from a blog. Have the adviser confirm the applicable current VSIC code for the registration dossier. Do not confuse VSIC (industry classification) with Vietnam Social Security contribution rules. Charter-capital amount, contribution deadlines, legal representative/residency and documents need current professional review; no universal minimum capital is assumed in this guide.

**Before incorporation, settle:** domestic election for a dual citizen; current Enterprise Law/Decree 296 amendments; current VSIC; company invoicing rules; owner-pay CIT deduction; insurance/union base. Ask a qualified local adviser to settle these points in writing. The advice allowance does not make them resolved.

### Food and fire: the two dangerous shortcuts

**Food:** plan for a certificate unless a current, applicable exemption can be documented. Even a certificate exemption would not establish compliant hygiene. Prepare a dossier checklist for the current authority: registration/address evidence, premises/layout and equipment, staff health/training evidence, potable water/ice and supplier traceability, cleaning/pest/waste controls and food-storage arrangements. This is a readiness checklist, not a verified exhaustive statutory document list. [Food-law transition](https://baochinhphu.vn/tiep-tuc-ap-dung-nghi-dinh-15-2018-nd-cp-ve-an-toan-thuc-pham-cho-den-khi-co-quy-dinh-moi-102260408123934123.htm) · [Food decree: exemption unresolved](https://datafiles.chinhphu.vn/cpp/files/vbpq/2018/02/15.signed.pdf)

**Fire insurance is not design approval.** The Ministry of Public Security’s Appendix VII explanation lists food-service total floor area **≥300m²**; certain mixed-use buildings **≥3 floors OR ≥500m²**; and residential-business premises business area **≥200m²** as compulsory-insurance triggers. These do not establish the separate managed-premises or design-appraisal thresholds, nor certify how the September 2026 amendment affects the actual building. A 100m² tenant can occupy a building that triggers obligations through another category. [Fire-insurance categories](https://bocongan.gov.vn/bai-viet/44-co-so-phai-mua-bao-hiem-chay-no-bat-buoc-tu-ngay-0172025-d1-t1734) · [Fire amendment](https://baochinhphu.vn/chi-dao-dieu-hanh-cua-chinh-phu-thu-tuong-chinh-phu-ngay-8-9-2026-102260908172131419.htm)

### An illustrative critical path to opening

- **Weeks 1–2:** settle nationality/vehicle and current rules; inspect ownership/use/building records and measured layout; seek only a conditional site commitment. Keep any site commitment conditional until those checks are complete.
- **Weeks 3–4:** eligible registration, address/tax/invoice setup, food/fire classification and final design/quotation. Resolve whether pre-work design approval is required before beginning works.
- **Weeks 5–8:** controlled fit-out and equipment installation; recruit/train and prepare hygiene/fire records; signage design and music coverage in parallel.
- **Weeks 9–12:** inspection/corrections, commissioning, invoice/POS tests and soft opening only after required clearances.

**Allow 8–12 elapsed weeks as a planning window, not a promise of HCMC processing performance.** Overseas documentation, foreign-investment requirements, failed inspections or major building works can extend it. It starts with structuring/site diligence, not three paid months of lease possession. Only one paid pre-opening rent month is in the base budget; add VND32m for each extra paid month. ## How this café can fail—and the controls that matter

These are the main failure mechanisms suggested by this plan’s economics and source gaps, **not a statistical ranking or invented failure rate**.

- **Buying ambience before proving repeat demand.** A busy opening is not a stable VND60,000 ticket. Test repeat local trade and dayparts; if paid demand cannot support the conservative break-even, shrink or wait.
- **A cheap but unusable lease.** Multi-floor area, no legal parking, weak visibility or costly whole-building fire issues can erase the apparent rent saving. Make technical/legal suitability a precondition, not an afterthought.
- **Capex consuming survival cash.** Enforce the opening cap, stage discretionary décor and retain contingency. Reconcile contractor scopes to avoid paying twice for M&E, furniture or AC.
- **Confusing gross margin with profit.** VAT-inclusive menu price is not revenue; founder labour is not free. Recipe waste, bought-in pastries and delivery commissions can destroy the 68% contribution assumption.
- **Understaffing or owner absence.** Six staff are not six people always present. Illness, leave, peak coverage, retention, overtime and training need a workable roster. This model requires Minh on site.
- **Weak cash and stock controls.** Use compliant POS/e-invoices, reconcile daily payments to recipes and inventory, log milk/coffee waste, and separate café and personal accounts. Unrecorded discounts or purchases are not harmless at this profit level.
- **Compliance assumed from an old checklist.** Investment, household tax, food and fire rules changed. No “under 40 seats” blanket exemption is established. An attractive premises is not a licence.
- **Menu sprawl and inconsistent quality.** Launch with the narrow menu above; add cooked food, alcohol, roasting or live performances only after the new compliance/cost case is researched. Standardise water, dial-in, recipes and cleaning.

### Decision gates for the move

**Before lease:** current nationality/structure advice, present-day site comparables, verified building use and fire/food pathway, measured seating/parking, itemised contractor and equipment quotes. **Before opening:** current fees/tax/payroll recheck, successful commissioning, trained staff, working invoice/POS process and reserve intact. **During launch:** compare realised ticket, transactions, waste, labour and channel cost weekly against this model; do not wait for the cash balance to diagnose demand.

**This is a conditional planning guide, not clearance to incorporate, lease or trade.** Resolve the legal, site and quotation gaps before committing your savings.
