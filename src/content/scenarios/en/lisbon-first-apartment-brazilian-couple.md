You want the keys to a T2, not a mortgage that leaves you short every month. **Start your search in Almada and Amadora at €270,000–€300,000, preferably below €290,000—subject to accepted net income and a positive stressed budget.** This is a conditional search range, not an approved loan or a promise of homes available at that price.

The example is a Brazilian couple in their early 30s, living in Lisbon since 2024 on residence permits, with €70,000 savings, €3,600 gross monthly salary and €2,000 monthly freelance receipts. The goal is to move by late 2027. Do not treat the two income figures as disposable household income: first find out what a bank will accept after taxes, contributions and business costs.

## Keep the purchase conditional

**Start with Almada and Amadora.** The examples below include a €279,000 T2 in Feijó and a €298,500 example in Amadora (card only). The main Lisbon-city examples and Oeiras are materially dearer; cheaper city exceptions exist but need careful tenancy/use checks. Alvalade is not realistic on this conservative plan. These are search priorities, not neighbourhood-wide price guarantees.

90% LTV

Modelled at 90%; lower financing may work depending on relief, valuation and closing costs. This ceiling is not an entitlement.

€1,096–€1,195

Loan-only monthly payments at €275k–€300k, 90% financing, 35 years and a 4% illustrative TAN.

€15,000

Keep untouched as an emergency reserve, separate from purchase costs and any renovation budget.

**Decision gates:** use this range only if accepted net income is around €3,800 or more, existing debts are negligible, financing and valuation support the cash model, and the stressed household budget leaves a positive buffer you are comfortable maintaining. The 90% scenario is illustrative, not the only workable financing level. With only €3,200 accepted net, even €275,000 produces a stressed deficit under the stated expenses: lower the price and/or demonstrate lower expenditure, or wait. At 80% LTV, reduce to roughly €230,000–€250,000 *if both qualify for youth tax relief*; without that relief, the cash ceiling is about €220,856. Given the inspected prices, waiting and saving would then be more sensible than draining the reserve.

**Do not rush a 2026 purchase for a promotion.** There is time to document a stronger freelance history before late 2027. No 2027 interest rate, price fall, tax threshold or extension of the public guarantee is assumed.

## Get the bank to assess your real income

**Yes, resident Brazilian applicants can be considered.** BPI's published mortgage eligibility includes Portugal-resident employees and liberal professionals; its Portuguese-nationality restriction is attached to nonresidents. That is evidence of access, not approval for these permits, income or an identical offer from every bank. Immigration residence and Portuguese *tax residence* must be checked separately. No universal nationality-specific LTV haircut or mandatory residence-duration rule was established.

### Lending-rules checklist

- **LTV:** own/permanent-home loans normally no more than 90% of the lower of price and bank appraisal. A bank can offer less. Other-purpose cap: 80%.

- **DSTI:** the current Banco de Portugal solvency page says total monthly debt service should generally not exceed **45% of income after taxes and compulsory contributions**. Include existing loans. This is not a comfortable spending target or a promise of approval.

- **Term:** current BdP page: up to 40 years at age ≤35; 35 years above 35. Santander also caps each holder's age plus term at 75. The calculations here use 35 years, not the longest available term.

- **Stress:** the BdP public-guarantee FAQ describes +1.5 percentage points for variable-rate loans longer than ten years. Our 4% → 5.5% calculation illustrates that case. The same FAQ still mentions 50% DSTI, unlike the dedicated current 45% page; use the stricter 45% and obtain the applicable methodology at the future application. Mixed-rate and retirement adjustments were not fully verified.

- **Creditworthiness:** banks consider regular income, age, employment, living expenses, liabilities and adverse future changes. BdP requires consideration of at least the previous three months' income; prepare evidence for it and no assumed future income growth. Banks may ask for more freelance history. No universal freelancer haircut was verified.

**Public guarantee is a separate programme.** The inspected official/bank materials require contracts by **31 December 2026**, plus age, Portuguese fiscal domicile, income-band, first-home/property and tax/social-security conditions. It does not compel approval. Do not base a late-2027 plan on a 100% loan or assume the guarantee is the same thing as youth IMT relief.

### Prepare the documents before applying

### Both applicants

Passport, valid residence titles, NIF, Portuguese address/tax-residence evidence; latest IRS return and nota de liquidação; bank statements, savings/source-of-funds evidence and all debt commitments. Confirm permit expiry/renewal evidence and any Brazilian records the chosen bank requires.

### Income

Employee: last three payslips and employer/contract statement. Freelancer: last three recibos verdes, start-of-activity declaration, IRS/assessment, bank credits; prepare longer accounts and client contracts as prudent supporting evidence, not a verified universal requirement.

### Property

Caderneta predial, registry/title and property plans; energy certificate and relevant use/licensing or exemption documentation. Have an independent professional check title, lawful use, condominium debts/minutes, works and condition before a binding purchase commitment.

## Purchase costs and young-buyer relief

**Being in your early 30s is a useful starting point, but it does not establish either buyer’s entitlement to relief.** Each must be ≤35 on the acquisition date, not an IRS dependent in that year, and satisfy the first-own/permanent-home and ownership-history tests. A residential property or fractional right at purchase or within the previous three years can disqualify a buyer; disclose any Brazilian property/inheritance rights too rather than assuming foreign assets do not count. Nationality is not a bar. Confirm tax residence, birthdays, ownership history and ownership shares before relying on relief.

The enacted **2026 mainland full-relief ceiling is €330,539**; partial relief extends to €660,982. Do not split a jointly acquired whole apartment into two half-price apartments to apply the thresholds. For common marital property, entitlement is assessed per spouse in equal shares. If only one qualifies, that buyer's qualifying share gets relief, not the whole purchase. Establish the correct treatment of unmarried/unequal ownership in the eventual tax declaration.

## Test the monthly budget before choosing the price

**You have €70,000 to plan around; the missing number is bank-accepted net income.** Do not add €3,600 gross pay to €2,000 freelance turnover and call the result €5,600 disposable income. Salary alone is €43,200 gross/year if paid 12 times or €50,400 if paid 14 times; those are alternatives, not established contract facts. Freelance receipts at the stated pace are €24,000/year before business expenses, tax and contributions (and any VAT treatment). Use net income evidenced by IRS, statements and payroll; do not assume that extra salary payments or every receipt will be counted.

For planning only, test accepted household net at **€3,200 / €3,800 / €4,400 per month**, already normalised by the hypothetical bank. These are assumptions, *not net-pay estimates*. Existing monthly debt is assumed €0. No new savings, appreciation, bonus or future income increase is included.

### Payment formula

`M = L × i ÷ [1 − (1+i)^(−n)]`, where `i = annual TAN / 12`, `n = 35 × 12 = 420`, and `L = 90% × price`. Rate inputs are 4% nominal planning rate (near the observed 3.939% example, rounded prudently), and 5.5% stress. They are not forecasts. At €275,000: `L = €247,500; i = 0.04/12; M = €1,095.87`; stressed `M = €1,329.12`. Insurance/fees are not in that instalment.

**Cash-side ceiling at 80% LTV:** retain €15,000, so only €55,000 can go to closing. With full youth relief and €3,000 other costs, `0.20P + 0.006 × 0.80P + €3,000 ≤ €55,000`, hence **P ≤ €253,906.25**. Without youth relief in the relevant band, `0.2828P − €7,457.96 ≤ €55,000`, hence **P ≤ €220,855.59**. These cash constraints bind well before a gross-income headline suggests.

### Household-budget test at €275,000

Add a **€250/month assumed owner-cost allowance**: life insurance €60, home insurance €20, condominium €60, IMI reserve €40 and maintenance €70. These are placeholders, not verified tariffs or an IMI assessment. Budget €1,800/month for other living costs, excluding current rent after moving. Then `€3,800 − €1,095.87 − €250 − €1,800 = €654.13` monthly margin; at 5.5% it falls to **€420.88**. At €3,200 net, margins are only **€54.13 / −€179.12**. That is why the theoretical €297,942.55 price ceiling at that income is not the recommended target.

**Downside checks:** a €300,000 purchase appraised at €280,000 permits at most €252,000 at 90%, not €270,000; equity becomes €48,000. With full youth relief, loan duty €1,512 and other costs €3,000, closing cash is **€52,512**, leaving €17,488. At 80% LTV on a €300,000 value, closing cash is **€64,440** even with relief, leaving only €5,560. Both demonstrate that valuation and bank LTV matter as much as the rate.

For scale, a 6% rate would make the €247,500 / 35-year payment **€1,411.22**. If accepted net temporarily fell to €2,500 (a hypothetical one-income shock, not a salary estimate), the €275k base-rate household budget above would run **€645.87/month short**. Preserve the reserve and improve the freelance evidence; do not stretch to the bank's absolute cap.

## From pre-approval to keys

The operational durations below are our planning allowances, **not guaranteed bank service times**. Pre-approval is not a year-long rate lock or final property approval. Recheck rules, taxes and rates in 2027.

### Now–March 2027 · make the file financeable

Reconcile net earnings and household spending, check tax/residence/ownership facts, organise freelance records and permit-renewal evidence. Keep the reserve separate. Use the public fee structures to prepare a shortlist; a real application and any sharing of personal documents should be a separate decision.

### April–June 2027 · pre-approval, then search

When you choose to apply, allow approximately 2–4 weeks for bank income review; freelancer or permit questions may take longer. Compare FINEs, actual LTV, valuation policy, rate structure, insurance and fees. Refresh approval as needed. Search Almada/Amadora at the conditional budget, not at the maximum advertised loan.

### June–September 2027 · property and CPCV

Allow approximately 2–6 weeks for title/condominium/condition review, negotiation and property documents. Before paying a binding deposit, negotiate a lawyer-reviewed financing-and-valuation condition and realistic completion deadline. A hypothetical 10% CPCV deposit on €275,000 is €27,500: it is credited toward the price, not added to closing equity. Refund protections must be written, not assumed.

### After property selection · appraisal and final credit decision

Allow approximately 2–4 weeks, with overlap possible, for valuation and underwriting. Resolve any valuation shortfall before committing further. Final written offer/FINE and draft contract are distinct from an initial simulation.

### Offer to deed · reflection and completion

Santander's pre-contract disclosure states a minimum **30-day binding offer** and no acceptance during the first **7 days** after receipt. Schedule the deed only after the reflection period and all conditions. Confirm acquisition-date tax/exemption declarations, payment evidence, insurance, remaining equity and the exact closing package. Use the signed statement to avoid paying the deposit twice.

### October–November 2027 · deed, registration, move

Reserve a further 1–3 weeks for coordination, then a move-in buffer before year-end. The lender pays the financed balance at completion under its arrangements; purchase/mortgage registration follows the chosen channel. Update fiscal address and genuinely occupy the whole property as the permanent home within six months; the benefit has a six-year maintenance period, subject to statutory exceptions. Keep receipts and registration evidence; youth relief can be lost if use/holding conditions are breached.

Rough active financing-to-deed planning window after finding a suitable property: 8–12 weeks, with contingency; phases may overlap and are not simply additive. No bank, broker, agent or service was contacted in preparing this guide.

[Santander’s general pre-contract disclosure](https://www.santander.pt/pdfs/particulares/credito-habitacao/CH_Informacao_Pre-Contratual_Geral.pdf) is the anchor for the offer/reflection periods, not a guaranteed processing schedule. Read [CIMT article 11](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cimt/Pages/cimt11.aspx) for the permanent-home use and relief-maintenance conditions. Have the financing, valuation and deposit protections checked before signing a CPCV.

## Settle these questions before you commit

Your next useful step is a bank-accepted income assessment and a real household budget—not a binding deposit. Keep these questions open until you have written, personal answers:

- **Income and permits:** what net salary and freelance income will the bank accept, how will it normalise extra salary payments, and what residence/renewal evidence does it need? No universal freelancer haircut or minimum residence duration has been established here.
- **Relief:** do both buyers pass the deed-date age, tax-residence, dependency and ownership tests? Brazilian property and inherited rights need case-specific confirmation; the effect of foreign rights is not conclusively resolved here. Check ownership shares, VPT and whole-home use too.
- **Loan and household stress:** confirm the full current methodology for variable or mixed rates, retirement adjustments, actual LTV, appraisal, insurance and existing debts. A regulatory ceiling is not a comfortable monthly spending limit.
- **Property:** check current availability, vacant possession, lawful use, title, condominium debts, works and condition. Five main-table examples were available only as cards. Neither the advertisements nor the broad transaction statistics establish a representative T2 value.
- **Closing costs and timing:** replace allowances with an itemised bank/conveyance quote and budget separately for renovation, furnishing and moving. Process durations are planning allowances, not promises. Recheck acquisition-date law, fees and rates before a late-2027 commitment.
