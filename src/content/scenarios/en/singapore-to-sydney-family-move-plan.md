**Worried about getting settled before school starts? Sort out the address first. Term 3 pupils return on 20 July 2027, but the planned permanent tenancy starts on 29 July 2027. By 15 June 2027, agree genuine residence evidence, year placement and a start date with the school. A short-stay booking may not meet catchment rules. If it does not, arrange an earlier real lease or another school-agreed route; do not simply wait for the tenancy to begin.**

**For the starting plan, set aside A$76,229.18. That includes a local car, refundable bond and 10% contingency, but not your still-unknown Singapore exit costs, tax, withheld-salary cash gap or time without income. Use it to start checking your own costs, not as a quote or a promise that the money will be enough.**

## Start with your family’s dates and documents

This example is for 2 adults and a 6-year-old, all Australian permanent residents, leaving a three-bedroom Singapore condo and arriving in Sydney on 1 July 2027. Before using the plan, fill in each person’s citizenship and Singapore pass or PR category, job contracts, the child’s exact birth date, the real school address, any income gap and Australian PR re-entry documents. Australian PR does not tell you which Singapore exit rules apply.

Prices and rules below are dated 5 November 2026, not your eventual travel or signing date. Refresh them before you commit. If you adapt the budget, keep its 63 linked calculations in step with changed inputs; zero calculation errors would still not tell you whether your own lease costs, taxes or school arrangements are covered.

Advance rent is already included in the permanent-rent allowance; do not add it again. Bond ties up cash even though it is not spent rent. To avoid relying on money that may arrive late, the total does not deduct CPF, refunded deposits, sale proceeds or future salary.

## Keep school, healthcare, clearance and driving moving

Prepare a real address and identity documents for [primary enrolment](https://education.nsw.gov.au/schooling/parents-and-carers/choosing-a-school-setting/enrolment/primary-school-enrolment.html). Schools near capacity may use a 100-point address check. School is compulsory by 6, but exact birth date and prior schooling determine the year. [Public-school guidance](https://www.service.nsw.gov.au/guide/nsw-public-schools) means PR tuition is free, not uniforms, activities or care. The [2027 calendar](https://education.nsw.gov.au/schooling/calendars/2027) has Term 2 ending 2 July 2027, staff development on 19 July 2027 and pupils returning on 20 July 2027. Arrange care through 19 July 2027 and confirm your school’s own start. Bring overseas vaccination records to an Australian provider so you can obtain an AIR statement. Under [immunisation guidance](https://www.nsw.gov.au/education-and-training/primary-and-high-school/starting-primary-school/enrolling-your-child/immunisation-requirements), missing vaccination does not automatically prevent enrolment, but outbreak exclusions may apply.

If you have been abroad for more than 12 months, [Medicare returning-PR guidance](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/enrolling-medicare-if-youre-australian-permanent-resident?context=60092) says to enrol again. Prepare passports and, under [Document guidance](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/documents-to-enrol-medicare-if-youre-permanent-resident?context=60092), 2 residence documents dated within 6 months: 2 Australian, or 1 Australian and 1 overseas. If using the lease example, you also need a utility account in the same name. Prepare what you can before departure, apply once you have evidence of living in Australia, and keep bridge cover. An old card does not guarantee confirmed coverage on the day you arrive.

Before packing, check [ABF personal-effects guidance](https://www.abf.gov.au/entering-and-leaving-australia/moving-to-australia/upe) and [concession by-law](https://www.abf.gov.au/importing-exporting-and-manufacturing/tariff-classification/current-tariff/schedule-4-by-law). Ordinary household-goods relief generally needs 12 months of ownership and use, with exceptions. Arrange transport before arrival and declare newer or restricted items on the current [B534](https://www.abf.gov.au/form-listing/forms/B534e.pdf). Relief from customs charges does not remove variable [DAFF](https://www.agriculture.gov.au/biosecurity-trade/travelling/moving-immigrating) screening, inspection or treatment costs. Clean and dry outdoor goods, shoes, bikes and appliances; check BICON, wood pests and packing materials. Use [COLS](https://www.agriculture.gov.au/biosecurity-trade/import/online-services/cols) for biosecurity submissions, separately from ABF customs clearance.

Buying a car locally? Check history, security interests, condition and registration expiry before paying. The dated [Vehicle duty](https://www.service.nsw.gov.au/transaction/check-motor-vehicle-stamp-duty) is A$3 per A$100 or part up to A$45,000, using the higher of price and market value; a car at the assumed A$20,500 gives A$615. Complete [Registration transfer](https://www.nsw.gov.au/driving-boating-and-transport/driving-nsw/vehicle-registration/transferring-or-cancelling-registration/transferring-registration-when-buying-a-vehicle) within 14 days. Existing CTP transfers with registration, but property insurance is separate. Arrange an [Australian-approved child restraint](https://www.nsw.gov.au/driving-boating-and-transport/driving-nsw/roads-safety-and-rules/safe-driving/child-seats) before driving; buying a seat in Singapore does not establish Australian approval.

If NSW residence starts on 1 July 2027, plan [Overseas licence conversion](https://www.service.nsw.gov.au/transaction/transfer-an-overseas-driver-licence) before 1 October 2027; aim for 30 September 2027 rather than the last moment. [Test exemptions](https://www.nsw.gov.au/driving-boating-and-transport/driver-and-rider-licences/visiting-or-moving-to-nsw/moving-your-overseas-licence/knowledge-and-driving-test-exemptions) depend on the licence issuer, class and exceptions, not simply living in Singapore. Recheck the 3-month rule and 2027 fees before relying on an overseas licence.

Ask payroll to work through tax and equity liabilities for your own circumstances.

Use the cancellation route for the pass you actually hold: [EP](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/cancel-a-pass), [S Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/cancel-a-pass), [ONE Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/cancel-a-pass) or any [Student's Pass](https://www.ica.gov.sg/information-and-e-services/long-term-visit-and-study/student-s-pass/student's-pass-cancellation). Check linked family passes and permission to stay until the flight; do not work after cancellation. Under [CPF guidance](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/service/article/what-happens-to-my-cpf-account-if-i-am-no-longer-a-singapore-citizen-or-permanent-resident), moving overseas is not the same as renouncing status or making CPF available as moving cash. Check [insurance continuity](https://www.moneysense.gov.sg/health-insurance/) before ending cover. If your child qualifies and you intend to return, consider [MOE leave of absence](https://www.moe.gov.sg/returning-singaporeans/going-overseas/loa-scheme) rather than automatically withdrawing; confirm eligibility with the school.

**Before committing, settle the actual contracts, school-address acceptance, family bedding, freight inclusions and the cash gaps still left blank.** Refresh prices in January–March 2027 and official rules and forms in May–June 2027. Check personal eligibility with the relevant employer, school or authority before submitting, paying or booking.
