When both of you return to work, a low fee helps only if care is actually available. **Prioritise an Anchor Operator infant-care place near home, keep a Partner Operator as the next choice, and agree a human backup for illness and closures.** A willing grandparent can bridge a delayed start without taking on an indefinite full working week. A live-in helper makes more sense if you also need substantial household support and can train and supervise her—not just because the levy is low.

The example is a Tampines family with one Singapore-citizen parent, one permanent-resident parent, S$11,500 gross monthly household income, a baby due in February 2027 and both parents working full-time by August 2027. The main calculation assumes a **Singapore-citizen baby at birth, lawfully married parents, a first child, a qualifying working mother/main applicant and assessed income of S$11,500**. Those details, including birth order and the mother's identity, still need confirmation. Chinese-language readers get the same scenario, not different eligibility rules.

At the published caps and with the full assumed subsidy, budget **S$646.15/month for AOP** or **S$706.10/month for POP**, before extras. These are conditional programme fees—not confirmed August places, subsidy awards or all-in quotations. Rules and prices were read on November 7, 2026; this guide does not refresh them. All money is Singapore dollars.

**Do not use the announced S$300 infant-care target for August 2027.** Progressive reductions start in 2028, aiming for 2030. The separate April 2027 changes affect Baby Bonus cash and CDA arrangements. Recheck implementation before committing. Treat this official [government announcement](https://www.madeforfamilies.gov.sg/ndr-2026-supporting-families/) as provisional for your plan and confirm the implementation details before paying.

## Compare recurring care costs before spending finite grants

Baby Gift and CDA funds can help pay a bill; they do not permanently reduce the underlying care cost. Compare the recurring routes first. Common baby expenses such as milk, diapers and healthcare, optional transport, late collection, extra activities and lost parental pay are excluded unless shown.

| AOP infant care | S$1,235 + 9% GST = S$1,346.15 | Less S$600 Basic and S$100 Additional | S$646.15 | Registration, deposit and extras; written future fee schedule needed |
| POP infant care | S$1,290 + 9% GST = S$1,406.10 | Less S$600 Basic and S$100 Additional | S$706.10 | S$59.95/month above AOP at caps; extras separate |

| Live-in helper | Assumed S$800 salary + S$60 concessionary levy + S$250 daily needs | No ECDA fee subsidy | S$1,110 routine cash; S$1,168.33 with reserves; S$1,256.06 first-contract monthly equivalent | Illustrative S$2,105.55 setup; accommodation, replacement and uninsured costs may add more |
| Grandparent + part-time cleaner | Assumed S$600 honorarium + S$100 travel + S$390 cleaning | No ECDA or levy subsidy for this arrangement | S$1,090; sensitivity S$490–S$1,490 with S$0–S$1,000 honorarium | Requires a willing full-day grandparent; relief childcare excluded |

The care rows use the [ECDA subsidy table](https://file.go.gov.sg/ecda-subsidy-table-cc-ifc.pdf), [AOP caps](https://www.ecda.gov.sg/parents/choosing-a-preschool/aop) and [POP caps](https://www.ecda.gov.sg/parents/choosing-a-preschool/pop). For an actual GST-inclusive private fee F, the conditional calculation is F − S$700, subject to minimum co-payment adjustment. Do not apply that formula without checking eligibility and the actual award.

GST, where charged by a registered operator, applies to the full fee **before** the parent's subsidy is deducted, not afterwards. [IRAS treatment](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/goods-services-tax-(gst)/charging-gst-(output-tax)/common-scenarios---do-i-charge-gst/provision-of-subsidized-services) and the [9% rate](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/goods-services-tax-(gst)/basics-of-gst/current-gst-rates) are official primary sources. The tax inputs remain provisional for this plan. Ask the centre for a written GST-inclusive bill; Skool4Kidz's published fee also supports the AOP gross figure.

These are not equivalent services. Infant care covers operating hours, not nights or sick days. A helper needs rest and is not automatically infant-trained. In the grandparent model, the grandparent provides all daytime infant supervision; the cleaner cleans only.

### What the home-care budgets really include

For the helper, add an assumed S$50 medical check every 6 months: S$50 ÷ 6 = S$8.33/month. Add S$50/month for leave, travel and contingency. Together with routine cash, that gives S$1,168.33/month. Salary, daily needs and reserves are planning inputs, not personalised quotes.

An unconfirmed [We Are Caring commercial reference](https://wearecaring.com/sg/en/costs) gives transfer setup of S$1,295 service + S$116.55 GST + S$70 permit + S$624 insurance/bond guarantee = S$2,105.55. Spread over 24 months, setup adds S$87.73, giving S$1,256.06/month when rounded from unrounded inputs. **Do not add insurance again:** it is already in setup. Actual payments are lumpy, not monthly. The same provider lists S$3,395.55 for an Indonesia/Philippines overseas package before flights; a first-time helper's SIP adds S$76.40. Training, accommodation preparation, later insurance renewal and uncovered medical costs may add more.

A [S$5,000 security bond](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/security-bond) for a non-Malaysian helper is a contingent guarantee, not an extra ordinary monthly expense. Parents provide weekly rest-day relief in this model. [MOM welfare obligations](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/employers-guide/rest-days-and-well-being) include food, accommodation, privacy, medical care and rest; at least one rest day each month cannot be compensated away. Assess infant-care competence and allow several weeks for demonstration and observation. Do not leave a new helper alone with the baby on her first day.

For grandparent care, the S$390 cleaning allowance is S$30/hour × 3 hours/week × 52 ÷ 12. Add S$600 honorarium and S$100 transport to reach S$1,090. With no honorarium it is S$490; with S$1,000 it is S$1,490. These are family-agreement sensitivities, not a market range. [Helpling's commercial headline](https://www.helpling.com.sg/pricing) starts at S$21/hour, but extras and GST scope need confirmation. S$30 is a conservative all-in assumption, not a booking price. Holidays, extra cleaning, paid relief care and grandparents' lost income are outside the model. Both commercial references remain unconfirmed, not final quotes.

## Check the applicant and income before trusting the net fee

The [ECDA eligibility rules](https://www.ecda.gov.sg/parents/preschool-subsidies/infant-and-childcare-subsidy-scheme/overview) require the child's Singapore citizenship and an ECDA-licensed programme. Infant care covers ages 2–18 months. Having one PR parent does not itself exclude an SC child; having one citizen parent is not proof that the baby already has citizenship.

The ordinary main applicant is the mother, working at least 56 hours/month, including full-time, part-time or freelance work. Specified single-father provisions exist. Both parents returning full-time is a sensible planning assumption, not approval. If employment changes before a July start, recheck the award.

The table updated October 6, 2026 takes effect January 1, 2027. At assessed HHI of S$11,500, the S$11,001–13,000 band gives S$600 Basic plus up to S$100 Additional, with a S$640 minimum parental co-pay. Both modelled fees exceed that floor; **do not add S$640 again**. The 2026 Additional amount at the same income was S$40; it is not the August schedule.

From January 1, 2027, the Additional Subsidy HHI ceiling rises from S$12,000 to S$15,000, and PCI ceiling from S$3,000 to S$3,400. [Income assessment](https://www.ecda.gov.sg/parents/preschool-subsidies/assessment-of-household-income) uses both parents' average gross income, not take-home pay. It includes bonuses, commissions, overtime, allowances, cash rewards and employee CPF contributions. Salaried income is averaged over 12 months using CPF or the latest available IRAS assessment within the last 2 calendar years. Personal spending is not deducted. Self-employed assessment and missing-data documentation can differ.

PCI ordinarily requires at least 5 qualifying related family members at the same registered address and counts their income too. Do not simply divide S$11,500 by a 3-person household. For the HHI calculation, both married parents' income counts even if their registered addresses differ.

| Income or approval case | Assessed monthly HHI | Maximum Additional | Conditional AOP net | Conditional POP net |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Main example | S$11,500 | S$100 | S$646.15 | S$706.10 |
| Base income plus 1 extra salary month | S$11,500 × 13 ÷ 12 = S$12,458.33 | S$100 | S$646.15 | S$706.10 |
| Base income plus 2 extra salary months | S$11,500 × 14 ÷ 12 = S$13,416.67 | S$40 | S$706.15 | S$766.10 |
| Basic approved, Additional not approved | Actual assessment needed | Not assumed | S$746.15 | S$806.10 |

These bonus examples are sensitivities, not facts about the family. The higher case falls in S$13,001–15,000 with a S$700 co-pay floor. Above S$15,000 ordinary HHI-based Additional stops; the qualifying working-applicant Basic remains S$600. A non-working infant-care applicant ordinarily receives S$150 Basic; special approval may be possible on specified grounds. Do not apply the separately expanded non-working **childcare** rules automatically to infant care.

Subsidies go to the preschool, and at least 1 day of attendance in the month is required. The preschool helps with the application, but parents must complete verification and keep cash available pending approval. If the child is not SC, do not assume any standard ECDA subsidy modelled here; request the actual non-SC tariff.

For a helper, the [levy concession](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/foreign-domestic-worker-levy/levy-concession) is S$60/month instead of the [normal first-helper levy](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/foreign-domestic-worker-levy/paying-levy) of S$300: a S$240 saving if the eligible SC child under 16 lives in the household. For a parent it starts from birth; allow for bill adjustments. It is not an income-tested cash payment. ECDA subsidies do not reduce helper wages, grandparent honoraria or cleaning bills.

Do not subtract annual tax relief as monthly cash. Income split, claimant, taxable incomes and other reliefs are unknown. No disability-care grant is assumed for an otherwise healthy infant.

## Use the centre label to understand fees, not to rank care

AOPs receive ECDA funding for affordable access and quality. My First Skool and Skool4Kidz are listed AOP operators. POP funding is branch-specific; check the appointed centre, not just its brand. The published POP term runs January 1, 2026–December 31, 2030.

The full-day infant caps used here are S$1,235 AOP and S$1,290 POP before GST—not the full-day childcare caps of S$610 and S$650. Private centres outside these schemes set their own fees, but eligible SC children at licensed programmes may still receive subsidies. Published caps are a planning basis read on November 7, 2026, not a guaranteed future tariff.

Choose on commute, reliable collection, actual infant placement, caregiver consistency, settling-in, communication, feeding and sleep practices, closures, hours and withdrawal terms. A scheme label is not a review, and a higher fee does not establish better care.

### Join eligible queues, but set a decision deadline

[My First Skool's FAQ](https://www.myfirstskool.com/about-us/faq/) allows year-round vacancy-dependent enrolment, interest up to 12 months before the preferred start and selection of up to 3 centres. For an unborn baby, interest opens 3 months before the expected delivery date. A due date of February 7, 2027 opens November 7, 2026; February 28, 2027 opens November 28, 2026. The exact due day matters. August 2027 is within the preferred-start window, but not every February pregnancy can register on the first example date. Availability can change and immediate care needs may receive priority.

[Skool4Kidz's registration checklist](https://skool4kidz.com.sg/registration/) asks for birth, health, parent, authorised-collector, bank and employment documents as applicable. This does not establish its pre-birth interest rule or mean an infant needs an age-inappropriate vaccination. Its centre pages offer interest registration, but pre-birth acceptance and branch lead times remain unconfirmed. Do not borrow another operator's policy.

There is no defensible numerical local wait: no confirmed August vacancy, queue length or median delay was established. A registration window is not a promised waiting time; do not assume a generic 3–6 months. Keep a small geographic spread within Tampines and your commute. **Aim for a written place and start date by May–June 2027.** That is a family risk-management deadline, not an operator promise. If only a queue position remains, activate a defined grandparent bridge or prepare a helper early enough for overlap.

## Keep Baby Gift cash separate from CDA

For this February 2027 first-child case, enrol in [Baby Bonus](https://www.life.gov.sg/family-parenting/benefits-support/baby-bonus-scheme) at birth and open the CDA and Child Savings Account as instructed. [The existing cash schedule](https://www.life.gov.sg/family-parenting/benefits-support/baby-bonus-scheme/csa) starts with S$3,000. Under the [SG Child Support Package transition](https://www.life.gov.sg/family-parenting/benefits-support/sg-child-support-package), enrolled children move automatically in April 2027. An applicable top-up by April 30, 2027 brings total cash support to S$10,000. If S$3,000 has arrived, the illustrative top-up is S$7,000—not another S$10,000.

Do not add the old S$1,500 six-month payment in August or the legacy S$11,000 total as well. Processing and account setup still matter. Set up the child's PayNow ID on an eligible ordinary personal/joint account before April, **not on the CDA**.

Cash is flexible but finite. Spreading S$10,000 over 12 months gives S$833.33/month of one-off funding, not a permanently cheaper care arrangement. Birth spending leaves less for care. The first S$2,000 annual Child Credits for this baby would be in 2028 when the child turns 1, not by August 2027. PSEA support at 17 is not infant-care cash.

The first-child [CDA](https://www.life.gov.sg/family-parenting/benefits-support/baby-bonus-scheme/cda) First Step Grant is S$5,000 with no parent deposit required. Through September 2027 the existing matching cap is S$4,000: deposit S$4,000 and receive S$4,000 match, giving S$13,000 before spending including the grant. Government support is S$9,000; the parents' deposit is still their own money.

From October 1, 2027 all matching caps adjust to S$5,000. A first child already matched to S$4,000 has S$1,000 extra headroom, not a fresh S$5,000. CDA use extends through the end of the year the child turns 16. It remains ring-fenced: eligible net preschool fees at an Approved Institution can be paid, but ordinary helper wages, family honoraria and cleaning bills cannot be treated as eligible. Confirm the actual branch's AI status and each charge before authorising payment.

Eligible invoices may initially mean S$0 from the parents' ordinary bank account, but the grant is depleted and extras remain payable. It is not free care forever. Do not subtract the same grant again from an already net fee.

If this is not the first child, the February-birth First Step Grant is S$5,000 for the second child and S$10,000 for third and later. Existing matching caps through September 30, 2027 are S$7,000 for the second, S$9,000 for third/fourth and S$15,000 for fifth and later. These depend on birth order, not income. Check transition treatment before depositing against a higher cap; do not assume matching already received will be clawed back. The illustrative S$7,000 April top-up applies to a first/second child initially receiving S$3,000.

## Make the return-to-work calendar workable

Infant care suits parents who can collect on time and name another adult for illness/closures. Grandparent care suits someone who actively wants the role, is physically able and agrees hours, honorarium, travel, breaks, feeding/safe-sleep rules and absence cover. Cleaning reduces chores, not continuous infant-care responsibility. A helper suits a family able to provide live-in accommodation, fair conditions and supervision. The cheapest nominal route can become expensive if it repeatedly causes unpaid absence.

| When | Your practical next step |
| --- | --- |
| November 2026 | Set collection radius and workday hours; shortlist infant programmes; join eligible pre-birth queues; ask whether grandparents want a backup role. Do not wait until the baby is 6 months old. |

Six months at home is not automatically six months of paid leave for each parent. [MSF schemes](https://www.profamilyleave.msf.gov.sg/schemes) list up to 16 weeks maternity, 4 weeks paternity and 10 weeks shared parental leave **between the parents**, subject to eligibility and employment arrangements. [Shared leave](https://www.profamilyleave.msf.gov.sg/schemes/shared-parental-leave) is generally after each parent's own maternity/paternity leave and within the child's first year. Agree a real employer-approved calendar. The announced increased childcare-leave scheme had no confirmed commencement date in the information read; do not assume it bridges this gap.
