**Worried about getting halfway through and running out of money? CAD 70,000 does NOT cover any of the unfunded 24-month options below. UNBC comes to CAD 82,225; Douglas reaches CAD 122,144. Brock can fit at CAD 67,615 only if you finish in 20 months and receive a confirmed CAD 25,333 fellowship. Do not count on assistantship wages to close the gap. If the actual offer does not fit, postpone or increase your funding.**

**Use this to plan, not as immigration or legal advice. Admission, a PAL, public-institution status or a programme title does not guarantee a permit, PGWP, job or permanent residence.**

## Start with your own qualifications and goals

This example is for a Filipino BS Business Administration graduate living in Manila, without dependants, considering public institutions in Ontario or British Columbia for 2027-09 entry. To make it yours, confirm your GPA, degree equivalency, quantitative preparation, English scores, work history and actual funding offers. A research master's is not a taught MBA substitute. Choose it because you want to do research, then check the programme and supervisor fit.

The information and budget assumptions were checked on 2026-11-05 and have not been refreshed here. **Published 2026 or 2026/27 fees are a starting point, not 2027-09 quotations, guaranteed places or future rules.** Before paying, ask for fees for your own intake. All money stays in CAD, without currency conversion.

## Count the whole stay, not just tuition

The budget covers 24 calendar months, including summers, with shared accommodation, no car and no dependants. It counts no Canadian earnings, tax refunds, unawarded scholarships or co-op income. School-billed medical and health plans, transit and books are in academic extras, not monthly living. Tuition stays at current rates for comparison. The separate contingency may help with increases, but cannot promise to cover them.

Each option includes CAD 11,000: CAD 5,000 for one-offs and CAD 6,000 kept as contingency. The one-offs are CAD 235 for the permit and biometrics, CAD 300 for medical, CAD 1,500 for applications, tests and documents, CAD 1,500 for transport and CAD 1,465 for arrival, winter clothing and setup. Only CAD 235 is an official government total here, assuming new biometrics. The rest are allowances: get actual clinic, test and travel prices. Do not add a tuition-credit deposit again. The [IRCC fee list](https://ircc.canada.ca/english/information/fees/fees.asp) lists CAD 150 for the permit and CAD 85 for biometrics if required.

Add a separate budget if you need optional co-op, prerequisites, repeat terms, major medical follow-up, family visits or post-study living and PGWP costs.

## What to ask each school before deciding

### Brock: check the fellowship, finish date and payment schedule together

[Brock Goodman MSc Management](https://brocku.ca/goodman/programs/msc/) is a full-time thesis programme in St. Catharines. Ask about the finish date: Goodman says 2 years / 5 terms, while the [central programme page](https://brocku.ca/programs/graduate/msc/) says 20 months / 5 terms. Published 2026 tuition is CAD 6,725.34 per term × 5 = CAD 33,626.70. The school's rounded tuition-and-related-fees figure is CAD 39,948, before living and books. Get the duration and fees for your offer rather than treating either as guaranteed.

The advertised international funding of CAD 43,028 is not all scholarship money: it is CAD 25,333 fellowship + CAD 17,695 assistantship earnings. Fellowship payments come at term beginnings. Wages depend on your duties and arrive over time; they are not an upfront tuition waiver. Read your own award conditions and payment dates, then make sure you have cash before the payments arrive.

For 24 months, CAD 100,948 − CAD 25,333 leaves your family paying CAD 75,615, still CAD 5,615 over budget. Finish in 20 months and living falls by CAD 8,000, bringing the family requirement to CAD 67,615 with CAD 2,385 left over. The separate CAD 6,000 reserve is already included. **This works only if you receive the fellowship and finish in 20 months.** Deducting the full advertised package gives CAD 57,920 before payroll deductions, but that depends on future wages. Do not use it as the recommended budget or guaranteed permit cash.

Check the academic fit before paying application costs. You need a relevant 4-year Canadian-equivalent degree, normally 78% over the final 2 years, GMAT/GRE with no advertised waiver, a research statement, references and an interview. Academic IELTS 7.0 overall is listed where applicable; check accepted alternatives. A relevant BSBA does not establish equivalency or admission. Applications are explicitly open for 2027-09. Use 2027-03-01 as the international recommended apply-by date inferred from the annual deadline, and reconfirm it. Admissions roll until full; the date does not reserve a place.

### UNBC: lower tuition still leaves a funding gap

[UNBC MSc Business Administration](https://www.unbc.ca/calendar/graduate/business-administration-msc) is a normally 2-year thesis and research programme in Prince George. The [2026/27 fee calendar](https://www.unbc.ca/calendar/graduate/fees) gives CAD 2,704.15 per semester × 6 = CAD 16,224.90 tuition, before fees, insurance, books and living. Under the [instalment policy](https://www.unbc.ca/graduate-administration/tuition-and-fees), thesis registration continues in summer and full international instalments continue while enrolled. The 3-instalment graduation minimum is not the usual full-programme price.

At CAD 82,225 without funding, you still need at least CAD 12,225 more family money or confirmed non-employment funding. Check admission too: a 4-year degree or equivalent, B / 3.00 over the last 60 credits, and enough mathematics, statistics and specialisation preparation. Prerequisites can cost more and delay entry. You also need to confirm research and supervisor fit. The [annual application cycle](https://www.unbc.ca/programs-and-admissions/graduate/graduate-application-intakes-and-deadlines) opens 10-01, with a first deadline of 12-15. **Treat 2026-12-15 for 2027-09 as a planning date to reconfirm: the published cycle does not fully label the year.** Do not assume late international consideration will be available.

### Douglas: field eligibility does not solve the cost problem

[International Supply Chain Management PBD](https://www.douglascollege.ca/program/pbdiscm) and [Finance PBD](https://www.douglascollege.ca/program/pbfinc) each take 2 years / 60 credits. Both require a recognized bachelor's or approved foreign equivalent and English requirements; supply chain recommends mathematics preparation. Supply chain lists New Westminster / Coquitlam. Before choosing housing for Finance, confirm the teaching campus and timetable; the budget location is only an assumption. These costs use the standard non-co-op versions. Supply chain's optional co-op has 69 credits and is not included.

[Current international tuition](https://www.douglascollege.ca/fees-and-finances/tuition-and-fee-estimator/tuition-and-fees-international-students) is CAD 682.40 per credit × 60 = CAD 40,944 for either programme. The annual 30-credit example is CAD 22,915.68 including listed medical, student fees and U-Pass, before books and living. The CAD 9,500 admission deposit goes toward tuition and fees. Read the refund conditions before paying; do not assume you can get it back. The [general calendar](https://www.douglascollege.ca/admissions/when-apply/international-students-application-dates-and-deadlines) opened 2026-10-01 for 2027-09 entry, with apply-by 2027-05-31 subject to earlier closure. The published fall permit-confirmation and refund/deferral-request cutoff is 08-25 at 23:59 Pacific. Confirm how it applies to your offer.

The [Registrar's reference PDF](https://www.douglascollege.ca/sites/default/files/docs/post-graduation-work-permit-program-eligibility-reference-document.pdf), dated 2026-05-12, maps Supply Chain to CIP 52.0203 and Finance to CIP 30.7104, financial analytics. Both were eligible when checked. **Not all business diplomas are excluded. But the programme name or your earlier BSBA cannot replace checking the exact code.** Even with field eligibility, the CAD 122,144 budget puts both beyond the available funds.

### Coast Mountain: do not treat this as the cheaper fallback

The [Business PDD catalogue](https://catalogue.coastmountaincollege.ca/programs/business-administration-post-degree-diploma/) lists 2 years / 54 credits, requiring a recognized bachelor's of at least 3 years plus English and mathematics prerequisites. The intake is TBD and the location is blank. Ask whether 2027-09 entry is offered and at which campus before going further. Older Terrace references do not confirm availability, and admission does not guarantee finishing within the advertised duration.

Fees effective 2026-08-01 for 2026/27 list CAD 12,518.28 for 27 credits annually. Despite the heading, that is not the whole programme. Holding rates flat, 2 years of tuition cost CAD 25,036.56; listed fees and books bring it to CAD 29,793.14 before basic medical and living. The [college PGWP PDF](https://coastmountaincollege.ca/docs/default-source/international-documents/pgwp-eligible-programs-list_cmtn_october-15-2025-%28updated%29.pdf), updated in its body on 2025-12-15 rather than the filename date, marks this PDD not eligible, CIP 52.0201. That code was also absent from the federal list when checked. Do not pay a deposit hoping the rule will change. Older policy commentary in the PDF is not current federal authority.

## Keep permit and PGWP checks beside the money decision

The [financial-support requirements](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit/get-documents/financial-support.html) list CAD 23,448 in annual living funds for a single person applying from 2026-09-01, plus first-year tuition and transport. Show credible funding for the whole longer programme without relying on Canadian work. This living minimum is not an extra fee or an all-in city budget. Document sponsors and where the money comes from; 6 months of bank statements are among the current examples. Recheck the indexed amount for 2027, which is not established here.

Under the [PAL rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit/get-documents/provincial-attestation-letter.html) checked, public-DLI master's degrees are exempt from 2026-01-01; include evidence of that exemption. Diplomas generally still need a PAL through the school, submitted with the application. A 2026 PAL cannot support a 2027 application. Recheck 2027 provincial allocations: they were not confirmed here, which does not mean none exist. Neither exemption nor allocation guarantees approval. Check the actual DLI, campus, programme and application requirements before committing.

The checked [PGWP eligibility rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/eligibility.html) require CLB/NCLC 7 in all skills for a master's degree, without a field restriction. For this new applicant, college diplomas require CLB/NCLC 5 and an eligible field. The [field list](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/eligibility/field-of-study.html) is frozen for 2026 only, not promised for 2027. Current rules allow qualifying field eligibility at study-permit or PGWP submission, with transitional provisions. Save the school's exact CIP mapping and the official list when you apply.

You also need an eligible DLI and programme, full-time semesters with a final-semester exception, and the required permit validity and status. For this future entrant, at least 50% must be in class in Canada; time outside Canada is deducted. Apply within 180 days of completion confirmation. New public-private curriculum-licensing entrants do not qualify. The [duration rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/about.html) allow an eligible master's of at least 8 months to support a 3-year PGWP even if shorter than 2 years. Other eligible programmes of at least 2 years may support 3 years. Passport expiry can shorten issuance, and generally only one PGWP is available. A 20-month master's is not automatically worse than a diploma on this point.

For [PGWP language evidence](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/get-documents.html), the accepted tests currently include in-person IELTS General Training, CELPIP-General, PTE Core, TEF Canada and TCF Canada. Results must be less than 2 years old at application. Admissions IELTS Academic or PTE Academic is not interchangeable. Arrange the right test near completion; do not assume today's rules will hold in 2029. If you continue living in Manila before this long stay, plan for a panel-physician medical and normally biometrics unless exempt. Follow the actual checklist, medical validity and biometric instruction letter. This budget is not a complete application checklist.

## Your next steps—and when to pause

1. Before spending on applications, confirm degree length and equivalency, GPA, quantitative preparation, accepted English evidence and genuine research interests. Prepare for UNBC's conditional 2026-12-15 milestone and Brock's recommended 2027-03-01 milestone, then reconfirm both cycles. School deadlines do not guarantee permit-processing times.
2. With an offer in hand, ask for actual 2027/28 and later tuition, mandatory fees, duration, prerequisites, confirmed awards and their conditions, and term-by-term payments. Keep fellowship separate from wages. Make sure cash is available before funding arrives; decline or defer an offer that leaves a gap. An application in progress is not a reason to book non-refundable travel or housing.
3. Try a more expensive month: CAD 300 extra adds CAD 7,200 over 24 months or CAD 6,000 over 20. A 10% tuition increase adds about CAD 1,622 at UNBC, CAD 3,363 at Brock or CAD 4,094 at Douglas. An extra UNBC semester adds CAD 2,704.15 tuition plus fees and living. Brock's small amount left over would not comfortably absorb these changes.
4. Before deposits and permit submission, recheck funds, PAL or exemption, DLI and campus, exact CIP where relevant, refund and deferral rules, and current Philippines instructions. Before graduating, recheck PGWP conditions and tests, and budget separately for life afterwards. Keep this as a plan: it does not contact schools, submit applications, pay, book or publish anything for you.
