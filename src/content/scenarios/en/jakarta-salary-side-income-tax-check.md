If your employer already deducts tax, freelance receipts can feel like money you can spend. **First check the family's tax arrangement and any valid NPPN notice. Do not choose the final-business regime just because the receipts are small.** In this example, annual salary withholding is **IDR 20,100,000**, but the actual household underpayment or overpayment is **unknown**.

Meet Rina, a **hypothetical** Jakarta employee aged 30, married to a working husband, with one child aged 3. Imagine monthly gross salary of IDR 18,000,000, THR of IDR 18,000,000 and design receipts of about IDR 6,000,000 per month since March. Assuming this continues through December, 10 design months produce **IDR 60,000,000**. These are illustrative inputs, not personal facts submitted by a real reader. Her husband's income, tax credits, family arrangement and actual payroll records are missing.

The rules and source access dates below are a **November 8, 2026** research snapshot. Amounts are in IDR; estimates are not assessments. Start by retrieving the family's Coretax profile/DUK, both spouses' payroll records and any 2026 NPPN acknowledgement—not by registering a new company.

## Keep payroll TER separate from the household calculation

For this working-husband example, Rina's payroll allowance is **TK/0, IDR 54,000,000, TER category A**. Marriage and a child do not automatically put her in category B. The [PP 58/2023 regulation](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pp-58-tahun-2023/view) and its [official TER table](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/e47c3fc4-a912-4bf1-bcad-335fee3f71f8/2023pp058.pdf) distinguish:

| Monthly TER category | Payroll statuses |
|---|---|
| A | TK/0, TK/1, K/0 |
| B | TK/2, TK/3, K/1, K/2 |
| C | K/3 |

Category A applies 8% above IDR 16,950,000 through IDR 19,750,000, and 15% above IDR 35,400,000 through IDR 39,100,000. There is no separate flat THR tax. Her employer does not add separately received design receipts to its payroll TER calculation.

Assume a full year with one employer, stated gross equal to taxable gross, no deductible employee pension/old-age contributions or other payroll adjustments, and THR paid before December. [PMK 168/2023](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/download/e60a82e0-b218-40f5-9d18-b924aa1e11ce/2023pmkeuangan168.pdf) requires annual reconciliation in the final employment tax period, rather than repeating TER indefinitely.

| Payroll calculation | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Annual gross: IDR 18,000,000 × 12 + IDR 18,000,000 THR | IDR 234,000,000 |
| Employment expense: 5%, capped at IDR 500,000/month and IDR 6,000,000/year | IDR 6,000,000 |
| Annual employment net income | IDR 228,000,000 |
| TK/0 PTKP deduction | IDR 54,000,000 |
| Taxable employment income | IDR 174,000,000 |
| First IDR 60,000,000 × 5% | IDR 3,000,000 |
| Remaining IDR 114,000,000 × 15% | IDR 17,100,000 |
| Annual salary tax | IDR 20,100,000 |
| Ordinary January–November month: IDR 18,000,000 × 8% | IDR 1,440,000 |
| THR month: IDR 36,000,000 × 15% | IDR 5,400,000 |
| January–November: 10 ordinary months + 1 THR month | IDR 19,800,000 |
| December final-period adjustment | IDR 300,000 |
| Alternative: THR paid in December; annual tax less 11 ordinary deductions | IDR 4,260,000 |

On those assumptions, payroll settles the salary tax without a shortfall or excess. If prior deductions exceed annual salary tax, the employer must refund the excess under PMK 168, ordinarily by the end of the following month. Earlier employment termination has its own final period. Actual BPJS-related taxable benefits, employee contributions, other compensation and THR timing can change the figures. **Correct payroll does not settle the family's salary-plus-business return.**

## Find the family's status before choosing a freelance method

The [DJP PTKP table](https://pajak.go.id/index.php/id/penghasilan-tidak-kena-pajak) distinguishes payroll from the combined household. For a married woman, extra payroll allowances require the non-earning-husband certification route; that exception is not established here because the husband works.

| Conditional combined-household allowance | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Own allowance | IDR 54,000,000 |
| Marriage addition | IDR 4,500,000 |
| Wife-income combination addition | IDR 54,000,000 |
| One qualifying fully dependent child | IDR 4,500,000 |
| K/I/1 total when those conditions apply | IDR 117,000,000 |

Check status at the beginning of 2026. A child aged 3 is not the whole dependency test; do not claim the child twice. The dependent allowance has a maximum of 3 qualifying dependants.

| Family arrangement | What it means for ordinary/nonfinal design income |
|---|---|
| KK: joint family | Combine Rina's salary and design net income with her husband's ordinary income in the household return, normally filed through the husband; salary PPh 21 is a credit |
| PH: written separation agreement | Separate returns still start with combined spouses' ordinary net income; allocate computed tax proportionally, then apply each spouse's credits |
| MT: separate tax-rights election | The same combined-income/proportional approach; not two independent progressive bracket ladders |
| HB: court-ordered separation | Independent computation requires the court-separated conditions; nothing in this example establishes them |

A separate NPWP does not automatically create independent brackets. [Income Tax Act Article 8 and its explanation](https://pajak.go.id/id/undang-undang-nomor-36-tahun-2008) distinguish the limited exception for a wife's income **solely from one unrelated employer** from salary plus additional business income. The explanation expressly combines salary with that business income. Use current allowances and brackets, not the old amounts in the statutory examples. The [DJP married-taxpayer explanation](https://pajak.go.id/index.php/id/perhitungan-pajak-wanita-kawin) and [PH/MT Coretax guidance](https://pajak.go.id/coretaxpedia/lapor-spt-bagi-istri-yang-memilih-terpisah) support checking actual NPWP and DUK status, including the PH/MT family-profile setting.

For ordinary design income, let H be the husband's annual ordinary net income and P the design taxable net profit. Under these assumptions, household taxable income is **H + IDR 228,000,000 + P − IDR 117,000,000**, floored at zero and rounded down to full thousands. Apply the [HPP Article 17 marginal brackets](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/harmonisasi-peraturan-perpajakan), then subtract actual permitted credits and instalments:

| Annual taxable slice | Marginal rate |
|---|---:|
| First IDR 60,000,000 | 5% |
| Over IDR 60,000,000 through IDR 250,000,000 | 15% |
| Over IDR 250,000,000 through IDR 500,000,000 | 25% |
| Over IDR 500,000,000 through IDR 5,000,000,000 | 30% |
| Over IDR 5,000,000,000 | 35% |

## NPPN needs a valid notice, not just a small turnover

Projected receipts are below IDR 4,800,000,000, but that alone does not establish NPPN eligibility. The [2026 DJP notification announcement](https://pajak.go.id/id/pengumuman/penyampaian-pemberitahuan-nppn-melalui-coretax-djp-tahun-pajak-2026) sets **March 31, 2026** as the deadline for an existing calendar-year taxpayer. **It is not a clock starting when freelance work begins.** Only genuinely newly registered taxpayers get the earlier of 3 months from registration or year-end. Check the correct tax unit's acknowledgement. Without valid notice, the announcement treats the taxpayer as choosing bookkeeping; do not backdate an election.

The [NPPN regulation](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/norma-penghitungan-penghasilan-neto) and [PER 17/PJ/2015 Annex I, page 323, row 1233](https://pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2019-06/Lampiran%201_PER_17_PJ_2015.pdf) give **32%** for KLU 74100 special/graphic design in Jakarta. The row's regional ratios are 32%, 31% and 29%; Jakarta uses the first column. If that classification and timely notice are valid, IDR 60,000,000 × 32% gives **IDR 19,200,000** ordinary net income. The ratio is deemed profit, not a tax rate.

**A genuine conflict needs resolving:** the [DJP designer explainer](https://pajak.go.id/id/desainer) says 46% for the same KLU/location, which would produce IDR 27,600,000. The regulatory annex is the stronger basis for this conditional illustration; it does not establish Rina's entitlement. Confirm the actual contracts, activity classification and conflicting guidance before filing.

## Compare bookkeeping with the amended final-business rules

Read [PP 55/2022](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pp-55-tahun-2022/view) **as amended by PP 20/2026, effective April 22, 2026**, using the [amendment's official page](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pp-20-tahun-2026/view) and [operative PDF](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/d057ff82-50e7-4127-b66b-f704a36f071d/2026pp020.pdf). **Final-regime eligibility is unconfirmed.** Small receipts or a personal bank account do not decide it.

The amended IDR 4,800,000,000 test concerns business and independent-professional turnover, including applicable spouse/family and related individual-company aggregation, not employment salary. Prior-year tests, prior ordinary-tax elections and transition facts matter. The exclusions cover independent professional services with expanded expert/artist categories, but do not expressly name every designer. Personal creative work could be excluded depending on the service. Some transition provisions preserve specified existing treatment through 2026; do not assume a later-year exclusion immediately disqualifies every receipt in 2026. The former 7-year individual time limit was deleted with Article 59.

The [current official DJP explanation of PP 20/2026](https://pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2026-06/Mudah%20Mengerti%20Perubahan%20Pengaturan%20PPh%20Final%200%2C5%25%20Sesuai%20PP%20Nomor%2020%20Tahun%202026%20tentang%20perubahan%20atas%20PP%20Nomor%2055%20Tahun%202022%20tentang%20Penyesuaian%20Pengaturan%20di%20Bidang%20Pajak%20Penghasilan.pdf) confirms the retained first-IDR 500,000,000 individual exemption for qualifying annual business turnover. For KK, do not assume a second exemption just because the wife earns design receipts. Other qualifying family-business receipts may already use it; PH/MT rules also need checking.

| Method / condition | Design amount in this example | Tax treatment |
|---|---:|---|
| NPPN, valid notice and correct KLU, 32% | IDR 19,200,000 net | Joins ordinary income; progressive household calculation |
| Books, IDR 10,000,000 genuinely deductible costs | IDR 50,000,000 profit | Joins ordinary income; costs need evidence |
| Books, no deductible costs | IDR 60,000,000 profit | Joins ordinary income; not a final turnover tax |
| Eligible final business, IDR 60,000,000 receipts, exemption unused and available | IDR 0 final design tax | 0.5% × max(IDR 60,000,000 − IDR 500,000,000, 0); salary remains outside the final-business rate |

Taxing all IDR 60,000,000 at 0.5% would give IDR 300,000 and ignore the exemption **in that eligible scenario**. It is not a reason to assume eligibility. Final business receipts belong in their separate reporting section, not ordinary net profit. **Zero final design tax does not prove a zero annual household balance.** The precise wife-owned, below-exemption business-plus-salary interaction is not established for Rina by the official examples reviewed; leave that full-household branch unresolved.

If neither a valid NPPN notice nor final eligibility exists, use bookkeeping. Profit is receipts less actual legally deductible, documented costs—not personal household spending. A monthly cost budget does not prove deductibility. At IDR 40,800,000 costs, or 68% of receipts, book profit equals the 32% norm; bookkeeping gives lower profit only above that cost level. **An economic comparison cannot cure a missed notice.**

## See how the missing husband's facts change the balance

**Invented household illustration only:** give the husband annual net salary of IDR 228,000,000 and K/1 payroll PTKP of IDR 63,000,000, yielding IDR 18,750,000 salary tax. Add Rina's IDR 20,100,000 credit: total assumed credits are IDR 38,850,000. Assume K/I/1 PTKP of IDR 117,000,000, no other credits or instalments, and the KK ordinary-income calculation. **None of these husband inputs is a fact about a real spouse.**

| Valid method / invented assumptions | Design ordinary net | Household taxable income | Household annual tax | Balance after assumed credits |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| NPPN 32%, timely notice and correct KLU | IDR 19,200,000 | IDR 358,200,000 | IDR 58,550,000 | IDR 19,700,000 |
| Books, IDR 10,000,000 deductible costs | IDR 50,000,000 | IDR 389,000,000 | IDR 66,250,000 | IDR 27,400,000 |
| Books, no deductible costs | IDR 60,000,000 | IDR 399,000,000 | IDR 68,750,000 | IDR 29,900,000 |

PH/MT does not turn those household balances into Rina's personal bill: allocate combined tax by each spouse's ordinary net-income share, then apply their own credits. Actual credits may also exceed liability and create an overpayment. **The real annual balance remains unknown**, not an assessed underpayment within this table's range.

## Keep a reserve, then replace it with the real calculation

While gathering the missing information, a provisional reserve of **35% of design receipts** means **IDR 2,100,000 per active month**. That leaves IDR 3,900,000 before design costs. **This is a cash-planning choice, not a tax rate, prediction or safe ceiling.** It does not replace required PPh 25 instalments; the correct tax unit's existing obligations are unknown and must be checked.

| Normal month with design receipts, before BPJS and other deductions | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Salary plus design gross | IDR 24,000,000 |
| Employer PPh 21 deduction | −IDR 1,440,000 |
| Separate design-tax reserve | −IDR 2,100,000 |
| Illustrative design operating-cost envelope, not a proven deduction | −IDR 1,000,000 |
| Left for family spending/saving | IDR 19,460,000 |
| December equivalent with the assumed smaller payroll adjustment | IDR 20,600,000 |

| Reserve / salary-cash planning item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Target across 10 design months, less amounts already saved | IDR 21,000,000 |
| November–December reserve alone | IDR 4,200,000 |
| Catch-up if nothing was saved before November | IDR 16,800,000 |
| Ordinary salary cash after assumed withholding | IDR 16,560,000 |
| THR-month salary cash after assumed withholding | IDR 30,600,000 |
| Extra THR-month cash, if still available to retain | IDR 14,040,000 |
| December salary cash after assumed adjustment | IDR 17,700,000 |
| Design reserve plus retained extra THR cash | IDR 35,040,000 |

The combined buffer covers the invented household balances above, **not every possible household bill** or missed instalment. December's lower payroll deduction is not automatically spare spending money. Reconcile the reserve to actual receipts, costs, family income and credits before releasing it.

## Prepare the right return, not just a payment

The ordinary 2026 annual SPT deadline is **March 31, 2027**. The [official consolidated tax-law book, KUP Articles 3, 4, 7 and 9](https://pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN%202023%207.1_0.pdf) supports the filing deadline, financial statements for bookkeeping, payment before filing and separate penalties. **Pay PPh 29 before submitting the return; payment alone is not filing.** Do not assume a future waiver or extension.

| Step | What to prepare or check |
|---|---|
| Identify the filer | Confirm KK/PH/MT/HB, NIK/NPWP and Coretax DUK with the spouse; do not submit an employment-only return merely because it is prepopulated |
| Reconcile employment | Request 2026 A1/BPA1, corresponding to legacy 1721-A1; check both spouses' gross, net and credits, including Rina's TK/0 payroll status |
| Assemble design and household records | Monthly receipts, contracts, invoices, actual costs, valid norm acknowledgement if used, other withholding, assets/bank balances, debts and family details; bookkeeping requires appropriate financial statements, while NPPN still requires turnover records |
| Create and review the correct return | SPT → Buat Konsep SPT → PPh Orang Pribadi → 2026 annual period → Normal; answer business/profession questions, reconcile imported data without duplicating income or credits, include PH/MT combined calculation where applicable, and keep final receipts separate |
| Pay, sign and confirm submission | Reconcile allowed credits/instalments; use billing/deposit if underpaid, sign with valid Kode Otorisasi DJP/certificate, confirm SPT Dilaporkan and retain the emailed BPE |

The [DJP final-period certificate FAQ](https://pajak.go.id/coretaxpedia/bupot-pph-21-desember) supports A1/BPA1 rather than monthly BPMP. Ask payroll in January as preparation, **not a claimed statutory delivery deadline**; that precise deadline is unconfirmed here. The [general Coretax filing workflow](https://pajak.go.id/coretaxpedia/lapor-spt-tahunan-orang-pribadi) is a useful starting point, not a promise that the future 2026 form/screens will be identical. Exact method-specific attachment identifiers remain unconfirmed. The [current BPE guidance](https://pajak.go.id/coretaxpedia/bukti-penerimaan-elektronik-bpe-spt-tahunan) says BPE is emailed, not a PDF in Dokumen Saya; use the email icon in SPT Dilaporkan to resend if missing.

Late filing ordinarily carries an individual annual SPT fine of **IDR 100,000**, subject to statutory exceptions. Late annual payment can attract the applicable Minister-set monthly interest, counting part of a month as a full month, capped at 24 months under the relevant rule. The rate uses the benchmark plus 5 percentage points divided by 12 at the applicable commencement date. **It is not a permanent 2% monthly rate; the rate for a future delay in 2027 is unknown.** A filing fine does not replace filing or payment of the tax.

## Organize the existing tax unit before creating anything new

Do not open a second personal NPWP or create a company by default. First reconcile the existing NIK/NPWP, family status and correct activity/KLU record. Keep client receipts and costs separately organized. A bank account or entity does not establish final-business eligibility. Whether OSS/NIB or another licence is needed is a separate **unconfirmed licensing question**; this guide does not say no permit is required.

Bring the adviser the actual spouse income and credits, beginning-year dependency/residency, BPA1 and payroll adjustments, THR month, NPPN registration/notice evidence, contracts/KLU, final-regime elections/turnover/transition history, receipts/costs and PPh 25 records. Those gaps decide the calculation. Future filing screens, certificate timing, interest and concessions also need checking. The practical first step remains one folder with the family profile, both payroll records and any norm acknowledgement.
