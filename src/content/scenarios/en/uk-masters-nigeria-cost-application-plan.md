**Worried your savings will get you onto the course but not through it? For a Nigerian Computer Science graduate with a 2:1 equivalent, an outside-London, one-year MSc can be a realistic academic goal. A £32,000 family budget needs more care. Start with the information dated 2026-11-05, then confirm your own admission and costs before paying.**

**Tuition plus living is not the whole bill. Even the lowest older-fee example needs a prudent cash allowance of £32,583, leaving a £583 gap against £32,000. Do not fill it with hoped-for part-time pay, an unawarded scholarship or a future Graduate-route job. Get the money in place before committing.**

**Use this as a starting point, not immigration or legal advice; a qualified adviser can help you check your own circumstances.**

## Start with what your family’s money must cover

Aim for September 2027 entry, studying alone outside London, without an optional placement. This information dates from 2026-11-05, not your application day. Use it to narrow your choices, then check the terms of your own offer.

Does £32,000 mean tuition and living only, or all your available cash? Before paying a deposit, get the written September 2027 fee, exact course dates, qualification and English decisions, deposit and refund terms, and CAS conditions. Price housing for the full course too. An older fee, course title or general country page is not an offer.

## Leave room for living, not just the visa minimum

The [Student money guidance](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa/money) gives outside-London maintenance of £1,171/month for up to 9 months, or £10,539. [HC 584, Implementation and APP ST9](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/statement-of-changes-to-the-immigration-rules-hc-584-3-september-2026/statement-of-changes-to-the-immigration-rules-hc-584-3-september-2026-accessible) sets a change already made but not yet in force: £1,203/month, or £10,827 for 9 months, for applications on/after 2026-11-30. That is the position dated 2026-11-05. Check the amount again before arranging your funds.

For 2027, start with £1,203 × 12 = £14,436 for living in each shortlisted city. This stretches a visa minimum across the year; it is not a rent survey, university quote or promise that you will have enough. Include summer and dissertation living; IHS is separate. With £32,000, the tuition-and-living-only fee ceiling is £17,564; the older £14,052 living estimate left £17,948. Neither leaves money for travel, visa or reserves.

Avoid counting money twice. A tuition deposit pays part of your tuition; it is not an extra fee. A refundable housing deposit ties up cash but is not another rent charge. Maintenance proof is not a fee either. If your savings are NGN, test exchange-rate changes and whether you can transfer the money when needed. A GBP budget does not tell you how much naira will be enough. Get actual rent, bills, food, transport and dissertation-period costs before accepting.

## Get the visa money and timing ready together

The [Student visa overview](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa) gives a £558 overseas fee, an earliest application point 6 months before the course, and a usual 3-week overseas decision time. Check again for 2027: neither price nor turnaround is guaranteed. For a course over 6 months, you may arrive up to 1 month early, but never before your permission starts. Wait for CAS readiness and follow the university’s actual cut-offs.

The [IHS charging guide](https://www.gov.uk/healthcare-immigration-application/how-much-pay) gives students a £776/year rate. [Appendix Student](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-student) normally allows 1 month before and 4 months after a course lasting at least 12 months. About 17 months of permission therefore means 1.5 annual charges, or £1,164. That is an example, not your checkout amount. Use exact CAS and visa dates; permission exceeding 18 months can enter a different charging band.

Keep enough for outstanding first-year tuition on CAS plus the applicable maintenance amount. Deduct only payments you can validly prove. The required balance must stay in place for 28 consecutive days, ending within 31 days before you apply. Nigeria was not on the differential-evidence nationality list in the dated guidance. Prepare the documents rather than expecting a waiver.

Use the [financial evidence guide](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/financial-evidence-for-student-and-child-student-route-applicants) and [Appendix Finance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-finance) to check acceptable, accessible accounts. Parent or legal-guardian funds need relationship proof and written consent. OANDA conversion uses the application date, so keep a currency margin and do not let the balance fall below the requirement. The cap for paid sponsor accommodation was £1,529, rising to £1,570 from 2026-11-30; private-landlord payments do not qualify. This budget does not rely on that deduction.

Here is how to avoid counting tuition twice: £17,000 tuition − £5,000 paid and evidenced + £10,827 maintenance = £22,827 bank evidence under the forthcoming rate. Under the old rate it is £22,539. This is an example, not a university deposit policy. Keep separate spendable cash for visa and IHS. An unpaid deposit cannot be deducted; paid tuition needs CAS confirmation or a sponsor receipt. Check the rules and exact CAS amounts before the holding period starts.

If you live in Nigeria, check the [TB requirement](https://www.gov.uk/tb-test-visa): it generally applies to a UK stay of at least 6 months if you have lived in a listed country for at least 6 months and were living there within the last 6 months. Total residence and recent residence are separate conditions. Use an [approved Nigeria clinic](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/tuberculosis-test-for-a-uk-visa-clinics-in-nigeria/tuberculosis-testing-in-nigeria), not any hospital. Certificates normally last 6 months from X-ray. Follow-up can take up to 8 weeks, and some expire sooner. May–June 2027 is a useful starting window for a summer application; confirm booking, price and validity first.

## Bringing family or staying to work? Check each route separately

**Budget for yourself, not a family move.** The [dependant rules](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa/family-members) generally do not allow a partner or children on an ordinary privately funded taught MSc. Exceptions include government sponsorship for a course over 6 months, or qualifying full-time postgraduate study lasting at least 9 months which, for starts on/after 2024-01-01, is a doctorate or research-based higher degree. The title “MSc” alone does not qualify. Family relocation costs are not included.

**Plan from the published rule, not a promised policy change.** The [Graduate overview](https://www.gov.uk/graduate-visa) and [Appendix Graduate GR8.1](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-graduate) give non-doctoral applications on/before 2026-12-31 a 2-year period; on/after 2027-01-01, it is 18 months. Doctoral graduates get 3 years. Starting a one-year MSc in September 2027 would usually mean applying in 2028. Work from 18 months, not a guaranteed 2 years.

The [Budget 2026 announcement](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/budget-2026-document/budget-2026-html), published 2026-10-28, proposed 2 years for high-compliance universities with strong labour-market outcomes. **It had not yet been put into GR8.1, and no qualifying-university list or operative or transitional terms had been established. Do not assume your shortlisted university qualifies, or replace the published 18-month period with the announcement.** Check again before enrolling and graduating.

Graduate permission is not automatic. You need a qualifying completed UK course, the provider’s completion notification and an application inside the UK before Student permission expires, plus the other eligibility conditions. The route cannot itself be extended and does not promise settlement. At the dated rates, £937 + 1.5 × £1,035 IHS = £2,489.50 for an example 18 months. This is outside the MSc budget and not a 2028 quote. Some scholarships require consent. Qualifying degree-level students with an eligible sponsor can normally work up to 20 hours/week in term time. Check your permission and dissertation term dates; summer is not automatically a work holiday. Do not rely on finding work or earning enough.

| June–July 2027 | Aim to apply with the 28-day period ending within 31 days. Pay visa and IHS, then follow identity and biometrics instructions. A usual 3-week service is not a reason to book non-refundable travel. |

| September 2027–September 2028 | Enrol, track spending and keep dissertation living money aside. Follow attendance and work rules. Check Graduate eligibility again; apply after completion notification but before Student permission expires. |

These are suggested windows, not university admissions deadlines. Start with Shared’s approved courses, then seek an academically suitable Lincoln offer in time for Global Leaders. Treat its conditional Nigeria discount as a fallback. Keep Wrexham as the lowest older-fee comparison, but get a new quote and follow the representative-only policy. If confirmed funding and accessible savings still leave a gap, pause before paying: seek lower costs or more money rather than hoping it will disappear.

**Before paying or applying, check your actual offer and fee, housing, visa and IHS costs, maintenance, dependant, work and Graduate rules, and scholarship terms. You can start comparing now without having every answer. Before making a commitment, make sure the answers and the money are there.**
