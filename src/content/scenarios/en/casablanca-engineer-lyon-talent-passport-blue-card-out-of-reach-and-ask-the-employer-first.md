You have an offer in Lyon at EUR 52,000 gross, a wife who is a pharmacist and a four-year-old son. Which permit can you actually get, and will one salary carry the family? **Short answer: the Blue Card needs EUR 59,373 gross a year, EUR 7,373 more than the offer, so it is out of reach. The realistic choice is a "talent" card, and which kind depends on two facts nobody has confirmed yet: whether the employer is an innovative company, and where the master's degree was awarded.** After social contributions the offer is about EUR 3,427.06 a month, and the example budgets leave EUR 818.31 or EUR 380.31 a month with Salma earning nothing.

This guide follows Youssef (32, software engineer, EUR 52,000 gross offer, start in spring 2027), Salma (30, pharmacist) and their son (4). It was written on 8 November 2026 from public pages only; nothing was filed and nobody was contacted. "Unconfirmed" marks anything that did not come from an official page, and "planning assumption" marks our own figure. **It is general information, not immigration, legal or tax advice.** The source materials are not published.

## The recommendation and the first step

**At EUR 52,000 gross, the Blue Card is out of reach.** The official threshold is EUR 59,373 gross a year, which is 1.5 times the EUR 39,582 reference salary. The realistic choice is the "talent" card, in one of two forms:

- **"Talent – entreprise innovante"** is the likely route, but only if the Lyon company is a young innovative company (JEI) or is recognised as innovative by the economy ministry, and only if Youssef's duties are linked to its R&D project. The minimum is EUR 39,582 gross, and the official page lists no French-diploma condition for this route. It is the best fit if the employer qualifies.
- **"Talent – salarié qualifié"** needs a master-level diploma obtained in France, or a diploma labelled by the CGE (Conférence des grandes écoles) at level 1. The service-public page says "obtained in France", so a Moroccan degree does not satisfy it as written.
- **If neither applies,** the two talent routes are closed at this salary. Either the contract is negotiated up to at least EUR 59,373, in which case the Blue Card works with a 3-year higher-education diploma or 5 years' experience (Youssef reports 8 years' experience; that was not checked), or the employer must use a different work-permit route that this guide does not cover. In that case the "talent-famille" card for Salma, which attaches to a talent card, would probably not exist either (our reading of the service-public page; family rights under other permits were not researched).

**First action, before anything is filed: ask the employer in writing** (a) whether it is a JEI or a ministry-recognised innovative company and whether Youssef's role is linked to its R&D, (b) which permit it expects to use, and (c) the contract type and length. The card runs for the contract length, up to 4 years, so a permanent contract (CDI) gives the longest card. Also check where the master's degree was awarded.

**Counterintuitive finding:** a common rule of thumb (our characterisation, not a sourced claim) is "Blue Card for skilled engineers, talent passport for everyone else". At this salary it is the reverse. The Blue Card's pay bar (EUR 59,373) is 50% higher than the talent card's (EUR 39,582), and its main extra, mobility to other EU states, is irrelevant for a plan based in Lyon (our reading). The "salarié qualifié" label sounds easier but needs a French diploma, so for a Moroccan-trained engineer the "innovative company" label is the one that matters.

**Limits to keep in mind.** The rules below are as read on the service-public pages (the main permit page was last verified on 1 June 2026, the family page on 1 May 2026). A decree of 26 October 2026 (n° 2026-1001) changes notification and implicit-refusal delays and some Blue Card rules from 30 October 2026. The pages say they are being updated and the new values are not published, so re-check before filing. The EUR 39,582 reference comes from an order (arrêté) of 21 August 2025; no 2026 or 2027 update was found, but the Légifrance text could not be read. Thresholds are revised by order, so verify them again when the visa file is opened.

## How the family comes, and whether Salma can work

- **Route:** "famille accompagnante", with no family-reunification procedure. A spouse (married, over 18) and minor children get a multi-year talent-famille card, valid for the remaining life of Youssef's talent card. From abroad the family applies for a long-stay visa at the French consulate, then requests the card on ANEF after arrival.
- **Can Salma work? Yes.** The card authorises any salaried or self-employed activity, her employer needs no work authorisation and is exempt from the foreign-employment tax.
- **But pharmacy is a regulated profession.** The card lets her take a job; it does not recognise her diploma. To practise as a pharmacist she needs the procedure in the next section. What she may do in the meantime (for example work in a pharmacy in a non-pharmacist role) was not confirmed from any official page. **Next step:** ask the Ordre or the CNG; do not assume.
- **Cost:** EUR 350 in stamps per talent-famille card (EUR 50 + EUR 300). Whether a 4-year-old needs a card or a different document is **unverified**: the page speaks of children aged 16 to 18 who want to work and adults who entered as minors.
- **Renewal:** after 5 years' residence on talent-famille, renewal cannot be refused and is for 4 years.
- **Republic-principles engagement:** a signed engagement must be attached to every first or renewal application. It is separate from the integration contract (CIR).
- **Health cover:** Youssef is covered from the first hour of work (PUMa). Salma, if not working, waits 3 months after arrival and needs her residence permit. A new contribution for non-working PUMa members was legislated in the 2026 social-security law, with the amount still pending. The son is attached to a parent. A collective employer mutuelle may be compulsory to join. Whether the "family covered after 3 months" wording applies to a non-EEA family is unclear. **Next step:** ask the CPAM.

## Salma's pharmacy diploma in France

A pharmacist with a diploma from outside the EU or EEA cannot simply register. The official path (CNG, the Centre national de gestion, and the Ordre national des pharmaciens) is:

- **EVC exam** (épreuves de vérification des connaissances), the first step of the authorisation procedure: two anonymous written tests of 2 hours each; a mark of 6/20 or less on any test is eliminatory; places are limited; maximum 4 attempts; registration is free. Registration for 2026 ran from 17 June to 21 July 2026 and the exam is held in Rungis. The 2027 dates are not published yet, so her first realistic attempt is the 2027 session (our inference).
- **Proof of French:** TCF, TEF or DELF at B2 or higher, or an attestation that her whole pharmacy course was taught in French. The page exempts nationals of French-speaking countries where French is an official language; Morocco's official languages are Arabic and Amazigh, so assume she needs the B2 proof unless her faculty can attest French-medium studies (whether the exemption covers Morocco was not found).
- **Documents:** diploma(s) and speciality diploma, translated by a sworn translator (or consular-certified if filing from abroad).
- **Hospital post** (parcours de consolidation des compétences, PCC): after passing, a full-time paid hospital post allocated through a national choice procedure; a Lyon post is not guaranteed. The CNG page quotes 2 years for doctors and 1 year for dentists and midwives; for pharmacists it mentions "two years of paid hospital duties" for hospital pharmacy, and the legal length was not confirmed.
- **Decision and roll:** a national commission decides, then registration on the Ordre's roll is compulsory (section D for a pharmacy employee, section H for hospital).

**Realistic reading (our inference, not an official statement):** several years before independent practice, with the hospital placement possibly outside Lyon. Deferral is possible only up to 18 months and only for pregnancy, health or exceptional family circumstances; the rules make no allowance for a spouse's job or young children. **Plan the family's first years assuming Salma's income is zero or non-pharmacy.** Not found: the Ordre's registration fees, the 2027 exam dates, and the fee and delay of the diploma-comparability attestation (France Éducation international), whose site blocked automated reading.

## From EUR 52,000 gross to net, and income tax with the family quotient

Social contributions 2026, for a full year at EUR 52,000, a private-sector executive (cadre) with no mutuelle or prévoyance. The Social Security ceiling (PASS) for 2026 is EUR 48,060, so the pay above it (EUR 3,940) falls in "tranche 2". CSG and CRDS apply to 98.25% of gross, which is EUR 51,090.

A bank or transfer service will give a worse rate than the indicative mid rate.

| Per part | 38,345.66 ÷ 2.5 | 15,338.26 |
| Tax on 2.5 parts | 11% × (15,338.26 − 11,600) × 2.5 | 1,028.02 |
| Check with 2 parts | Tax would be 1,666.02, so the family advantage is 638.00 (the cap of EUR 1,807 per half-part is not binding) | 1,666.02 |
| Décote (couple) | 1,483 − 45.25% × 1,028.02 | 1,017.82 |
| **Tax after décote** | 1,028.02 − 1,017.82 | **10.20** |

So a one-earner family at this salary owes almost no income tax in a full year (EUR 10.20 a year, EUR 0.85 a month). The first year, with arrival in spring 2027 and nine months' pay, is lower still: gross EUR 39,000, net taxable about EUR 31,955, about EUR 28,759 after the 10% allowance, EUR 11,504 per part, which is below the start of the 11% band (EUR 11,600 in the scale used), so zero tax by this arithmetic (our calculation, with the Social Security ceiling prorated at 75% for the contribution estimate).

**Caveats.**

- The scale for 2027 income is not yet known; the 2027 finance bill reportedly raises the scale by about 2.1% (press snippets only, unconfirmed, not voted).
- Tax residence in the arrival year, the household's tax status, and the France–Morocco tax treaty were not researched. Salary earned before arrival, or in Morocco, may be handled differently.
- Withholding at source starts with the employer's "neutral rate" until a personalised rate is passed on. The 2026 neutral-rate grid was not read, so early payslips may withhold more than the final tax; the excess is refunded after the annual return.
- If Salma starts earning, tax rises.

## School for a 4-year-old

- **École maternelle:** schooling is compulsory from age 3 and enrolment in a public maternelle is free. Enrolment is in two steps: the town hall (mairie) first, with a certificate naming the school, then the school director.
- **Lyon newcomers** can pre-register all year. The city asks for a recent proof of address (lease or utility bill under 3 months), proof of parental authority and the vaccination record, so the lease normally comes before school enrolment (our reading). If the sector school is full, Lyon's pre-registration page says the child goes on a waiting list.
- **Canteen:** priced by a municipal family quotient built from the French tax notice of 2024 income. A family with no French notice pays the maximum, EUR 7.30 per lunch, about EUR 124 a month (17 lunches × EUR 7.30) until a quotient can be computed.
- **Other Lyon charges (2026-27 grid):** morning care is free; after-school sessions cost EUR 2 to EUR 6 (maximum 10 a year); Wednesday-morning workshops cost EUR 38 to EUR 212 a year. Villeurbanne tariffs and the holiday-centre grid were not found.

- **Official ceiling (rent control):** Lyon and Villeurbanne have rent control. The Métropole's dataset (2025-26 grid, parsed by us) caps base rent for a 65 m² unfurnished T3 at about EUR 812–1,060 (Lyon 3e) and EUR 812–1,040 (7e and Villeurbanne) excluding charges; furnished about EUR 923–1,202 (3e) and EUR 923–1,170 (7e, Villeurbanne). The cap depends on the exact zone and building age. The Métropole's address-based simulator, linked from service-public, is the official check. A "complément de loyer" can sit on top only in special cases. The 2023 prefectural order was annulled in October 2025; the Métropole's press release says the rule never stopped applying. A new 2025 order was reportedly issued but was not read, so **check the current one before signing.**
- **Deposit:** at most one month's rent excluding charges for an unfurnished flat. Agency tenant-fee rules were not researched; the three ads show EUR 851 to EUR 912.
- **Guarantee:** Visale (Action Logement, free guarantor) allows rent up to EUR 1,575 including charges for people over 30. Whether a newly arrived foreign worker qualifies, and whether it can be requested before arrival, was not verified. Expect landlords to ask for strong proof of income and a guarantee; a lease signed from abroad is a practical hurdle.
- **CAF housing aid (APL, ALF, ALS):** foreigners qualify with a valid residence permit; resources are counted over the last 12 months; entitlement starts the month after the claim and is paid on the 5th. A private non-conventionné flat falls under ALF or ALS. At EUR 52,000 gross with one earner, a large award is unlikely (our judgement, not computed; only the official CAF simulator can answer). The budget below assumes EUR 0 of aid.
