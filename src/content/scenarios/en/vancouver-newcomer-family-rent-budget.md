**One $82,000 salary can support a careful, no-car family budget—but paid child care can push it into deficit. At $2,800 rent, estimated take-home of $5,210/month leaves $465 before paid care. Add an $800 care fee and you are short by $335; at $3,000 rent, that shortfall becomes $535. I would search around $2,600–$2,800, choose the train line around the job, and keep arrival savings separate. Do not sign at the rent ceiling on the hope of a $10/day place or an unapproved benefit.**

This plan is for a Filipino family moving from Manila: two adults and one 4-year-old, new permanent residents arriving May 1, 2027, with one parent taking a Burnaby job. All money is CAD. The salary estimate is a **2026 tax-year proxy, not verified 2027 payroll**. These prices, advertisements and rules were checked on November 6, 2026; check them again for your arrival. No home, landlord or future vacancy has been verified, and nothing has been booked or applied for.

## Put the monthly decision before the house hunt

The baseline assumes one parent cares for the child at home, mostly home cooking and no car. These are allowances, not household-specific quotations. Ask payroll about actual take-home and both parents about care coverage before setting a hard rent limit.

| Monthly item | CAD/month | What to check |
|---|---:|---|
| Rent | $2,800 | Search target; $3,000 rent adds $200 |
| Electricity and heat | $100 | May be lower with heat included; electric heating or a percentage of a house bill may be higher |
| Home internet | $70 | Installation and promotion expiry can cost more |
| Two mobile plans | $70 | SIM-only; no phone financing |
| Groceries | $850 | Mostly home cooking; no regular restaurant pot |
| Transit | $220 | One sampled two-zone pass $156.70 plus $63.30 for the other adult; child free |
| Tenant insurance | $35 | Not a quote; compare cover and deductible |
| Laundry, clothing and household items | $150 | Coin laundry may add up |
| Child activities and miscellaneous | $150 | Not daycare |
| Medical and dental out-of-pocket reserve | $100 | Not insurance; employer benefits unknown |
| Emergency savings | $200 | A modest reserve, not strong protection against job loss |
| Total before paid child care | $4,745 | $465 left against $5,210 net |
| Same plan at $3,000 rent | $4,945 | $265 left before care |

Two adults commuting on two-zone passes would cost $313.40 at the sampled fares, **$93.40 more** than this transit pot. There are no car payments, debts, remittances, flights, immigration costs or large purchases in the baseline. If your family needs any of those, add them before deciding the rent works.

| Paid care added once to that baseline | Left at $2,800 rent | Left at $3,000 rent |
|---|---:|---:|
| $200/month, only with a confirmed funded place at that fee | $265 | $65 |
| $800/month fallback estimate; no ACCB counted | −$335 | −$535 |
| $1,200/month higher fallback estimate | −$735 | −$935 |

**Bridge medical insurance is also extra:** provisionally allow $300–$700/month for the family during May–July, then obtain an actual quote and check exclusions. Even without paid care, that would reduce the $465 baseline margin to between $165 left and a $235 shortfall while those premiums apply. Lower rent helps, but does not turn an unfunded full-time-care plan into a comfortable one. At $3,000, rent alone is roughly 44% of gross salary and 58% of the estimated net.

Canada Child Benefit may be available to eligible permanent-resident families, but its amount and start depend on CRA assessment and family income. **$0 CCB and $0 ACCB are counted here.** The temporary-resident 18-month rule is not a general PR waiting rule. Check [CRA's CCB eligibility](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/child-family-benefits/canada-child-benefit/who-apply.html) for your own family rather than spending a hoped-for payment.

### Where the salary estimate comes from

The full-year model uses BC employment and residence, ordinary basic personal credits, the Canada employment amount, CPP/CPP2 and EI. It excludes spouse credits, RRSP deductions, workplace pension, union and benefit deductions, dependant-related credits and child benefits. Part-year newcomer residence, foreign income and payroll timing can change the result; ask the employer for a 2027 estimate.

| Annual calculation, using 2026 inputs | CAD |
|---|---:|
| Gross salary | $82,000 |
| Estimated federal income tax | $9,636.20 |
| Estimated BC income tax | $4,178.76 |
| CPP including CPP2 | $4,526.45 |
| EI | $1,123.07 |
| Net salary after those deductions | $62,535.52 |
| Monthly average, before rounding | $5,211.29 |
| Monthly budget figure, rounded down | $5,210 |

For a reproducible check: base CPP is $3,519.45, first additional CPP $711, and CPP2 is ($82,000 − $74,600) × 4% = $296. Deducting the enhanced CPP of $711 + $296 gives taxable income of $80,993. Federal tax is $58,523 × 14% + ($80,993 − $58,523) × 20.5% − 14% × ($16,452 basic amount + $1,501 employment amount + $3,519.45 base CPP + $1,123.07 EI) = $9,636.20, rounded. BC tax is $50,363 × 5.6% + ($80,993 − $50,363) × 7.7% − 5.6% × ($13,216 basic amount + $3,519.45 base CPP + $1,123.07 EI) = $4,178.76, rounded.

Those inputs come from the dated [CRA rate tables](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/tax-rates-brackets/current-year.html) and [T4032-BC payroll guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/payroll/t4032-payroll-deductions-tables/t4032bc-july/t4032bc-july-general-information.html). The model uses BC's **5.6% annual lowest rate**, not July's **6.14% catch-up withholding rate**. This is our calculation, not a CRA payroll result or tax advice. An eligible spouse amount might improve it, but is not counted.

## Choose the line, then the neighbourhood

For a job near **Metrotown, Royal Oak or Edmonds**, begin with the Expo corridor: south Burnaby or station-near New Westminster. For **Brentwood, Gilmore, Lake City or Production Way**, begin with the Millennium corridor, including north Burnaby and Burquitlam. Burnaby is not one station; an inexpensive home on the wrong line may cost you the daycare pickup.

The rail minutes below come from [TransLink's station table](https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/skytrain/skytrain_station_travel_times.pdf). Door-to-door ranges are estimates assuming a home within about 10 minutes' walk and a workplace near the destination station. Add workplace buses, care detours and shift-time waits. Check the [line map](https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/skytrain) and run the actual addresses through the [Trip Planner](https://www.translink.ca/trip-planner).

Look beyond new towers: older apartments and suites may fit the budget. Inspect heat, damp, street noise and the actual walk. A Metrotown address is not automatically station-side. |

About 30–55 min door to door. | Burquitlam is the practical first look for a Millennium Line job. Farther east works if the rent/space trade is worth it. The dated [fare table](https://www.translink.ca/transit-fares/pricing-and-fare-zones) lists adult monthly passes at **$117.20 for one zone** and **$156.70 for two zones**. Burnaby–New Westminster can stay in one zone; Vancouver–Burnaby or Surrey/Coquitlam–Burnaby usually needs two on weekday daytime rail. Zones are not a count of cities. Children 12 and under ride free. Recheck your route, fares and service for May 2027.

## No Canadian credit: explain the gap with a tidy application

Lead with the job offer, not an apology. Name both adults as applicants and list all occupants honestly. A useful pack has:

- A short introduction, desired move-in and permanent-resident arrival date, without invented local history.
- The $82,000 employment offer, start date and any probation or conditions; share an employer contact only with permission. Add pay statements when available.
- A recent bank letter or redacted balance statement showing accessible savings. Hide account numbers and unrelated transactions. Philippine credit and rent-payment history may help, but the landlord need not treat them as Canadian credit.
- A consenting Manila landlord and employer reference, with contact details and time zone; offer translations if needed. A willing Canadian guarantor may help, but check their written obligations first.
- Appropriate ID shown securely to a verified recipient, rather than passport copies, SIN and full banking details sent to every ad. Credit checks need consent; SIN should be optional. See the dated [BC privacy guidance](https://www.oipc.bc.ca/documents/guidance-documents/2202).

A simple cover note:

> We are a family of three moving from Manila as permanent residents on May 1, 2027. One parent has accepted a Burnaby role at $82,000 annually. We do not yet have Canadian credit. We can provide the offer, consenting references and evidence of savings. We are looking for a long-term two-bedroom home.

Do not lead with six or twelve months of prepaid rent: you need that cash to settle, and a scam becomes more expensive. Missing Canadian credit does not allow a landlord to demand an oversized security deposit or application fee under the covered-tenancy rules below.

### Before any money leaves your account

The dated [RCMP scam guidance](https://rcmp.ca/en/bc/richmond/news/2025/08/richmond-rcmp-warns-public-about-rental-deposit-scams) advises physically viewing before sending money. From Manila, a short landing stay and local viewings are the safer route. A trusted person's viewing can help, but does not replace checking who controls the unit.

Look up the management company's own published number independently; cross-check the address, photos and rent. A stolen photo, lockbox access or signed PDF alone does not prove authority. Pause at pressure to pay now, an absent landlord, an unusually low price, crypto, gift cards or transfer demands for an unseen home. Do not follow an unverified advertiser's credit-check/payment link.

Verify the home and authority, then sign a sound agreement, then pay a lawful deposit with a receipt naming the property, landlord and amount. There is no application review or processing fee. [WelcomeBC's housing guide](https://www.welcomebc.ca/start-your-life-in-b-c/living-in-bc/housing) is another useful starting point. No landlord has been contacted or ownership checked for these ads.

## Keep the deposit and tenancy rules beside the rent

These are dated rules for **covered BC residential tenancies**, not legal advice or a guarantee for every accommodation type. Sharing a kitchen or bathroom with the owner falls outside ordinary Residential Tenancy Act coverage. A self-contained suite is a different question; a temporary holiday stay may also have different protection. Check the actual arrangement before assuming the same rights apply.

| Rule | What it means before signing |
|---|---|
| Security deposit: maximum half one month's starting rent | Up to $1,400 at $2,800 rent, or $1,500 at $3,000. First month's rent is separate; a full extra “last month” security deposit is not the standard. No application-processing fee. See [RTB deposits and fees](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/starting-a-tenancy/deposits-fees). |
| Allowed pet: maximum another half month as damage deposit | At $2,800 rent, up to $1,400 extra. One pet deposit, not one per animal; ordinary pets can be restricted and there is no extra monthly pet fee. Guide/service dogs have different treatment. Follow [specialist RTB pet guidance](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/during-a-tenancy/pets-and-tenancy), not a general housing page's shorthand. |
| Written agreement and standard terms | Both sides sign/date; landlord supplies a copy within 21 days. Put rent, term, occupants, utilities, laundry, parking and storage in writing. Use [RTB-1](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb1.pdf) or a form containing required standard terms. Illegal terms do not override the Act. See [RTB agreements](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/starting-a-tenancy/tenancy-agreements). |
| Annual rent increases | Dated limits: 2026 cap 2.3%; 2027 cap 2.2%. Normally at least 12 months from first payable rent or the last lawful increase, plus three full months' written notice on RTB-7. They do not cap a new advertisement's asking rent; separate above-limit processes exist. See [RTB increases](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/rent-rtb/rent-increases). |
| Your likely first ordinary increase is a 2028 issue | If rent is first payable May 1, 2027, the earliest ordinary increase is May 1, 2028. The 2028 cap is not established here; do not carry 2.2% forward automatically. |
| Inspections and deposit return | Complete signed move-in and move-out reports, with dated photos and receipts. Generally the landlord must return deposits with applicable interest or apply to retain them within 15 days after the later of tenancy end and receipt of your written forwarding address, unless a statutory exception applies. They cannot simply invent a deduction. |
| Fixed term does not automatically mean move out | Most continue month-to-month unless ended lawfully; narrow vacate-clause exceptions exist. Leaving a fixed term early can cost money. Ordinary month-to-month notice is at least one full rental month; that is not a free cancellation right for a fixed lease. |

The [Residential Tenancy Act](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/02078_01), particularly sections 4, 38 and 42, supports the coverage, return and increase-timing distinctions. The consolidation inspected was current to October 27, 2026. Recheck the rules before your May commitment.

## Child care: a low fee helps only when there is a place

At a participating **$10 a Day ChildCareBC** centre, enrolled families receive that centre's lower fee. It is not a voucher for any daycare. Operators manage their own waitlists, and the province says many families cannot access a space. Citizenship is not a programme restriction; your practical hurdles are the child's age group, hours, location and an actual place. Read the dated [family FAQ](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/family-social-supports/caring-for-young-children/childcarebc-programs/10-a-day-childcarebc-centres/faq-families).

Use the [participating-centre list](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/family-social-supports/caring-for-young-children/childcarebc-programs/10-a-day-childcarebc-centres/10-a-day-centres-list) and [BC child care map](https://maps.gov.bc.ca/ess/hm/ccf/) to find providers along the home–work route. Participation or licensed capacity is **not a vacancy**. No May 2027 place is confirmed for this family. Do not choose a lease because a nearby funded centre appears on the map.

| Fee or support | How to budget it honestly |
|---|---|
| Funded operator example: $10/day, maximum $200/month | [VSOCC](https://vsocc.org/childcare-programs/waitlist-enrollment-fees/) publishes this for current funded programmes and requires a BC address and proof to apply. Its enrolment priorities are not simply first-come-first-served. New centres opening after January 2026 are not currently eligible for that funding; fees are not yet set. Neither a place nor a universal May 2027 price is promised. |
| Non-$10/day operator example: $579.76/month | [Burnaby Neighbourhood House](https://burnabynh.ca/programs-and-services/child-care-programs/early-years-program-0-5-years-old/) lists five-day care for ages 3–5 at this fee, already after fee reduction, for May 2026–April 2027 only. It expires immediately before arrival. May 2027 fee and space are unknown; do not subtract another provider reduction. |
| CCFRI provider fee reduction | For ages 3 to kindergarten, up to $545/month in group care or $500 in family care at participating licensed providers. Not income-tested; families do not apply. Part-time reduction is prorated. Preschool's up-to-$95/month reduction is different, and short sessions do not cover a working day. These are reductions, not final fees or a confirmed May 2027 funding promise; ask about meals and extra hours. See [official family rates](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/family-social-supports/caring-for-young-children/childcarebc-programs/child-care-fee-reduction-initiative-provider-opt-in-status/information-for-families). |
| Affordable Child Care Benefit, ACCB | Income-tested and separately applied for. The broad range generally reaches incomes up to $111,000, but an $82,000 salary alone proves neither eligibility nor an amount. BC residency/address, eligible status, both parents' circumstances and qualifying care/activity matter. Looking for work may qualify under programme rules; a non-working parent does not guarantee an award. Budget $0 until approved. The calculation process permits an income declaration as an alternative to CRA income information; follow the [official eligibility process](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/family-social-supports/caring-for-young-children/childcarebc-programs/child-care-benefit/information-for-families/eligibility-estimate-funding). |
| Fallback: $800–$1,200/month | Planning estimate after any applicable provider fee reduction but before ACCB, not a centre quote or a price ceiling. A full-price non-participating place can cost more. Ask for the exact age-group fee, hours, closures, deposit and optional charges. |

Work out who covers the child while the employed parent starts work and the other looks for a job. Investigate non-$10/day care too. Never deduct CCFRI again from a fee already described as net.

Check the birthday against [BC kindergarten rules](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/education-training/k-12/support/full-day-kindergarten): entry starts in September of the year the child turns five. If that is 2027, ask the chosen district about September 2027 registration; if 2028, allow longer for preschool-age care. If already school-eligible on arrival, ask about joining then. School still leaves before/after-school, commute and holiday gaps.

## Arrival cash is a separate pot

At $2,800 rent, first month plus maximum security deposit is **$4,200**; at $3,000 it is **$4,500**. The refundable deposit ties up cash—it is not monthly spending. Keep liquid savings for a delayed first full paycheque and a temporary stay while you verify a home locally.

| First-month cash need at $2,800 rent | CAD | Basis and limit |
|---|---:|---|
| First rent and maximum security deposit | $4,200 | $2,800 + $1,400; no pet assumed |
| Temporary family stay, 14 nights | $2,100–$3,220 | Estimated $150–$230/night all-in, not booked; get a cancellable full-price quote including taxes and cleaning. Deliberately overlaps first rent as a verification/delay buffer |
| Beds, furniture, cookware and linens | $1,500–$2,500 | Basic new and carefully checked used items; delivery may add cost |
| First-month non-rent living costs | $1,745 | Baseline excluding rent and $200 savings; includes groceries, utilities, transit and tenant insurance. No daycare; do not add those rows twice |
| Airport and initial luggage transport | $100 | Estimate; destination and vehicle size matter |
| Connections, moving and setup | $150 | Estimate; strata elevator/move-in fees may exceed it |
| First-month bridge medical insurance | $300–$700 | Placeholder, not a quote; pre-existing conditions may be excluded and several months may be payable upfront |
| Unallocated arrival buffer | $1,000 | Not a replacement for an emergency fund |
| Total | $11,095–$13,615 | Approximately $11,100–$13,600; use the unrounded upper amount when reserving cash |

At $3,000 rent, add **$300**, not $500: first rent and half-month deposit rise together, and the non-rent row stays unchanged. That is roughly **$11,400–$13,900**. Keep more if the temporary stay stretches or premiums are due upfront. An allowed pet adds up to $1,400 deposit at $2,800 rent; add actual paid care and any lawful space-holding deposit separately. Flights and shipping are excluded.

Separate emergency runway matters too: two months of the baseline is $9,490, approximately **$9,500**, before paid care. Do not count the same savings as both landing spending and untouched runway.

### Protect the health gap before flying

The dated [MSP waiting-period guidance](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/health-drug-coverage/msp/bc-residents/eligibility-and-enrolment/how-to-enrol/coverage-wait-period) says the balance of the residency month plus two further months. Accepted May 1, 2027 residency would normally point to August 1, 2027 coverage, subject to Health Insurance BC's decision—not automatic approval.

Apply on arrival and arrange private cover before flying. The landing pot includes only the first month's estimated premium; allow another **$600–$1,400 for June–July** if paying monthly. An insurer may collect all three months upfront. Check age/health exclusions and actual family cover; the $100 medical/dental reserve is not a substitute.

## What to do, in order

| When | Useful next step |
|---|---|
| Now to January | Get the exact Burnaby job address, shifts, start date and first payday. Ask about pension/benefit deductions and dependent cover. Gather consenting Manila references and check the child's birthday. |
| February–March | Choose two station corridors. Build a care shortlist and understand each provider's waitlist, address and priority rules. Treat $10/day as an opportunity, not the only plan. |
| March–April | Refresh two-bedroom ads, tenancy rules and care fees/funding. Price temporary lodging and bridge insurance. Compare total ongoing housing cost; do not pay an unseen landlord to “secure May”. |
| After landing May 1 | View locally, verify management authority and test the actual commute with the child and stroller. Check taps, appliances, locks, ventilation, damp, noise and real bedrooms. Resolve the exact rent, inclusions and moving fees before signing and paying a lawful deposit. |
| Before keys or immediately after | Complete the condition inspection, utilities and tenant insurance. Apply for MSP, check school registration and eligible family benefits. Keep receipts, a signed tenancy copy and an offline record folder. |

The strongest next move is small: get the job address and a payroll estimate, then price housing and child care together. A real second bedroom is valuable; so is enough cash left to manage the first few months.
