Worried your first Japan trip will become a race between stations? **Give Tokyo four nights, Kyoto three and Osaka two; fly into Tokyo and out of Kansai, and buy train tickets separately.** Set aside **¥136,988 / £684.94 per person for ten days, including a 15% reserve**, before flights and hotels. For two adults, that is **¥273,976 / £1,369.88**, using an assumed £1=¥200—not today's exchange rate.

**What you can plan here:** a relaxed first visit in October 2027, with ten days across Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka, yen spending, rail-pass maths, bags and booking reminders.

For a first Japan 10 day trip, start with nearby sights rather than a long wish list. This Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka route for October 2027 gives you a Japan 10 day budget excluding hotels, compares JR Pass with individual tickets and helps you decide whether to forward your bags.

This plan was prepared **November 5, 2026** for two UK friends, with a separate check on **November 5, 2026**. Use **October 12–21, 2027** as example dates: ten days in Japan including arrival and departure, with nine hotel nights. That is not ten full sightseeing days or ten UK-to-UK calendar days. Nothing is booked. Before paying, check your dates' fares, special closures, available rooms and tickets, and weather nearer the time. The [sources and planning notes](#sources-and-access-dates) explain the dated figures and outstanding questions.

## The route, without turning every day into a race

For convenient bases, look around Ueno/Tokyo Station, Kyoto Station and Namba in Osaka. These are area suggestions, not hotel endorsements or a promise of rooms. All times below are **Japan local time**. Give yourself flexibility: the walking and transfer durations are estimates.

| Day / date in 2027 | A manageable plan | Stay | Per-person allowance, before reserve |
|---|---|---|---:|
| 1 · Tue October 12 | Tokyo arrival, hotel bag drop, food and recovery. No timed attraction. Allow roughly 2–3 hours from touchdown to hotel, longer with queues. | Tokyo | ¥11,100 |
| 2 · Wed October 13 | Sensoji around 08:00; breakfast and Nakamise as shops open; Ueno Park and Ameyoko. Swap outdoor time for Tokyo National Museum in rain. | Tokyo | ¥10,000 |
| 3 · Thu October 14 | Meiji Jingu around 08:30 → Harajuku/Omotesando → Shibuya. Optional Shibuya SKY timed entry; daytime is fine if sunset sells out. Dispatch large luggage first if forwarding. | Tokyo | ¥15,500 |
| 4 · Fri October 15 | Nezu/Sendagi/Yanaka from about 10:00, snacks and a long cafe stop. Keep the evening free. | Tokyo | ¥9,000 |
| 5 · Sat October 16 | Reserved Tokyo/Shinagawa–Kyoto train, suggested departure around 09:30. Allow half a day overall. Hotel bag drop, then Nijo Castle around 14:00–14:30 only if comfortably on time. | Kyoto | ¥24,270 |
| 6 · Sun October 17 | Kiyomizudera around 07:00 → Sannenzaka/Ninenzaka → southern Higashiyama and public Gion streets. Lunch and a rest, not another cross-city temple circuit. | Kyoto | ¥9,700 |
| 7 · Mon October 18 | Bus/shared taxi uphill to Otagi Nenbutsuji around 09:00–09:30; downhill Saga-Toriimoto walk and Arashiyama riverside. Full version roughly 5–8 km with hills/steps. | Kyoto | ¥10,800 |
| 8 · Tue October 19 | Leave bags at hotel; Fushimi Inari around 07:00, lower gates and a partial uphill walk. Retrieve bags, lunch, conventional JR to Osaka, then Namba. Shinsekai dinner optional. | Osaka | ¥9,780 |
| 9 · Wed October 20 | Choose Osaka Castle museum **or** a late start/shopping; Kuromon for lunch, Namba browsing and Dotonbori food after dark. | Osaka | ¥11,900 |
| 10 · Thu October 21 | Ordinary Nankai Airport Express to KIX according to flight time. Aim for airport arrival about three hours before international departure; protect additional terminal/walking/disruption time. | Depart | ¥7,070 |

The budget keeps museum admission even if you skip the rainy-day swap or Osaka Castle. It allows ¥3,500 for SKY and ¥3,000 per person for one luggage shipment. Daily totals already spread out airport, intercity and bag costs; they aren't just meals and sights.

**Make space rather than add stops:** skip Nijo if the transfer runs late. In wet weather, shorten the upper Saga walk; use cafes and a shorter riverside visit instead. Yanaka and Saga are slower alternatives, not promises of empty “secret” places. Respect private-lane signs and people's privacy in Gion. If flights force a Tokyo departure, cut the second Osaka night, return on Day 9 and sleep in Tokyo before flying; that is a different route and budget.

## Why individual rail tickets win here

Use these adult ordinary-class **published prices, checked November 5, 2026**, to compare your options. Recheck before buying for October 2027:

- **Tokyo–Kyoto reserved Nozomi: ¥13,970**, regular season, from the [smartEX fare PDF dated April 1, 2025](https://smart-ex.jp/en/product/plan/service/pdf/service_fares_reserved.pdf), still linked by its [service page](https://smart-ex.jp/en/product/plan/service/). It bundles basic and limited-express fares; do not add another basic fare. Conventional station connections cost extra.
- **Kyoto–Osaka: ¥580**, plus **Namba–KIX ordinary Nankai Airport Express: ¥970**, from [JR West's comparison table, fares as of April 1, 2026](https://www.westjr.co.jp/company/ir/library/fact/pdf/2026/fact09.pdf). Osaka Station is not Namba; local transfers are separately budgeted. The airport figure is not reserved-seat Rapi:t.
- **Core basket: ¥15,520 / £77.60 per person**, or ¥31,040 for two. Tokyo arrival-airport travel and city rides are outside this basket but inside the overall budget below.

A [seven-day ordinary national JR Pass](https://japanrailpass.net/en/purchase/price/index.html) is ¥50,000 through the official online channel, or ¥53,000 via overseas JR-designated agencies. Using Hikari with the online pass plus Nankai is **¥50,970**, already **¥35,450 more** than the basket. Keeping Nozomi requires the [¥4,960 pass-holder special ticket](https://japanrailpass.net/en/use/special-ticket/): **¥55,930**, a **¥40,410 gap**. Some local JR journeys are covered, but this modest route does not justify that premium. This is a route-specific comparison, not “JR Pass is never useful.”

**Check that a pass is still sold, not just that its price page exists.** [JR West's Kansai Area Pass page](https://www.westjr.co.jp/travel-information/en/tickets-passes/jrwest-rail-pass/kansai/) says sales ended **September 30, 2026**. The ¥2,800–7,000 table left on that page is not something you can newly buy for 2027.

Use one rechargeable [IC card per person](https://www.jreast.co.jp/en/multi/pass/suica.html) for compatible local journeys. Its balance is prepaid money, not an unlimited pass or a Tokyo–Kyoto Shinkansen ticket. Do not charge both a top-up and the same resulting fare as separate spending.

## Bags and booking reminders

Measure length + width + height. [JR Central](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/info/oversized-baggage/) says ordinary luggage **over 160 cm through 250 cm** requires an oversized-baggage-space seat reservation, without an extra seat fee; over 250 cm cannot go aboard. The deck-storage trial does not remove the over-160 cm rule. Unreserved oversized luggage incurs ¥1,000 and staff-directed handling; do not plan around paying that penalty.

Want to avoid hauling cases onto the train? First ask both hotels whether they accept forwarding. Send them **October 14, requesting October 16 delivery**, and keep two nights' essentials, medicines, valuables and documents with you. [Yamato](https://www.kuronekoyamato.co.jp/ytc/en/send/preparations/ta-q-bin.html) describes next-day or two-day delivery, with route exceptions. Its [Kanto→Kansai table](https://www.kuronekoyamato.co.jp/ytc/en/search/estimate/all_list.html) lists sizes 120/140/160 at **¥1,970 / ¥2,310 / ¥2,630 per case**; confirm your size, weight and route. The ¥3,000/person allowance covers one case each on one shipment, not another Kyoto–Osaka shipment or airport delivery.

For October 16 rail, the [conventional reserved-seat window](https://www.westjr.co.jp/global/en/ticket/overview/) derives to **September 16, 2027, 10:00 JST** (02:00 UK BST). [SmartEX early bookings](https://smart-ex.jp/en/reservation/useful/accept_time/) can be requested one year ahead, but exact train/seat is unconfirmed until a month ahead; oversized-baggage seats are excluded from that early window. Two official pages disagree on the exact confirmation hour—check your actual message, not an assumed 08:00 or 14:00.

Check operators six–eight weeks before departure, then again in the final week: prices, seasonal rail calendar, closures, passport rules and bag acceptance. Flights, hotels and restaurants open sales at different times. Check each one rather than relying on a universal release date or assumed availability.

## October: comfortable layers, not a foliage guarantee

[JNTO](https://www.japan.travel/en/guide/october/) places the best Tokyo/Kyoto autumn color in November; October peak color is more associated with northern or higher places. Keep the three-city route rather than adding hotel moves to chase leaves.

For a packing guide, the [Tokyo](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/etrn/view/nml_sfc_10d.php?prec_no=44&block_no=47662&month=10&view=), [Kyoto](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/etrn/view/nml_sfc_10d.php?prec_no=61&block_no=47759&month=10&view=) and [Osaka](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/etrn/view/nml_sfc_10d.php?prec_no=62&block_no=47772&month=10&view=) JMA 1991–2020 normals for **October 11–20** give average highs/lows of **22.1/15.0°C, 23.6/14.5°C and 23.9/16.2°C** respectively. The rain figures are **70.6 / 50.7 / 48.2 mm over those ten days**, not every day and not a 2027 forecast. Bring layers, rain protection and grippy shoes. Before moving cities, check the short-range weather and train alerts.

## A few questions worth settling before you go

You can find these practical questions in the **operators' own FAQs**, read November 5, 2026. Use the linked answers to check your tickets and bags, rather than relying on a travel anecdote.

**Can someone else use the space behind the last Shinkansen row?** [JR Central asks this](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/info/oversized-baggage/#section04). Designated oversized space is for passengers with the corresponding reserved seats, not a communal free-for-all. Reserve the appropriate seat or arrange forwarding.

**Will a SKY QR code be enough, or do we need passports?** This is an explicit [SKY FAQ question](https://www.shibuya-scramble-square.com/sky/faq/). For entry from **November 19, 2026**, applicable overseas official-site and official Klook/KKday/Trip.com tickets use passport authentication for **every entrant**, matching the purchase details; QR-only entry is insufficient. This is an announced future change as of this draft's November 5 check; recheck before the October 2027 visit.

**Is SKY refundable if the rooftop closes?** The same official FAQ distinguishes **before-entry** free cancellation for eligible tickets when the roof is closed at the designated time from **no refund after entry**. Check your ticket's conditions; do not assume every bad-weather visit is refundable.
