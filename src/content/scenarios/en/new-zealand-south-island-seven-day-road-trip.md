Worried your holiday will become a race between car parks? Stay in Te Anau twice, let someone else drive Milford Road, then head north through Wānaka, Aoraki and Tekapo. This plan keeps the longest planned self-drive to about 3.5 hours, with walks you can skip without spoiling the day.

The example is for two people travelling from Sydney, from March 6, 2027 to March 13, 2027: seven nights across eight calendar dates, with the last morning reserved for departure. The NZ$4,500 envelope excludes international flights and assumes both travellers are Australian citizens. Chinese-speaking readers can use the same route, but must check their own passports and Australian visa status rather than copy that entry assumption.

The four-hour limit here means time you personally drive. Milford by coach still involves 4–6 hours of passenger road travel. If your limit means all road travel, this route does not meet it. A scenic flight would need a separate price and weather plan.

Prices and rules below retain the November 6, 2026 snapshot; this page has not refreshed them. Public tariffs and “from” prices are not confirmed March availability. The budget is a set of spending targets, not a package you can already buy. Amounts are NZD, without conversion; bare local dollar prices are treated as NZD where noted.

If access closes, keep your Te Anau night, follow the operator's cancellation/refund terms and make it a local rest day. There is no short road detour to Milford. There is no spare full day for a retry either: adding one means cutting a later overnight, not driving through the night to catch up.

## Fuel: a dated sample, not a March pump price

Regional samples were Otago $3.540/L and Canterbury $3.435/L. This private sample is not an official census, a Queenstown pump quote or a March forecast.

Main transfers are about 950 km; plan 1,100 km including local access and a modest margin. At an estimated 7 L/100 km: 1,100 ÷ 100 × 7 = 77 L; 77 × $3.486 = $268.42, rounded to $270. Milford fuel is already in the coach ticket. At $3.60/L, the same fuel costs $277.20; every $0.50/L change adds $38.50. Distance and consumption are estimates. Use the required fuel grade and fill up before remote stretches.

## Entry: your passport matters more than departure from Sydney

The following rules retain the November 6, 2026 check, not a promise of March policy. Recheck each traveller's official requirements before non-refundable spending.

- Australian citizens: normally no advance visa application, NZeTA or IVL when travelling on a valid Australian passport and meeting entry/character requirements; a resident visa is normally granted on arrival. Still complete the Traveller Declaration. See [INZ's Australian traveller guidance](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/visit/what-you-need-to-visit-new-zealand/australian-citizens-and-permanent-residents-travelling-to-new-zealand/).
- Australian permanent residents: normally need an NZeTA unless already holding a valid NZ visa; no IVL. Have current Australian PR or Resident Return Visa travel conditions allowing return to Australia, linked to the passport, and printed evidence. [Australian Resident Visa guidance](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/visas/australian-resident-visa/) does not make temporary residents eligible merely because they live in Australia.
- Other passports or Australian temporary residents: check the actual category. Visa-waiver travellers generally need an NZeTA; others may need a visa. [INZ's Chinese and Pacific traveller pathway](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/visit/what-you-need-to-visit-new-zealand/chinese-and-pacific-island-visitors-travelling-from-australia/) covers eligible Chinese and listed Pacific passport holders travelling directly from Australia with eligible Australian visas. The October 2026 update says “until further notice”; check again for March.

[Official NZeTA fees](https://nzeta.immigration.govt.nz/) were $17 via app or $23 online per person; [IVL](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/process-to-apply/applying-for-a-visa/fees-processing-times-and-refunds/paying-the-international-visitor-levy/) was $100 per person where payable. Two Australian PRs needing new NZeTAs: $34 app / $46 web, no IVL. Two non-exempt NZeTA visitors: $234 app / $246 web. NZeTA validity is two years; most requests process within 72 hours, complex cases longer. Apply early rather than relying on the usual processing time.

Complete a free [NZ Traveller Declaration](https://www.travellerdeclaration.govt.nz/about/) for each person. For this direct flight, submit from 24 hours before the Sydney journey begins. It replaces the old arrival card, has a paper fallback and is separate from NZeTA.

Clean hiking shoes and used outdoor gear before packing. [MPI's declaration guidance](https://www.mpi.govt.nz/bring-send-to-nz/bringing-and-posting-items-to-nz/how-to-declare-items-when-arriving-in-nz) covers food including packaged snacks, relevant plant/animal items, soil and used outdoor equipment. Declaring means officers assess it, not automatic permission to bring it in. If unsure, declare or ask; failure to declare can mean an instant $400 fine.

A car at $120/day instead of $90 adds $210 plus its card fee. Grand Suites at the $545 floor adds $145 against the Tekapo target. Together they exceed the $215 buffer. Price the car and Tekapo first.

After-hours charges, an eighth billed rental day, unknown cleaning fees or different entry status use the contingency or require a revised total. Major road-closure disruption is not fully funded. Helicopters, paid stargazing and premium activities are excluded. Keep the drives short, walks optional and final night near the airport; the aim is to have time to stop when the mountains come out.
