You can keep Hanoi, an overnight bay cruise, Hue, Hoi An and the Mekong without spending your nights on the road. **Take two domestic flights, use a private car on the central coast and do the Delta as a day trip from Ho Chi Minh City (HCMC). No overnight buses.** Keep the Hanoi night after the cruise rather than racing to a flight.

The example is a retired couple from Busan, both **66**, with South Korean passports. They arrive in Vietnam on **3 March 2027** and leave on **17 March 2027**: **15 days / 14 nights**, not Korea flight dates. There are **13 hotel nights + 1 cruise night**. Chinese-speaking travellers can use the same pacing ideas, but a translated guide does not transfer Korean passport rights or Korea-issued insurance eligibility to another nationality.

For both travellers, the planning total is **VND 95,680,000 / KRW 5,315,556**, including **15% contingency**. It sits below a **KRW 6,000,000** ceiling excluding international flights, with **KRW 684,444** additional headroom. This is an unquoted budget, not a promise of availability. All supplier examples were read on **7 November 2026**, not confirmed for March 2027.

## Keep the slow mornings—and make the cruise earn its place

Hanoi → Ha Long overnight → Hanoi → Hue → Hoi An via Hai Van → HCMC ↔ Mekong day trip.

The cruise is the main pacing dependency: both examples have dawn programs. **Before paying, confirm you can skip early excursions, stay aboard and eat later.** Otherwise change the cruise or omit it. Choose late-morning or afternoon domestic flights; those times have not been selected or confirmed. A long overnight train may sound relaxing, but here it trades a comfortable bed for a long transfer. The shorter afternoon heritage train is the optional rail experience.

## Entry: this visa-free assumption is specifically Korean

The [Korean MOFA notice](https://vn.mofa.go.kr/vn-ko/brd/m_2203/view.do?page=1&seq=1345931), published **11 March 2025** and amended **13 March 2025**, supports up to **45 days from entry** for Korean nationals under the policy effective **15 March 2025–14 March 2028**. This single-entry trip fits; no paid e-visa is budgeted. [Vietnam Embassy Japan](https://vnembassy-jp.org/en/vietnam-extends-visa-exemption-policy) and [government news dated 8 March 2025](https://en.baochinhphu.vn/print/viet-nam-waives-visas-for-citizens-from-12-countries-until-march-2028-111250308085955058.htm) corroborate that policy, not every entry condition.

- Keep at least six months' passport validity at entry. For 3 March 2027, that reaches **3 September 2027**; allow extra margin. Admission is still a border decision: check the admitted-until date.
- Carry return/onward evidence, hotel details and **two blank pages**. [Official tourism guidance](https://vietnam.travel/plan-your-trip/visa-requirements/) specifies the onward evidence/pages, but those additional conditions were not confirmed against immigration or Korean MOFA rules. Hotel details are prudent preparation, not an asserted universal separate rule.
- **Visa exemption does not settle the arrival declaration.** Check the [PAI portal](https://prearrival.immigration.gov.vn/) and Korean MOFA near travel. [Singapore MFA](https://hanoi.mfa.gov.sg/mission-updates/pre-arrival-declaration-for-foreign-travellers-entering-vietnam/) advises completion upon arrival or no more than **three days before**, retaining the QR. An [indexed immigration notice](https://immigration.gov.vn/khai-thi-thuc-dien-tu/cap-thi-thuc-dien-tu) recommends the system; a [Delhi embassy pilot notice dated 21 May 2026](https://vietnamembassydelhi.in/notice-on-the-implementation-of-the-pre-arrival-information-application-for-foreigners-entering-viet-nam/) says voluntary at Tan Son Nhat, while an [indexed US embassy notice dated 17 June 2026](https://vn.usembassy.gov/notice-implementation-of-pre-arrival-declaration-for-international-visitors-arriving-at-airports-in-vietnam/) says mandatory at airports. These do **not establish the compulsory status or timing for this Hanoi arrival**. No official PAI fee is established here.

If exemption stops applying or the trip changes, check the [official e-visa portal](https://evisa.gov.vn/). The published fallback is up to **90 days**, single/multiple entry; examples are **USD 25 / USD 50 per person**, equivalent to VND 650,000 / 1,300,000 and KRW 36,111 / 72,222. For two applicants: USD 50 / 100 = VND 1,300,000 / 2,600,000 = KRW 72,222 / 144,444. **The live tariff and full current portal instructions remain unconfirmed**; indexed portal excerpts and tourism guidance are not a live accepted application. Tourism guidance's **3–5 working days** is not a processing guarantee; allow extra time. These fees are outside the visa-free budget. Recheck in February 2027 and again before departure, especially before nonrefundable purchases.
