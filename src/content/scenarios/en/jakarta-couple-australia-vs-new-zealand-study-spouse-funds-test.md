You want Rina to start a master's abroad in 2027 with Bayu working beside her, you have IDR 650 million, and you cannot tell which country is the safer bet. **Short answer: money is the real problem in both countries, because the visa funds test alone is more than IDR 650 million in every example checked. If you find the funding, lean New Zealand. Choose Australia instead only if Bayu's income is your main lever.**

This guide follows Rina (about 30, IELTS 7.0) and Bayu (IELTS 5.5, with trade qualifications that may not be recognised locally, and no job assured), a couple in Jakarta. It was prepared on 8 November 2026 from official pages read that day. Nobody was contacted and nothing was applied for. "Unconfirmed" marks anything that did not come from an official page, and "assumption" marks our own planning figure. **It is general information, not legal or immigration advice.** The source materials are not published.

## The recommendation and why

**Money first, then the country.** IDR 650 million is about AUD 51,950 or NZD 64,427. The visa funds test (first-year tuition plus living costs) is about **IDR 1,289 million** for the Brisbane example, **IDR 832 million** for the Auckland 2-year data science master's and **IDR 906 million** for Auckland's 15-month accounting master's. None fits inside IDR 650 million. Bayu's wages help pay living costs over time, but they do not cover the funds test on the day you apply, and they do not cover tuition.

- **Next step:** before you choose a country, look for a scholarship, a cheaper programme, or a sponsor or loan. Then re-run the funds table below with the new fee.
- **If a funding route is found, lean New Zealand** on cost and rules. The partner of a Level 9 student gets open work rights, with no hours cap stated. The post-study work visa is 3 years, while Australia's Temporary Graduate (485) visa is 2 years, needs a fresh IELTS at 6.5 and costs A$5,750. The verified two-year cost is also lower: about IDR 2,111 million for Auckland's 2-year data science master's against IDR 2,786 million for the University of Queensland's.
- **Two limits on the New Zealand lean:** Auckland's accounting master's lasts only 15 months, and the partner visa lasts only as long as the student visa. We also could not confirm that these Auckland programmes are Level 9, which the partner work visa requires; check this first. The partner visa fee of NZ$1,355 is also **unconfirmed** (see the comparison table).
- **Choose Australia instead if Bayu's income is the main lever.** The minimum wage is A$26.44 an hour (about IDR 330,817) against NZ$23.95 (about IDR 241,632), and Home Affairs says the family of a coursework master's student can work more than 48 hours a fortnight. At full time and minimum wage, Bayu's net pay would be about IDR 495 million a year in Australia against IDR 346 million in New Zealand (a scenario, not a forecast). But Australia is the more expensive of the verified routes.
- **The 2 October 2026 Australian rule:** since 2 October 2026, a student visa applicant cannot include a partner or children unless an exemption applies, and family cannot be added after the visa is granted. Home Affairs lists an exemption for eligible nationals of ASEAN countries and names Indonesia. **"Eligible" is not defined beyond the passport list on the pages read.** **Next step:** confirm with Home Affairs or a registered migration agent that you both qualify before you pay anything. Both of you must apply together, from outside Australia.
- **How sure are we?** This is our reading of the evidence, not an official conclusion. Large gaps remain: only one Australian programme fee (the University of Queensland's) and the Auckland ones could be verified. Melbourne fees and rents, listings, the couple's health cover price and Indonesia's evidence level for Australia were not found.

## Visa and funds checklist

**Australia (Student 500).**

- A Confirmation of Enrolment, plus overseas student health cover (OSHC) for both of you from arrival.
- Genuine Student statements (Ministerial Direction 106): your ties in Indonesia, why this course and provider, and the benefit afterwards. Rina's job and tax history helps, and evidence is weighed.
- English: IELTS 6.0 is the visa minimum, taken within 2 years of lodging. Universities may ask for more (the Queensland data science master's wants 6.5 overall and no band under 6.0). Rina's 7.0 is enough.
- Funds for 12 months: first-year tuition, plus A$29,710 (student), A$10,394 (partner) and A$2,000 travel (a guide for applicants outside Australia). Show a history of funds, not a one-day balance. The alternative is partner or parent income of at least A$102,500 with family (A$87,856 without), shown by tax assessments; bank statements and employer letters are not accepted for that route.
- Fees: A$2,050 plus A$1,255 is A$3,305 (the estimator says "price starts from" and may exclude a second instalment).
- Apply from outside Australia, both of you in one application. Processing starts about 1–12 weeks after lodgement, depending on the university's allocation status rather than your nationality (Ministerial Direction 115; the 2027 planning level of 295,000 is "not a cap"). Actual processing days were not found.

**New Zealand (Fee Paying Student Visa plus Partner of a Student Work Visa).**

- An offer of place for a Level 9 programme, with tuition for 1 year (or the course, if shorter) paid or approved in principle.
- NZ$20,000 a year of living funds for Rina, NZ$4,200 for Bayu, a ticket out (or money for one) and medical and travel insurance.
- Fees: student visa NZ$485 plus a NZ$265 levy, which is NZ$750. Whether the NZ$100 International Visitor Levy applies is **not confirmed**. The partner fee is **unconfirmed**; the budget uses NZ$1,355.
- Universities: half of student visas are decided in 2 weeks and 80% in 4 weeks (working days; the page updates weekly). Apply at least 3 months ahead. The partner visa processing time was not found. The partner visa can be applied for together with the student visa but is granted only after it.

## What is still unconfirmed

- **The "eligible" ASEAN exemption:** Indonesia is named, but "eligible" is not defined beyond the passport list on the pages read.
- **Visa fees:** the New Zealand partner fee (NZ$1,355) is our inference from the nearest fee-guide row, and the NZ$100 International Visitor Levy is not confirmed. - **Bayu's pay:** the award rates are higher but his classification is not established, and his credentials and the real job market are unknown. All budget results depend on the assumptions marked above, so they are scenarios, not forecasts.
